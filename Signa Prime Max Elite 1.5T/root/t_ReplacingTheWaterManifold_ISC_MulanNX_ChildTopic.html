Replacing the water manifold

About this task

Procedure

  1. Removing the system cabinet front cover and water manifold access cover. Refer to SC Cover Removal.
  2. Disconnect the all IN and OUT quick couplers of water manifold.
    ItemDescription
    1XFA- X
    2XFA- Y
    3XFA- Z
    4XFD- PS
    5Water In
    6Water Out
    Note Be caution of water drop on the leak sensor while removing the quick couplers.
  3. Cut the tie wraps securing the actuator to the water manifold and pull out the actuator.
  4. Remove the screw securing the water manifold.
  5. Loosen the screws then remove the water manifold.
  6. Install the new water manifold in the reverse order of removal.
  7. Install the actuator back to the water manifold.
  8. Recover the system cabinet covers.

Finalization

  • Removing the Integrated System Cabinet (ISC) cover
