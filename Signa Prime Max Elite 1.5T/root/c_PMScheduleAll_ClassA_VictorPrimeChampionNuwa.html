  • Planned Maintenance
    • Planned Maintenance (PM) schedule overview

      Planned Maintenance (PM) tasks are developed from design requirements and operations/use assumptions to provide for the minimum required maintenance that maintains equipment design performance, high image quality, and safety. GE HealthCare advanced service methods are developed to maximize efficiency and use system access time as productively as possible, as described in the life cycle maintenance statement.
