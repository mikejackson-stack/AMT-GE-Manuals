Checking patient table function

Prerequisites

Personnel requirements
Required personsPreliminary requirementsProcedureFinalization
1-30 minutes-
Warning
Strong magnetic field
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field that the magnet produces.
Do not bring any ferrous tools or equipment into the magnet room.
Topic ID: task_hmh_cx1_d2b

Checking table up and down for Low Height Fixed Table

Procedure

  1. Press Down pedal on the dock FRP cover ensure that the table moves to the down most condition.
  2. Press Up pedal on the dock FRP cover ensure that the table moves to the topmost condition.
  3. Ensure that the LPCA latch comes and latches to the cradle assembly once table reaching to the top most condition.
Topic ID: task_uy5_mx1_d2b

Checking cradle in and out

Procedure

  1. Press cradle in button on the operator control panel (OCP), ensure that the cradle move inside the bore.
  2. Press cradle out button on the operator control panel (OCP), ensure that the cradle move outside the bore.
  3. Observe for smooth cradle motion without interference with the bridge.
Topic ID: task_eny_1y1_d2b

Checking emergency egress

Procedure

  1. Press cradle in button on the operator control panel (OCP) to make the cradle in about 500 mm.
  2. Make sure that you can rotate the handle on the cradle and pull the cradle out to the home position.
Topic ID: task_zvs_ry1_d2b

Checking home sensor function

Procedure

  1. Press cradle in button on the operator control panel (OCP) to make the cradle in about 500 mm.
  2. Press Down pedal on the dock FRP cover, and make sure that table is not coming down.
