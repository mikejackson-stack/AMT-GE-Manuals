Required conditions
  • System main breaker must be OFF. Refer to Applying LOTO - Integrated System Cabinet (ISC).
  • ISR ( RFM, MTD, OM) must be moved out from scan room when drained coolant.
  • Pumps must be powered off before coolant is drained.
Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.
Warning
Failure to properly drain unit of coolant will result in damage to unit due to freezing during transit.
CAUTION
Slip injury
Coolant tank is very flexible. May spill to the ground while draining the coolant.
Drain the coolant carefully to avoid coolant spill.
Important
It is recommended to discharge remaining coolant into a storage container. Dispose of coolant in accordance with local regulations.
  • Draining coolant from chiller/ICC pump and tank.
  • Draining coolant from SBGD (SBGA, SBPS).
  • Draining coolant from ISR (RFM, OM, MTD).

Draining coolant from chiller pump

About this task

CAUTION
COOLANT LEAK OUT
THE EXHAUST VALVE MUST BE CLOSED WHEN DRAINING COMPLETED.

Procedure

  1. Prepare flexible water tank and draining hose with coupling nut.
    CAUTION
    NEVER drain the unit by hand pump. It may damage the pump seal permanently!
  2. Connect the hose connector to the pump draining nipple.
  3. Check below valves, make sure they are closed.
  4. Open the exhaust valve by turn the valve counter- clockwise and drain valve, the draining will start.
    Note Must open the exhaust valve, otherwise whole tank water will be drained.
  5. Wait until the draining stops from the hose.
  6. Close the drain valve and exhaust valve.
  7. Return the coolant hose and tank.
Topic ID: task_yvw_fcs_lhb

Draining coolant from SBGD

Procedure

  1. Connect the one drain hose with manual hand pump, then connect to the component (SBGA/ SBPS).
  2. Place the other drain hose to the tank, then connect to the quick connector on the component (SBGA/SBPS).
  3. Pump the manual pump to remove the coolant.
  4. Exchange the place of pump and tank, then push out the remaining coolant.
  5. Repeat above step until the coolant was drained.
  6. Return the hand pump, coolant hose and tank.
Topic ID: task_gjw_fds_lhb

Draining coolant from ISR

Procedure

  1. Move the component (RFM / OM / MTD) out of the scan room.
    Warning
    Strong magnetic field
    The hand pump includes ferrous material, which can become a dangerous projectile in the presence of the magnetic field that the magnet produces.
    Do not drain the coolant in magnet room.
  2. Connect the one drain hose with manual hand pump, then connect to the component.
  3. Place the other drain hose to the tank, then connect to the quick connector on the component.
  4. Pump the manual pump to remove the coolant.
  5. Exchange the place of pump and tank, then push out the remaining coolant.
  6. Repeat above step until the coolant was drained.
  7. Return the hand pump, coolant hose and tank.
Topic ID: task_dsc_wds_lhb

Draining coolant from chiller tank

Procedure

  1. Unscrew the cap from the chiller tank.
  2. Prepare draining tank and draining hose with L connector.
  3. Insert L connector to the tank draining nipple. The valve inside will open automatically by connecting the mating connector and draining will start.
  4. When the coolant fills Tank almost full (about 4 minutes), remove the L connector and replace the Tank. Then, connect the L connector to nipple to restart the draining.
  5. Wait until the draining stops from the hose. (Total time about 10 minutes for two tanks).
  6. Return the coolant hose and tank.
Topic ID: task_nwf_3mr_klb

Draining the coolant from gradient coil

Procedure

  1. Move the gradient coil out of the scan room.
    Warning
    Strong magnetic field
    The hand pump includes ferrous material, which can become a dangerous projectile in the presence of the magnetic field that the magnet produces.
    Do not drain the coolant in magnet room.
  2. Place the open end of the hose into the tank.
  3. Connect the manual hand pump to the other open end of hose.
  4. Pump the manual pump to remove the coolant.
  5. Exchange the place of pump and tank, then push out the remaining coolant.
  6. Repeat above step until the coolant was drained.
  7. Return the hand pump, coolant hose and tank.
Topic ID: task_rht_cz5_qlb

Draining the coolant from ICC pump

Procedure

  1. Prepare flexible water tank and draining hose with coupling nut.
    CAUTION
    NEVER drain the unit by hand pump. It may damage the pump seal permanently!
  2. Connect the hose connector to the pump draining nipple.
  3. Close the six valves.
    Note (For Starter ICC compressor filter (5965902) replacement) Make sure the valve ⑦ is closed.
  4. Close the ball valve.
  5. Open the drain valve, the draining will start.
    Note About 5L coolant will drain out of pump during drainage.
  6. Wait until the draining stops from the hose.
  7. Return the coolant hose and tank.
