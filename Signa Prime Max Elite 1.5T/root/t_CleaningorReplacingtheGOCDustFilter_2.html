Cleaning or replacing the Global Operator Cabinet (GOC) dust filter

Follow this procedure to clean or replace the Global Operator Cabinet (GOC) dust filter.

Procedure

  1. Make sure that you have read and understood the electrical safety requirements before starting this procedure. See Electrical Safety.
  2. Shut down and power off the host computer. See Shutting down the host computer.
  3. Loosen the bracket screws on the PC rear panel and remove the bracket.
    Disregard and remove any warranty void seals on the rear PC panel.
  4. Remove the two screws that secure the left side panel of the GOC.
    Note The side panel has a short ground lead connecting it to the main chassis. When removing the side panel, do not strain this ground lead.
  5. Disconnect the short ground lead that connects the side panel to the GOC main chassis at the center of the lead.
  6. Remove the left side panel and put it in a safe place.
  7. Remove the dust filter.
  8. Clean the dust filter and reinstall it.
    • If replacing the computer, remove the Velcro strips from the old computer and install them on the new computer.
    • If the old filter or Velcro strips are not reusable, install a new set.
  9. Reconnect the ground cable and reinstall the GOC left side panel.

Finalization

  1. Start up the host computer/system. See Starting up the host computer .
  2. Do a check scan. See Doing a check scan.
