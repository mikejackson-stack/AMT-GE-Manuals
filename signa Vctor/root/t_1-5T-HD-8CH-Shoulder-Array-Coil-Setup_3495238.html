1.5T HD 8CH Shoulder Array Coil Setup

Prerequisites

Required conditions
The following coil configuration names must be installed to run this tool: HD 8ch Shoulder.

Procedure

  1. Position the coil in the cradle, connect the cable.
  2. Place phantom positioner on table.
  3. Place the phantom onto the positioner.
  4. Place coil over the phantom. Ensure that the cable rests on the cable guide notch at the bore-end of the phantom positioner (white arrow) and the coil housing fits snug against the cable guide notch.
  5. Landmark on phantom holder markings (black arrow) and advance to scan.
  6. Do MCQA test. See Multi-Coil Quality Assurance (MCQA) Tool.
