Installing the rear pedestal

About this task

Procedure

  1. Notice
    If without dollies, the weight of the rear pedestal requires at least two people.
    Move the rear pedestal with two persons to make the cables from the magnet run through it.
    1Do not handle the bar on both side when moving the rear pedestal.
    2Make the cables run through the rear pedestal.
  2. Fix the rear pedestal to the front bridge by installing four M10 hex nuts (2109877-3) and washers (2228717-2)
  3. Lock the rear pedestal to the rear endbell by installing screws on both left and right sides.
  4. Install the flexible cable holder onto the front bridge by screws.
  5. Install flexible cable screws.
  6. Route the drive belt and fix by two screws (1107-M4C016-40).
  7. Check belt tension by closing tension lever.
    Note Tension lever should lock crisply into position without excessive force (which could cause later problems). If adjustment is necessary, loosen clamp and adjust position of belt one tooth at a time until desired tension is obtained.
  8. Instal the belt cover by 4 screws (1107-M4C020-40) and trim excess belt length..
  9. (For new rear pedestal installation) Remove the sensor flag from the defective part and install on new rear pedestal.
    1Sensor Flag
  10. Install all cable connections on the right side of rear pedestal.
