Setting up 1.5T Head Neck Array (HNA) with anterior part

Procedure

  1. Position the HNU pad into the coil lower base.
  2. Position the phantom and phantom positioner on the pad.
  3. Position the TL Unified phantom. Lock the anterior part.
  4. Connect the HNA cable to P Port.
  5. Landmark on the HNA coil marking
  6. Press Advance to Scan.
  7. Press Move to Scan to move the coil to the scan position.
    Note Make sure the cables do not get snagged.
  8. Run the MCQA Check.

    Select the coil which you want to check from the menu.
