1.5T HD Knee Array Coil Setup and SNR Test

Prerequisites

Required conditions
The following coil configuration names must be installed to run this tool: HDTRKneePA, HDTRKneeService and HDTRK1chService.

Procedure

  1. Place the lower section of the coil on the patient table as shown in Place the lower section.
  2. Align the alignment mark on the coil to Zero position on the scale as shown in Align the alignment mark.
  3. Place the large cylindrical unified phantom into the Positioner as shown in Place the large cylindrical unified phantom.
  4. Place this Phantom-positioner setup on the lower part of the coil as shown in Place this Phantom-positioner.
  5. Ensure that the phantom positioner is aligned with the posterior coil section as indicated by the marks on the positioner and the coil (Phantom positioner aligned)
  6. Carefully align the connector pins on the lower part of the Coil with the upper part of the coil and latch both the coil sections into place as in Align the connector pins. (The scanner will not operate if the coil sections are not latched correctly.)
  7. Connect the coil to respective port of the system LPCA and Landmark the coil at the cross mark available near the handle of the coil (Land mark) and advance to scan.
  8. Perform the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance (MCQA) Tool.
