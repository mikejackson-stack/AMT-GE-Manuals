Overview

Follow this process to replace field replaceable units for the 1.5T HD 8 Channel Neurovascular Array by Invivo. (M3335M)

Topic ID: id_ov-1525795663636

Procedure

Cable Continuity Check

To Be Performed by Authorized Service Engineer Only. See the 1.5T HD 8 Channel Neurovascular Array Troubleshooting procedure.

Cable Replacement Procedure

Notice
To be performed by Authorized Service Engineer Only.
The 4 coil connector mounting screws are located at the superior end of the coil. Remove the 4 coil connector mounting screws . Remove the coil connector by pulling away from the coil in the superior direction.

The steps for installation of the new cable are simply the reverse order of the steps described for removal.

Mechanical Hardware Check

Exercise the superior and right/left coil latches. The latches must move smoothly and lock positively into place. The latches must not loosen or open during the course of an examination.

Mechanical Replacement of Hardware

Disassembly of Anterior Coil Section
In order to perform the following procedures:
  • Replacement of Right/Left Latch.
  • Replacement of Superior Latch.

It will be necessary to remove the anterior coil cover from the anterior coil section.

  1. Loosen, but do not remove, the 17 screws that fasten the anterior coil cover to the anterior coil section.
    Note Screws numbered 5, 6, 10, 11, and 16 are longer than the other screws.
  2. Carefully lift the anterior coil section away from the anterior coil cover.
  3. Place the anterior coil section aside, being careful to keep the screws stored in their respective holes.
  4. During FRU replacement and cover re-installation, assure that the electronics in the anterior coil section are not disturbed.
  5. The steps for cover re-installation are simply the reverse order of the steps described for removal.
Replacement of Right/Left Latch
In order to perform the Replacement of the Right/Left Latch, it will be necessary to remove the anterior coil cover from the anterior coil section. Follow the procedure detailed in Disassembly of Anterior Coil section, then continue below.
  1. Open the Right/Left Latch Assembly FRU package, and find the FRU Replacement Instructions, see Right/Left Latch Assembly FRU Replacement Instructions.
Replacement of Superior Latch
In order to perform the Replacement of the Superior Latch, it will be necessary to remove the anterior coil cover from the anterior coil section. Follow the procedure detailed in Disassembly of Anterior Coil section, then continue below.
  1. Remove the 4 screws that fasten the superior latch assembly to the anterior coil cover.

  2. Replace the superior latch using the Superior Latch Assembly FRU.
