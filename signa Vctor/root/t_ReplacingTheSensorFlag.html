Replacing the sensor flag

Procedure

  1. Move the LPCA to IN direction as shown illustration. (Reference Value 180 mm to IN direction)
  2. Check that there is Sensor Flag at position of illustration.
  3. Loosen the belt tension with belt tension lever.
  4. Remove the Sensor Flag by removing the 2 screws.
  5. Install the new sensor flag by reversing the order of the removal.
  6. To ensure the cables under bridge have been tightened by ribbons.
  7. Move table advance to reach home position, and double check there is no contact between cables (under bridge) and home sensor flag when cradle belt is moving.
