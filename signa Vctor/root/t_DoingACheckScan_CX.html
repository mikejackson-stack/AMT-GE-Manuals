Doing a check scan (for CX platform)

About this task

Procedure

  1. Follow the steps below to put the DQA III phantom on the table.
    1. Put the phantom positioner onto the table. R/L direction will be fit.
    2. Put the phantom onto the positioner and make sure that it is level and not rotated.
  2. Landmark on the center line of the phantom. The laser must be in the middle of the line. Press Advance to Scan.
  3. From the Dashboard, to begin a scan, click the New Patient icon.
  4. From the New Patient screen, enter patient ID (other than geservice) into the Patient ID field and enter patient weight (e.g., 111 lbs or 50 kg ) into the Weight field.
  5. From the New Patient screen, select I accept First level Mode dB/dt and First Level Mode SAR*.
  6. Click Accept & Begin Exam.
  7. Open the Protocol screen, and click the Protocol icon from the Task Manager scan controls toolbar.
  8. Select 3-plane localizer protocol from the Protocol list drag and drop or double-click it.
  9. Edit the protocol, click the Setting icon, and select Classic Mode.
  10. Click Connector icon and check the coil setting is right..
  11. Set Anatomy, then click Apply.
  12. Click Accept and Begin Exam.
  13. Enter the following image parameters.
    OptionDescription
    Frequency field of view (FOV) (Freq. FOV)30.0
    Phase FOV1.00
    Slice Thickness5.0
  14. When complete, click End Exam from the bottom of the Task List.
  15. Review the images to make sure that the orientation is correct.
    Axials:
    1GE logo in upper right corner
    2A is shown
    Coronal & Sagittal:
    3Comb in upper left corner
    4CS shown in upper right corner
