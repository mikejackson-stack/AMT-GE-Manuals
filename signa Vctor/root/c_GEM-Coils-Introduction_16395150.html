Environmental Requirements

Storage Requirements
All coils should be stored in the scan room.
For HNU Coil
  • Dimensions: L 495 mm (19.5 in) x W 388 mm (15.3 in) x H 354 mm (13.9 in)
  • Weight: 8.8 kg (19.4 lb)
For AA Coil
  • Dimensions as stored on the patient positioner: L 556 mm (21.9 in) x W 673 mm (26.5 in) x H 280 mm (11 in)
  • Weight: 6.3 kg (7.9 lb)
For PA Coil and PA Filler
  • Dimensions: L 1288 mm (50.7 in) x W 414 mm (16.3 in) x H 100 mm (3.9 in)
    Note Length includes the system cable.
  • Weight: 6.8 kg (14.9 lb)
For PV Coil
  • Dimensions in folded position for storage: L 697 mm (27.4 in) x W 642 mm (25.3 in) x H 248 mm (9.8 in)
  • Weight: 9.2 kg (20.2 lb)
