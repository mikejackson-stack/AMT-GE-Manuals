Checking the Emergency Off function

Procedure to verify the Emergency Off function.

Prerequisites

Required conditions
The host software is shut down before turning off the GOC host breaker.

About this task

An Emergency Off button is located on the optional GE Main Disconnect Panel. The other Emergency Off buttons are located by the customer.

Note The AC power for the oxygen monitor, Magnet Rundown Unit (MRU) and the magnet monitor are not controlled by the Emergency Off button.

Procedure

  1. Make sure that the ICN and Host PC (GOC Host) is shut down. See Shutting down the ICN and host computer.
  2. Turn off the GOC breaker on PDU.
    1GOC Breaker
    Note If you do not turn off the breakers, the host computer restarts when the power is applied. You must wait for the host computer to boot before you can test the additional Emergency Off buttons.
  3. Press one of the Emergency Off buttons (the location of buttons is determined by the customer).
    Verify that power was removed from the PDU by checking the input voltage test points.
  4. On the optional GE Main Disconnect Panel, switch the Integrate System Cabinet Breaker and System Power Switch to the On position.
    Note When you press the EMO Reset button, you should hear the contactor engage. This is another indication that the Emergency Stop button was pressed and is active.
    1Compressor breaker
    2ISC breaker
    3System power switch
    4Compressor ON LED
    5ISC power ON LED
    6Emergency power off button
    7Chiller #1 breaker
    8Chiller #2 breaker
  5. Repeat the above steps for the other Emergency Off buttons.
    Normal locations for the Emergency Off buttons are in the magnet room and in the operator room.
  6. Turn on the GOC breaker on the PDU.
