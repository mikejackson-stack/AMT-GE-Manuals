Lowering the dockable table cradle

Make primary latch adjustments and lower the cradle.

Procedure

  1. Remove the support and lower the cradle.
  2. Install the two washers and two screws securing the cradle to the tabletop. Turn the screws just until they are snug.
  3. Dock the table.
  4. Move the cradle into the bore until the primary latch assembly is exposed.
