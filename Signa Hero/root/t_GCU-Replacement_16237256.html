Procedure

  1. Release the bands from the shipping base, flip the slide plate, and slide the FRU Box.
  2. Open GCU FRU Box, turn the top cover upside down.
  3. Open ICC Cover and remove it.
  4. Confirm that ISC power is off and LOTO is performed. Then, check the followings.
    1. GCU/CCU pressure meter (black meter) is at zero.
    2. ICC Control Unit TCU display is OFF.
  5. Turn off the F50 Power.
    1. Turn off the “Drive Switch”.
    2. Turn off the “Main Power Switch”.
  6. Shutdown Chiller or open bypass loop per hospital facility chiller valve integrity.
  7. Notice
    Close the facility water valve for the ICC for safe operation.
    Turn OFF the supply and return valves for Facility Water in FPU.
  8. Drain the supply water to release the water pressure per following steps.
    1. Connect the hose to drain port of water supply.
    2. Insert the other side of hose into the flexible tank.
    3. Open valve and check that the pressure meter becomes zero.
    4. Close the valve and remove the hose.
  9. Drain the coolant in GCU Tank.
    1. Unscrew the cap from the GCU tank.
    2. Prepare two 20L Draining Tanks and draining hose with L connector.
    3. Insert L connector to the tank draining nipple. The valve inside will open auto-matically by connecting the mating connector and draining will start.
    4. When the coolant fills Tank to half full (about 4 minutes), remove the L connector once and replace the Tank. Then, connect the L connector to nipple to restart the draining.
    5. Wait until the draining stops from the hose. (Total time about 10 minutes for two tanks).
  10. Drain the coolant in GCU Pump.
    1. Prepare 20L Draining Tank and draining hose with coupling nut.
    2. Connect the hose connector to the pump draining nipple.
    3. Open the valve and draining will start.
    4. Wait until the draining stops from the hose. (Minimal coolant in the pump.)
    5. Close the valve which is opened in Step 10.3.
  11. Loosen screws around the ICC rear cover and remove the ICC rear cover by lifting up a little.
    Note If there is any difficulty to remove the cover, remove screws.
  12. Notice
    Water Spill Notice!
    Quick disconnect connector for GCU does not have auto shutoff valve.
    Fold and bind each GCU hoses using tie wraps to prevent coolant spill.
    At Scan Room, fold and bind each GCU hoses using tie wrap to prevent from draining.
  13. Hold the towel by one hand under the hose and disconnect hose connectors of GCU. Then, connect the hoses together until you hear the click sound.
    Note Here is the tips how to connect the quick disconnect for VRMw.

    1. Pull the coupler grip.

    2. Insert the male connector.

    3. Push both connectors together until you hear the click sound.

  14. Disconnect the Blue Quick Disconnect hoses for GCU and CCU.
    Note CCU connectors are removed since they prevent the GCU from being slid out.
    Note When disconnecting the Blue Quick Disconnect hoses, disconnect quickly to avoid excessive spilling of coolant

  15. Disconnect 2X1 and 2X11 Cables from Control Unit.
  16. Setup Hoist. Refer to Hoist Setup.
  17. Place FRU Box top cover in front of the ICC.
  18. Loosen four screws and carefully withdraw GCU from the chassis.
    Note Before withdrawing the GCU, lift up the stopper.
  19. Attach the lifting brackets to both sides of the GCU. The lifting brackets can be found in the GCU FRU crate.
    Warning
    Potential crush hazard
    If the replacement part accidentally falls, it can cause serious injury.
    Do not put any part of the body below the replacement part.
  20. Hook the winch and spreader bar from the hoist kit to the lifting bracket.
  21. Note Make sure the hook is seated in the lifting bracket. If not, the weight can shift causing harm to the equipment or engineer.
    Ratchet the winch to tighten the chain.
  22. Press the release tabs in on each side and remove the 2nd slide rail from 1st slide rail on GCU.
  23. Push the rail slider into the cabinet before lowering GCU.
  24. Use the hoist and slowly lower the GCU Chassis to the top FRU Box.
  25. Remove the slide rails from GCU.
  26. Slightly lift up the GCU, rotate the FRU top cover by 90 degree, and lower the GCU into the FRU Box.
  27. Remove lifting brackets and attach them to the top of new GCU.
  28. Move the new FRU in front of ICC.
  29. Using Hoist, slightly lift up new FRU, rotate the box by 90 degree, and install slide rails.
  30. CAUTION
    Pinch Hazard!
    Slowly and carefully slide and restore GCU to the ICC not to pinch the finger between GCU and ICC.
    Restore the GCU by reverse order of removal.
  31. Restore all hoses.
    Note Hold the towel under the hose when restoring the hoses.
    Note Do not forget to restore the VRMw Hoses
    Note Here is the tips how to connect the quick disconnect for VRMw.

    1. Pull the coupler grip.

    2. Insert the male connector into the coupler.

    3. Push both connectors together until you hear the click sound.

  32. Restore all cables.
  33. Open valve for Facility water.
  34. Restart Chiller or close bypass loop for ICC facility water supply per hospital facility chiller valve integrity.
  35. Transfer the coolant to the other empty tank using funnel about 3~4L (1 gallon or so) for one hand operation.
  36. To prepare for a safe coolant refill, turn the sub breakers at the ISC Power Distribution Unit (PDU) off in the order below.
    1. (For the first rotation of coolant refill only) Turn the RF Amp sub breaker off.
    2. (For the first rotation of coolant refill only) Turn the Gradient sub breaker off.
    3. Turn the System Cooling sub breaker off.
  37. Remove LOTO. Refer to Section 4.5 in Removing LOTO - Integrated System Cabinet (ISC). (First rotation of refill only. Skip from second rotation.)
  38. Refill the coolant per following steps.
    1. Remove cap of GCU tank. Then, insert the tank funnel extension and funnel into the opening on top of the tank. (For first rotation of refill only. Skip from second rotation.)
    2. Notice
      When restoring the coolant from the tank, special care needs to be taken not to spill over the machine or floor.
      Pour the coolant into the funnel to fill the tank so that it will be within green level.
      Note Use the tank with 3~4 L (1 gallon) coolant for refill. If it becomes empty, refill the coolant.
    3. Turn on the “System Cooling” sub breaker at ISC.
    4. Wait for about 30 seconds and check the following conditions. If not, continue from Step 35 to Step 38.
      Note Water level in the tank will reduce after the first several pump operations and coolant can be refilled.

      Confirm the following bullets

      • No leakage at hose connections.

      • Indicator “5” of TCU is shown.

      • GCU pressure meter is in between 4.5 and 6.9 bar.

      • All drained coolant is restored into the tank.

    5. (For ICC with vent valve with transparent hose Only) Pace the plastic bag under transparent hose.
      Note Vent valve with transparent hose is removed from GCU/CCU after end of Q4, 2017 per VCP with engineering analysis. No operation is needed if not exist.

      Then, turn the vent valve using square section key by 3 turns and wait for 10 seconds.

      Then, close the venting valve. By doing this, the air bubbles in the pump are vented away.

      Note There is a case that nothing comes out from the transparent hose
    6. Remove funnel and extender. Then, restore cap to GCU.
  39. Turn on the “Gradient” and “RF Amp” sub breaker at ISC.
  40. Turn on F-50 power.
    1. Turn on the “Main Power Switch”.
    2. Turn on the “Drive Switch”.
  41. Check that GCU is correctly running and there is no leakage from hoses connected to GCU.

Finalization

  1. Perform check scan.
  2. Disassemble the hoist kit and restore it to the stored location.
  3. Package the FRU Box crate including the defective part and return.
