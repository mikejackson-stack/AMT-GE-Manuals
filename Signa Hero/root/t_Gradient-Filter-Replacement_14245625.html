Procedure

  1. Remove left front cover per following steps.
    1. Open left front cover and remove a screw to disconnect GND cable.
    2. Unlock the cover.
    3. Lift up and remove the cover.
    Note: If the service area is narrow, do the same for right front cover.
  2. Drain the coolant.　Refer to Draining Operation before replacing RF Amp, SSSA, or SSSPS.
  3. Warning
    Failure to properly drain unit of coolant will result in damage to unit due to freezing during transit.
    Disconnect the IN and OUT quick couplers of corresponding SSSA from the water manifold.
    aRF Amp
    bSSSA (X)
    cSSSPS
    dSSSA (Y)
    eSSSA (Z)
  4. Connect the IN and OUT of quick coupler, and tie the hose as illustration.
  5. Set the Universal Lift Hoist onto ISC top.　Refer to Hoist Setup.
  6. Perform the following steps.
    1. Disconnect two connectors.
    2. Loosen six screws.
    3. Remove Leak sensor assy.
  7. Loosen fixing screws and remove ISC PW Cover by lifting up a little.
    Note: If furnished closet door prevents from removing, the PW cover can be separated in two pieces by removing 6 screws at center.
  8. Remove Gradient Cable Cover per following steps.
    1. Loosen 12 screws.
    2. Lift up a little and remove the Gradient Cable Cover.
  9. Remove three Gradient Filter Covers by removing two screws per each cover.
  10. Perform the following steps.
    1. Disconnect Gradient Cable using 8 inch adjustable wrench.
    2. Remove 17 screws fixing G-Filter and Pen Panel.
    3. Notice
      Please note that the two connection screws should be removed once replacement is completed. Otherwise, SSSA itself won’t be removed from ISC chassis in case of trouble.
      Fix G-Filter to SSSA using two connection screws.
  11. Disconnect cables and Loosen 4 screws from SSSA.
    1. Disconnect the all connectors and terminals from front panel of SSSA.
    2. Loosen 4 screws tightening SSSA front panel to the chassis.
    3. Withdraw SSSA.
  12. Install the hoist brackets to the chassis of SSSA with 6 screws.
  13. Perform the following steps.
    1. Hook the winch and spreader bar from the hoist kit to the lifting bracket named “SSSA WITH FILTER”.
    2. Ratchet the winch to tighten the chain.
    3. Press the release tabs in on each side and remove the 2nd slide rail from 1st slide rail.
  14. Push the rail slider into the cabinet before lowering.
  15. Use the hoist and slowly lower the SSSA Chassis to the floor.
    Note: When lowering the SSSA Chassis, it may be easier to rotate SSSA 90 degree as following illustration.
  16. Remove gradient filter.
    1. Remove two screws.
    2. Slowly pull out Gradient Filter from SSSA.
  17. Install new Gradient Filter to SSSA and fix them with two screws.
  18. Notice
    Do not forget to remove the two screws connecting Gradient Filter and SSSA at Penetration Panel side once installation is done. Otherwise, SSSA itself won’t be removed from ISC chassis in case of trouble and it may damage the cabinet.
    Remove two screws.
    Notice
    When tightening the Gradient cable, use 8 inch adjustable wrench and stop tightening when white block insulator start rotating. If white block insulator is damaged by rotating, insulation of the part will be lost.
    Gradient Cable Connection
    Install SSSA by reverse order of removal.
    Note: Make sure to connect all cables.

Finalization

  1. Restore the Power. Refer to Removing LOTO - ISC.
  2. Perform DQA II.
  3. Perform check scan.
