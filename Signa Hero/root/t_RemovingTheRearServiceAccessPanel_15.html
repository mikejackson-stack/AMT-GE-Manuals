Removing the magnet rear service access panel

Removes the rear service access panel from the magnet to allow access to components mounted on the left-hand side of the magnet.

Prerequisites

Personnel requirements
Required personsPreliminary requirementsProcedureFinalization
1-5 minutes-
Required conditions
Front service cover is removed.

Procedure

  1. Slide the rear service access panel rearward until it stops.
  2. Lift the panel upward and remove it from the magnet enclosure.
