Removing LOTO - MDP

Remove LOTO from the Main Disconnect Panel (MDP) or facility Power Distribution Unit (PDU).

About this task

Notice
Potential B0 drift interference
B0 drift can be affected if the gradient coil temperature is not cooled down.
You must wait 1 hour after restoring power before doing certain calibrations and/or a customer scan.

Procedure

  1. Alert necessary personnel that power is being restored.
  2. Remove the lock and tag.
  3. Turn on the main breaker of the MDP or facility PDU.
  4. Rotate the larger main breaker labeled INPUT clockwise to the red On (1) position
  5. Press the green EMO RESET button.
