Warning
ELECTROCUTION HAZARD!
Dangerous and or fatal voltages are present in the energized cabinet.
Follow LOTO steps as given in first section of this document to disable and verify safe voltage levels.

Procedure

  1. Perform LOTO for ISC. Refer to Lockout / Tagout for ISC.
  2. Open ICC Cover.
    Note If Service space is narrow, remove the door.
  3. Remove the bottom reinforcement bar by removing 6 screws.
  4. Turn F50 Power OFF.
    1. Turn off the “Drive Switch”.
    2. Turn off the “Main Power Switch”.
  5. Confirm that ISC and Cryocooler power are OFF.
  6. Close the following valves.
    Notice
    Facility water valves position needs to be restored after replacement. Please check the position before closing. If Facility has water flow meter, record the flow value.

    Close shut-off valves of FPU.

  7. Drain the ICC primary water circuit to the tank per following steps.
    1. Connect the draining hose to the emergency valve (a) and place the Service Tank to the other side. Then, open valve of (a).
    2. If no more coolant leak from (a), connect the other hose to emergency valve (b) and connect hand pump to the other end. Then, open valve of (b).
      Note Leave the valve (a) opened.
    3. Push out the remaining coolant to Service Tank.
      Note Leave the valve (a) and (b) opened.
    4. Once draining is completed, close valves (a), (b), and (c). Then, remove hoses.
  8. Close water valves for F-50.
  9. Notice
    Wear a pair of gloves to protect your hand.
    Disconnect F-50 supply and return hoses from the elbow rotating adaptors.
    Note While rotating the hex head connector, the hose will also follow the rotation movement. Since the elbow of connector rotate freely, rotate the hose backward as needed.
    Note Remaining water in the hose will spill. Prepare towels before disconnecting.
  10. Notice
    If for facility coolant water includes chemical, follow the local regulation for disposal of coolant water.
    Disconnect the FPU sensor cable connector from 3X11 of Control Unit.
  11. Disconnect GCU and CCU Quick Disconnects.
  12. Close the valves for F–50.
  13. Slide out the FPU to access to the FPU sensor per following steps.
    1. Loosen a captive screw.
    2. Slide the FPU out from ICC to access to the FPU Sensor.
  14. Remove the sensor according to the following steps.
    1. Remove the pin which is fixing the sensor.
    2. Pulling out the sensor to up direction.
  15. Confirm the followings before installing new FPU sensor.

    • Water shield o-ring must be at the connector top (border) shown below.

    • Barcode label is the front side when installing.

  16. Install FPU sensor by reverse order of removal.
    1. Install new sensor assy.
    2. Insert Pin.
    3. Restore FPU.
    4. Fix FPU to chassis with captive screw.
  17. For F-50 water hose connectors (supply and return), remove the remaining shield tape with tool such as precision driver for both connectors. Then, wind the shield tape by two turns.
  18. Restore the hose connectors and cables.
    1. Restore Supply and Return Hose connectors for F-50.
    2. Restore Quick disconnects for GCU and CCU.
    3. Restore disconnect cable to 3 x 11 connector of Control Unit.
  19. Open the valves in the following order.
    1. Slightly open facility water valves.

      1. Open Return valve.

      2. Slightly open Supply valve.

    2. Open shutoff valves for FPU.
    3. Open valves for F50.
    4. Check there is no water leakage at water connectors.
    5. Open valves of supply and return of the facility water as it was. If facility flow meter exists, adjust the water flow as recorded.
    Note Specification of facility water flow is between 50L/min and 80L/min (13.2gpm~21.1gpm) Typical Value of water flow is 60L/min (15.8gpm).
  20. Restore bottom reinforcement bar with 6 screws

Finalization

  1. Turn on the F50 cryocooler.
    1. Turn on the “Main Power Switch”.
    2. Turn on the “Drive Switch”.
  2. Turn on ISC Power. Refer to Removing LOTO - Integrated System Cabinet (ISC).
  3. Perform Water Flow Adjustment per following steps.
    1. Insert SSA Service Key.
    2. Open Common Service Desktop and select Diagnostics > System > Power/Support System > Cabinet Monitor Functional Diag.
    3. Click Run and check Customer Coolant Flow Rate and check if displayed Customer Coolant Flow Rate is in between 50L/min and 80L/min.
      Note Typical Value of water flow is 60L/min (15.8gpm)
    4. If the displayed value is between 50L/min and 80L/min, go to next step (3.5)

      If coolant flow rate is out of specification, adjust the FPU bypass valve until the coolant flow satisfies the specification.

      • To increase Facility Water Flow : Turn the Bypass valve to Clockwise. Maximum Water Flow when Bypass valve is at Vertical Position.

      • To reduce Facility Water Flow : Turn the Bypass valve to Counter Clockwise. Minimum Water Flow when Bypass valve is at Horizontal position.

      Note Click Run Button of Functional Diag each time when measuring.
    5. Check the F-50 Water Flow by Magnet Monitor. (Press Data key, then press Down key to show Water_Flow)
    6. Adjust the water flow by adjusting the F-50 supply water valve so that water flow becomes within 7~10 L/min. If it’s difficult to adjust the water flow within 7~10 L/min, adjust the water flow by referring to admissible range (water flow vs. water temperature) shown in Cryocooler Water Cooling Requirements.
  4. Perform check scan.
