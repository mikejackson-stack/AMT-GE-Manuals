About this task

Overview

Limit Switch Location is shown below.

1Up Limit Switch
2Down Limit Switch

Procedure

  1. Perform the following steps to remove two covers.
    1. Remove five screws and slide out the Motor Cover.
    2. Remove two rubber caps, remove six screws, and remove side cover. Do the same for the other side.
  2. (For Telescopic Cover) Move the table to the down limit and remove the six screws which fix the top telescopic cover.
  3. Move the table to the up limit position.
  4. (For Bellows Cover)
    1. Remove the six screws securing the bottom of the bellows cover; three on the left-hand side and three on the right-hand side.
    2. Lift up the bellows cover and secure it in the up position with six strips of Velcro.
  5. Perform LOTO for ISC.
  6. Insert the screw rod and rotate it to clockwise till it engages to carriage plate.
  7. Disconnect cable connector for Limit switch.
  8. Remove the two screws and remove the defective limit switch.
  9. Restore the Limit Switch by reverse order of removal.

Finalization

  1. Turn the system power ON. Refer to Removing LOTO - ISC.
  2. At the top most position, measure the height difference between the guide ridge (cradle rolling area) of tabletop and guide ridge (cradle rolling area) of the bridge. (Spec: within 1 mm)

    If adjustment is needed, refer to Leveling Table and Top Height Adjustment.

  3. Restore the telescopic or bellows cover.
  4. Perform Table Functional Check.
  5. Perform one test scan.
