About this task

This procedure describes the replacement procedure for two USB extender cables (ISC internal cable(ICE to ISC top) and system cable (GOC to ISC)).

Note It’s important to replace both cables. Especially root part number of both cable should be the same after the replacement. (e,g. 5725077 and 5725077-2)

Procedure

  1. Warning
    ELECTROCUTION HAZARD!
    Cabinet top area that includes power cable wiring Will be accessed in this procedure.
    perform LOTO at MDP or Facility PDU.
    Perform LOTO for MDP(Main Disconnect Panel) or Facility PDU .
  2. Remove cable duct covers per following steps.
    1. Loosen two bottom screws, remove two screws, and lift up Lower Cable Duct Cover to remove it.
    2. Loosen one top screw, remove one screw, and lift up Upper Cable Duct Cover to remove it.
  3. Open clamp and velcro band. Then, disconnect USB cable from ICE.
  4. Cut tie wraps that is connecting the USB cables in the cable duct.
  5. Remove two screws and loosen one screw. Then, slide the plate to the left and remove it.
  6. Remove ISC connector cover by removing 4 screws.
  7. Perform the following steps.
    1. Loosen 4 screws from right Top FAN and remove it.
    2. Flip and put it on the top of the Cabinet.
  8. Loosen two screws and remove top plate cover.
  9. Remove USB Cable to the front of the cabinet per following steps.
    1. Access USB Cable from the opening of Fan.
      Note For USB Cable (5725077–2), remove a screw which is connecting the cable clamp to the chassis.
    2. Gradually pull the cable to the front.
    3. Remove the cable to the front.
  10. Restore new USB cable in reverse order of the removal for ISC.
    Note For USB Cable (5725077–2), fix the cable clamp to the chassis with a screw.
  11. Route the USB extender system Cable (E3037) from GOC to ISC and connect it to both Z420 and ISC top cable.
    Note For time saving, leave the old USB extender system cable as routed on the cable tray. If old cable is left on the tray, bent each ends of the cable, secure them with tie wraps, and put tapes (e.g. packing tape) on each ends with hand writing note as "Don’t use".

Finalization

  1. Restore the Power. Refer to Removing LOTO - Main Disconnect Panel (MDP).
  2. Confirm that ice_term opens without any failure by referring to Running ice_term.
  3. Perform check scan.
