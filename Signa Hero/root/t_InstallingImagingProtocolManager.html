Installing Imaging Protocol Manager (IPM)

Provides guidance on installing the Imaging Protocol Manager (IPM) option.

Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

About this task

Imaging Protocol Manager (IPM) is a cloud-based protocol management software solution that helps to maintain and standardize modality devices (for example, CT and MR) protocols at a centralized location by creating protocol libraries, standardizing imaging workflows, identifying protocol variations, improving adherence to protocol compliance set up by the clinical practice to meet regulatory requirements, and achieving improved quality of care, operational efficiency, and optimized patient experience.
There are two parts to the service documentation for this feature:
  • MR: This manual covers the procedures completed on the MR scanner to connect it to the IPM application.
  • IPM application: See Imaging Protocol Manager (IPM) Restricted Service Manual (DOC2090972) in the online documentation library for procedures that are done on the IPM application to connect it to the MR scanner.
Important If this is your first time connecting an MR scanner to IPM, it is strongly recommend you work with an experienced TSE/RSL/OLE. Confer with the IPM Project Manager to determine the best support strategy for doing this procedure.

Protocol import/export criteria

Only site protocols are available to be exported or imported through the IPM application. The whole protocol (directory) is considered as a unit and is transferred during the pull/push operation. Embedded images in protocol notes are also transferred as part of the protocol.

Compatibility checkers on the cloud and on the device check equality for fields such as grad coil and field strength before pushing and committing the protocol. When a new protocol from the device is imported into IPM on the cloud, it is assigned a unique identifier based on the timestamp and its MAC address. When a protocol is sent to the device from IPM, it also is given a unique identifier. The MR scanner must be InSite checked out to enable registering with the cloud.

General IPM workflow on the scanner
  • Register: Registers scanners into the organization registry. Connects and reconnects the MR scanners.
  • Pull: Imports protocols to the cloud library from multi-modality scanners (CT, MR) and allows basic editing.
  • Baseline: Forms a standard library in the cloud containing the protocols required to be pushed to the MR scanners.
  • Push: Distributes protocols to MR scanners throughout the hospitals within the organization.
  • Commit: Replaces/replaces all the protocols available in the MR scanner with the protocols pushed to the individual MR scanners.
  • Restore: Restores the MR scanner to an earlier revision of protocols after a Commit.
