Replacing the dual drive RF amplifier

This procedure describes how to replace the dual drive RF amplifier.

Required conditions

System power must be OFF. Apply LOTO to the ISC. See Applying LOTO - Integrated System Cabinet (ISC).
Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

Warning
Electrocution hazard
High voltage present.
Use proper LOTO procedures before servicing and/or maintaining.
Warning
Possible personal injury or equipment damage
This unit could fall when using the hoist tool.
Before putting weight on the hoist chain, be sure that the links are aligned properly. This helps keep the chain from binding up or becoming kinked, which is nearly impossible to correct when the chain is under tension.

About this task

This document contains dual drive RF amplifier replacement procedure. This unit is in the Integrated System Cabinet (ISC) (shown below). The dual drive RF amplifier unit is about 152 kg (335 lb). The unit weighs enough that the use of a hoist kit is required. This document details the correct way to use a hoist kit to remove the dual drive RF amplifier unit and replace with the field replaceable unit (FRU) safely.

Procedure

  1. Make sure that you have read and understood the electrical safety requirements before starting this procedure. See Electrical Safety.
  2. Apply LOTO to the ISC. See Applying LOTO - Integrated System Cabinet (ISC).
  3. Remove the left front cover per the following steps.
    CAUTION
    Power must be off at minimum 5 minutes before the removal of the RF amplifier.
    1. Open the left front cover and remove a screw to disconnect the GND cable.
    2. Unlock the cover.
    3. Lift up and remove the cover.
    Note If the service area is narrow, do the same for the right front cover.
  4. Do the Hoist Setup procedure to set up the hoist.
  5. Disconnect the connector and remove the water leak sensor tray for the RF amplifier by removing two screws.
  6. Drain the coolant. Do the Draining Operation before replacing RF Amp, SSSA, or SSSPS.
  7. Remove the bracket above the RF amplifier by removing two screws.
  8. Note Before the next step, prepare to absorb any leakage from the coolant lines with a towel in hand as the line is connected. Do not let any fluid touch the white leak sensor strips. The sensor strips are located in the cabinet in the front and under the RF amplifier.
    Disconnect the cables on the front of the dual drive RF amplifier, and disconnect the coolant lines.
  9. Carefully move the cables and lines to the side of the cabinet, allowing the surrounding area to be free from any obstruction of movement for when the amplifier will be pulled out.
  10. Unscrew the six attachment screws. The Prodrive RF amplifier has four attachment screws.
  11. Using the two front handles, carefully slide out the dual drive RF amplifier unit.
  12. Attach the lifting brackets to both sides of the dual drive RF amplifier. The lifting brackets can be found in the dual drive RF amplifier FRU crate. The attachment screws are captive screws and remain with the lifting brackets.
    Warning
    Potential crush hazard
    If the replacement part accidentally falls, it can cause serious injury.
    Do not put any part of the body below the replacement part.
  13. Do the following steps.
    1. Hook the winch and spreader bar from the hoist kit to the lifting bracket.
    2. Ratchet the winch to tighten the chain.
    3. Press the release tabs in on each side out the RF amplifier.
  14. Remove the top of the wooden crate and turn it on its base.
  15. Ratchet the winch to lower the dual drive RF amplifier into the package.
  16. Remove the hook from the lifting bracket.
  17. Unscrew the lifting brackets from the sides of the dual drive RF amplifier.
  18. Roll the removed dual drive RF amplifier to the side.
  19. Remove the center sections of the FRU crate.
  20. Bring the FRU to the front of the cabinet.
  21. Attach the slide rails to the FRU.
  22. Attach the lifting brackets to the sides of the new amplifier.
  23. Install the dual drive RF amplifier using the reverse order of the removal.
    Note Put the lifting brackets and attachment screws back into the replacement FRU crate.
  24. Connect cables and coolant lines to the dual drive RF amplifier.
  25. Disassemble the hoist kit.

Finalization

  1. Set the RF amplifier breaker to the OFF position.
    Notice
    The RF amplifier may be damaged if power is on without coolant inside. To avoid this situation, set the RF amplifier breaker to the OFF position before powering ON for a new FRU.
  2. Restore the power. Remove ISC LOTO. See Removing LOTO - Integrated System Cabinet (ISC).
  3. Wait until the CCU pressure meter (black meter) moves for about 40 seconds.
  4. Turn the RF amplifier breaker to the ON position.
  5. Do RF Amplifier and UPM Calibration .
  6. Do Dual drive quadrature tool.
    Note

    For MR31.0 and later, Dual drive quadrature tool has been retired, please perform Calibrating for quadrature.

  7. Do Doing the System Performance Test (SPT).
    Note If all finalization tasks are not completed, a warning window will show. More information can be found in System Health Alert
  8. Do a check scan. See Doing a check scan.
  9. After attaching the center sections of the FRU package to the removed FRU unit base, attach the top of the FRU crate to prepare the FRU for return.
    Note Make sure the lifting brackets with the attachment screws are in the FRU crate before returning.
    Note The dual drive RF amplifier has two center sections.
