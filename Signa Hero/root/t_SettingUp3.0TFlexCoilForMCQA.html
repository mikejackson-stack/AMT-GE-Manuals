Setting up 3.0T Flex coils for MCQA

Prerequisites

Note Coils are not shipped with phantoms. Phantoms come in a unified phantom set with the system.

Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

Procedure

  1. Remove any other surface coils or pads from the table.
  2. (For 16ch Large Flex Array) follow below steps:
    1. Place two TL Unified Phantoms on the cradle top and aligned in the S/I direction. Make sure two phantoms are aligned and touch each other on the short edge.
    2. Lay the coil on top of the intersection of the phantoms. Ensure the cable exit to the left side of the bore. Secure the coil to the phantom with strap if needed.
  3. (For 16ch Medium Flex Array) follow below steps:
    1. Place the pad on the table.
    2. Wrap the coil to the Large Cylindrical Unified Phantom, and secure the coil to the phantom with velcro tape, then place it on the pad. Ensure the cable exit to the right side of the bore.
  4. (For 16ch Small Flex Array) follow below steps:
    1. Place the pad on the table.
    2. Wrap the coil to the Small Cylindrical Unified Phantom, and secure the coil to the phantom with velcro tape, then place it on the pad. Ensure the cable exit to the right side of the bore.
  5. Route the coil cable to the center of the table, and connect the cable to the P2 port.
    Note Please avoid looping the cable.
  6. Position the laser light crosshairs to align with the landmark on the coil. Landmark on the center of the coil shown as below.
  7. Press Advance to Scan.
  8. Run the MCQA check from Service Browser > Image Quality tab.
    Note Select 3.0T_16Ch_Flex_Large_Array_by_RFT (or equivalent platform option) in the Current Coil field.
