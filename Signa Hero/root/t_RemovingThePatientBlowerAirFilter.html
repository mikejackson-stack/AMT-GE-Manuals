Removing the patient blower air flow filter

Remove the patient blower air flow filter.

Procedure

  1. Make sure that you have read and understood the electrical safety requirements before starting this procedure. See Electrical Safety.
  2. Shut down and power off the host computer. See Shutting down the host computer.
  3. Apply LOTO to the ISC. See Applying LOTO - Integrated System Cabinet (ISC).
  4. In the magnet room, loosen the air filter clamp and remove the patient blower air flow filter for standard and remote siting configuration.
