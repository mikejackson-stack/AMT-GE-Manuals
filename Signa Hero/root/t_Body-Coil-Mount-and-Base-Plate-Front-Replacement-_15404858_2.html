About this task

Overview

Use this procedure to replace the body coil mount and base plate (front).

Body Coil Mount Front Assembly Removal

Procedure

  1. Undock the patient table and move it out of the room.
  2. Remove the left and right control panel covers. Refer to Removing the magnet left and right control panel covers.
  3. Remove the dock interface cover.
  4. Perform LOTO for ISC. Refer to Applying LOTO for ISC.
  5. Remove the front endbell. Refer to Removing the front endbell.
  6. Remove the rear endbell. Refer to Removing the rear endbell.
  7. If installed, remove the 1-piece bridge. See Removing the 1-piece bridge.
  8. Disconnect the two (2) cooling air ducts at the rear end of the body coil.
  9. Disconnect the HART ID cable at the rear end of the body coil.
  10. Disconnect the CH1 and CH2 drive cables from the RF body coil in the rear.
  11. Disconnect the bore light cable connectors. (Front End: 2 connectors, Rear End: 2 cables).
  12. At rear end of the magnet, remove four M6 bolts and washers securing the RF body coil.
  13. On the front end of the magnet, remove two M6 bolts and washers from the bases of RF coil centering assembly..
  14. Remove the air flow sensors and tape them to the magnet enclosure in a position that will not interfere with the body coil removal.
  15. From the front of the magnet, pull the body coil forward until the front end is outside the bore.
    CAUTION
    Risk of injury!
    Space between VRMw and Body Coil is very narrow, creating a pinch point.
    Grasp the Body Coil at the proper grab points (handles) when positioning it in the bore.
  16. Remove the four M6 screws and large glass laminate washers. Remove the body coil mount front assembly.

Front Base Plate Replacement

Procedure

  1. Notice
    If the Front Base Plate is not damaged or body coil mount is not replaced from old type to new type, skip this section and go to Restoration for restoration.
    Confirm the front base plate is not damaged. If the front base plate is not damaged or body coil mount is not replaced from old type to new type, skip this section and go to Restoration.
  2. CAUTION
    Risk of injury!
    Space between VRMw and Body Coil is very narrow, creating a pinch point.
    Grasp the Body Coil at the proper grab points (handles) when positioning it in the bore.
    Move the body coil location back to create enough clearance to access the base plate.
  3. Remove the lower mounting block by removing two screws and washers on the magnet front end.
  4. Mark the alignment lines for the base plate on the magnet.
  5. Remove the original base plate.
  6. On the new base plate, adjust the height of screw to make the height approximately equal to the original base plate. Then, tighten the lock nuts.
    Note This adjustment is the rough adjustment for centering the body coil in the magnet bore. Fine adjustment will be done in body coil isolation procedure later. There is no specification in this step.

  7. Attach the leveling block to the base plate with two screws (1000-M10C35-33).
  8. Align and install the new front base plate on the Magnet with four screws (1000-M10C45-33) and washers (2000-M10C-10).
  9. Place the non-magnetic level on the level block and confirm that the level bubble is centered between the lines. If not, loosen the four screws and level the base plate position.
  10. CAUTION
    Risk of injury!
    Space between VRMw and Body Coil is very narrow, creating a pinch point.
    Grasp the Body Coil at the proper grab points (handles) when positioning it in the bore.
    Move out the body coil slightly.

Restoration

Procedure

  1. Restore the new body coil mount front assembly.
    Note If the body coil was not replaced, reuse the original screw and washer.

    If the body coil is replaced, use the large glass laminate washers (5988407) and M6x30 screws (1000-M6C030-33) included in the body coil FRU.

  2. CAUTION
    Pinch Fingers!
    Space between VRMw and Body Coil is very narrow.
    Wear a pare of gloves and bring the Body Coil at the proper grab points.
    Restore the body coil to the original location.
  3. Connect the front bracket to the base bracket.
  4. Secure the body coil to the rear bracket with four M6 bolts and washers.
    Note If the body coil is not replaced, reuse the same washers and screws which were originally used for fixing the rear of the body coil.

    If the body coil is replaced, use the large glass laminate washers (5988407) and M6x30 screws (1000-M6C030-33) which are included in body coil FRU.

  5. Perform RF body coil alignment. Refer to Body Coil Alignment.
  6. Reconnect the bore light cable connectors. (Front End: 2 connectors, Rear End: 2 cables)
  7. Restore the air flow sensors.
  8. Restore the two (2) cooling air ducts at the rear end of the body coil.
  9. Reconnect the CH1 and CH2 drive cables to the RF body coil.
  10. If installed on this model, restore the 1-piece bridge. See Installing the 1-piece bridge.
  11. Restore the rear end bell and the rest of the rear covers. Refer to Installing the rear endbell.
  12. Restore the front end bell and bridge. Refer to Installing the front endbell.
  13. Restore the dock interface cover.
  14. Restore the right and left side covers.
  15. Restore the patient table.

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Remove LOTO. Refer to Removing LOTO - Integrated System Cabinet (ISC).
  2. Move the table up and restore the dock interface cover.
  3. Perform Dual Drive Quadrature Calibration. Refer to Dual drive quadrature tool.
    Note

    For MR31.0 and later, Dual drive quadrature tool has been retired, please perform Calibrating for quadrature.
