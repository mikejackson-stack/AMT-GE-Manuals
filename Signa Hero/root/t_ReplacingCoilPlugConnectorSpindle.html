Replacing the coil plug connector spindle

Replace the spindle tip on the coil connector.

About this task

Follow this procedure to replace the spindle tip on the DVMR coil connector.

Procedure

  1. Important: The new spindle contains beryllium copper and is nickel plated. Wash your hands thoroughly after completing this procedure.
    Move the coil with the defective spindle to a suitable location outside of the magnet room.
  2. Make sure that all components (shown below) are included in the HDv P-Port Replacement Spindle Tip Kit.
    Note: The countersunk screw is 8 mm long and has a red coating. A hexagonal socket is required.
  3. Put the unpopulated connector found in the Spindle Tip Replacement Kit (5150668-33) into the coil plug connector, and rotate the handle clockwise to lock the position as shown.
  4. Using the T10 Torx or a 2 mm Allen wrench, loosen the screw on the unpopulated connector (as shown) with only one rotation counterclockwise.
  5. Remove the unpopulated connector from the coil plug connector.
  6. Loosen and remove the screw from the plug using either the T10 Torx or a 2 mm Allen wrench as shown. Discard the screw.
    Note: The screw from the plug must not be reused. If the screw is reused, it may loosen and the spindle may fall off.
  7. Replace the spindle tip and the countersunk screw, and turn the screw using a 2 mm Allen wrench, but do not tighten.
  8. Put the unpopulated connector into the plug again, and rotate the handle clockwise into the locked position as shown in Figure 2 and Figure 3.
  9. Hand-tighten the screw, and turn it an additional 1/10 turn (1.24 N m) with the 2 mm Allen wrench as shown.
  10. Remove the blank P-port plug, and save the plug and Torx bit driver for future use.
  11. Dispose of the removed parts according to the Safety Data Sheet (SDS) provided with the parts kit.
