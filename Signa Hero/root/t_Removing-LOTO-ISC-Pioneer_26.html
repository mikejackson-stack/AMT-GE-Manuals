Removing LOTO - ISC

Removing locks and tags, and restoring power to the ISC (Integrated System Cabinet) Power Distribution Unit (PDU).

About this task

Notice
Potential B0 drift interference
B0 drift can be affected if the gradient coil temperature is not cooled down.
You must wait 1 hour after restoring power before doing certain calibrations and/or a customer scan.

Procedure

  1. Alert necessary personnel that power is being restored.
  2. Remove the lock and the tag from the PDU INPUT circuit breaker.
  3. Rotate the larger main breaker labeled INPUT clockwise to the red On (1) position.
  4. Press the green EMO RESET button.
  5. Boot Signa and log on to scanning level.
