Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

DANGER
STRONG MAGNETIC FIELD!
FERROUS MATERIALS CAN BECOME DANGEROUS PROJECTILES IN THE PRESENCE OF THE MAGNETIC FIELD PRODUCED BY THE SIGNA MAGNET
Do not Bring any ferromagnetic tools or equipment into the magnet room.

About this task

This procedure describes the removal of cables in the cradle. To remove the defective cable, it’s necessary to remove all of the cables (from the cradle) which are in the same group described below.

Group 1: Cables in Left Cable Track

  • P1 Cable

  • P4 Cable

  • Resp Tube

  • Squeeze Bulb (SB) Tube

Group 2: Cables in Right Cable Track

  • P2

  • TDI PA

  • PPG

  • ECG

After cable removal in side of cradle assembly, the defective cable will be replaced from the Table Control Box area.

Common procedure between Group1 and Group2 cable removal

Procedure

  1. (For Bellows cover)
    1. Remove the six screws securing the bottom of the bellows cover; three on the left-hand side and three on the right-hand side.
    2. Lift up the bellows cover and secure it in the up position with six strips of Velcro.
  2. Move the table to the up limit position.
  3. Insert the screw rod and rotate it to clockwise until it engages with the carriage plate.
  4. Move the cradle to the in-limit position.
    Note By moving the cradle to the in-limit position, the cradle can move freely from the home position to the in-limit position after releasing the cradle.
  5. Apply LOTO to the ISC. See Applying LOTO - Integrated System Cabinet (ISC).
  6. At the rear of the magnet, remove the bore end cradle cover by removing four screws.
  7. Release the cradle using the egress handle.
    Note Do not move back to the home position to avoid locking. Leave approximately 500 mm (align T&G switch end and cradle top cover end).
  8. Complete the following steps to remove the handle end cradle cover.
    1. Lift the table top on the handle end to get access to the cradle cover screws.
    2. Insert the table support pad under the cradle.
      Note There is a string for encoder. Insert the support pad by escaping the string.
    3. Remove six screws from the sides and remove the handle end cradle cover.
  9. Remove the cable truck with defective cable using the following steps.
    1. Identify the group including the defective cable. Then, remove two hex screws fixing the cable track under the cradle.
    2. Remove the support pad.
  10. Complete the following steps to remove the cradle hardtop cover.
    1. Move the cradle to the bore end and slide out the slide rails little by little.
      Note Move the cradle at the proper location in the magnet to slide out the rails.
    2. Remove the cradle hardtop cover by pulling up the bore end slightly and sliding it out.
    Note Left and right slide rails are different parts. The gap of two alignment lines is narrow for handle end of slide the rail.
  11. Disconnect the cable connectors from TDI Posterior DMS Array coil (two 16W16 and one Sub-D Connectors).
  12. Remove six screws and remove the TDI Posterior DMS Array coil.
  13. Complete the following steps.
    1. Remove the cross ribs across the length of the cradle.
    2. Remove the cross ribs fixing the cable.

Removal of cable in the cradle

About this task

Remove Group1 cables or Group2 cables which includes the defective cable.

Topic ID: id_SL13924384-1241947

Group 1 cables removal from cradle

Procedure

  1. Remove the P1 cable.
  2. Remove the P4 cable.
    1. Cut the cable ties securing the P4 cable in position on the cradle section.
    2. Remove the three screws and remove the P4 port connector.
    3. Remove the four screws and remove the mic.
  3. Remove the respiratory tube and squeeze bulb tube.
  4. Complete the following steps to remove the Group 1 cables.
    1. Remove the three screws and remove the swivel link.
    2. Remove the O-ring.
    3. Remove the four screws for the guide block.
    4. Move up the link rod so that the P4 and air tubes can go though.
    5. Move the P4 and air tubes to align to the other cables.
    6. Remove the O-Ring.
    7. Remove the shoulder screw.
    8. Move the assembly to make space for cable removal.
    9. Move the Group1 cables toward the back.
    Note Turns of O-ring are shown in illustration below.
  5. (For P2 cable replacement only) Disconnect the dummy load cable.

Group 2 cables removal from cradle

Procedure

  1. Remove the P2 cable.
  2. Remove the PPG and ECG cables by loosening the screws.

Cable removal from track

Procedure

  1. Remove all of the cables in the same Group from the bottom openings of the cradle.
  2. Complete the following steps.
    1. Move the cradle into the magnet bore.
    2. Using the tape, mark the cable so that the cable will be restored with the same length.
  3. For the group with defective cable, complete the following steps.
    1. Remove the two screws on the table top assembly fixing cable track.
    2. Remove two screws fixing the bracket and remove the bracket.
  4. Disconnect the defective cable from the table control box.
  5. Remove the coil cables (M1356~M1365) at the DPP RRx using the following steps.
    1. Remove the lower side cover assembly (left) using the following steps.
      1. Remove the nameplate cover and remove two screws.
      2. Fix the side cover dolly to the lower side cover assembly with turning two knobs.
      3. Disconnect the connectors for light.
      4. Pull to unlatch the hook and move the lower side cover assembly.
    2. Disconnect the coil cables (M1356~M1365) from DPP RRx.
      1. Loosen eight captive screws.
      2. Flip the DPP RRx to access the bottom.
      3. Disconnect the coil defective cables.
    3. Withdraw the coil cables to the table side.
  6. For the defective cable or cable truck, cut the tie wraps on the lower base assembly and withdraw to the table top.
  7. Remove the defective cable through the opening from the table bottom.
  8. Snap open the cable track buckles all the way of cable track.
  9. Pull out the (defective) cables slowly and remove the cable.
  10. Notice
    Restore the cables in cable track neatly. Otherwise, it may cause table movement failure.
    • Mark on the new cable at the same marking length of defective cable. Then, restore the new cable in the cable track.
    • Make sure the cables in track are stored straightly.
    • If track buckles are broken, replace the new one with the FRU kit.
    Restore the cable and cable track by reverse order of removal. Make sure the cables in track are stored straightly per illustration below.
  11. Route the cables as shown in illustration below to avoid the possible “motor phase error” caused by crosstalk noise between P2 cable and Motor cable.
    Note Descriptions of Note1, Note2, Note3 are as follows.

    1. Separate the motor cable and P2 cable as illustration above.

    2. Cables should be routed neatly and in parallel from the top opening for the cable truck assembly.

    3. To avoid the interference with telescopic cover and cable damage, follow the two points below.

      1. Fix all cables tightly using two cable ties at cable track black teeth as shown in illustration.

        Notice
        All cables, especially thin small cables, should be well tightened to the cable track black teeth, to avoid the cable loose or cable movement in the cable track.

      2. Pull the cables as much from the top opening and fix them with tie wraps at the holes of the frame.

  12. Route the cables neatly and in parallel under the Table Control Box and restore all cable connections except the TDI Posterior Array Coil.
  13. Restore the TDI Posterior Array Coil in reverse order of removal and connect cables.
  14. Restore top cradle top cover.
  15. Latch the cradle by pushing it to in-limit.
  16. Remove ISC LOTO. See Removing LOTO - Integrated System Cabinet (ISC).
  17. Move the cradle to the home position and restore the side rails by moving cradle toward in-limit little by little.
    Note Left and right slide rails are different parts. The gap of two alignment lines is narrow for handle end of slide the rail.
  18. Restore the bore end cradle cover.
  19. Remove the screw rod.
  20. Restore the telescopic or bellows cover.

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Do a table functional check. See Table Functional Check.
  2. Complete the following calibration / Functional check.

    • If any P-port cable was replaced, do the coil datapath diagnostic.

    • Complete dual drive quadrature. See Dual drive quadrature tool.

      Note

      For MR31.0 and later, Dual drive quadrature tool has been retired, please perform Calibrating for quadrature.

    • If the ECG cable was replaced, do the ECG leads functional check. See ECG Leads Functional Check and Installation.

    • If PPG cable was replaced, Do the peripheral pulse probe check. See Peripheral Pulse Probe Check.

    • If squeeze bulb tube was replaced, do the pneumatic patient alert system check. See Pneumatic Patient Alert System Check.

    • If respiratory tube was replaced, do the respiratory gating signal checks. See Respiratory Gating Signal Checks.

  3. Do a check scan. See Doing a check scan.
