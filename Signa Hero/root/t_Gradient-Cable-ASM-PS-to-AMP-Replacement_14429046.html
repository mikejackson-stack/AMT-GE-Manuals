Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

DANGER
STRONG MAGNETIC FIELD!
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of magnetic field produced by the magnet.
Do not Bring any ferromagnetic tools or equipment into the magnet room.
DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!!
The gradient amplifiers act as constant load sources, and will send maximum current to any load including any human who get across the outputs.
To prevent fatal electric shock, follow LOTO steps as given in first section of this document to disable and verify safe voltage levels.

Procedure

  1. Perform LOTO for ISC. Refer to Applying LOTO - ISC.
  2. Notice
    It will typically take about 10 minutes to discharge the capacitor in SSSPS after ISC Power is off.
    Use this 10 minutes for the rear covers removal before accessing the Gradient Cable ASM.
    Open ISC Cover.
  3. Confirm that there is no input voltage at the terminal of SSSPS using DVM.
  4. Perform the following steps.
    1. Disconnect two connectors.
    2. Loosen six screws.
    3. Remove Leak sensor assy.
  5. CAUTION
    Electric Shock Hazard!
    Voltage exists in the capacitor of SSSA and SSSPS.
    Disconnect the SSSA and SSSPS before removing the Power Panel Assy.
    Perform the followings to disconnected from rear power panel. Do the same for the related SSSA and SSSPS.
    1. Loosen 4 screws.
    2. Withdraw SSSA/SSS-PS.
  6. Loosen fixing screws and remove ISC PW Cover by lifting up a little.
    Note If furnished closet door prevents from removing, the PW cover can be separated in two pieces by removing 6 screws at center.
  7. Remove Gradient Cable Cover per following steps.
    1. Loosen 12 screws.
    2. Lift up a little and remove the Gradient Cable Cover.
  8. At Pen Wall side, remove cover for SSSD Power Panel Assy.
    1. Loosen screws for key holes shown in illustration below.
    2. Remove three screws at left bottom area and one screw at center joint area.
    3. Lift up the cover and remove it.
  9. CAUTION
    Electric Shock Hazard!
    Voltage exists in the capacitor of SSSA and SSSPS.
    Make sure SSSA and SSSPS are withdrawn before removing the Power Panel Assy.
    Confirm that there is no voltage at the terminal of Gradient Cable Asm using DVM.
  10. Remove defective Power Panel Assy.
    1. Remove four screws and remove Gradient Cable Asm for defective axis.
    2. Remove two screws and remove defective Gradient Cable Asm for SSSPS.
  11. Restore the new assy by reverse order of removal.
    Note Make sure to tighten each screw.

Finalization

  1. Restore the Power. Refer to Removing LOTO - Integrated System Cabinet (ISC).
  2. Do a check scan. See Doing a check scan.
