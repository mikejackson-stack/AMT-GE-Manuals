Safety
Warning
Risk of personal injury
Severe injury can occur if the table is accidentally lowered during maintenance.
When doing maintenance with the table in the Up position, make sure the lock bar is safely engaged.
Warning
Projectile hazard
The rotary pump contains steel. The pump's primary substance is steel and risks an extremely dangerous projectile hazard if working in the magnet room.
Make sure you are doing the pump replacement outside of the magnet room.

About this task

There are different video clips showing the process to:

  • Drain the table hydraulic fluid
  • Remove air bubbles
Note

These videos are for reference only and are not intended to replace the existing service manual procedures. See Checking and filling hydraulic fluid for detailed instructions.

Procedure

  1. Replace the hydraulic fluid only. Refer to the Replace Hydraulic Fluid Only video.
    Note

    Three points to be aware of during the video:

    • To unclip the hose clamp, twist the clip ends in opposite directions.

    • Note the point in the video when the hose end is covered to prevent hydraulic fluid from draining.
    • The fluid height in the reservoir will change depending on the height of the table. For the final fill, the table is raised to full height; make sure the reservoir is only half full.
  2. Extract air bubbles from the hydraulic lines. Refer to the Extract Air Bubbles video.
