Field Replaceable Units

Field Replaceable Unit (FRU) part numbers are listed by category.

Cables may be listed as individual FRUs or as part of a cable collector. Collectors may be LONG or SHORT. SHORT cables are used when equipment room cabinets are located near the rear of the magnet room. LONG cables are used when equipment room cabinets are located near the front of the magnet room.

Some FRUs are optional or have multiple options available. Check the full description for different sizes, finishes, or system features.

Some parts are identified as RoHS compliant. When ordering a replacement part, request the same number, if possible. If the original part is not available, an RoHS part may be substituted.

FRUs that are part of option catalogs are typically shown in the area where they would be installed.
