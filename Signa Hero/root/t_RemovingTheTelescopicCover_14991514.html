Removing the telescopic cover

Procedure on how to remove the telescopic cover for the fixed table.

Prerequisites

Personnel requirements
Required personsPreliminary requirementsProcedureFinalization
1-2-30 to 60 minutes-
Note Personnel requirements depend on the ability to lower and raise the table. If the table can be lowered and raised, then one person is needed. If the table cannot be lowered or raised, then two personnel are needed.

Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

DANGER
Strong Magnetic Field
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field produced by the magnet.
Do not bring any ferromagnetic tools or equipment into the magnet room.

Procedure

  1. Remove the dock interface cover.
    1. Remove the front lower trim ring.
    2. Remove the two screws.
    3. Unhook two points at bottom.
  2. Remove the table base cover.
    Note Do not disconnect the Up/Down foot pedal cables to the connector from J1 of table control box if the table moves up and down. Otherwise, remove the cables.
  3. Remove the motor cover and side covers.
    1. Remove the five screws and slide out the motor cover.
    2. Remove the two rubber caps and six screws, and remove the side cover. Repeat the same procedure for the other side.
  4. Move the table to the down limit if possible for better serviceability.
    Note If the table cannot move down, refer to the Note in Step 6.
  5. Remove the six screws from the bottom telescopic cover.
  6. Remove the six screws securing the upper telescopic cover to the table frame.
    Note If the table cannot move down, do the following:
    1. Remove the 4 screws from the upper telescopic cover. Do not yet remove the two screws at the diagonal position.
    2. With two people, hold the upper telescopic cover at the diagonal position and remove the remaining two screws.
    3. Slowly move down the telescopic cover in parallel.
  7. If possible, move the table to up limit for better serviceability.
  8. Remove the upper telescopic cover.
    1. Using a hex wrench at one corner, remove the two screws.
    2. Remove the upper telescopic cover.
    Note The illustration that follows shows the best case if the table can move up to the upper limit position. If this is difficult, separate all covers.
  9. Remove the covers one by one, starting from the outside and working your way in.
    1. Using a hex wrench at one corner (upper and lower), remove the four screws.
      Repeat the step for the remaining corners.
    2. Remove the covers, starting from the outside and working your way in.
    Note The following illustration shows the best case if the table can move up to the upper limit position. If this is difficult, separate all covers.
    Note For installing a removed telescopic cover as part of troubleshooting or for a separate hardware repair, see Installing the telescopic cover.
