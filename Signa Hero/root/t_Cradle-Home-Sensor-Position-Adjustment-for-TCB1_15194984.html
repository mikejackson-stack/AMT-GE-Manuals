About this task

This procedure describes Cradle home sensor position adjustment for TCB1 (5500801) so that cradle home sensor switch works properly. If you don’t know the TCB type, check the part number on the label.

Procedure

  1. Note

    In this procedure, threshold of cradle home sensor switch will be adjusted when cradle is attached to the bridge or bridge cover. This adjustment is required so that Table won’t stop the motion during up or down movement by unlatching the switch.

    Move the cradle at highest position.
  2. If not removed, remove Dock Interface Cover.
    1. Remove front upper and lower trim rings.
    2. Remove two screws.
    3. Unhook two points at bottom.
  3. Slide Table base cover slightly so that Table Control Box can be seen per following steps.
    1. Remove Cap and remove screw at three locations.
    2. Slide the Gap Cover forward.
    3. Lift up the rear of left side Table base cover and slightly slide apart of both Table base covers so that Table Control Box can be seen.
    Note

    Do not remove the cables for up/down pedal since it will be used during the procedure.

  4. Perform the following steps to remove 2 covers.
    1. Remove 5 screws and slide out the Motor Cover.
      Note

      Need pull force to slide out the motor cover.

    2. Remove 2 rubber caps, remove 6 screws, and remove side cover.
    3. Remove 4 screws and remove bottom cover.
      Note

      Screw length for bottom cover is different from the screws for the other covers.

    Note

    It’s accessible to the home sensor adjustment parts by removing the bottom cover (c) only even though the work space is very narrow.

  5. Move the cradle down about 150mm from highest position so that cradle surface faces both bridge surface and bridge cover surface. See illustration below.
  6. Push the cradle to magnet side by hand until it hit to the Bridge or Bridge Cover. Do not release Egress handle when pushing.
    Note

    Home sensor position will be adjusted at this position.

  7. Confirm that Home sensor LED is not lit.
    Note

    HOME LED is located at the 5th LED from the right. To confirm, there is a print on the top of TCB.

    • If it is not lit, go to Step 8.

    • If it is lit, move the home sensor position until the LED is tuned off. Rotate the adjusting screw to CCW direction until the LED is tuned off by referring to the steps in Step 8. Then, perform Step 8.

  8. Adjust the home sensor according to the following steps.
    1. Access to Home sensor location and loosen two screws.
    2. Slowly rotate the adjusting screw by 1/4 turn to CW direction with fingers.
    3. Check if Home LED on TCB is lit or not.
      Note

      HOME LED is located at the 5th LED from the right. To confirm, there is a print on the top of TCB.

    4. Continue Step 8.3 and Step 8.2 until Home LED on TCB is lit.
    5. Tighten two screws in Step 8.1 after completion of the adjustment.
    Note

    Sensor location moves to magnet bore direction when adjusting screw is turned to CW direction.

  9. Turn Off and ON the PHPS2 switch to reset the home sensor value.
  10. Move the Table to the highest position.
  11. Perform the following check.
    1. Using In/Out button of Operator Control Panel, move the cradle position so that the display shows 2mm~5mm.
      Note

      Move the cradle by 2mm~5mm is the right side operator control panel. Note that the left side panel is a mirror image of the right side.

    2. Perform the following sub steps.

      Reference video for cradle out operation

      Note

      Video file format is mp4 and it is not supported in Google Chrome and Mozilla Firefox. Please use Internet Explore or Safari as browser.

      1. Keep stepping on the [Down] foot pedal.

      2. Push [Out] button a little.

      3. Wait 2 seconds as interval.

      4. Continue 11.2.2 and 11.2.3 until the Table start to move down.

    3. Once Table move down, check that the Table moves down smoothly without interfering with the Bridge or Bridge cover throughout the full stroke (Up-limit to Down-limit).

      • If Table move down without any interference with Bridge or Bridge cover, adjustment is completed. Go to Step 12.

      • If there is interference with Bridge or Bridge cover, adjustment is required. Go back to Step 5.

  12. Restore covers in reverse order of removal.
    1. Restore bottom cover with four screws.
      Note

      Screw length for bottom cover is different from the screws for the other covers.

    2. Restore side cover with 6 screws and restore 2 rubber caps. To restore the cover to the original location, see the following steps.

      1. Insert the drawer part of side cover into the brackets for the drawer at Patient Table.

      2. Tilt the cover a little and insert the ramped end of the side cover behind the corner of Table Top. Then, restore the side cover to the original location.

      3. Fix the cover with 6 screws and restore 2 rubber caps.

    3. Restore the Motor Cover with 5 screws.
    4. Restore the Gap Cover location.
    5. Restore Table base cover with three screws and rubber caps.
    6. Hook the bottom of Dock Interface Cover and restore it with two screws..
    7. Restore front upper and lower trim rings.

Finalization

  1. At operator console, check the latest messages with time in Error log tab to identify the initial condition in error message field.
    Note

    There are several messages related to PHPS2 switch Off/On.

  2. Press [Home] button at OCP.
  3. Move the cradle in by 100 mm. Then, move the cradle to home position. Confirm that there is no error displayed in error log by cradle operation.
