Removing the oil catch pan

Removes the oil catch pan from the dockable patient table.

Prerequisites

Personnel requirements
Required personsPreliminary requirementsProcedureFinalization
1-5 minutes-

Procedure

  1. Make sure the clearance at the bore end of the table is greater than the length of the oil catch pan [approximately 4 ft (1.2 meters)].
  2. Un-hang the catch pan from the link rods.
  3. Slightly stretch the sides of the catch pan and slide it towards the bore end of the table.
  4. Inspect the catch pan for oil stains (red). If oil stains are present, inspect the hydraulic cylinder assembly and hoses for leaks.
