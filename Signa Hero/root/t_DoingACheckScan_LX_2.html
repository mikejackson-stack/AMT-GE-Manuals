Doing a check scan

About this task

Make sure that you use the DQA phantom, so that the image can be analyzed for correct orientation.
Notice
Depending on site configuration and/or rules, you may want to temporarily disable Auto Archive or Auto Network.

Procedure

  1. Follow the steps below to put the DQA III phantom on the table.
    1. Put the phantom positioner onto the hollow for HNU. R/L direction will be fit to the hollow.
    2. (For plastic positioner) Push the phantom positioner toward the magnet until the two bottom bars reach the end of the hollow.
    3. Put the phantom onto the positioner and make sure that it is level and not rotated.
  2. Attach the head coil to the cradle and position the DQA phantom inside the coil.
  3. Landmark on the center line of the phantom. The laser must be in the middle of the line. Press Advance to Scan.
  4. Click the New Worklist icon.
  5. Select a Patient ID workflow (other than geservice).
  6. Enter for weight: 111.
  7. Click Show All Protocols....
  8. When the Protocol Selector menu opens, click Protocol Details.
  9. Select the Protocol Library: GE or GEHC.
  10. Select Adult.
  11. Click the Template tab.
  12. Select 3 - Plane 2D Localizer and click the arrow to move the selection to the box on the right.
  13. Click Accept.
  14. Click Save.
  15. Click Start Exam.
  16. Enter the following image parameters.
    OptionDescription
    Frequency field of view (FOV) (Freq. FOV)30.0
    Phase FOV1.00
    Slice Thickness5.0
  17. Select Body Coil.
  18. Click Task and select Change/Select Anatomy.
  19. Select Anatomical Region (Any).
  20. Click Accept.
  21. Click Save RX.
  22. Click Scan.
  23. Review the images to make sure that the orientation is correct.
    Axials:
    1GE logo in upper right corner
    2A is shown
    Coronal & Sagittal:
    3Comb in upper left corner
    4CS shown in upper right corner
