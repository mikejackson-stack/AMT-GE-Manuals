Removing the facility water connection

Remove the facility water connection.

Required conditions
The Integrated Cooling Cabinet (ICC) facility water valve must be turned off.
Make sure the hospital facility chiller valve is functional to avoid excessive loads on the facility water pumps and lines.

About this task

Note When replacing the facility water connection, it is necessary to drain the primary side of the ICC.

Procedure

  1. Make sure that you have read and understood the electrical safety requirements before starting this procedure. See Electrical Safety.
  2. Shut down and power off the host computer. See Shutting down the host computer.
  3. The ICC is powered from the Integrated System Cabinet (ISC), so you must turn off ISC power. Apply LOTO to the ISC. See Applying LOTO - Integrated System Cabinet (ISC).
  4. Make sure that the Gradient Cooling Unit (GCU) and CCU pressure meters (black meters) read zero, and that the ICC control unit Temperature Control Unit (TCU) display is OFF.
  5. Turn off power to the F-50 compressor.
    1. Put the DRIVE switch on the compressor's front panel in the OFF position.
    2. Put the MAIN POWER switch on the compressor's front panel in the OFF position.
  6. Shut down the chiller or open the bypass loop.
  7. Close the facility water valve that the customer installed in their facility loop, or fold the hose that the FE is about to replace on the top of the interface fitting and secure the folded hose with a cable tie wrap.
  8. Drain the ICC primary water circuit into a 20L draining tank.
    1. Connect one end of the draining hose to the emergency valve (1) and put the other end in the draining tank. Then, open the emergency valve (1).
    2. When no more coolant drains from the emergency valve (1), disconnect the draining hose, connect it to the emergency valve (3), and open the emergency valve (3).
      Note Leave the emergency valve (1) opened.
    3. When no more coolant drains from the emergency valve (3), disconnect the draining hose, connect it to the dirt separator drain valve, and open the drain valve.
      Note Leave the emergency valves (1) and (3) opened.
    Note The water/glycol mixture must be filled into an approved container and be disposed of properly or reused.
  9. Make sure no more coolant drains. Then close the emergency valves and the dirt separator drain valve.
  10. Disconnect the facility water connectors from the top of the ICC.
