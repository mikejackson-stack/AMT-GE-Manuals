About this task

Overview

Use this procedure to replace the Body Coil Mount and Base Plate (Rear).

Topic ID: id_SL15183889-1330679

Body Coil Mount Rear Assembly Removal

Procedure

  1. Undock the patient table and move it out of the room.
  2. Perform LOTO for ISC. Refer to Applying LOTO for ISC.
  3. Remove the front endbell. Refer to Removing the front endbell.
  4. Remove the rear endbell. Refer to Removing the rear endbell.
  5. If installed, remove the 1-piece bridge. See Removing the 1-piece bridge.
  6. Disconnect the two (2) cooling air ducts at the rear end of the body coil.
  7. Disconnect the HART ID cable at the rear end of the body coil.
  8. Disconnect the CH1 and CH2 drive cables from the RF body coil in the rear.
  9. Disconnect the bore light cable connectors. (Front End: 2 connectors, Rear End: 2 cables)
  10. At rear end of the magnet, remove four M6 bolts and washers securing the RF body coil.
  11. On the front end of the magnet, remove two M6 bolts and washers from the bases of RF coil centering assembly.
  12. Remove the air flow sensors and tape them to the magnet enclosure in a position that will not interfere with the body coil removal.
  13. CAUTION
    Risk of injury!
    Space between VRMw and Body Coil is very narrow, creating a pinch point.
    Grasp the Body Coil at the proper grab points (handles) when positioning it in the bore.
    From the front of the magnet, pull the body coil forward until the front end is outside the bore.
  14. Remove the body coil mount rear assembly.

Rear Base Plate Replacement

Procedure

  1. Notice
    If the Rear Base Plate is not damaged or body coil mount is not replaced from old type to new type, skip this section and go to Restoration for restoration.
    Confirm the rear base plate is not damaged. If the rear base plate is not damaged or body coil mount is not replaced from old type to new type, skip this section and go to Restoration.
  2. On the rear end of the magnet, mark alignment lines on each end of the centering assembly base plate.
  3. Remove the base plate.
  4. Adjust the height of screw of the new base plate to make the height approximately equal to the old base plate. Then, tighten the lock nuts.
    Note This adjustment is the rough adjustment for centering the body coil in the magnet bore. Fine adjustment will be done in body coil isolation procedure later. There is no specification in this step.

    • A≒A’

    • B≒B’

  5. Align and install the new rear base plate on the magnet with 2 screws (1000-M10C45-33) and washers (2000-M10C-10).
  6. Install level block with two screws (1000–M10C35–33).
  7. Place the non-magnetic level on the level block and confirm that the level bubble is centered between the lines. If not, loosen the two screws and level the base plate position.
  8. Remove the leveling block.
  9. Install two screws and washers in the base plate.

Restoration

Procedure

  1. Install the new body coil mount rear assembly.
  2. CAUTION
    Risk of injury!
    Space between VRMw and Body Coil is very narrow, creating a pinch point.
    Grasp the Body Coil at the proper grab points (handles) when positioning it in the bore.
    Restore the body coil to the original location.
  3. Connect the front bracket to the base bracket.
  4. Secure the body coil to the rear bracket with four M6 bolts and washers.
    Note If the body coil is not replaced, reuse the same washers and screws which were originally used for fixing the rear of the body coil.

    If the body coil is replaced, use the large glass laminate washers (5988407) and M6x30 screws (1000-M6C030-33) which are included in body coil FRU.

  5. Perform RF Body Coil Alignment. Refer to Body Coil Alignment.
  6. Reconnect the bore light cable connectors. (Front End: 2 connectors, Rear End: 2 cables)
  7. Restore the air flow sensors.
  8. Restore the two (2) cooling air ducts at the rear end of the body coil.
  9. Reconnect the CH1 and CH2 drive cables to the RF body coil.
  10. If installed on this model, restore the 1-piece bridge. See Installing the 1-piece bridge.
  11. Restore the rear endbell and rest of rear covers. Refer to Installing the rear endbell.
  12. Restore the front endbell and bridge. Refer to Installing the front endbell.
  13. Restore the patient table.
Topic ID: id_15771727

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Note Required calibrations and functional checks after body coil replacement are done in the RF body coil tuning procedure, below.
    Remove LOTO. Refer to Removing LOTO - Integrated System Cabinet (ISC).
  2. Perform Dual Drive Quadrature Calibration. Refer to Dual drive quadrature tool.
    Note

    For MR31.0 and later, Dual drive quadrature tool has been retired, please perform Calibrating for quadrature.

  3. Perform the system performance test (SPT). Refer to Doing the System Performance Test (SPT).
