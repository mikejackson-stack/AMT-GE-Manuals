Doing DQA calibration with the DQA III phantom

The DQA tool determines correct gradient polarity and correct gradient wiring by scanning a series of axial and coronal images of the DQA III phantom for systems below sentence MR 31.0 or sites that do not have a 17cm Sphere with Spout (SWS) phantom.

Prerequisites

Doing DQA calibration with the DQA III phantom is for systems running software versions prior to 31.0, or sites that do not possess a 17 cm Sphere with Spout (SWS) phantom.
Note For systems running software version 31.0 and later and sites that have a SWS phantom, see Doing DQA calibration with the Sphere With Spout (SWS) phantom

Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

About this task

The DQA tool determines proper gradient polarity and proper gradient wiring by scanning a series of axial and coronal images of the DQA III phantom for systems running software versions prior to 31.0 or sites that do not possess a 17 cm SWS phantom.

The DQA tool determines proper gradient polarity (positive to positive and negative to negative) and proper gradient wiring (X amp drives X coil, Y amp drives Y coil, Z amp drives Z coil) by scanning a series of axial and coronal images of the DQA III phantom. The tool also verifies that the proper phantom is being used. After proper gradient orientation is confirmed, the tool adjusts for Z isocenter and gradient calibration. If Z iso adjustments or gradient calibration adjustments are required, the DQA tool does the applicable iterations to complete. Therefore, the DQA tool time to complete may vary.

Procedure

  1. Make sure Checking and aligning the laser light was completed. Pay close attention when positioning the alignment light on the phantom, because this procedure requires an accurate landmark.
    If problems are encountered while doing this calibration, see Troubleshooting the Daily Quality Assurance (DQA) II tool.
  2. Put the DQA III phantom on the table.
    1. Put the phantom positioner onto the hollow for HNU. R/L direction will be fit to the hollow.
    2. (For plastic positioner 5554497) Push the phantom positioner to the magnet until the two bottom bars reach the end of the hollow.
    3. Put the phantom onto the positioner and make sure that it is level and not rotated.
  3. Landmark on the center line of the DQA III phantom. The laser must be in the middle of the black circumferential line on the phantom. Push the Advance to Scan button.
    Note Never use the touch-and-go landmarking strip when manually landmarking as part of a calibration tool procedure.
  4. Start the DQA tool:
    • (For non-proprietary mode) From the Common Service Desktop, select Calibration > Calibration Tools > DQA Calibration, then click Click here to start this tool.
  5. On the Gradient Calibration window, select DQA.
  6. In the new window that displays, click Start.
    All phantom images acquired during the DQA tool can be viewed in the browser. View these images if problems occur.
    To stop the DQA tool without restarting it, click Abort at any time.
  7. As the DQA tool progresses, the status displays in the Progress area. If problems are encountered, they display in the Actions Required area.
    Note After resolving the issues in the Actions Required section, click Restart to restart the DQA tool.
  8. After the tool successfully completes its tasks, click Exit.
    Note The finalization steps are included at the end of this procedure.
  9. Review images to verify proper body scan.
