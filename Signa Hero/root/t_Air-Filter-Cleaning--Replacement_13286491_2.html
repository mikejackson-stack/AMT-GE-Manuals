Table 1. Safety
Warning
Strong magnetic field
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field that the magnet produces.
Do not bring any ferrous tools or equipment into the magnet room.
Warning
Electrocution hazard
Dangerous and or fatal voltages are present in the energized cabinet.
Follow LOTO steps as given in first section of this document to disable and verify safe voltage levels.

Procedure

  1. Visually check the air filter. If cleaning or replacement is necessary, do the following:
  2. Do LOTO for ISC. See Lockout / Tagout for ISC.
  3. In the magnet room, loosen the clamp of the air filter and remove the air filter for standard and remote siting configuration.
  4. If cleaning is needed, clean the filter outside of magnet room.
  5. Install the air filter by reverse order of removal.

Finalization

  1. Turn on the ISC power. See Removing LOTO - ISC.
  2. Do a Check Scan. See check scan.
