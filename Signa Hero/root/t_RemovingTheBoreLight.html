Removing the bore light

Removes a bore light assembly from the body coil.

Prerequisites

Personnel requirements
Required personsPreliminary requirementsProcedureFinalization
1-10 minutes-
Tools and test equipment
ItemQuantityPart numberManufacturer
Nonmagnetic Titanium Service Tool Kit, Small Set15113258-
Required conditions
The front bridge is removed.

About this task

Procedure

  1. Make sure that you have read and understood the electrical safety requirements before starting this procedure. See Electrical Safety.
  2. Shut down and power off the host computer. See Shutting down the host computer.
  3. Apply LOTO to the ISC. See Applying LOTO - Integrated System Cabinet (ISC).
  4. Remove the covers:
    1. Remove the rear bridge cover. See Removing the rear bridge cover.
    2. Remove the rear bridge inner cover. See Removing the rear bridge inner cover.
    3. Remove the bridge. See Removing the bridge.
  5. Remove the cable tie(s) securing the bore light electrical cable to the body coil.
  6. Remove the two screws securing the bore light to the body coil.
  7. Remove the bore light brackets and light guide panels from the body coil on both sides.
