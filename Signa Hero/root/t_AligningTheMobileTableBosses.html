Measuring the dockable table bosses

Measures the relative heights of the dock and dockable table bosses.

Prerequisites

Personnel requirements
Required personsPreliminary requirementsProcedureFinalization
1-5 minutes-

Procedure

  1. Download and open the Alignment Data Sheet Macro (DOC2647017) from the online documentation library.
  2. Notice
    Potential equipment damage
    The hook can become overstressed and bend, requiring replacement.
    Do not attempt to fully engage the dock pedal if the force needed is too strong.
    Notice
    Potential equipment damage
    The ODU connector pins may become bent, requiring table cable replacement.
    Do not electrically dock the table prior to the table being properly aligned.
    Mechanically dock the table.
    Note: If the table will not mechanically dock, adjust the dock hook tension. See Adjusting the dock hook tension.
  3. Measure the vertical distance between the dock boss and the table boss on the left and right sides.
  4. Input the measurements into the eDOC Height table on the eDOC Height sheet of the spreadsheet.
