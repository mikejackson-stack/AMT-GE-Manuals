Installing the Gradient Cooling Unit (GCU) heat exchanger

Steps on how to install a replacement Gradient Cooling Unit (GCU) heat exchanger.

Procedure

  1. Attach the new GCU heat exchanger to the sheet metal part.
  2. Install the heat exchanger into the cabinet and attach it with screws at the five locations shown using an 8 mm hex head wrench.
  3. New flat gaskets are provided for positions 3 and 4. Flat gaskets are not needed for positions 1 and 2. Secure the connections at points 1, 2, 3, and 4.
  4. Insulate the GCU heat exchanger plumbing connections at positions 3 and 4 with the insulation provided.
  5. Make sure there is enough available coolant near the ICC.
    Note The coolant fluid is dyed for easy detection of leaks. This also helps distinguish between a leak and any abnormal condensation on the hoses.
  6. Close the FPU drain, city water supply, and city water return valves (if they are open).
    ItemDescriptionItemDescription
    1FPU supply and return valves (2)4FPU drain valve
    2Bypass valve5City water return valve
    3City water supply valve6Cryo compressor hoses (2)
  7. Open both the facility supply and return valves using the blue handles.
  8. Open the GCU secondary supply and return valves at the rear of the magnet.
  9. Refill the GCU using the tank funnel extension.
    Note The GCU tank contains about 17L (4.5 gal).
    1. Unscrew the cap from the tank.
    2. Connect the funnel extension to the tank fill port and insert the funnel.
    3. Fill the tank until the coolant level is within the filling level on the tank indicator.
    4. Screw the cap back on the tank.
  10. Set the circuit breaker for the GCU (4Q2) to OFF.
  11. Remove MDP LOTO. See Removing LOTO - Main Disconnect Panel (MDP).
  12. Turn the GCU valve to the filling position.
  13. Start the pump by turning the GCU (4Q2) circuit breaker to the ON position.

    Result

    The pump will draw coolant from the reservoir and begin filling the system.
  14. Monitor the coolant level in the tank.
    • If the coolant level falls below the operation level, stop the pump by turning the circuit breaker to the OFF position. Examine connections for leaks and add more coolant as needed to reach the filling level. Restart the pump and repeat this step as needed.
      Note The pump may stop on its own during early filling due to insufficient flow. This is normal.
    • If (after 5 to 10 minutes) the coolant level remains the same and no air bubbles are visible in the tank, stop the pump by turning the circuit breaker to the OFF position.
  15. Move the GCU filling valve to the operation position.
  16. Make sure there are no leaks. See Checking the Gradient Cooling Unit (GCU) or Cabinet Cooling Unit (CCU) heat exchanger for leaks.

Finalization

  1. Start up the host computer/system. See Starting up the host computer .
  2. Do a check scan. See Doing a check scan.
