HD 8-Channel Body Array Coil Setup for SNR Test

Prerequisites

Personnel requirements
Required personsPreliminary requirementsProcedureFinalization
1-15 minutes-
Tools and test equipment
ItemQuantityPart numberManufacturer
TL Unified Phantom (for 1.5T)25343347-
Required conditions
The following coil configuration names must be installed to run this tool: 8ch Body U service and 8ch Body L service.

Procedure

  1. Place the posterior section of the coil on the table. Place the TL unified phantoms over the posterior section as in Place the TL unified phantoms.
  2. Place the TL unified phantoms such that the TL unified phantoms are well centered on S/I direction as shown in Alignment in S/I.
  3. Place the TL unified phantoms such that the TL unified phantoms are well centered on R/L direction as shown in arrows in Alignment in R/L.
  4. Place the anterior section on the TL unified phantoms such that the coil is well centered in both S/I and R/L directions with respect to the phantom set as shown in the Place the anterior section 1 and Place the anterior section 2.
  5. Fasten the Velcro straps so that the coil firmly snugs the phantoms.
  6. Connect the coil to respective port of the system LPCA.
  7. Landmark the coil at the center cross mark as shown in Landmark and advance to scan.
  8. Perform the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance (MCQA) Tool.
