Cabinet Power Switch Replacement

Table 3. Safety
DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!
To prevent possible fatal electric shock, shutdown the host computer and perform Applying LOTO - Global Operator Cabinet (GOC) and Operator Workspace before starting this procedure.

Procedure

  1. Shutdown application software on the Host computer. Lockout/Tagout power sources. Refer to Applying LOTO - Global Operator Cabinet (GOC) and Operator Workspace.
  2. Remove the service cover and disconnect ground cable
    1. Remove two screws.
    2. Lift up and remove service cover. (Be careful to strain the ground cable)
    3. Disconnect ground cable.
  3. Remove two screws and remove switch cover.
    1. Remove two screws.
    2. Remove switch cover.
  4. Disconnect connector from rear of Power Switch.
  5. Rotate plastic nut and remove it that securing the switch.
  6. Pull the Power Switch to the front and remove it.
  7. Install new power switch by reverse order of removal.
  8. Restore GOC by reverse order of removal.

Finalization

  1. Restore power to the GOC. Refer to Removing LOTO - Global Operator Cabinet (GOC) and Operator Workspace.
  2. Perform Doing a check scan.
