Setting up the Bird 5000-XT wattmeter

This procedure describes how to configure the Bird 5000-XT wattmeter to measure maximum forward power.

Procedure

  1. Press the OK button on the wattmeter.
  2. Press MENU. Press ▼ to SCALE MENU and press OK.
  3. Set the range by entering the value listed in Bird 5000-XT wattmeter ranges, based on calibration type, and press ENTER.
    Table 1. Bird 5000-XT wattmeter ranges
    Calibration typeRange
    Body Channel50,000
    Head Channel5,000
  4. To check the setting, press ▼ to SCALE MENU and press OK.
  5. Press OK to exit. Press ◄ to return to measurement mode.
  6. Press ▲ to make sure the scale reads W (or µW or kW on some wattmeters).
    • If the scale reads dBm, press ▲ again to toggle the scale to read W (or µW or kW) on the TOP forward power scale.
    Ignore the BOTTOM reflected power scale value. It is not used during maximum power calibration.
  7. Press MENU.
  8. Press ▼ to select ELEMENT TYPE and press OK.

    Result

    This shows the sensor type configured in the wattmeter. This procedure uses sensor type 43, which is optimized for peak power measurement.
  9. Make sure that the output reads 43 and press OK.
  10. Press OK. Press ◄ to return to measurement mode.
  11. Press ▲ to change the unit of measurement to FORWARD PEAK.
  12. Locate the Element Offset Factors card in the kit and find the offset for “1.5T Body Output.” The offset is used to ensure accurate measurement.
  13. Press MENU on the wattmeter. Select OFFSET and press OK.
  14. Enter the numerical value that was shown on the kit’s offset card and press OK.
    The wattmeter rounds to three significant digits.
    Note If the offset value is negative, first enter the numerical value, then the minus symbol (-).
  15. Press ◄ to return to measurement mode.
  16. Press MENU. Press ▼ until SMOOTHING is selected and press OK.
  17. Press ▼ until 4 Samples is shown and press OK.
  18. To make sure that SMOOTHING has been set, press ▼ until SMOOTHING is selected and press OK.
  19. The wattmeter should show 4 Samples. Press OK.
  20. Press ◄ to return to measurement mode.

Results

The meter is ready for calibration.
