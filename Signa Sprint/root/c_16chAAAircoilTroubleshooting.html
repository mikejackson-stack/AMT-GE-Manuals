1.5T 16CH AA AIR Coil Troubleshooting

What to look for during a Failure?

  1. Coil is Not recognized
    • Do you see any issues with the pins at the P-port coil cable connector? (Bent, loose or etc.)
    • Are you using the Correct coil at the Correct system? (1.5T vs 3.0T)
    • Is the correct software version installed?
  2. MCBias Fault
    • Do you see any issues with the pins at the P-port coil cable connector? (Bent, loose or etc.)
    • Try a different port to downgrade the problem to coil or the system port or the DPP.
    • Perform MCBias check using the MCBias tool in service browser to confirm the issue.
  3. MCQA Fault
    • Do you see any issues with the pins at the P-port coil cable connector? (Bent, loose or etc.)
    • Is your coil and phantom set up align in accordance to MCQA test guidelines?
    • Did you Align the center of the coil with the laser landmark (at 1080 mm from home)?
    • Perform the following test steps to execute the test. The purpose of this test to ensure the functionality of the coil elements. Once the test is completed, please ensure the failure from the test below matching is the same channel/element from the MCQA test.
