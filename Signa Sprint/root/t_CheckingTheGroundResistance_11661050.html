Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

DANGER
Fatal Electric Shock Hazard!
Lethal voltages are present in the power distribution unit (PDU) even if all PDU breakers are off.
Disconnect and complete LOTO on the power from the system at the facility's main disconnect. See Applying LOTO - Main Disconnect Panel (MDP).
Required conditions
Any required electrical code inspection is complete.
Installation of the facility disconnect device is complete.
All system components and cables are installed. See the installation instructions in the System Installation Manual.

About this task

This document contains a method for measuring ground resistance using different brands of safety analyzers. Only one of the safety analyzers listed in Tools and test equipment is required.
Ground resistance checks are required for all major system components including:
  • Magnet
  • Patient table (fixed table systems) or table dock (dockable table systems)
  • Integrated Cooling Cabinet (ICC)
  • Penetration Panel (PP)
  • Global Operator Cabinet (GOC)

Review and understand the required conditions before beginning this procedure.

Procedure

  1. DANGER
    Fatal Electric Shock Hazard!
    There are lethal voltages in the power distribution unit (PDU) even if all PDU breakers are off.
    Make sure power at the main disconnect is off, locked out, and tagged out before proceeding.
    Apply MDP LOTO. See Applying LOTO - Main Disconnect Panel (MDP).
  2. Do the ground resistance check using the procedure for the safety analyzer on site.

Ground resistance checks using Microguard

Procedure

  1. Connect the milliohm adapter module to the power outlet of the Microguard.
    Note Because the Microguard is being used solely as a voltmeter in this application, any other meter capable of resolving 0.1 millivolts can be substituted in the next step. Meters other than the Microguard are currently in use. Refer to the appropriate tool catalog for further information.
  2. Attach the Microguard red and black leads to meter posts of milliohm module.
  3. Set the Microguard Patient Lead Tester switch to All Other Tests.
  4. Set the Microguard Selector switch to 20 Microamps/Millivolts.
  5. Set the Microguard Input switch to Red and Black Probes.
  6. Attach the Kelvin leads. Connect one lead to the PDU ground located on the Integrated System Cabinet (ISC) and the other lead to the frame of the subsystem component under test.
    Note If extra ground cable is needed to reach the component under test, measure the ground resistance of the ground cable and subtract the cable’s resistance from the component measurement in the following step.
  7. The meter displays the voltage between the system cabinet and the component under test. To obtain resistance in milliohms, multiply the displayed value by 2.
  8. Make sure the measured resistance is less than 100 milliohms (corresponding to a meter reading of 50 millivolts). If the component fails this measurement, make sure the lead is securely connected to the component under test and reposition the lead if required. If the lead is secure and the check still fails, add an additional ground to the component and retest.
  9. Repeat Step 6 through Step 8 for the remaining components listed at the beginning of the procedure.
  10. Restore the system:
    1. Turn the Microguard power off.
    2. Remove the leads and the milliohm adapter from the Microguard.
    3. Close all cabinet doors.

Ground resistance checks using Dale safety analyzer

Procedure

  1. Connect one clamp-type lead to the EXTERNAL jack on the top of the Dale safety analyzer. Connect the other clamp-type lead to the CHASSIS jack on the top of the Dale safety analyzer.
  2. On the Dale safety analyzer, set the main selector switch to W - RESISTANCE.
  3. Plug the Dale safety analyzer into an AC outlet.
  4. On the Dale safety analyzer, make sure that the two OK LEDs light up to indicate that the AC outlet is properly wired.
  5. With leads not touching each other, the display should read 1.00. With the leads touching (copper to copper) the display should read 0.00 to 0.02.
  6. Attach the leads. Connect one lead to the PDU ground located on the Integrated System Cabinet (ISC) and the other lead to the bare frame of the subsystem cabinet under test.
    Note If extra ground cable is needed to reach the component under test, measure the ground resistance of the ground cable and subtract the cable’s resistance from the component measurement in the following step.
  7. Make sure the measured resistance is less than 100 milliohms. If the component fails this measurement, make sure the lead is securely connected to the component under test and reposition the lead if required. If the lead is secure and the check still fails, add an additional ground to the component and retest.
    Note The Dale safety analyzer reads resistance in ohms. For example, a reading of 0.50 equals 500 milliohms.
  8. Repeat Step 6 through Step 7 for the remaining components listed at the beginning of the procedure.
  9. Restore the system:
    1. Unplug the Dale safety analyzer from the AC outlet.
    2. Remove the leads from the safety analyzer.
    3. Close all cabinet doors.

Ground resistance checks using Fluke ESA612

Procedure

  1. Turn on the Fluke meter and select the point to point option on the meter.
    Note At facilities with 110-120 VAC, a fault error may appear when the meter is powered on. Press F4 OK to clear the error and continue.
  2. Select resistance or F2.
  3. Attach the leads into the red and black input jackets and short the leads together.
  4. Select zero leads or F4.
  5. Connect one lead to the PDU ground located on the Integrated System Cabinet (ISC) and the other lead to the bare frame of the subsystem cabinet under test.
    Note If extra ground cable is needed to reach the component under test, measure the ground resistance of the ground cable and subtract the cable’s resistance from the component measurement in the following step.
  6. Make sure that measured resistance is less than 100 milliohms. If the component fails this measurement, make sure the lead is securely connected to the component under test and reposition the lead if required. If the lead is secure and the check still fails, add an additional ground to the component and retest.
  7. Repeat Step 5 through Step 6 for the remaining components listed at the beginning of the procedure.
  8. Restore the system:
    1. Turn off the Fluke safety analyzer.
    2. Remove the leads from the safety analyzer.
    3. Close all cabinet doors.
  9. Remove MDP LOTO. See Removing LOTO - Main Disconnect Panel (MDP).

Finalization

Do a check scan. See Doing a check scan.
