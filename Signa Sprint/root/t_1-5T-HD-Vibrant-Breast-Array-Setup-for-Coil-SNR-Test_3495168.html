Required conditions
The following coil configuration names must be installed to run this tool: HDBreastServ, HDBreastRtServ, and HDBreastLtServ.

About this task

Follow this process to prepare for the automated SNR test using the 1.5T HD Vibrant Breast Array by GE/USAI (M3087JG).

Procedure

  1. Place the coil on the patient table and insert the phantom positioner into the coil as shown in Coil and phantom positioner.
  2. The position of the spherical phantoms from the side of the coil is as shown in spherical phantoms.
  3. Place the phantoms on the phantom positioner as shown in the Phantoms and Landmark.
  4. Connect the coil to respective port of the system LPCA.
  5. Landmark on the center of the bridge between both the chambers (Phantoms and Landmark) and advance to scan.
  6. Perform the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance (MCQA) Tool.
