Checking the e-clips on the patient table scissors

Check the e-clips on the patient table scissors.

Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

Warning
Personal injury possibility
When doing maintenance on the table in its up position, severe personal injury can occur if the table is accidentally lowered during maintenance.
Make sure the lock safety bar is safely in place.

Procedure

  1. Raise the patient table to the full Up position.
  2. Raise the bellows to the Up position. See Raising the dockable table bellows .
  3. Lower the table lock safety bar.
  4. Lower the table to make sure all of its weight is resting on the lock safety bar.
    1Safety bar up (unlocked)
    2Safety bar down (locked)
  5. Visually inspect the two pin joints where the patient table scissors attach to the tabletop.
  6. Make sure the e-clip is installed and properly seated into the groove of the scissor pin.
    • If the e-clip is properly installed, continue to the Finalization steps. See Finalization.
    • If the e-clip is damaged or missing from the scissor pin, continue to Step 7.
  7. If the e-clip is damaged or missing from the scissor pin, check whether the scissor pin has walked out of the patient table scissors.
    • If the scissor pin has not walked out, replace with a new e-clip (46-220277P23).
    • If the scissor pin has walked out, a hazardous condition results and the entire table must be replaced. DO NOT attempt to re-install the scissor pin and e-clip; the table is unstable.
    Warning
    Potential crush hazard
    If the replacement part accidentally falls, it can cause serious injury.
    Do not put any part of the body below the replacement part.

Finalization

  1. Raise the table and then raise the table lock safety bar for normal operation.
  2. Check the operation of the table. When the table is docked, make sure that the table moves up and down properly.
  3. Lower the bellows to the Down position. See Lowering the raised dockable table bellows.
