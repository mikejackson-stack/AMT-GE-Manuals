Finalizing the Integrated Cooling Cabinet (ICC) deionization procedure

Follow these steps to finalize the Integrated Cooling Cabinet (ICC) deionization procedure.

Procedure

  1. Wipe down all wet surfaces.
  2. Close the ICC door.
  3. Coil the power extension cord and put on it top of the deionization and filter support bracket.
  4. Coil the deionization kit hoses using the Velcro straps. Close the case and clasps.
  5. Do a check scan. See Doing a check scan.
