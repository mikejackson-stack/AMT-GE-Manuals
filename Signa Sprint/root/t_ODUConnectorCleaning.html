ODU connector cleaning

Instructions for cleaning and lubricating a P-port receptacle, and properly storing the cleaning supplies.

About this task

The P-Port receptacle must be cleaned and lubricated on each of the system P-ports (P1 and P2 on the LPCA and P3 and P4 on the 32-channel table). Also, the coil connector must be cleaned and tested for proper fit to each P-port receptacle.

Procedure

  1. Important Use only the cotton swabs provided with the P-Port Spindle Lubrication Kit to clean the spindle tip. Clean with a dry swab. Do not use alcohol or cleaning products when cleaning the spindle tip. Do not apply any lubricant to the spindle tip. Avoid bending any pin contact.
    Use the cotton swabs included in the P-Port Spindle Lubrication Kit to clean the spindle tip on the coil connector of each coil on site, removing all dirty lubricant and dust particles from the spindle tip, especially from the groove. (See the illustrations below.)
  2. Important Make sure to inspect the lubricant expiration date prior to use. Do not use out of date material.
    Clean and lubricate all the system P-port connectors as shown below.
    Note Special lubricant (included in the P-Port Spindle Lubrication Kit) and cotton swabs are required for cleaning and lubricating the P-port receptacle on the scanner.
  3. During the cleaning process, inspect the receptacle contact surface and remove any debris.
  4. After all the P-Port connectors are cleaned and lubricated, cap and store the syringe with any unused portion in the original labeled packaging for future use. Make sure the outer packaging is clean of lubricant, remove the protective gloves, and wash your hands to remove any lubricant residue.
  5. Cap and store the syringe with any unused portion in the original labeled packaging for future use.
  6. After the syringe is empty or has an expired date code, dispose of the syringe according to the Safety Data Sheet (SDS) provided with the kit.
