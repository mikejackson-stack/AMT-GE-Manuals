Vent Requirements Inside the Magnet Room

  1. The customer is responsible for design, installation, and maintenance of all cryogenic venting materials inside the Magnet Room.

    1. The cryogenic vent system must be erected and connected to the waveguide outside the Magnet Room per guidelines given in Vent Construction and Vent Exit before the magnet is installed.

    2. If the magnet cannot be connected to the cryogenic vent within 24 hours of delivery, the Emergency Exhaust Vent must be installed and functional. The Emergency Exhaust Vent must be on at all times until the cryogenic vent is connected, or it must be wired to an oxygen monitor such that it starts automatically if oxygen levels in the Magnet Room are lower than normal. See Emergency Exhaust Vent Requirements.

  2. The cryogenic vent must not transfer any load to the magnet vent adaptor.

  3. GE HealthCare provides a flanged vent adaptor that is 203 mm (8 in.) OD and 610 mm (24 in.) long. GE installs it straight up from the magnet (inline with the waveguide in the RF shield). The vent tube can be cut as short as 102 mm (4 in.). See Figure 1.

    Note:

    All welds on the pipe must be ground down to a smooth 203 mm (8 in.) OD so that it can be clamped to the Ventglas with enough force.

  4. The customer must provide any additional vent tube (above the 10 to 32 mm (0.4 to 1.25 in.) gap) beyond the 610 mm (24 in.) provided. See c_Construction.html#id_2022482__fig_kj3_twn_flb.

  5. Other cryogenic venting systems are allowable (for example, sidewall, ceiling offset) as long as all other cryogenic venting requirements are met.

  6. Do not remove or modify the vent adaptor bolted to the magnet.

    Note:

    The GE-supplied flanged adaptor must be bolted directly to the magnet vent adaptor bolt flange.

  7. The vent must be located within 6.35 mm (0.25 in.) of the location (in relation to isocenter) shown in c_VentLocation.html#id_2026381__fig_l5f_xr2_hkb.

  8. A 10 to 32 mm (0.4 to 1.25 in.) isolation gap must be included at the top of the GE HealthCare supplied vent tube. See c_Construction.html#id_2022482__fig_kj3_twn_flb.
