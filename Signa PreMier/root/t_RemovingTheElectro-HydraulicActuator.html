Removing the EHA actuator

Removes the patient table up or down acutator from the patient table.

Prerequisites

Personnel requirements
Required personsPreliminary requirementsProcedureFinalization
2-65 minutes-
Tools and test equipment
ItemQuantityPart numberManufacturer
Nonmagnetic Titanium Service Tool Kit, Small Set15113258-
Electrohydraulic Actuator Manual Relief Tool15500235-
Circlip Pliers1--

About this task

Warning
Strong magnetic field
If the magnet is energized, tools and loose materials that contain ferrous material will be strongly attracted to the magnet and may become dangerous projectiles.
Only remove and install the actuator outside the magnet room.

Procedure

  1. Make sure the screw rod is installed.
    1Screw rod
  2. Remove four screws securing the upper and lower EHA shaft stoppers.
    1Upper shaft stopper screws
    2Upper shaft stopper
    3Lower shaft stopper screws
    4Lower shaft stopper
  3. Remove the upper and lower EHA shaft stoppers.
  4. Note: The relief screw is torque controlled at manufacturing. Do not reuse the EHA once the relief screw has been released.
    Loosen the EHA manual relief screw.
  5. Insert the nose pins of the circlip pliers into the eyelets of the circlip and apply force to the plier handles to expand the circlip.
  6. Remove the circlip and the pin connecting the EHA to the carriage plate.
  7. Remove the circlip and the pin connecting the EHA to the clevis block.
  8. Remove the EHA from the patient table.
