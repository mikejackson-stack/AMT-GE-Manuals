Installing the Resoundant active driver

About this task

The preferred location for the Resoundant active driver is on the floor. Depending on what is most convenient on site, a locally obtained table, or locally obtained wall brackets/shelf (which can support the weight of the unit) could be used as well.

When the unit is positioned on the floor, install the riser plate included in the accessories box.

Procedure

  1. Tilt the Resoundant active driver on its back.
  2. Unscrew the four rubber feet from the active driver.
  3. Screw the four rubber feet into the bottom of the riser at the four threaded locations.
  4. Screw the riser to the bottom of the Resoundant active driver.
  5. Tilt the unit forward onto its rubber feet.
