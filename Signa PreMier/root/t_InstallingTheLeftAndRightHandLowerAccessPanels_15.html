Installing the magnet left- and right-hand lower access panels

Installs the left- or right-hand lower access panel onto the magnet front cover.

Prerequisites

Personnel requirements
Required personsPreliminary requirementsProcedureFinalization
1-5 minutes-
Required conditions
Table interface cover is installed.

About this task

The procedure for installing the left-hand and right-hand lower access panels is identical. Follow the procedure below to install either or both access panel(s) as required.
Note: These figures are for reference only and may not be specific to your product.

Procedure

  1. Align the two ball and catch strikers on the back of the access panel with the ball and catch latches on the magnet front cover.
  2. Install the lower access panel onto the magnet front cover. Make sure the ball and catch latches retain the panel.
