Removing the rear base cover

Removes the rear cover from the dockable table base.

Prerequisites

Personnel requirements
Required personsPreliminary requirementsProcedureFinalization
1-5 minutes-
Tools and test equipment
ItemQuantityPart numberManufacturer
Nonmagnetic Titanium Service Tool Kit, Small Set15113258-
Required conditions
The right-hand base side cover is removed.
The left-hand base side cover is removed.

Procedure

  1. Remove the three screws securing the rear base cover to the table base.
  2. Lower the table approximately 5 cm.
  3. Step on the up and electrical dock pedals while simultaneously pressing the undock pedal fully. This will create enough space to remove the cover.
  4. Remove the cover from the table base.
    Note: Do not engage the down table pedal while removing the cover.
