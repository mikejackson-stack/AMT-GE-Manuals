Removing the P1/P3/PE cable assembly

Removes the P1/P3/PE cable track assembly from the dockable patient table.

Prerequisites

Personnel requirements
Required personsPreliminary requirementsProcedureFinalization
2-30 minutes-
Tools and test equipment
ItemQuantityPart numberManufacturer
Nonmagnetic Titanium Service Tool Kit, Small Set15113258-
Required conditions
The P1/P3/PE cable is removed from the cradle.
The table side covers, base covers, and bellows cover are removed from the table.

Procedure

  1. Warning
    Personal injury possibility
    When doing maintenance on the table in its up position, severe personal injury can occur if the table is accidentally lowered during maintenance.
    Make sure the lock safety bar is safely in place.
    Lower the table lock safety bar.
  2. Lower the table so its weight is resting on the lock bar.
  3. Disconnect the motor power and motor signal cables from the motor assembly.
  4. Disconnect the string encoder connector from the string encoder.
  5. Disconnect the Electro-CardioGram (ECG) cable from the Physiological Acquisition Controller (PAC).
  6. Disconnect the PAC fiber optic cable from PAC connector J1.
  7. Disconnect the P2 RF Tx cable from the P1 transmit connector.
  8. Disconnect the P2 bone conducting headphone cable from the P1 ODU headphone connector.
  9. Remove the two screws securing the up-limit switch terminal lugs to the up-limit switch.
  10. Disconnect the home switch and clutch signal connectors from the home switch and the clutch.
  11. Remove the two screws securing the base cover bracket to the table.
    Note: This bracket is being removed for better access to the ODU connectors.
  12. Remove the base cover bracket.
  13. Remove the patient alert tube from the P3 ODU connector.
  14. Remove the six screws securing the P1, P3, and PE ODU connectors to the ODU plate (two screws per connector).
  15. Cut the required cable ties securing the cables to the table cable brackets.
  16. Remove the two screws securing the left-hand lower cable track to the bottom of the tabletop.
  17. Remove the two screws securing the left-hand lower cable track to the slider plate.
  18. Remove the two screws securing the left-hand cable protection plate to the tabletop.
  19. Remove the two screws securing the left-hand upper cable track to the top of the tabletop.
  20. Slowly route the cable assembly up through the tabletop opening.
  21. Remove the cable assembly from the table.
