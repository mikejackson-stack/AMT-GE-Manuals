Opening the gradient Power Distribution Unit (PDU) top tray

Open the gradient PDU top tray to do troubleshooting or replacement tasks.

Prerequisites

Personnel requirements
Required personsPreliminary requirementsProcedureFinalization
1-15 minutes -
Tools and test equipment
ItemQuantityPart numberManufacturer
Nonmagnetic Titanium Service Tool Kit, Small Set1 Kit5113258-
Nonmagnetic Titanium Service Tool Kit, Large Set5112581-

Procedure

  1. Open the Integrated System Cabinet (ISC) front doors far enough to provide clearance and access to the gradient PDU inside the cabinet.
    Note: The front doors can also be removed if there is not enough space to open them all the way. Disconnect the ground wire and lift the doors up off the hinges and away from the cabinet.
  2. Turn the AC breaker to the Off position.
  3. Remove the screws on the bracket securing the three Power Supply (RPS) AC power cables to the PDU tray.
  4. Pull the three Power Supply (RPS) AC power cables out from the front of the gradient PDU.
    Note: Hang the disconnected cables over the ISC door to give easier access to the gradient PDU panel.
  5. Disconnect the two drip sensor connectors that are above the gradient PDU.
  6. Disconnect the CNJ1 control cable from the gradient PDU panel.
  7. Loosen the three floating screws that attach the front cover to the gradient PDU.
  8. Remove the front cover.
  9. Disconnect the CNJ2 control cable from the gradient PDU panel.
  10. Disconnect the nine RADLOK connectors. Use a screwdriver to push and release the lock tab. After a RADLOK is unlocked, it will slide off of the pin.
  11. Using an 8 mm nut driver, loosen the four screws that secure the gradient PDU top tray.
  12. Pull the metal handle with force to open the top tray.
    Note: You may need to pull several times to fully open the top tray.
