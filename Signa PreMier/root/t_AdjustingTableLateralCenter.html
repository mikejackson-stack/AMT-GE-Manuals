Adjusting the table lateral center

Adjusts the lateral position of the patient table relative to the bridge when the center measurement is not within limits.

Prerequisites

Personnel requirements
Required personsPreliminary requirementsProcedureFinalization
2-10 minutes-
Tools and test equipment
ItemQuantityPart numberManufacturer
Nonmagnetic Titanium Service Tool Kit, Small Set15113258-
Nonferrous Metric Ruler1--
Required conditions
The patient table is installed.
The table is at the top most position.
LOTO ISC is applied.
The table bottom covers are removed.

Procedure

  1. Prepare the table for adjustment:
    1. Mark lines on the floor along both sides of the frame edge.
    2. Loosen the anchor stud nut.
    3. Loosen the four bolts securing the dock frame to the magnet.
    1Dock frame bolts
    2Dock frame anchor stud nut
    3Dock frame side marks
  2. Using the alignment lines as a reference, move the table laterally until the difference between the table edge line and the bridge edge line is within 0 to 0.5 mm.
  3. Tighten the anchor stud nut and dock frame bolts.
