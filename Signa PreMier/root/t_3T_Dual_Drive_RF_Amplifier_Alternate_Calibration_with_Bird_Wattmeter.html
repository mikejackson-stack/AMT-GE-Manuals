Alternate calibration procedure for 3T Dual Drive RF Amplifier with Bird wattmeter

About this task

There have been reports of not being able to run RF Calibration or UPM Calibration according to specification without receiving Peak Power Faults. This procedure will walk through a process whereby Bird wattmeter readings can be measured/adjusted as low as 14 kW with a specification of 15 kW (+500W, -1000W), as long as RF Amp Readings (Internal sensor) are within a specific range (≤ 17.5 kW), and the difference between Body Ch 1 and Ch 2 are also within a specific range (500W).

Procedure

  1. In the command shell, type ice_term (or mgd_term) to bring up the ICE or SCP terminal.
    Note: The Shell window can only be launched when the EA3 user is included in the authorized EA3 group. Users not in this EA3 group will not have access to launch the Shell window. If you are not logged on as the proper logon user, log out and then log back on as the correct EA3 user with the authorized permissions.
  2. (For Channel 1 calibration)
    1. Follow the procedure for the Body Channel 1 connections and wattmeter setup.
      Note: Make sure that RF ENB switch is in the disabled (down) position when making connections.
    2. At TG=200, slowly adjust Channel 1 gain until the Bird wattmeter reading is as close to the nominal of the specification of 15 kW as possible (+500W, -1000W).
      1. If the RF amp trips before reaching 15 kW:
        1. In the SCP terminal, type printPower and record the Channel 1 Peak Fwd (Window) value in watts.
          Note: printPower may read 0 because of the fault. You may need to restart with lower TG and move the TG value up to just prior to the RF Amp trip (eg: by 1 TG count), and read printPower. If the RF Amp tripped due to a Peak Power Fault, restart and slowly bring up the TG value to before it trips.
        2. Slowly reduce the CH1/HD gain adjust on the front panel of the RF amp until the value on the SCP terminal is ≤ 17.5 kW at TG=200, as close to 17.5 kW as possible. Go to Step 2.c.
      2. If the RF amp does not trip before reaching 15 kW:
        1. In the SCP terminal, type printPower and record the Channel 1 Peak Fwd (Window) value in watts.
        2. If Peak Fwd (Window) value on the SCP terminal is ≤ 17.5 kW, go to Step 2.c.
        3. If Peak Fwd (Window) value on the SCP terminal is > 17.5 kW, reduce the CH1/HD gain adjust on the front panel of the RF amp until the value is ≤ 17.5 kW, as close to 17.5 kW as possible and repeat 2.b.ii.1.
    3. Make sure the Bird meter reading is 15kW (+500W, -1000W) and record the Channel 1 value.
  3. (For Channel 2 calibration)
    1. Follow the procedure for the Body Channel 2 connections and wattmeter setup.
      Note: Make sure that RF ENB switch is in the disabled (down) position when making connections.
    2. At TG=200, slowly adjust Channel 2 gain on the front panel of the RF amp until the Bird wattmeter reading is as close to the nominal of the specification of 15 kW as possible (+500W, -1000W).
      1. If the RF amp trips before reaching 15 kW:
        1. In the SCP terminal, type printPower and record the Channel 2 Peak Fwd (Window) value in watts.
          Note: printPower may read 0 because of the fault. You may need to restart with lower TG and move the TG value up to just prior to the RF Amp trip (eg: by 1 TG count), and read printPower. If the RF Amp tripped due to a Peak Power Fault, restart and slowly bring up the TG value to before it trips.
        2. Slowly reduce the CH2 gain on the front panel of the RF amp until the value on the SCP terminal is ≤ 17.5 kW at TG=200, as close to 17.5 kW as possible. Go to Step 3.c.
      2. If the RF amp does not trip before reaching 15 kW:
        1. In the SCP terminal, type printPower and record the Channel 2 Peak Fwd (Window) value in watts.
        2. If the Channel 2 Peak Fwd (Window) value on the SCP terminal is ≤ 17.5 kW, go to Step 3.c.
        3. If the Channel 2 Peak Fwd (Window) value on the SCP terminal is > 17.5 kW, reduce the CH2/HD gain adjust on the front panel of the RF amp until the value is ≤ 17.5 kW, as close to 17.5 kW as possible and repeat 3.b.ii.1.
    3. Make sure the Bird wattmeter reading is 15 kW (+500 W, -1000 W) and record the Channel 2 value.
  4. Compare the Channel 1 and 2 Peak Fwd values and record the difference in values |Channel 2 power output - Channel 2 power output|.
    • If the difference is greater than 500 W, adjust the channel with the higher value to reduce difference to 500 W or less (because the procedures above tries to be as close to the nominal of 15 kW as much as possible).
    • If the difference is 500 W or less, proceed to step Step 5.
  5. Confirm Channel 1 and Channel 2 power readings meet these specifications:

    • RF Amp power output (Bird wattmeter reading) is 15 kW (+500 W, -1000 W).

    • Peak Fwd (Window) value is ≤ 17.5 kW.

    • |Ch1 power output - Ch2 power output| is ≤ 500 W.
