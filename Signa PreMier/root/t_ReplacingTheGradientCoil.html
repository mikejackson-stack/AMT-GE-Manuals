Required conditions
Order the Nonmagnetic 80 N m Fixed Torque Wrench Kit (5790177) before starting this procedure. This is necessary for the Gradient Coil connection during a replacement or an upgrade. There is a new requirement torque specification of 80 N m, which is out of range for the existing non-magnetic torque wrench (5534134 or 5534134-2). The new torque wrench supports 80 N m with a deep well 19 mm socket.
Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.
Warning
Arc flash
If you do not use the correct torque wrench where the 80 N m torque requirement is given, this can cause arc flashing.
Use the Nonmagnetic 80 N m Fixed Torque Wrench Kit (5790177) where specified in this procedure.

About this task

The HRMw Gradient Coil (5768102) and the HRMw Gradient Coil with Braided HOS cable (5863473) are interchangeable unless the High Order Shim coils are being used. If the HOS feature is being used and the same gradient coil is not available for the replacement, see the SIGNA Premier High Order Shim (HOS) Upgrade Manual (5834925-1EN) for the additional parts required and the necessary upgrade procedure for installing the HOS parts.
