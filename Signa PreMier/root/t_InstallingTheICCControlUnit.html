Installing the ICC control unit

About this task

Procedure

  1. Remove the eye bolt from the control unit that was just removed and install it in the tapped hole located in the middle of the new control unit.
  2. Hook the winch from the hoist kit to the lifting eye bolt.
    Make sure the hook is seated in the lifting eye bolt. If not, the weight can shift causing harm to the equipment or engineer.
  3. Ratchet the winch to tighten the chain and lift the ICC control unit into position.
  4. Carefully place the control unit into the chassis and install the two pivot screws at the sides.
    Note: You may need to adjust (tighten or loosen) the tension on the chain to install the two pivot screws.

  5. Remove the control unit lifting eye bolt from the hole on the top of the ICC control unit.
  6. Attach the ground connection on right side of the ICC control unit.
    1. Remove the hex nut, lock washer, and washer from the bolt.
    2. Attach the ground lead to the bolt.
    3. Install the washer, lock washer, and hex nut onto the bolt.
  7. Complete the installation.
    1. Slowly push up on the control unit handle to pivot the control unit up and into place.
    2. Install the retaining screws on each side of the control unit.
    3. Reconnect the the XJ34, XJ35, XJ37, and XJ38 power cables to the control unit..
    4. Reconnect the XJ30, XJ31, XJ33, and J2 signal cables to the control unit.
    5. Reconnect the ICC main power cable.
  8. Disassemble the hoist kit and return it to the storage location. See Removing the hoist.

Finalization

  1. Restore the power. See Removing LOTO - ISC.
  2. Perform the control unit functional check.
  3. Do a check scan. See Doing a check scan.
