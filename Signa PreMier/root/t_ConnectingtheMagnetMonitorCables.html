Connecting the magnet monitor cables

Follow this procedure to connect the magnet monitor cables.

About this task

Table 1. Magnet monitor cables
Run #MAG MONConnection 2
E3020MAG MON > J10ISC > Cabinet IF > J13 AC/unblank
E3034MAG MON > J5Site Ethernet
826MAG MON > J9FJ3 (from F-50 CRY)
827MAG MON > FJ3ICC-F-50 CRY > Flow/Temp Sensor and FJ4
833MAG MON > FJ4ICC > F-50 CRY > JR
PowerMAG MON > PowerCustomer Outlet

Procedure

  1. Connect and route E3020 from MAG MON > J10 to ISC > Cabinet IF > J13 AC/unblank.
  2. Connect and route E3034 from MAG MON > J5 to Site Ethernet.
  3. Connect and route 826 from MAG MON > J9 to FJ3 (from F-50 CRY).
  4. Connect and route 827 from MAG MON > FJ3 to ICC-F-50 CRY > Flow/Temp Sensor and FJ4.
  5. Connect and route 833 from MAG MON > FJ4 to ICC > F-50 CRY > JR.
  6. Connect and route Power MAG MON > Power to the Customer Outlet.
  7. Remove LOTO from the MDP for the cryocooler breaker only. Turn on the cryocooler.
