Dockable table theory of operations

Describes the theory of operations for the dockable table.

Warning
Strong magnetic field
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field that the magnet produces.
Do not bring any ferrous tools or equipment into the magnet room.
The following illustrations identify the main parts of the table.

Patient transport

The patient transport is a portable patient table that can be rigidly attached to the magnet enclosure using a dock assembly. The dock assembly provides a secure clamping station to which the transport hook may be affixed during patient transfer.

The table can be raised and lowered whether it is attached to the magnet enclosure or at a remote location.

During operation, casters can be locked to prevent movement of the unit during patient transfer.
Note: The direction lock caster facilitates easy maneuvering and does not lock the table from moving.
CAUTION
Patient stability
Table not locked
To prevent the table from moving, make sure the three-locking casters and the direction lock caster are locked.
Note: The directional lock pedal will only lock the left front wheel (dock end). The remaining three wheels will swivel freely.

Hydraulics

A single-acting hydraulic pump, located at the docking end of the table, provides hydraulic pressure to raise the table. The hydraulic pump receives its power either from an electric motor in the dock, or from a foot pedal located at the rear of the table. The table is lowered by gravity when the down valve, located at the base of the hydraulic cylinder on the table, is opened. This valve is mechanically linked to a foot pedal on the table and, when the table is docked, to a set of pedals located on either side of the dock assembly.

Pedal functions

Five foot pedals are located at the rear of the table. The table below explains the pedal functions.
Table 1. Pedal description
Mechanical dockActivates the docking mechanism to lock the table to the magnet enclosure without electrically connecting it to the magnet.
Table undockDetaches the table from the magnet enclosure.
Electrical dockActivates the docking mechanism to insert the electrical connector pins into the electrical ports on the dock.
Table upOperates the hydraulic pump that raises the table, using a mechanical linkage connected to the foot pedal.
Table downLowers the table by opening the down valve.

Cradle interlock

The cradle interlock prevents the table from being undocked or lowered unless the cradle is in the proper position on the tabletop. The figures below show the position of the undock link when the cradle is and is not in the proper position, the undock link and down link raised preventing the table from being undocked and lowered, and the undock link and down link lowered, enabling table undock and down functions.
Inadvertent movement of the table is also prevented by two mechanical latches built into the transport.
The cradle cannot move out of the tabletop unless the patient transport (table) is docked. This is ensured by the primary latch (dock link), which locks the cradle.
The figure below shows the primary latch with the table in the undocked and docked positions. When the table is undocked, the primary latch extends and engages the cradle, preventing it from moving off of the table. When the table is docked, the primary latch is retracted and allows the cradle to move off of the table.
The cradle can move forward only by approximately 100 mm if the table is not docked and the front latch has not locked the cradle. When the table is undocked, the secondary latch pin extends and engages with the cradle, allowing it to only move approximately 100 mm off the table. When the table is docked, the secondary latch pin is retracted and allows the cradle to move off of the table.
When the table is docked, the pin on the dock assembly pushes in the bumper on the table. The bumper pushes a rod in the table, pulling the secondary latch cable and retracting the secondary latch.

Armboard

The table has an armboard on each side that can be locked into multiple positions when they are adjusted to or above the level of the cradle. They can be raised to:
  • provide patient restraint.
  • support the arms of the supine patient for purposes of administering an injection while the patient is lying on the table.
When not in use, the armboards can be lowered.

Up-limit switch

The table has a roller-switch that indicates when the table is fully up.

Handling emergencies

In case of emergencies when the patient must be removed out of the magnet bore before or during the scan, the following procedure must be followed:
  1. Calmly move to the magnet bore.
  2. Reach the emergency egress handle and squeeze the switch inside the handle, and the system will automatically push the patient out at high speed.
    Note: Squeeze the switch until the patient end of the cradle is 750 mm or less from the patient end of the patient transport; do NOT pull the cradle.
    Note: If the system power is off, manually pull the cradle onto the patient transport.
  3. Undock the patient transport and exit the magnet room.

Table transport emergency release

The table transport emergency release is used in the event the undock pedal on the patient transport does not function. To release the transport with the emergency release, make sure the cradle is fully retracted from the magnet bore at the home position. Grasp the handle on the red lever and pull straight out to release the table.

Regular inspection and cleaning of patient transport

Note: In case of any repairs or replacements, GE Healthcare will make available the relevant information or parts. Contact your nearest GE Healthcare representative.
Do the following checks once every week or sooner according to usage:
  • Pump the table up to full height and then bring the table down using the down pedal. Listen for any abnormal noises. Visually check for any oil leakages below the patient transport.
  • Use a mild soap solution for cleaning the patient transport of body fluids collected.
  • Visually check whether all the caster wheels are rotating freely and the lock/unlock function is working properly.
