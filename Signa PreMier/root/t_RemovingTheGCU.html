Removing the Gradient Cooling Unit (GCU)

Remove the GCU from the Integrated Cooling Cabinet (ICC).

Procedure

  1. Release the bands from the shipping base, flip the side plate down, and roll out the FRU box.
  2. Open GCU FRU box, turn the top cover upside down, and set it aside for later use.
  3. Open the Integrated Cooling Cabinet (ICC) front doors far enough to remove the Facility Plumbing Unit (FPU).
    Note: The front doors can also be removed if there is not enough space to open it all the way. Disconnect the ground wire and lift the doors up off the hinges and away from the cabinet.
  4. Make sure the hoist is installed. See Installing the hoist.
  5. Prepare the ICC control unit for GCU removal.
    1. Disconnect the XJ35 power cable from the control unit.
    2. Disconnect the XJ31 signal cable from the control unit.
    3. Remove the retaining screws from each side of the control unit.
    4. Slowly pull on the control unit handle to pivot it down and out of the way.

      Result

      Once the control unit is tilted completely forward, the hose connections to the Integrated System Cabinet (ISC) manifold on top of the GCU are accessible.
  6. Prepare the supply and return hoses for removal.
    1. Close the supply and return line valves at the gradient coil (magnet cooling manifold).
    2. Using a 38 mm wrench, loosen the GC supply (A) and GC return (B) connections just enough to allow some air into the line.
      Note: To avoid excess spillage, wait a few minutes to let the hoses drain back into the GCU before fully disconnecting.
  7. Disconnect the supply and return hoses from the GCU.
  8. Return the ICC control unit back to its original position and secure it in place with the retaining screws previously removed.
  9. Make sure the valves are closed at the gradient coil.
  10. Drain the coolant from the GCU. There is also additional coolant inside the piping.
    Note: The capacity of the GCU tank is 15L.
    1. Prepare a 20L draining tank and a draining hose with a quick disconnect (supplied with the ICC).
    2. Insert the quick disconnect to the pump drain valve.

      Result

      Once connected, the valve inside will open automatically and the tank will start to drain.
    3. Repeat for the dirt trap drain valve (B) and the heat exchanger drain valve (C).
  11. Disconnect the ground wire from the bottom-front of the GCU.
  12. Loosen and disconnect the heat exchanger fittings.
  13. Remove the retaining screws and carefully pull on the handle to withdraw the GCU from the chassis.
  14. Attach the hoist brackets to the GCU using the included hardware. The hoist brackets can be found in the GCU FRU crate.
  15. Hook the winch from the hoist kit to the hoist bracket.
    Note: Make sure the hook is seated in the hoist bracket. If not, the weight can shift causing harm to the equipment or engineer.
  16. Ratchet the winch to slightly tighten the chain just enough to take the weight off of the slide rails.
    Note: The hoist and bracket illustrations below are representative. Your system components and parts will vary.
  17. Press the release tabs in on each side and push the outer slide rail back into the ICC.
    Note: Make sure the outer slide rails are completely inside the cabinet before lowering the cooling unit.
  18. Put the FRU box top cover in front of the ICC.
  19. Use the hoist to slowly lower the GCU onto the FRU box top cover.
  20. Remove the slide rails from GCU by removing the screws holding them in place.
    Note: The same slide rails will be used to install the replacement GCU.
  21. Slightly lift up the GCU, rotate the FRU top cover by 90 degrees, and lower the GCU into the FRU box.
  22. Remove the hoist brackets.
    Note: The same hoist brackets will be used to install the replacement GCU.
