Draining coolant from cryocooler compressor water lines

Use this procedure to drain coolant from the cryocooler compressor water lines. This procedure must be done while replacing the FPU or cryocooler compressor.

Prerequisites

Personnel requirements
Required personsPreliminary requirementsProcedureFinalization
1-30 minutes-
Tools and test equipment
ItemQuantityPart numberManufacturer
Coolant tank1--
Hand pump1--
Required conditions
Cryocooler compressor and system main breaker must be OFF. Refer to Applying LOTO - MDP.
Review Electrical Safety before applying LOTO.
Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.
Notice
When connecting or disconnecting the hoses, connect or disconnect quickly to avoid the excessive spilling of coolant.
Make sure you use a towel in one hand below drain valves any time you disconnect any of the hose disconnects.

Procedure

  1. Remove the ICC bottom brackets that secure the cryocooler compressor within the ICC.
  2. Close the small black valve (A) on the cryocooler compressor supply side at the bottom.
    Connection/supplyDescription
    ASmall black valve (cryocooler supply side)
    BSmall red city water supply valve
    CCryocooler compressor supply
    DCity water supply line
    ECity water return line
    FCryocooler compressor return
    GCryocooler return side
  3. Remove the cryocooler compressor water supply line at the cryocooler compressor side and insert it into the drain tank.
  4. Wait until all the coolant is completely drained from the FPU and cryocooler compressor lines.
  5. Close the small black valve (G) on the cryocooler compressor return side at the top.
  6. Remove the cryocooler compressor water return line at the cryocooler compressor side and insert it into the drain tank.
