Installing the rigibelt drive (actuator)

Install the rigibelt drive (actuator) to the patient table.

Procedure

  1. Gradually feed the belts into the cradle.
  2. Install the rigibelt actuator.
    1. Insert the rigibelt actuator and tighten the belt.
    2. Raise the rigibelt actuator slightly to clear the shaft located under the rigibelt.
    3. Slide the rigibelt actuator back.
    4. Secure the two screws.
  3. Slide the rigibelt connector into place.
  4. Move the connection block inward to secure the rigibelt connector.
  5. Secure the two screws that connect the rigibelt and connector block until the connector block cannot be moved.
  6. Manually move the cradle to the home position to allow access to the connector block.
  7. Install the gear box assembly by securing 4 screws.
  8. Tighten the upper clutch screw with an Allen wrench to connect the gearbox shaft to the clutch.
  9. Install the two motor assembly cables.
  10. Install and secure the covers:
    1. Install the bottom cover and secure the four screws.
    2. Install the rear top cover and secure the six screws.
    3. Slide the motor cover in and secure the five screws.
