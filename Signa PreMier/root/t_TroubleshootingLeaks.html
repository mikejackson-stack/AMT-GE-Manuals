Troubleshooting leaks

Identify and troubleshoot leaks on your series magnet.

Prerequisites

Tools and test equipment
ItemQuantityPart numberManufacturer
Pair: Nonferrous Safety Shoes1 per person--
Safety Glasses1 per person--

About this task

Operation of the magnet in the Controlled Zero Boil-Off (CØBO) mode with internal helium pressure > psig increases the potential of helium leaks. A number of small helium leaks will have a significant effect on boil-off and result in the measurable loss of helium over time.
  • Ice or condensation on plenum plumbing indicates a leak in the helium plumbing.
  • Major helium leaks will depressurize the helium vessel and can result in cryopumping and icing inside the vessel.
Observe the Magnet Monitor helium level data over a month or more. A slow helium level decrease is evidence of leaking helium.

Commercial leak detector fluids or instruments available in the HVAC industry can be used to find small leaks in the helium plumbing and venting. Some examples available through HVAC distributors are Bird Dog Quart Spray (DOG-1Q), Big Blue (Rt100S or Rt100G), Snoop, and Portable Leak Detector.

Procedure

  1. Check the Magnet Monitor operation if pressure is less than the set point.
    Note: Helium pressure less than what the Magnet Monitor is set at indicates a leak. When the pressure is less than the set point, examine the following areas for leaks with a leak detector fluid or instrument [if you are using leak detector fluid, make sure to keep each location being examined completely covered (for example, using a sponge) for sufficient time to identify slow leaks].
    1. All external plumbing joints.
    2. , instrument lead cover, and feed-through pins.
    3. lead and fill port caps.
    4. Pressure gauge.
  2. Repair any leaks found, and then test the repairs.
  3. Check for leaks at the burst disc.
    Note: Small (micro) bubbles may appear between the gasket and flange. This is typical for this gasket material. Make sure the and there are no major leaks [continuous bubbles over 2 mm (0.08 inches) in diameter]. Foamlike bubbles may show.
    1. Ramp the magnet down to zero field. See Ramping down a magnet to zero field.
    2. Remove the vent adapter.
    3. Check the burst disc for cracks or significant leaks around the gasket.
    4. Replace any cracked or leaking burst disc. See Replacing a burst disc.
  4. If the root cause of an issue is not found after completing troubleshooting, collect the information below, and call the Online Center for assistance.
