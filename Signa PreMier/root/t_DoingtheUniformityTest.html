Doing the Uniformity test

Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

About this task

The Uniformity Test can be executed in two separate modes: Elliptical and Quadrature.
  • Quadrature mode uses the standard RF setting. Amplitudes of channel 1 and channel 2 are equal, and the phase difference between channel 1 and channel 2 is 90 degrees.
  • Elliptical mode uses the elliptical settings of 3 dB difference between channel 1 and channel 2 and a phase difference of -30 degrees.

Procedure

  1. Remove any surface or head coil from the bore.
  2. P the elliptical phantom in the center of the cradle, and use pads to support the phantom and test fixture.
  3. Set the angle of the phantom to 0 degrees.
  4. Landmark on the center of the phantom. Push Landmark and Advance to Scan.
  5. Let the phantom solution settle for five minutes before you scan.
    Note: This prevents flow-related artifacts, which can affect the test results.
  6. Install the service key.
    Note: A localizer body scan must be run prior to the automatic uniformity tool.
  7. At the operator console, select New Pt and prescribe the scan as shown below.
    ID:geservice
    Name:Localizer
    Weight (Lb):150
    Patient Entry:Head First
    Patient Position:Supine
    Coil:Body
    Mode:2D
    Pulse Seq:FPGR (Gradient Echo, Seq, Fast)
    Plane:3-Plane
    FOV:40 cm
    Phase FOV:1
    Slice Thickness:3.0 mm
    Center:S/I = 0, R/L = 0, A/P = 0
    Spacing:S/I = 0, R/L = 0, A/P = 0
    # of Slices:S/I = 1, R/L = 1, A/P = 1
    Chem SAT:None
    Freq Dir:Unswap
    Freq Matrix:256
    Phase Matrix:128
    Grad Mode:Whole
    NEX:2
    Autoshim:Auto
  8. Select Scan and let the scan complete.
    Note: Do not landmark again between scans.
  9. In a command window, type the following:
    cd /usr/g/service/mclass/uniformity
touch .uniformity_enable
run_uniformity_tool

    The Uniformity Tool screen shows with the scan status and results.

    Note: All buttons are inactive during a scan. Only the Abort Scan button is active during a scan (but is inactive when the scan is idle).

    Result

  10. From the Dual Drive Mode menu, select Quadrature Mode or Elliptical Mode.
  11. From the Data Acquisition Mode menu, select Four Scans.
  12. Click Scan and Analysis.
  13. Click Yes when the system asks Have you landmarked?
  14. Rotate the phantom to horizontal when the system tells you to, wait five minutes for it to settle, and then click Continue to start the scan.
  15. Note: In the following step, you will gather information that you can use to help you center the body coil.
    From the CSD, go to Utilities > Log into AGP/SCP > Click here to start this tool.
    1. In the SCP Terminal window, type: printPower
    2. Record the Body Peak Fwd (Window) value in the Uniformity Phantom Data Table.
      Note: The Body Peak Power value is required to reposition the coil. It is not needed to check uniformity. This value must be collected while the system is pulsing RF.

      Result

      After the first scan completes, a dialog shows that prompts you to reposition the phantom.
  16. When asked to position the phantom, rotate it counterclockwise to the 45-degree plane and then wait five minutes for the phantom to settle. Click Continue to start the scan.
  17. Record the Body Peak Fwd value.

    Result

    After the second scan completes, a dialog prompts you to reposition the phantom.
  18. When asked to position the phantom, rotate the phantom counterclockwise to the 90-degree plane and then wait five minutes for the phantom to settle. Click Continue to start the scan.
  19. Record the Body Peak Fwd value.

    Result

    After the third scan completes, a dialogue prompts you to reposition the phantom.
  20. When asked to position the phantom, rotate it counterclockwise to the 135-degree plane and then wait five minutes for the phantom to settle. Click Continue to start the scan.
  21. Record the Body Peak Fwd value.

    Result

    The scanner stops after scanning in the 135-degree position and automatically processes and shows the results.

    The screen indicates if the uniformity test passed or failed.

    Note: There are different specifications for Quadrature mode and Elliptical mode.
  22. Record the TG and uniformity (U) values in the Uniformity Phantom Data Table.

    You can get the TG values from the Uniformity Tool screen shown in Illustration 2.

    Note: The body peak power or RF amplifier peak forward (FW) power values can change during a scan, but you can determine a general reading within a few seconds. Your method to record this value must be consistent.
