Moving the gradient coil with a cart

Procedure

  1. Raise the jack about 50 mm (2 inches) and position the jack assembly (5191132) in the housing. Position the cradle on the jack.
  2. Move the cart to the patient end of the magnet, and position it in front of the magnet with at least a 50.8 mm (2 inch) gap between the cart and the magnet interface ring (required for lower wedge removal).
  3. Center the cart left-to-right with respect to the magnet bore.
  4. Release the hand lever on the cart handle to set the brakes. This will keep the cart from moving after the cart is aligned with the bore.
  5. Obtain the male insertion tube (2284929), tube standoff (5729027), additional tube standoff (5191626), and tube pilot.
    1. Position the tube standoff on the male insertion tube and secure with four SCHS 3/8 x 16 x 1.5 inch Hex Socket Screws.
    2. Position the additional tube standoff on the tube standoff and secure with four SCHS 3/8-16 x 4 inch screws.
    3. Position the tube pilot on the additional tube standoff and secure with fourSCHS 3/8 x 16 x 1.25 inch Screws.
  6. Remove the PVC shield from the brass thread of the male insertion tube.
  7. Move the male insertion tube through the tube guide roller assemblies at both sides of the gradient coil.
  8. Push the pilot shaft through the support plate mounting bearing, and insert a nonmagnetic safety pin through the hole in the pilot shaft.
  9. Connect the female and male insertion tubes from the shipping crate.
  10. Check the centering of the cart left-to-right with respect to the magnet bore, and adjust the longitudinal alignment of the cart as necessary.
  11. Remove all but the four bottom wedges. Keep wedges 1-2 on the patient end, and 1-2 on the service end.
  12. Raise the tube jack and the support plate mounting bearing at the same time to lift the gradient coil.
  13. Remove all four remaining wedges from the patient side and service side.
  14. Lift the coil high enough to remove the gradient mounting pad, and remove it from the patient end (front) of the magnet.
    Note: Keep the coil as close to level with the magnet bore.
  15. Lower the gradient coil and adjust it left-to-right to keep the coil centered in the magnet bore. Adjust as necessary.
  16. Slowly push the gradient coil out of the magnet bore while watching the clearances to the left, right, top, and bottom; adjust the tube jack and support plate mounting bearing as required to keep the coil from touching the magnet bore surface.
  17. Gradually lower the tube jack and the support plate mounting bearing until the weight of the gradient coil settles on the cart.
  18. CAUTION
    Possible Equipment Damage or Personal Injury
    Failure to properly secure coil to cart can result in equipment damage or injury.
    HRMw coil must be properly secured to coil cart.
    Properly secure gradient coil to coil cart with shipping brackets (5357473).
  19. CAUTION
    Possible Equipment Damage or Personal Injury
    Removing the insertion tool removes the support for the HRMw coil.
    Make sure the coil is properly secured to the cart before removing the insertion tool.
    Remove the insertion tool from the gradient coil using following steps.
    1. Remove safety pin from the support plate.
    2. Slide out the insertion tool until the joint can be seen.
    3. With one person holding the male insertion tube to keep it from rolling, two people remove the female tube from the male tube.
    4. Remove the male insertion tube with two people.
    Note: Each of the two pieces of the gradient insertion tool weighs less than 35lb (15.9kg).
  20. Move the gradient coil and cart out of magnet room with three people
  21. Remove the mounting plates from the gradient coil.
  22. Secure the gradient coil to the cart for shipment using the bracket listed:
    • Part number 5836225 for TRM, BRM, and XRMB gradient coils
    • Part number 5850819 for XRMW and VRMW gradient coils
