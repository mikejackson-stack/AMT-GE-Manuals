Calibrating UPM reflected power for head channel

About this task

After calibrating forward power, follow the procedure below to calibrate reflected power.

Procedure

  1. On the front of the exciter, set the RF ENB switch to the disabled (down) position.
  2. On the front of the RF amplifier, disconnect the coupler from J3 HEAD RF OUT.
    Leave J3 open and unconnected.
  3. On the front of the exciter, set the RF ENB switch to the enabled (up) position.
  4. Select values for the following settings within the tool:
    SettingValue
    Nuclei1H
    TaskCalibrate UPM
    RF ChainHead
    Power TypeReflected
  5. Click Start.
  6. Click Yes when the prompt asks if the hardware setup is correct.

    Result

    The tool reports the steps as they progress. In about 3 minutes, the Status field on the tool will show PASS or FAIL.
  7. Based on the status choose one of the following options:
    • FAIL – Refer to Troubleshooting the UPM.
    • PASS – Continue on to the next step.
  8. When the head channel is calibrated for reflected power, on the front of the exciter, set the RF ENB switch to the disabled (down) position.
  9. On the front of the RF amplifier, connect the coupler to J3 HEAD RF OUT.
  10. On the front of the exciter, set the RF ENB switch to the enabled (up) position.
