Disabling TR, DD, and RF inputs for head channel

Prerequisites

Notice: Do not perform head output power calibration until all processes for the body channel have been completed.

Procedure

  1. Make sure that the system is idle and all coils have been removed from the magnet bore.
  2. Open the front door of the ISC.
  3. Disable the following switches on the driver module and the exciter.
    1. On the front of the driver module, set SW2 to TR DISABLE (down) and set SW3 to DD DISABLE (down) to disable the TR and DD fault detection.
      Note: Leave SW1 set to HV ENABLE (up).
    2. On the front of the exciter, set the RF ENB switch to the disabled (down) position.
