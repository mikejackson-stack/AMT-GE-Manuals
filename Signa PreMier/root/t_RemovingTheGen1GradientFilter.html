        • Doing DQA calibration

          The DQA tool determines proper gradient polarity and proper gradient wiring by scanning a series of axial and coronal images of the DQA III phantom.

        • Doing the System Performance Test (SPT)

          The system performance test (SPT) automatically executes Eddy Current Check, SNR, Stability, and Coherent Noise tests. To do SPT in PM mode, you must launch SPT from the PM Assist menu.

          This procedure uses echo planar image (EPI) scans and the raw data mode of recon to analyze the raw data for white pixel artifacts. These raw data artifacts can exhibit themselves as corduroy artifacts in images.

        • Doing a check scan

          A check scan is done at the conclusion of many procedures. This functional check assures the unit is in good clinical working order.

Removing the Gen1 gradient filter

Follow this procedure to remove a Gen1 gradient filter - either the MNS-specific model or the non-MNS model.

Procedure

  1. On the scan room side, remove the gradient filter cover screws and cover.
  2. Starting on the left side of the gradient filter at the Z cable, remove the cable bracket screws and cable brackets.
  3. Remove the cable bolts and screws.
  4. Remove the grounding cables.
    Note: The gradient filter has two grounding pegs and three grounding cables. If a grounding peg has two grounding cables connected, remove both cables.
  5. Remove the cable from the filter.
  6. Repeat the steps to remove the remaining two cables (Y and X).
  7. On the equipment room side, remove the gradient filter cover screws and cover.
  8. Starting on the left side of the gradient filter at the X cable, remove the cable bracket screws and cable brackets.
  9. Remove the cable bolts and screws.
  10. Remove the grounding cable.
  11. Remove the cable from the filter.
  12. Repeat the steps to remove the remaining two cables (Y and Z).
  13. Remove the screws surrounding the filter mounting plate and remove the plate from the pen panel.
  14. Remove the gradient filter.
