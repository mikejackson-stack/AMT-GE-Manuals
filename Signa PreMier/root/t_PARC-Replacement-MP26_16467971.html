Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

DANGER
Fatal electric shock hazard
Hazardous voltage is present throughout the cabinet when it is energized.
To prevent fatal electric shock, disconnect power and do lockout/tagout on the Power Distribution Unit (PDU) before beginning any work. See the MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
CAUTION
Heavy component
The SPiCE (PARC) weighs 32.6 kg (72 lbs) and lifting it alone could result in a back injury.
Two people are required to lift the SPiCE (PARC).
Notice
Potential component damage
Electrostatic discharge (ESD) can cause permanent damage to electronic components.
Observe the following ESD precautions:
  • Work on a static-free mat.
  • Wear a static strap to make sure that any accumulated electrostatic charge is discharged from your body to the ground.
  • Create a common ground for the equipment being worked on by connecting the static-free mat, static strap, and peripheral units to that piece of equipment.

Removing the SPiCE (PARC)

Procedure

  1. Identify if you will be keeping the old hard drives or using the new hard drives that come along with the new SPiCE (PARC). It is suggested to keep the old hard drives so that the customer does not have to archive the data in the current SPiCE (PARC).
    1. If using the new hard drives, do a SaveInfo to save the host settings: Guided Install > Utilities > Save/Restore > Save Information & Save GI.
      Note: The SPiCE (PARC) must be up and the SRM green to save PET calibration files. PET calibration files will not be saved if the SPiCE (PARC) is down.
    2. Reboot the system to root logon.
    3. Power off the SPiCE (PARC).
  2. Disconnect the 10 GbE coin link (1) to the PROP.
  3. Disconnect the iDRAC remote management cable (2) from the switch.
  4. Disconnect the 1Gb PDD cable (3) from the PROP.
  5. Disconnect the 1 Gb subnet cable (4) from the switch.
  6. Release the SPiCE (PARC) rail locks.
  7. Assemble the universal hoist (see Hoist service kit and lifting accessories).
    Notice
    Inspect the strap for defects.
  8. Remove the yellow lifting strap from the FRU crate, wrap the strap around the SPiCE (PARC), and attach the strap to the universal hoist.
  9. Unlock the rails.
  10. Lift the SPiCE (PARC) away from the rails.
  11. Identify whether the SPiCE (PARC) has A5000 Graphics Processing Units (GPUs) installed. If installed, remove the GPUs and install them into the replacement SPiCE (PARC). See Replacing the SPiCE (PARC) GPU (software version 30.1 and later).
    Note: Systems with A5000 GPUs also have additional SSD installed. Transfer the SSD to the new SPiCE (PARC). See Replacing the SPiCE (PARC) hard drive (MP26 and later).

Installing the SPiCE (PARC)

Procedure

  1. Assemble the universal hoist (see Hoist service kit and lifting accessories).
    Notice
    Inspect the strap for defects.
  2. Put the yellow lifting strap on the ground, and put the SPiCE (PARC) on the yellow lifting strap. Attach the yellow lifting strap to the universal hoist.
  3. Lift the SPiCE (PARC), and put it on the rails.
  4. Lock the rails onto the SPiCE (PARC).
  5. Remove the yellow lifting strap, and push the SPiCE (PARC) into the cabinet.
  6. Connect the 10 GbE coin link (1) to the PROP.
  7. Connect the iDRAC remote management cable (2) to the switch.
  8. Connect the 1GbE PDD cable (3) to the PROP.
  9. Connect the 1 GbE subnet cable (4) to the switch.
  10. Remove LOTO from the PDU in the PET cabinet, turn the cabinet on, and press the EMO reset on the PET PDU.
    Note: The three DIP switches for the PET cabinet ethernet switch should be in the off position.

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Turn on the PET chiller.
  2. Configure the SPiCE (PARC) software:
    1. In Guided Install, select PET Configuration, and click the Configure PARC button (1).
      Note: When Configure PARC (1) is done as part of a post-installation service, PET raw data stored on the PARC is preserved, and the message below will not show.
    2. When the message PARC configuration will erase data on PARC. Do you want to continue? shows, click Yes.
    3. It takes 2 to 3 minutes for the system to request the Q.Core/SPiCE (PARC) setup disk. If prompted, insert this disk into the drive and click Continue. It takes approximately 15 minutes to load the PARC.
      Note: This does not apply to software version 30.
    4. If prompted, Please remove the optical media from the drive, remove the DVD and click Continue.
    5. When the PARC configuration successfully completed! message shows, click OK.
    6. Power off PARC.
    7. If using the old hard drives, remove all 8 hard drives from the old PARC and install them into the new PARC. See Replacing the SPiCE (PARC) hard drive (MP26 and later).
      Note: Return the old SPiCE (PARC) with the new hard drives.
    8. Restart the host computer.
    9. If using new hard drives, do a RestoreInfo.
