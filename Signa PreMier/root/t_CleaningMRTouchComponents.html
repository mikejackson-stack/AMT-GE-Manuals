Cleaning MR Touch components

Procedure and precautions for cleaning MR Touch components.

Procedure

DANGER
Electric shock hazard
Cleaning components of the MR Touch option while the active driver module is connected to electric power may result in electric shock.
Disconnect the MR Touch components from electric power before attempting to clean them. Wait for all components to dry completely before reconnecting power.
Note:
  • Do not rub the decals on MR Touch equipment during cleaning. Over time, the decals will rub off.
  • Do not use solvent-based or alcohol-based cleaning solutions for cleaning the active driver module.
  1. Clean MR Touch components with a damp cloth moistened with a solution of bleach diluted with water or a 5% hydrogen peroxide solution. The damp cloth must not have excess fluid. No fluid may drip from the damp cloth.
  2. Dry the MR Touch components by wiping the components with a clean, dry cloth.
  3. Allow all MR Touch components to completely dry in the air before you reconnect electrical power.
