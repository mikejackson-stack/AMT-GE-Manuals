Removing the gradient power supply

Prerequisites

Required conditions
ISC LOTO is applied.

Procedure

  1. Make sure power is no longer reaching the system PDU and gradient PDU by measuring the voltage at the test points.
  2. Open the ISC front doors far enough to provide clearance and access to the inside of the cabinet.
    Note: The front doors can also be removed if there is not enough space to open them all the way. Disconnect the ground wire and lift the doors up off the hinges and away from the cabinet.
  3. Before disconnecting any hoses, make sure that the ICC is turned off and put paper towels below the manifold drain valves to collect any coolant spillage.
  4. Disconnect the RPS IN (blue) and OUT (red) lines from the water manifold.
  5. Disconnect 48V power cables from J27 of all three RPS.
  6. Disconnect fiber optic cables from J5 of all three RPS.
  7. Disconnect the output and axis ID cables from J3 of all three RPS.
  8. Disconnect the output cable from the RPS being removed by pushing down on the side tabs to release connector.
  9. Remove the four nuts securing the RPS being removed and any RPS mounted below it in the ISC.
  10. Starting from the lowest RPS in the ISC, slide out the RPSs below the RPS to be removed enough to allow the RPS input cables to pass between the cabinet and the lower RPS power cable grounding bracket.
  11. Remove the eight screws securing the RPS input connectors cover to the RPS.
  12. Disconnect the input cables by depressing the button and lifting up on the rad-lock connectors.
  13. Remove the two screws securing the power cable grounding bracket to the RPS.
  14. Install the lift bracket on the RPS.
    Note: The lift bracket is stored in the FRU box.
  15. Prepare for RPS removal.
    1. Hook the winch chain to the RPS lifting bracket.
    2. Ratchet the winch to tighten the chain.
    3. Press in the release tab on each side of the RPS to remove the inner (RPS) slide rail from outer (cabinet) slide rail.
      Adjust the winch as needed to release any excess tension in order to separate the rails.
  16. Push the outer (cabinet) slide rail into the cabinet before lowering the RPS.
    Use a towel or gloves to get a firmer grip on the rail.
    Notice
    The bearings, rail and guide have sharp edges.
    The slide rail may cause cuts.
    Handle the slide rail with care.
  17. If space is limited, rotate the RPS 90 degrees before lowering.
  18. Position the RPS box top on the floor directly under the RPS.
  19. Use the hoist winch to slowly lower the RPS until it rests in the RPS box top.
  20. Remove eight screws, four on each side, securing the inner slide rails to the RPS.
  21. Remove inner slide rails from both sides and set them aside for later use.
    Note the orientation for re-installation.
  22. Remove the lift bracket from the RPS and place it back into the RPS crate.
  23. Drain coolant from the RPS.
    Notice
    Coldplate Damage
    The RPS is repairable and the coldplate may be damaged if the RPS is shipped with coolant inside.
    Completely drain the coolant from the RPS.
