Installing the P1/P3/PE cable assembly

Installs the P1/P3/PE cable track assembly into the dockable patient table.

Prerequisites

Personnel requirements
Required personsPreliminary requirementsProcedureFinalization
2-30 minutes-
Tools and test equipment
ItemQuantityPart numberManufacturer
Nonmagnetic Titanium Service Tool Kit, Small Set15113258-
Consumables
ItemQuantityPart numberManufacturer
7.31 x 0.184 Self-Locking Cable TieAs required46-208758P3-

Procedure

  1. Make sure the table lock safety bar is lowered and the weight of the table is resting on it.
  2. Remove the extra cable ties installed at the upper cable track end nearest to the P-port connectors for shipping. There should only be one cable tie around the entire cable bundle at the end of the cable track.
  3. Route the ODU connectors and cables, and the lower cable track from the tabletop to the table base through the left-hand tabletop opening.
  4. Route the Electro-CardioGram (ECG) cable from its exit at the left-hand upper cable track through the left-hand tabletop opening, and then along the left-hand Touch-and-Go (TnG) cable securing the cables to the two cable tie mounts at the bore end of the table.
  5. Remove the cable ties securing the cables to the left-hand upper cable track strain relief bracket.
  6. Install the two screws securing the left-hand upper cable track strain relief bracket to the top of the tabletop.
  7. Install cable ties securing the cables to the left-hand upper cable track strain relief bracket in the same locations and configuration as they were removed.
    Important: The cable ties must be reinstalled immediately after the strain relief bracket is secured to the tabletop. The cable ties are critical to cable track function.
  8. Install the two screws securing the left-hand cable protection plate to the tabletop.
  9. Install the two screws securing the left-hand lower cable track strain relief bracket to the bottom of the tabletop.
  10. Install the two screws securing the left-hand lower cable track strain relief bracket to the slider plate.
  11. Install the P1, P3, and PE ODU connectors to the ODU plate.
  12. Install the two screws securing the P3 ODU connector to the ODU plate.
    Important: Do not overtighten the ODU screws. Tighten one-half turn past free-spinning. Overtightening may affect the alignment of the connector and result in damage to the pins.
  13. Route the following cables from the P3 ODU in the right-hand inner cable bracket.
    • Motor power and motor signal cables
    • String encoder cable
    • Physiological Acquisition Controller (PAC) fiber optic cable
  14. Install the cable tie securing the cables to the right-hand inner cable bracket as shown.
    Important: Make sure the cables and the cable tie(s) are within the inner radius of the cable bracket. Cable(s) or cable tie(s) outside of the inner radius may interfere with table operation or damage the cable(s).
  15. Route the remaining cables from the P3 ODU in the left-hand inner cable bracket.
  16. Install cable tie(s) securing the cables to the left-hand inner cable bracket.
    Important: Make sure the cables and the cable tie(s) are within the inner radius of the cable bracket. Cable(s) or cable tie(s) outside of the inner radius may interfere with table operation or damage the cable(s).
  17. Route the following cables in the right-hand lower cable track, exiting the track at the top of the track and routing around the bellows cover bracket as shown.
    • Motor power
    • Motor signal
    • String encoder
    Note: Open the right-hand lower cable track to route the cables if it is not already open.
  18. Route the ECG cable in the right-hand lower cradle track from the upper opening to the lower exit.
  19. Route the PAC fiber optic cable through the track to exit the back of the track where the PAC 1-wire and PAC power cables exit.
  20. Close the right-hand lower cable track.
  21. Route the two motor cables and the string encoder cable with the dummy load cable along the side of the table, installing cable ties as shown below.
  22. Connect the motor power, motor signal, and string encoder connectors.
  23. Install cable ties securing the motor and signal encoder connectors to the cable tie mounts on the bottom of the tabletop.
  24. Route the ECG cable along the PhotoPlethysmoGraphy (PPG) cable on the inside of the support bar to the PAC and install cable ties securing the cables to cable tie mounts on the bar.
  25. Route the PAC fiber optic along the existing PAC cables through the PAC mounting plate to the PAC as shown.
  26. Connect the ECG cable to the PAC ECG connector and the PAC fiber optic cable to PAC connector J1.
  27. Secure the PAC cables to the PAC mounting plate with cable ties.
  28. Make sure that all of the PAC cables are routed below the edge of the PAC mounting bracket as shown.
  29. Connect the patient alert tube to the top left (as viewed from the rear) side of the P3 ODU connector.
  30. Install the two screws securing the P1 ODU connector to the ODU plate.
    Important: Do not overtighten the ODU screws. Tighten one-half turn past free-spinning. Overtightening may affect the alignment of the connector and result in damage to the pins.
  31. Route the cables from the P1 ODU in the left-hand middle cable bracket.
  32. Install cable tie(s) securing the cables to the left-hand middle cable bracket.
    Important: Make sure the cables and the cable tie(s) are within the inner radius of the cable bracket. Cable(s) or cable tie(s) outside of the inner radius may interfere with table operation or damage the cable(s).
  33. Connect the P2 bone conducting headphone cable to the P1 ODU headphone connector.
  34. Install the two screws securing the PE ODU connector to the ODU plate.
    Important: Do not overtighten the ODU screws. Tighten one-half turn past free-spinning. Overtightening may affect the alignment of the connector and result in damage to the pins.
  35. Connect the P2 blue RF Tx cable from the P2 ODU connector to the P1 transmit connector from the P1 port.
  36. Route the cables from the PE ODU in the left-hand outer cable bracket.
  37. Install cable tie(s) securing the cables to the left-hand outer cable bracket.
    Important: Make sure the cables and the cable tie(s) are within the inner radius of the cable bracket. Cable(s) or cable tie(s) outside of the inner radius may interfere with table operation or damage the cable(s).
  38. Route the up-limit switch cable from the PE ODU connector to the left-hand support bar and secure the cable to only one loop of the cable tie mount on the inside of the bar.
    Important: Make sure there is at least 25 mm (1 inch) of slack behind the cable tie mount with the electrical sled all the way forward.
    Important: Make sure the cable is tightly tied to only one loop of the cable tie mount.
  39. Connect the terminal lugs of the up-limit switch cable to the up-limit switch. Make sure the terminals are oriented vertically and the black lug is connected to the middle terminal, and the red lug is connected to the lower terminal.
  40. Install the base cover bracket.
  41. Install the two screws securing the base cover bracket to the table.
  42. Route the home switch and clutch signal cable from the top of the lower left cable track around the bellows cover bracket and along the side of the table towards the handle end of the table.
  43. Install cable ties securing the home switch and clutch signal cable to the tabletop.
  44. Connect the home switch connector to the home switch.
  45. Connect the clutch signal connector to the electro-magnetic clutch.
  46. Install cable ties securing the home switch and clutch signal cables to the cable tie mounts on the tabletop.
  47. Push the cables in the left-hand lower cable track such that the cable slack is at the top exit of the track.
  48. While holding the slack at the top exit of the track, adjust the position of the slide plate until the edge of the slide plate is aligned with the edge of the support bracket. Make sure the distance between the spring mounting locations shown is 90 ± 5 mm from outer end to outer end.
  49. When the distance is within tolerance, install four cable ties securing the cables to the end of the cable track plate. Split the cable into two approximately equal bundles and install two of the cable ties on each half bundle.
  50. Install the spring onto its support.
  51. Install two cable ties securing the cables to the top exit end of the cable track, one cable tie per half bundle.
  52. Route the slack of the cables exiting the top of the lower cable track forward and install four cable ties securing the cables to the cross-beam cable bracket. Install one cable tie on each half bundle at each end of the bracket.
    Important: Make sure the cables are within the outer edges of the bracket. Cable(s) or cable tie(s) outside of the edges of the bracket may interfere with table operation or damage the cable(s).
  53. Install cable ties securing the cables to the multiple cable tie bracket. Secure one white cable per cable tie and two smaller cables per cable tie.
    Note: For ease of installation:
    1. Loosely install the cable tie around the cable(s).
    2. Slide the cable tie onto the bracket hook.
    3. Hold the square portion of the cable tie while pulling the cable tie tight.
      Important: Pulling the cable tie tight without holding it may cause the bracket hook to bend.
  54. Raise the dock safety bar.
