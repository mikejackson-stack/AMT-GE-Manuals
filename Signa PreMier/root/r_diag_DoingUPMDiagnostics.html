Doing UPM diagnostics

The UPM diagnostic lets you select either an I/O or analog data test.

Diagnostic path - I/O and analog data

Diagnostics > System Function > RF > UPM Diagnostics

Diagnostics > Hardware Location > Integrated System Cabinet > RF > UPM Diagnostics

Purpose

The UPM diagnostic reads the power supply information from the UPM board.

Components tested

I/O data
  • Chassis power supply
  • Chassis power supply temp
  • Isolated 5 volts
  • FPGA initialization
  • Flash power
  • RF NB board and cable
  • RF MNS board and cable
  • Unblank cable
  • RF NB negative 12 volt
Analog data
  • UPB negative 12 volt
  • UPB positive 12 volt
  • RFNB positive 12 volt
  • RFBB positive 12 volt
  • UPB positive 5 volt
  • RFNB positive 5 volt
  • RFBB positive 5 volt
  • UPB positive 3.3 volt
  • RFNB positive 3.3 volt
  • RFBB positive 3.3 volt
  • UPB positive 1.5 volt
  • UPB positive 1.65 volt
  • RFNB VREF
  • RFBB VREF
  • RFNB board temperature
  • RFBB board temperature

Test sequence

  1. On the UPM Diagnostics screen, select one of the following tests in the Diagnostic Selections column:
    • I/O data
    • Analog data
  2. Specify the duration in the Test Duration (Seconds) field in the Test Parameters column.
  3. Click Run.

Results

Test results - I/O and analog data

A blue color indicates a normal result. A red color indicates an error.
