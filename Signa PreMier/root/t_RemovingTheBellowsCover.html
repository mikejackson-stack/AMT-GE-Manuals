Removing the bellows cover

Removes the bellows covering the lower portion of the patient table.

Prerequisites

Table 1.
Personnel requirements
Required personsPreliminary requirementsProcedureFinalization
1-20 minutes-
Tools and test equipment
ItemQuantityPart numberManufacturer
Nonmagnetic Titanium Service Tool Kit, Small Set15113258-
Required conditions
Patient table motor cover is removed.
Patient table side covers are removed.

Procedure

  1. Open the bellows cover zipper.
  2. Disconnect each rod, one at a time.
  3. Remove the six screws securing the bottom of the bellows cover; three on the left-hand side and three on the right-hand side.
  4. Remove the four screws, two at the head end and two at the foot end of the table.
  5. Remove the six screws at the top of the bellows cover, three on the left-hand side and three on the right-hand side, and lower the bellows cover.
  6. Remove the cover from the table.
