Connecting the safety analyzer to the component for testing

Select the component to which you will connect for testing.

Procedure

  1. Identify the component to which you will connect the safety analyzer.
    Table 1. Required ground resistance checks
    What to testWhen to test
    Global Operator Cabinet (GOC)After power and ground cable connection to GOC
    Integrated Cooling Cabinet (ICC)After power and ground cable connection to ICC
    Patient Table (PT)After patient table installation.
  2. Connect the safety analyzer to the component for testing.
Please choose from the following:
  • Connecting to and testing the Global Operator Cabinet (GOC)
    Measuring the ground resistance between the Integrated System Cabinet (ISC) and Global Operator Cabinet (GOC).
  • Connecting to and testing the Integrated Cooling Cabinet (ICC) and platform Integrated Cooling Cabinet (pICC)
    Measuring the ground resistance between the Integrated System Cabinet (ISC), and ICC and pICC.
  • Connecting to the PT GND point
    Connecting the safety analyzer to the patient table ground point.
