Checking the Emergency Stop function

Make sure the Emergency Stop function is operational.

About this task

The System Power Distribution Unit (SPDU) Emergency Stop buttons are located on the Operator Workspace keyboard and on the Magnet Enclosure cover (two buttons, left and right).

Procedure

  1. Press one of the Emergency Stop buttons.
    Note: These are representative illustrations. Your configuration may look different.
  2. Make sure the power to the gradient subsystem and RF subsystem is shut off:
    1. Check the Gradient Power Distribution Unit (GPDU) for each gradient driver module and make sure that the Enabled LED is off.Check the RF cabinet to make sure that it is off.
    2. RF amplifier verification: Look at the bank on the front of the RF amp and make sure the AC Power LED is off.
    Note: Activation of the Emergency Stop button for the ISC will remove the high voltage from the gradient power supply and the RF amplifier. However, be aware that the SPDU voltage will stay enabled in the ISC after the Emergency Stop button is pressed.
  3. On the SPDU, press the EMO Reset button to return power to all affected cabinets.
    Note: When you press the EMO Reset button, you should hear the contactor engage. This is another indication that the Emergency Stop button was pressed and is active.
  4. Repeat the steps in this section for each of the other Emergency Stop buttons.
