Connecting to and testing the PA port E connector

Connecting to and testing the PA port E connector.

Procedure

  1. Remove the cradle hardtop cover to access the PA port E connector.
    • (For fixed table) Remove the bore-end cradle cover and the cradle hardtop cover. See Removing the bore-end cradle cover and Removing the cradle hardtop cover.
    • (For dockable table) Remove the handle-end cradle cover and the cradle hardtop cover. See Removing the dockable table cradle hardtop cover.
  2. Disconnect the PE coil ports from the patient table. To avoid equipment damage the cables should be removed from the PA coil in the following order: C3-C2-C1.

    Result

    Three PE coil ports are part of the PA port E connector:
    • PE coil port C3 (white connector)
    • PE coil port C2 (black connector)
    • PE coil port C1 (black connector)
    Note: The following image shows the fixed table, the same connections apply to the dockable table for the PA-MCR tool connection.
  3. Connect the PE coil ports in the order given to avoid equipment damage.
    1. Connect PE coil port C1 (black connector) to J3 on the MCRv tool.
    2. Connect PE coil port C2 (black connector) to J4 on the MCRv tool.
    3. Connect PE coil port C3 (white connector) to cable C8 (37 pin sub-D male connector).
    4. Connect cable C8 (25 pin sub-D female connector) to J2 on the MCRv tool.
    Note: The following image shows the fixed table, the same connections apply to the dockable table for the PA-MCR tool connection.
  4. Run the test. See Running the coil datapath diagnostic.

Results

Note: If a test fails for PA Port E:
  1. Make sure that the connectors are firmly seated.
  2. Make sure that the screws are properly tightened.
  3. Rerun the test.

If the test fails again:

  1. Reseat the connectors.
  2. Reseat all test cable connections to the MCRv tool connectors.
  3. Rerun the test.
If the test continues to fail, run the test at the corresponding DS-DPP RRx port.
Correspondence between DS-DPP RRx module and PA port E connection
DS-DPP RRx moduleOther coil connection
DS-DPP RRx 3 PA Port E

Finalization

  1. Disconnect cable connectors C3, C2, and C1 from the MCRv tool.
  2. Notice
    Equipment damage
    MEMS circuitry in the posterior array coil may be damaged.
    Connect the C3 connector to the PA coil last.
    Disclaimer: Newly installed or updated systems may or may not have the MEMS power supply.
    Connect cable connectors C1, and C2, and C3 to the posterior array coil.

    Disclaimer:

    Software version RX29 and later introduces support for a non-MEMS system configuration. Existing or upgraded systems will continue to use the MEMS power supply. Newly installed or upgraded systems may not have the MEMS power supply. Identify the presence or absence of MEMS on your system by looking for the MEMS DC Power Supply Assembly (part number 6859128) in the power supply rack of the ISC.
