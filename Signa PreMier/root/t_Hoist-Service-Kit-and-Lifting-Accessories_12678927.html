Set up of the hoist service kit and lifting accessories for use in specific replacement procedures.

Safety
Warning
Possible personal injury or equipment damage
This unit could fall when using the hoist tool.
Before putting weight on the hoist chain, be sure that the links are aligned properly. This helps keep the chain from binding up or becoming kinked, which is nearly impossible to correct when the chain is under tension.
CAUTION
Lock Out/Tag Out required
Do the correct LOTO procedure for the type of replacement being done.
The cabinet should not have any power while assembling and using the hoist kit.

About this task

The hoist service kit and lifting accessories assist in the removal and replacement of Equipment Room FRUs that weigh 16.3 to 156.5 kg (36 to 345 lbs). The hoist consists of a service tool and system components. The winch with chains and spreader bar for lifting the replacement FRUs are in the hoist service kit. The support tube (located ) and main lifting beam (located in the PGR cabinet) are system components.

This document reviews the fundamentals of the hoist, discussing proper setup of the hoist service kit and lifting accessories for use in specific replacement procedures.

Procedure

  1. , remove the support tube. The support tube is secured with two mounting bolts.

    There are seven openings in the support tube to be used in multiple configurations.

  2. Note:

    To put the support tube correctly onto the cabinet, follow the proper orientation outlined in .

    Put the support tube on top of cabinet in which an item is being replaced, and secure the support tube using a wrench to tighten the two bolts onto the top of the cabinet.
  3. Remove the main lifting beam from the PGR cabinet. (The main lifting beam is located on the right side of the PGR cabinet and is secured with Velcro straps.)

    To take out the main lifting beam, you must unscrew and remove the connector from J9 on the Z-axis XPS unit.

  4. The main lifting beam slides onto the top of the cabinet. Cables or hoses may have to be moved out of the way, but none need to be disconnected . There is a rear bracket for each position. The main lifting beam hook plate slides into the rear bracket (shown in Figure 4). Make sure that the rear bracket is tight and secure.
    Note: The bracket lines up with the support tube's orientation as detailed in x. If the bracket is not aligned properly to receive the main lifting beam, then the support tube orientation setup is incorrect for the cabinet.
  5. For the PGR cabinet, remove the gradient cable cover by unscrewing two screws on each side (shown in Figure 5), then remove the needed beam slot cover by unscrewing the one thumbscrew (shown in Figure 6).
  6. Slide the winch into the main lifting beam, and secure the winch in with the pin so it does not slide out. (The winch is able to slide within the main lifting beam to allow proper alignment with the removed item or FRU lifting bracket.)

Finalization

  1. Remove the winch by removing the pin from the main lifting beam and sliding out the winch; put the winch (including hook and chains) into the hoist service kit box.
  2. Remove the main lifting beam, and return it to the PGR cabinet.
    1. Insert the hook end of the beam into the cabinet edge first, then put the bottom part of the beam in.
    2. Secure it into the cabinet with the Velcro straps and pin at the lower end of the beam.
    3. After the main lifting beam is inserted in the cabinet, plug in the XPS J9 connector on the Z-axis.
  3. Unscrew the support tube from the top of the cabinet, return it to the , and secure it with the two bolts.
