Filling the Integrated Cooling Cabinet (ICC) primary water circuit

Procedure

  1. Remove the bags covering each valve.
  2. Make sure the facility water hoses are connected to the ICC.
  3. Close the facility plumbing unit (FPU) drain line upper/city water return, FPU drain line (dirt separator), and FPU drain line bottom/city water supply.
  4. Close the ICC bypass and ICC supply valves.
    Important: All valves will be in the open position when the cabinet arrives.
  5. If the cryocooler has not yet been installed, make sure the F-50 cryocooler supply and return lines are closed.
    Note: The ICC may be attached directly to the magnet for magnet life support prior to installation.
  6. If the cryocooler is installed, open the cryocooler compressor supply and return line valves.
    Notice
    Risk of personal injury and product damage.
    Do not exceed the maximum pressure in pressure gauge.
  7. Slowly open the ICC return valve fully if it is closed.
  8. Slowly open the ICC supply valve slightly. Listen for the sound of the coolant starting to flow and stop.
  9. Check for leaks. If no leaks are found, open the ICC supply valve all the way.
  10. Make sure the pressure gauge is reading 6 bar or less. If the pressure gauge reads greater than 6 bar, direct the site to adjust inlet pressure to within specification.
  11. Make sure the FPU flow is 114-132 L/min (30-35 gpm) as shown on the host computer.
    Note: The typical water flow is 125L/min (33 gpm).
    Note: If the host computer is not available to provide the overall flow rate, check the magnet monitor to confirm the flow rate to the cryocooler compressor is within specification once the cryocooler is running (no flow alarms). However, the FPU flow must be checked once the host computer is available to provide the overall flow rate.
  12. If the facility flow meets the Signa Premier PIM requirements, no changes in facility flow will be required. If the flow is above the maximum flow of 132L/min (35 gpm) any bypass valve in the customers facility side or the ICC bypass valve can be used to reduce through the ICC flowmeter and heat exchanges.
  13. Adjust the F-50 cryocooler supply line valve if the flow to the cryocooler is above the limit of 10L/min (.26 gpm)
    Note: If the FPU flow is adjusted after the host computer is available, the flow to the cryocooler should also be adjusted.
  14. Check for leaks.
  15. Run the cryocooler.
    See GE3.0T LC Magnet and Cryogen Subsystem (5670009).
  16. Make sure the valves are in the correct position.
