Setting up hardware for doing Multi-Nuclear Spectroscopy (MNS) functional tests

Set up the the MNS Service T/R Interface and QA probe before doing the MNS tests.

Procedure

  1. Install the MNS Service T/R Interface into the P1 connector on the cradle.
  2. Put the grafidy base plate on the patient bed near the top of the bed.
  3. Put the QA probe in the center hole on the grafidy base plate and lock the probe into place.
  4. Connect the BNC connector on the QA probe cable to the MNS Service T/R Interface.
  5. Make sure that the cable path between the QA probe and the MNS Service T/R Interface is straight, and not coiled. See Figure 3.
  6. Move the cradle to the 345 mm position. Turn on the laser guides, and align the grafidy base plate crosshair to the laser. This position keeps the QA probe away from the PA coil that is located in the cradle.
  7. Push Landmark, then Advance-to-Scan.
    Note: The two LEDs on the MNS Service T/R Interface should be on. The green LED is an indication of correct preamp bias, and the blue LED is an indication of correct TR bias. If either of the LEDs is not on, do system troubleshooting.
