Connecting to and testing the Integrated Cooling Cabinet (ICC) and platform Integrated Cooling Cabinet (pICC)

Measuring the ground resistance between the Integrated System Cabinet (ISC), and ICC and pICC.

Procedure

  1. Connect one lead from the safety analyzer to the PDU ground located on the ISC.
    CAUTION
    Death or serious injury
    Failure to properly install the ground strap may result in improper electrical grounding of the door and a risk of electrical shock that could lead to death or serious injury.
    Make sure the ground strap is properly installed and securely fastened when the door is reinstalled.
  2. Attach the other lead to the ICC ground point.
    Note: Complete this test after making the power and ground cable connections to the ICC.
  3. Make sure that the measured resistance is less than 100 milliohms. If this fails, recheck and reposition the lead connections and examine the power cable connection. If the reading fails, add an additional ground cable to the cabinet that is failing.
    Note: If additional cable is needed to measure the ground resistance, field engineers must repeat the steps in Preparing the safety analyzer with the added cable attached to account for the resistance added by the cable.

Finalization

Restore the system. See Restoring the system.
