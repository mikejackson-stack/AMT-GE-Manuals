Removing the gradient coil

Procedure

  1. Attach two adapter plates (5338223) to the gradient coil patient end at the 3 and 9 o'clock positions with M10 x 20 mm bolts and M10 nuts.
  2. Attach adapter plate (5338224) to the gradient coil patient end at the 12 o'clock position with M10 x 20 mm bolts and M10 nuts.
    1Install Link Plate C (5338224) (12 o'clock)
    2Install Link Plate B (5338223) (3 o'clock and 9 o'clock)
  3. On the service end of the magnet, attach two adapter plates (5338223) to the gradient coil service end at the 4 and 8 o'clock positions with M10 x 20 mm bolts and M10 nuts.
  4. Attach adapter plate (5338224) to the gradient coil service end at the 12 o'clock position with M10 x 20 mm bolts and M10 nuts.
  5. Install the two tube guide roller assemblies (5308402) and mounting plates (5161983, 5161985) from the gradient coil insertion and lift kit shipping crate. Make sure the tube guide roller assemblies are tightly attached to the HRMw mounting plates.
    Table 1.
    Image 1

    Patient end - TRM gradient, (5338223)

    Service end - BRM, DF, XRMB gradients

    Image 2

    Service end - TRM gradient, (5338224)

    Patient end - BRM, DF, XRMB gradients
  6. Attach the adapter plate (5338224) to the gradient coil service end at the 12 o'clock position with M10 x 20mm bolts.

    Make sure that all bolts that secure adapter plates to the installation plates are securely tightened.

    Note: Different adapter plates are used for the front and rear of the gradient coil.
  7. Align the mounting holes of the patient end mounting plate (5161985), including guide roller assembly, with adapter plates (5338223, 5338224), which are already attached to the gradient coil. Attach the installation plate to the adapter plates using M10 x 20mm bolts and M10 nuts.
    See Figure 2.
  8. Align the mounting holes of the service end mounting plate (5161983), including guide roller assembly, with adapter plates (5338223 and 5338224), which are already attached to the gradient coil. Secure installation plate to the adapter plates using M10 x 20mm bolts and M10 nuts.
    See Figure 2.
  9. Remove two axial stops from rear end.
    ItemDescription
    1M10 x 30 mm bolt, brass
    2M10 nut, brass
    3Rear axial stop
    4Stud on axial stop
  10. On the service end of the magnet, remove the RF shield ground strap (5768286) from the magnet and gradient.
    1. Remove the RF shield ground strap from the gradient by removing the M10 x 35 Stud (2116325-7), M10 Nord-Lock Washer (5214524-6), and M10 Flange Nut (2274863-2). Discard the Nord-Lock washer upon removal.
      Note:

      Never use the nonmagnetic torque wrench to loosen connections. The torque wrench has a range of 10 to 50 N m (8 to 35 lb-ft), and removing Nord-Lock© or Spiralock© threads may exceed the limit of this wrench and destroy the ratchet components.

    2. Remove the M10 x 16 Brass HCS Bolt (5110638-61) from the magnet.
  11. On the service end of the magnet, install eight (UA/3TLC) or six (G3/W-WB) M10 x 280 stainless steel studs (5766679) at the locations indicated.
    1UA/3TLC series magnet
    2W-WB/G3 series magnet (side studs at 3 o'clock, 4 o'clock, 7 o'clock and 8 o'clock)
  12. Position the top tube support plate spacer (5729471) at the 12 o'clock position.
  13. Confirm the spacers are on the correct side of the tube support assembly . If necessary, move the tube support plate spacers to the outside face.
  14. Attach a tube support plate spacer (5729022) on the studs at the 4 o'clock (3) and 8 o'clock (1) positions and secure with M10 nuts.
  15. Position the tube support plate assembly (2284928-2) on the spacers using the studs.
  16. Secure the top with two M10 nuts (2),
  17. Secure the right side (3) with an M10 nut.
  18. On the left side (5), if the tube support spacer is used, attach using M10 x 80 bolt and nuts. If the tube support spacer is not used, attach using an M12 x 35 bolt and nut.
