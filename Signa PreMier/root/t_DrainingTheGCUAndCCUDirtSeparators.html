Draining the Gradient Cooling Unit (GCU) and Cabinet Cooling Unit (CCU) dirt separators

Follow this procedure to drain the Gradient Cooling Unit (GCU) and Cabinet Cooling Unit (CCU) dirt separators.

Prerequisites

Personnel requirements

1-20 minutes-
Tools and test equipment

Required conditions
If you are doing this as part of Deionizing and filtering Integrated Cooling Cabinet (ICC)coolant, follow the required condition as explained in that procedure, otherwise you can do this task with the system in operation.
Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

Procedure

  1. Remove the yellow cap from the drain that is labeled drain valve dirt trap from the GCU or CCU tank.
  2. Connect the drain hose that comes with the ICC to the GCU or CCU drain valve dirt trap and put the other end in a draining tank.
  3. Drain the coolant for 5 to 10 seconds and then remove the hose connection. This will remove any dirt from the drain valve.
  4. Put the yellow cap back on the drain valve dirt trap.
    Note: When doing deionization, the yellow cap does not need to be put back on the drain valve dirt trap.
  5. Refer to the appropriate Safety Data Sheet (SDS) and the site requirements to dispose the coolant.
