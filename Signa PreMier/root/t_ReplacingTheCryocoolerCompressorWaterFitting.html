Replacing the cryocooler compressor water fitting

Removal and installation instructions for replacing the cryocooler compressor water fitting.

Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

Procedure

  1. Apply ISC LOTO. See Applying LOTO - ISC.
  2. Apply cryocooler LOTO. See Applying LOTO - Cryocooler Compressor.
  3. Open the Integrated Cooling Cabinet (ICC) doors and disconnect the ground wire.
  4. Lift the door to disengage the hinge, remove the door, and set it aside.
  5. Remove the bottom reinforcement bar by removing the screws at the locations shown.
  6. Locate the facility plumbing unit (FPU) at the bottom right corner of the ICC.
  7. Close both the facility supply and return valves using the blue handles.
  8. Close the cryocooler compressor water supply and return shut-off valves, if they are open.
  9. Remove the cryocooler compressor supply 37° flare hose fitting from the right angle fitting.
  10. Put towels (or a small container) under the cryocooler compressor supply 37° flare hose fitting and the cryocooler compressor supply water fitting assembly to drain water.
  11. Remove the cryocooler compressor supply water hose from the right angle water inlet fitting.
  12. Use the provided Teflon® tape only at position 1 (see Figure 7) on the new cryocooler compressor water fitting assembly, and mount the assembly to the compressor water inlet.
  13. No sealing material is allowed at the 37° flare hose connection (position 2 in Figure 7). Connect this side of the water fitting assembly to the 37° flare hose.
  14. Refill the system, and make sure to open cryocooler compressor water supply and return valves after the service to allow flow again.
  15. Reinstall the bottom reinforcement bar.
  16. Reinstall the ICC doors and connect the ground wire.
  17. Remove cryocooler LOTO. See Removing LOTO - cryocooler compressor.
  18. Remove ISC LOTO. See Removing LOTO - ISC.

Finalization

  1. Open the facility water valves and examine all the hose connections to make sure there are no leaks.
  2. Turn on the Integrated System Cabinet (ISC) System Power Distribution Unit (PDU) and Gradient PDU power.
  3. Do a check scan. See Doing a check scan.
