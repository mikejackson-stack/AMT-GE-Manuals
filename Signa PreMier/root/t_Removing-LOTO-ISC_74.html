Removing LOTO - ISC

Remove LOTO from the gradient and system PDU breakers on the MDP before restoring power to the ISC.

Procedure

  1. Alert necessary personnel that power is being restored.
  2. Remove the locks and tags from the system Power Distribution Unit (PDU) breaker and the gradient PDU breaker on the MDP.
  3. Turn on the system PDU breaker.
  4. Turn on the gradient PDU breaker.
  5. Press the green EMO Reset button on the system PDU.
  6. Make sure power is reaching the system PDU and gradient PDU by measuring the voltage at the system PDU and gradient PDU test points as follows:
    1. Put the multimeter knob in volt AC.
    2. Measure the voltage between the following test points:
      • Vr to Vs
      • Vr to Vt
      • Vs to Vt
      The voltage at the test points should be a 100:1 step down of actual voltage reading as appropriate to the system. For example, if the system is configured as 480V then the multimeter reading should show 4.8V.
