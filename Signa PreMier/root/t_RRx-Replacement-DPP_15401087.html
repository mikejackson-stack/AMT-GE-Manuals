Replacing the Digitized Per Pin Remote Receiver (DPP RRx)

Removal and installation instructions for replacing the Digitized Per Pin Remote Receiver (DPP RRx) in the magnet outer electronics.

Prerequisites

There are 2 types of valid DPP RRx Modules:
  1. Gen 2 LN DPP: 5641325.
  2. Gen 2 Direct Sample (DS) DPP RRx: 5789759.

When stock of LN DPP RRx 5641325 and/or DPP Rx Dist 5434906 run out, the Field Engineer will have to replace all of the DPP RRx and Rx Dist with DS DPP RRx 5789759 and DS Rx Dist 5434906-2.

Additionally a TwinAx 100 ohm terminator 5758591 needs to be installed onto J30 of the exciter when installing the DS DPP RRx modules.
Note: If the terminator is not installed, the potential impact is an increase in radiated emissions from the cabinet. The lack of the termination on the LO port means the exciter is broadcasting a 112MHz signal into free space. This may get into the images as CNT if the noise finds a path into the screen room. This noise level may not be high enough to actually fail regulatory EMC limits at the system level, but it would show up as a noise spur in an EMC sweep.

Gen 2 DS DPP RRx and Gen 2 DS RxDist require software version RX28.

Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

Warning
Strong magnetic field
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field that the magnet produces.
Do not bring any ferrous tools or equipment into the magnet room.
Warning
Electrocution hazard
Dangerous voltages are present around the magnet subsystem when energized.
Lock and tag out power to the magnet room before starting work.

About this task

Five DPP modules are installed on the side electronics plate. The five modules are identical and can be installed in any of the five mounting positions.
