Calibrating the Multi-Nuclear Spectroscopy (MNS) amplifier

Follow this procedure after the MNS installation and upgrade to calibrate the CPC 8 kW MNS amplifiers.

Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

DANGER
Fatal electric shock hazard
Fatal electrical voltages exist throughout the MNS cabinet.
To prevent fatal electric shock, make sure the MNS RF amplifier is disabled when configuring the calibration tools.
Warning
Personal injury
Possible RF burn when disconnecting heliax cables from the RF amplifier.
Make sure the scan desktop icon shows the Idle message and the MNS exciter RF enable switch is in the Disable (down) position.
Notice
Possible equipment damage
Operating the RF amplifier with an improper load can cause permanent damage to the amplifier.
Do not operate the RF amplifier with an improper load connected to its output.

About this task

After following the SIGNA Premier MNS Upgrade and Installation Manual (5834926-1EN), follow this procedure to calibrate the CPC 8 kW amplifier for 3.0T.

The CPC MNS amplifier is a new amplifier. Cables connect to the top of the cabinet for the CPC MNS amplifier. There are now two versions of the CPC MNS amplifier: Gen1 (5411744) and Gen2 (5750811), which only contains two modules located at the bottom of the cabinet..

Note: The CPC MNS amplifier is only compatible with SIGNA Premier and DV systems, MR750, and MR750w. It is not compatible with HD systems.

Calibration setup

Topic ID: id_SL5438064-1058084

System setup

Procedure

  1. Make sure the system is idle and all coils have been removed from the magnet bore.
  2. Open the front door of the ISC cabinet.
  3. On the front of the driver module, set the T/R switch to Disable. This disables the T/R fault reporting.
    Note: Leave HV and DD set to Enable.
  4. Move the RF Enable S2 switch on the MNS exciter to the DISABLE (down) position.
Topic ID: task_gqv_zw5_klb

Configuring calibration hardware for the MNS amplifier

Procedure

  1. Insert the 50 kW element (green 4300A374-2) into the forward sensor port, and make sure the arrow points from input to output.
  2. As a safety measure, place the 5 kW (blue 4300A374-6) or blank slug element into the reflected port, and make sure the arrow points from output to input (if applicable).
  3. Lock the elements into place using the locking tab on each sensor.
    AWattmeter sensor
    BForward sensor element
    CBlank slug
    DSensor locking tabs
  4. Use a digital multimeter to measure the resistance between the dummy load RF connector center pin and the shield. Confirm a reading of 50Ω (± 5Ω). Replace the dummy load if the resistance is out of range.
  5. Plug the fan for the dummy load into a service outlet. Never use this dummy load without its fan running.
    Notice
    Possible equipment damage
    The dummy load must be cooled or it can overheat and become damaged.
    Do not apply RF power to the dummy load when its fan is not on.
  6. Disconnect the MNS output cable.
    • (For the Gen1 CPC amplifier, part number 5411744) Open the rear door and disconnect J3 from the combiner module at the top of the cabinet.
    • (For the Gen2 CPC amplifier, part number 5750811) Open the rear door and disconnect J2 from the power amplifier at the bottom of the cabinet.
  7. Make the coupler connections, as shown below.
    • (For the Gen1 CPC amplifier, part number 5411744) Connect the input of the coupler to J3 (output connector). Do not connect the coupler at the top of the MNS cabinet as the cable may not reach the dummy load.
    • (For the Gen2 CPC amplifier, part number 5750811) Connect the input of the coupler to J2 (output connector). Use an adapter (included in the kit) to make this connection.
  8. Connect the output of the RF coupler to the dummy load.
  9. Plug the wattmeter power supply into a service outlet (batteries are often not charged).
  10. Connect one end of the wattmeter cable to the RS232 sensor connector, and the other end to the coupler.
  11. Move the RF Enable S2 switch on the MNS exciter to the ENABLE (up) position.
  12. The power measurement kit can have one of two Bird wattmeters. The setup instructions are different for each.
    • (For Bird 5000-EX) Continue to Setting up Bird 5000-EX wattmeter to measure forward MNS maximum power.
    • (For Bird 5000-XT) Continue to Setting up Bird 5000-XT Wattmeter to measure forward MNS maximum power.

Setting up Bird 5000-EX wattmeter to measure forward MNS maximum power

About this task

This procedure is for the Bird 5000-EX. If you have a Bird 5000-XT, see Setting up Bird 5000-XT Wattmeter to measure forward MNS maximum power.

Procedure

  1. Press the ON button on the wattmeter. (If the coupler and sensor are not correctly attached to the amplifier you will see a No Sensor indication.)
  2. Press the arrow ▲ button under Scale.
  3. Enter 50000 to set the range.
  4. Press ENTER.
  5. Check the setting by pressing the arrow button under Scale.
  6. Press ENTER to exit.
  7. Press the arrow button under Fwd Units. Make sure the scale reads W (or kW on some wattmeters). If the scale reads dBm, select the arrow button under Fwd Units again to toggle the scale to read W (Watts) or kW (kiloWatts) on the TOP, forward power scale. Ignore the BOTTOM, reflected power scale value. It is not used during maximum power calibration.
  8. Press SHIFT.
  9. Press the arrow button under Setup.

    This shows the sensor type configured in the wattmeter. This procedure uses sensor type 43, which is optimized for peak power measurement.

  10. Make sure that the output reads 43, and then press ENTER.
  11. Press ENTER.
  12. Press the arrow ▲ button under Type and then toggle to change the unit of measurement to Peak.
  13. All kits require an offset to make sure of accurate measurement. Locate the Element Offset Factors card in the kit. Find the offset for 3T MNS 8kW Output.
  14. Press OFFSET on the wattmeter. Enter the numerical value from the offset card and press Enter.
    Note: To enter a negative value, first enter the numerical value, then the minus symbol (–).
  15. Continue to Calibrating the MNS amplifier.

Setting up Bird 5000-XT Wattmeter to measure forward MNS maximum power

About this task

This procedure is for the Bird 5000-XT. If you have a Bird 5000-EX, see Setting up Bird 5000-EX wattmeter to measure forward MNS maximum power.

Procedure

  1. Press the OK button on the wattmeter.
  2. Press the MENU button. Press the ▼ button to SCALE MENU, and then press OK.
  3. Press 50000 to set the range, and then press OK.
  4. Check the setting by pressing ▼ to SCALE MENU, and then press OK.
  5. Press OK to exit. Press ◄ to return to measurement mode.
  6. Press ▲ to make sure the scale reads W (or uW or kW on some wattmeters). If the scale reads dBm, press ▲ again to toggle the scale to read W (or uW or kW on the TOP, forward power scale. Ignore the BOTTOM, reflected power scale value. It is not used during maximum power calibration.
  7. Press MENU.
  8. Press ▼ to select ELEMENT TYPE, and then press OK.

    This shows the sensor type configured in the wattmeter. This procedure uses sensor type 43, which is optimized for peak power measurement.

  9. Make sure that the output reads 43, and then press OK.
  10. Press OK. Press ◄ to return to measurement mode.
  11. All kits require an offset to make sure of accurate measurement. Locate the Element Offset Factors card in the kit.

    Find the offset for 3T MNS 8kW Output.

  12. Press the MENU button on the wattmeter. Select OFFSET, and then press OK. Enter in the numerical value that was shown on the kit’s offset card and press OK. (The wattmeter rounds to three significant digits.) Press ◄ to return to measurement mode.
    Note: To enter a negative value, first enter the numerical value, and then the minus symbol (–).
  13. Press MENU. Press ▼ until SMOOTHING is selected, and then press OK.
  14. To make sure that SMOOTHING has been set, press ▼ until SMOOTHING is selected, and then press OK.
  15. The wattmeter should show 4 Samples. Press OK.
  16. Press ◄ to return to measurement mode.
  17. Continue to Calibrating the MNS amplifier.
Topic ID: task_wtn_zs4_ftb

Configuring the Bird TT800V wattmeter

Procedure

  1. Press the power button on the top right section of the Bird TT800V wattmeter unit to turn on the wattmeter.
  2. Plug the M5014G sensor into the USB port located on the right side of the wattmeter.

    Result

    The application should automatically open after plugging in the sensor and show a list of available sensors.
  3. Select the M5014G sensor.
  4. Click the elipisis on the top right corner of the screen and select Sensor Mode.
  5. Select 43 Peak.
  6. Click the menu bar on the top left corner of the screen and select Configuration.
  7. Select Smoothing(0,8,16,32,64,128).
  8. Select a smoothing level value.
  9. Locate the Element Offset Factors card in the kit and find the offset for the procedure being run.
    This output is used to make sure of an accurate measurement.
  10. Click the menu bar on the top left corner of the screen and select Configuration.
  11. Select Input Offset and enter the value found on the Element Offset Factors card.
  12. Select Forward Scale and enter 50000 for body channel or 5000 for head channel to set the range.
  13. Click the menu bar on the top left corner of the screen and select Readings.

Calibrating the MNS amplifier

About this task

Important: (For Premier systems) There is no need to connect the MNS 3T Service T/R Interface to calibrate MNS RF output.

Procedure

  1. Landmark in the center of the head coil area and select Advance to Scan.
  2. Start the RF/UPM tool:
    1. (For non-proprietary Service Tools)
      1. On the Common Service Desktop, select Calibration > UPM Tool.
      2. Select Click here to start this tool.
  3. Configure the UPM tool as follows:
    1. Set Task to Launch RF Cal.
    2. Set RF Chain to MNS.
    3. Select Start.
    4. When a dialog window appears asking if the system is properly set up, click Yes.

      The system sets the proper sequence for RF calibration of the MNS channel. This process takes about 1 minute.

    5. A dialog window titled RF Amp Calibration appears. Do not select anything.
    6. Click the Patient icon to go to the Scan workplace.
Topic ID: id_SL12727926-1058084

For CPC amplifier

Procedure

  1. Slowly increase TG to 200 or until the wattmeter reads 8 kW. Specification is 7.2 kW to 8.2 kW.
    Note: Make attenuator adjustments slowly. Give the wattmeter about 5 seconds between changes to settle. If the amplifier faults, reverse the previous adjustments and restart the calibration procedure through the UPM Tool.
  2. Make sure that the RF output is 7.2 kW to 8.2 kW at TG of 200.
    Note:

    (For the Gen1 CPC amplifier, part number 5411744) Control switches are used to adjust RF output. See Figure 30.

    (For the Gen2 CPC amplifier, part number 5750811) A potentiometer is used to adjust RF output. See Figure 31.

  3. Adjust for proper RF output by either toggling the switches on the original amplifier, or by adjusting the potentiometer on the new amplifier.
  4. After the amplifier is calibrated to 7.2 kW to 8.2 kW at TG = 200, select Done to end the manual prescan. Switch to the UPM Tool and select Success in the RF Amp Calibration dialog window.
  5. If the RF amplifier faults, close the Manual Prescan window and click Failure on the RF Amp Calibration dialog window. Click Start again on the UPM Tool interface and attempt the RF amplifier calibration procedure again.
