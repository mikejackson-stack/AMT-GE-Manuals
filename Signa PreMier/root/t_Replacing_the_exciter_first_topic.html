          Replaces any of the threes gradient power supplies, x-axis, y-axis, or z-axis.

        • Replacing the Gradient Amplifier (RGA)

          Follow this procedure to replace the gradient amplifier (RGA). The RGA unit weighs enough (about 90.7 kg [200 lbs]) that a hoist kit is required. There are currently two types of gradient amplifiers. One has RADLOK connections, the other has lug connections.

        • Replacing the High Order Shim (HOS) filter

          Remove the High Order Shim (HOS) filter from the Integrated System Cabinet Penetration Wall (ISC PW) and install a replacement filter.

        • Replacing the High Order Shim (HOS) power supply

          Removes either the NavShim (5778344) or ProDrive (5847514) High Order Shim (HOS) power supply from the Integrated System Cabinet (ISC) and installs a replacement NavShim or ProDrive HOS power supply.

        • Replacing the ISC top fan assembly

        • Replacing the Patient Handling Power Supply (PHPS) 2

          This procedure describes the removal and installation of the Patient Handling Power Supply 2 (PHPS2), which is a core component of the Patient Handling Sub-System (PHSS). The PHPS2 provides power to the other components in the PHSS, and serves as an interface between them and the equipment room.

        • Replacing the PS filter box

          Replace the PS filter box in the Integrated System Cabinet (ISC) Penetration Wall (PW).

        • Replacing the RF amplifier

          This procedure contains information on replacing the dual drive RF amp, which is located in the ISC cabinet. The dual drive RF amp unit weighs enough [about 335 lbs {152kg}] that a hoist kit is required.

Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.
