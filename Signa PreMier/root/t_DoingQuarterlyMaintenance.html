Doing quarterly maintenance

Prerequisites

Notice
Potential magnet quench
Do not press the RUNDOWN button throughout this process.
Do not reconnect the MRU cable if you find it disconnected. GE HealthCare strongly recommends that you stop using the system and immediately call your qualified service representative.

About this task

Procedure

  1. Make sure the green CHARGER POWER LED is on.
  2. Push and hold the TEST BATTERY button for 15 seconds. The green BATTERY TEST LED should turn on and stay on while the TEST BATTERY button is pushed.
  3. Toggle the TEST HEATER switch to the A position. The green HEATER TEST LED should turn on and stay on until the toggle is released.
    Note: Wait at least five seconds before switching between position A and B. Not doing so many cause the red HEATER ACTUATED LED to turn on.
  4. Toggle the TEST HEATER switch to the B position. The green HEATER TEST LED should turn on and stay on until the toggle is released.

    If the MRU test does not function as described in each of the steps above, with the specified LED turning on in each step, GE HealthCare strongly recommends that you stop using the system and immediately call your qualified service representative.

  5. If the green BATTERY TEST LED does not turn on or turns off during the 15 second test, replace the batteries and check charger voltage level.
    1. Connect the DVM to test point +20.7V and test point GND. The meter should read 20.7V DC +/- 0.1V DC. If not, remove fuse F2 and adjust R3 for 20.7V DC. Then reinstall F2.
    Notice
    Potential magnet quench
    Failure to heed this warning may cause a magnet quench.
    Before connecting P2 to J2, make sure that the AC supply main's power has been applied to the MRU for at least 3 minutes and that the RUNDOWN button has not been actuated for at least 3 minutes. The heater voltage is off before connecting to the magnet.
  6. If the green HEATER TEST LED does not turn on, push the TEST HEATER LED button. If the green HEATER TEST LED does not turn on, replace MRU.
