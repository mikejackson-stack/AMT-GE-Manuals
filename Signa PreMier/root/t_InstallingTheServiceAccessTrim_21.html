Installing the magnet service access trim

Installs the service access trim. The trim panel covers the screws securing the front and rear service access panels.

Prerequisites

Personnel requirements
Required personsPreliminary requirementsProcedureFinalization
1-5 minutes-
Required conditions
Front service access panel is installed.

Procedure

  1. Align the six ball catch strikers on the back of the service access trim with the six latches on the front service access panel.
  2. Install the service access trim onto the front service access panel. Make sure all six ball catch latches retain the panel.
