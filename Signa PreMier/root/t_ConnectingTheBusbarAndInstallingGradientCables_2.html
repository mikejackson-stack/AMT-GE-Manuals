Connecting the busbar leads to the gradient coil

About this task

Unless otherwise specified, the UA/3TLC magnet is shown in each step. The installation procedure is the same for each type of upgrade.

Procedure

  1. Put a new Nord-Lock washer (5214524-8) over each M12 x 30 A4-80 SmartBolt (5765900-50).
    Note:
    • The busbar connection to the gradient coil is very important. Make sure the Nord-Lock washers are in correct orientation and that the nut is torqued correctly.
    • Always use the new Nord-Lock washers that come with the FRU package. DO NOT reuse the old Nord-Lock washers.
    • Nord-Lock washers consist of two pieces, which are generally glued together. Always use a complete set with two pieces joined together in correct orientation. DO NOT use a separated single piece. If the Nord-Lock washers are separated, or the nuts are loosened, discard the washers and replace them with new Nord-Lock washers only. If new Nord-Lock washers are not available, contact the online center for further instructions.
    • The Nord-Lock washer must be installed after the lug. DO NOT install the Nord-Lock washer between the lug and terminal.

      The proper order of installation of the gradient cable hardware is the bolt, Nord-Lock washer, then lug as shown in Figure 2 image. The order is critical.

  2. Align the V groove of the lugs with the gradient coil terminal so that the lug is flush with the terminal.
  3. Tighten the indicator bolts (SmartBolts) with your fingers until the lugs are flush with the gradient terminal.
    Note: Do not reuse Nord-Lock washers.
  4. The SmartBolt indicator must be red when loose (otherwise it is damaged); if damaged, order new bolts and install. Use the nonmagnetic 80 N m fixed torque wrench (5790177) with a 19 mm socket on the extension to tighten the SmartBolts.
    Note: Overtightening can cause damage to the SmartBolts.
  5. Torque the indicator bolts in the direction of the arrow until the torque wrench clicks.
  6. Make sure the window in the top of the SmartBolt is black. If it is not black, re-torque using the nonmagnetic 80 N m fixed torque wrench.
    Note: When the SmartBolt is red, it is loose. Image shows right side SmartBolt black, left side red.
  7. Remove the screw and non-threaded half of the busbar cable clamp from each of the X, Y, and Z leads. Save the screw and threaded half; discard the non-threaded half of the clamp. On UA/3TLC magnets, only disassemble the clamp closest to the bore.
    1Screw
    2Busbar cable clamp
  8. Inspect the terminal safety covers. Make sure the white material is present.
  9. Reinstall the threaded half of the busbar cable clamp on each of the X, Y, and Z lead pairs. Install the safety cover on each clamp over the leads. Reuse the screw to secure each cover.
  10. Make sure installation is correct.
    1. Make sure the terminal safety covers make contact with the gradient cables.
    2. Inspect the cover assembly to ensure no gap exists between the gradient flange and terminal cover.
    3. Make sure the safety cover does not extend inward more than 5 mm beyond the I.D. of the gradient coil.
    4. The white filler material should extend beyond the black cover to fill any gaps.
    5. Adjust the safety cover orientation as needed.
