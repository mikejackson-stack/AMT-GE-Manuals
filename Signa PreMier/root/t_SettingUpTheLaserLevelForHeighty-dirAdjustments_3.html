Setting up the laser level for measurements

Set up the laser level and tripod for use in taking measurements where the horizontal or vertical laser lines bisect or align with parts of the patient table.

Procedure

  1. Attach the laser level (5831466) to the coupling on the tripod (5829559) and turn on the vertical and horizontal laser lines.
  2. Make sure the laser level is in self-leveling mode.
  3. Set the laser level height on the tripod such that the horizontal laser line can be seen in the rear of the scan room. Accuracy is not important for the height of the laser level.
    Important: The laser level and tripod contain ferrous material. Keep the laser level and tripod outside of the 200G line.
    Note: For very small room sizes there may be little space for the tripod. In this case, raise the tripod's center post fully, shorten the tripod legs, and orient the tripod legs at a more vertical angle.

Laser alignment

About this task

Note: The thickness of the laser line can span up to 2 mm depending on the distance from the laser level/tripod to the measurement point. Make sure to consistently measure the same laser line location on the scale.
Note: For the first measurements, it is useful to turn off the horizontal laser line and use only the vertical laser line.
Note: Always measure approximately 100 mm away from the edge of the bridge and the tabletop to avoid the profile changes of the parts.

Procedure

  1. Undock and move the table away from the magnet.
  2. Put the Horizontal Alignment Laser Target Tool (5830232) on the left side at the front of the bridge.
    Note: Early bridges may have machined channels for the rolling surface. These surfaces may prevent the tool from sitting level. The vertical rulers can be removed from their bases and used under the tool to correct.
    Note: Make sure the tool is assembled correctly. The bottom and side edges of the ruler must be against the bottom and side lips of the tool base. If the edges are not properly aligned, loosen the screws and adjust the ruler. Tighten the screws after adjustment or if they are loose.
  3. Put the Horizontal Alignment Laser Target Tool - Tall (5830232-2) on the left side at the rear of the bridge.
    Note: Make sure the short target is approximately 10 cm in from the front end of the bridge. Make sure the target is not resting on the chamfered surface. Make sure both short and tall targets are flush against the cradle vertical wheel bearing surface.
  4. Align the laser level and tripod such that the vertical laser line is approximately parallel to the bridge and strikes the short and tall targets along the horizontal scale at 0.
  5. Record the values the laser line strikes through on the short and tall targets in millimeters. Acceptance criteria for aligning the vertical laser line is a difference of 2 mm or less.
    Note: Make sure to capture if the number is positive or negative. The vertical laser line intersecting a number to the left of the zero on the scale is negative and to the right of the zero on the scale is positive.
    Note: These values are used as a datum reference plane for the following alignments. It is helpful to mark two locations, at the front and rear of the room along the vertical laser line in case the laser level is bumped or moved.
