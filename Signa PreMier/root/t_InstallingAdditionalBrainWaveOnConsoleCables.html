Installing additional BrainWave On Console cables

Topic ID: task_pww_ss3_ytb

Cedrus Lumina Hardware

Procedure

  1. In the control room and equipment room:
    1. Attach the cable to one of the 9-pin filters at the sub-panel. It is recommended to use port J203 if available.
    2. Insert the other end of the cable into the back of the Cedrus Lumina control unit, and put it on the GOC.
  2. In the magnet room:
    1. Attach the shielded cable to the other side of the 9-pin filter inside the magnet room at the sub-panel.
    2. Attach the other end of the shielded cable to the OTEC unit inside the magnet room.
    3. If you are installing video hardware on a Dell T5820 PC, install the Dongle mini DP male to DP female (5795077) to DisplayPort to VGA Video Adapter Converter (5748906) and then use the VGA adapter Converter to connect to the display (either monitor or goggles).
      Note: If the display connectors are still not compatible, request the third-party display solution provider to supply adapters as necessary.
  3. To connect the trigger cable:
    1. Open the PEN cabinet. Install the trigger cable on J16 on the exciter. This cable can go through the top of the PEN cabinet.
Topic ID: task_jy4_ys3_ytb

NNL Hardware

Procedure

  1. ResponseGrip – Operator Room Setup
    1. Route the ResponseGrip Bundle optical cable in the operator room to the ResponseGrip Interface.
    2. Connect the ResponseGrip Bundle to the corresponding inputs on the ResponseGrip Interface, follow the blue and gray color coding and labeling on the connectors.
    3. Connect the trigger cable to the appropriate input on the SyncBox and the other end to J16.
    4. Connect the Response Grip Interface to the stimulus presentation PC using the USB cable or the patch cable.
      • One end of the USB cable is connected to the mini-USB port and other end to an available 2.0 USB port on the stimulus presentation host PC or docking station/blackbox of the tablet PC.
      • The patch cable is connected to the RJ-45 port and to the matching port on the SyncBox following the labeling on the cable.
    5. Connect the 9V power supply to the 9V DC inlet using the provided splitter cable. Both the SyncBox and Response Grips are powered from the same power supply.
    6. Make sure the ResponseGrip Interface is switched off and then connect the power cable to the 9V power supply and the mains outlet.
    7. Turn on the ResponseGrip Interface. The light indicator on the ResponseGrip Interface should now be active.
  2. ResponseGrip – magnet room setup
    1. Feed the cable trough the waveguide into the operator room, and route to the desired location in the magnet room.
    2. Connect the ResponseGrip handles to the ResponseGrip bundle when all cable routing has been finalized.
    3. Slide the end of response grip bundle and response grip cable using the sloth and hand tight to secure the manual connection.
  3. Connect the trigger cable. Open the PEN/ISC cabinet. Install the trigger cable on J16 on the exciter. This cable can go through the top of the PEN/ISC cabinet.

Topic ID: task_bzs_mj1_15b

NNL In Room LCD Display

About this task

Note: For entire setup in detail, refer to NordicNeuroLab LCD monitor (InroomViewingDevice) LCD 3.1 - User Manual

Procedure

  1. The in room monitor and LCD Power Supply (LPS) needs to be located in the magnet room outside the 20 mT (200 Gauss) line. LCD Power cable fiber cable is connected to LPS and LCD monitor.
  2. Run the fiber optic cable from the LPS to the fiber transmitter in the operator room.
  3. Connect the power supply cable for fiber transmitter to stimulus PC.
  4. The other end of the fiber transmitter is connected with HDMI cable and the other end of the HDMI cable goes to the EDID adapter, which then goes to blackbox (or to display port on T5820/T5810 using mini-display adapter)
    Note: To run the NNL software, connect the license key to the blackbox.
