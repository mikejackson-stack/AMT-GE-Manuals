Removing LOTO - MDP

Remove LOTO from the MDP or the facility PDU before restoring power to these items. Facility-level LOTO must be done by an electrician.

Procedure

  1. Alert necessary personnel that power is being restored.
  2. Remove the lock and tag.
  3. Restore power.
    • For facility-level LOTO: Restore power to the facility disconnect.
    • For MDP LOTO: Restore power to the MDP at the main breaker.
  4. Press the green EMO Reset button.
  5. Make sure power is reaching the system PDU and gradient PDU by measuring the voltage at the system PDU and gradient PDU test points as follows:
    1. Put the multimeter knob in volt AC.
    2. Measure the voltage between any two test points (VR, VS, and VT).
      The voltage at the test points should be a 100:1 step down of actual voltage reading as appropriate to the system. For example, if the system is configured as 480 volts then the multimeter reading should show 4.8 volts.
