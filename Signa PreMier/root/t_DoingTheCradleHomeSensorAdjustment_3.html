Doing the cradle home sensor adjustment

Adjusts the physical position of the cradle home sensor.

Prerequisites

Personnel requirements
Required personsPreliminary requirementsProcedureFinalization
1-55 minutes-
Tools and test equipment
ItemQuantityPart numberManufacturer
Nonferrous Metric Ruler2--
Nonmagnetic Titanium Service Tool Kit, Small Set15113258-
Required conditions
The patient table base covers are removed.
The patient table motor cover is removed.
The patient table side covers are removed.
The patient table bottom cover is removed.

Procedure

  1. Move the cradle to the home position.
  2. Move the cradle until there is a distance of 2 mm between the end of the cradle and the end of the table top.
    Note:
    • The actual distance is important. Measure directly with rulers. Do not rely on just the IRD display.
    • This figure is a sample to illustrate assembly and part location. It may not be specific to your product.
    12 mm
    2Rulers
    3Table top surface
    4Cradle end surface
  3. Loosen the two screws on the cradle home sensor.
  4. Inspect the cradle home sensor LED on the table control box.
    • If the LED is illuminated, push the block for the cradle home sensor to the left and rotate the adjusting screw counter-clockwise to move the cradle home sensor until the LED is no longer illuminated.
    • If the LED is not illuminated, proceed to the next step.
  5. Push the block for the cradle home sensor to the left, and slowly rotate the adjusting screw until a click is heard or the LED illuminates.
    Note: The switch moves 1 mm for each turn of the screw.
    1Sensor adjusting screw
    2Screwdriver
    3Bad sensing location
    4Good sensing location
  6. Tighten the cradle home sensor switch screws while holding the switch in position.
