Removing the 1-piece bridge

Remove the 1-piece bridge assembly.

Prerequisites

Personnel requirements
Required personsPreliminary requirementsProcedureFinalization
2-20 minutes -
Tools and test equipment
ItemQuantityPart numberManufacturer
Nonmagnetic Titanium Service Tool Kit, Small Set15113258-
Required conditions
Electrical Safety

Procedure

  1. Shutting down the host computer.
  2. Applying LOTO - ISC.
  3. Undock the patient table and move it away from the magnet.
  4. On the service end, remove the UTNS antenna assembly and retain the parts for installation on the replacement bridge.
    1. Remove the nut securing the BNC adapter.
    2. Remove the washer from the BNC adapter.
    3. Remove the BNC adapter from the bracket.
  5. On the underside of the bridge assembly, disconnect cable M3507 from the end of the travel switch connector.
  6. Remove the eight screw covers and eight screws (four on each end) securing the 1-piece bridge assembly.
  7. With two people: on the service end, one person lifts the end of the bridge up until the rollers are clear of the edge of the bore. From the patient end, the second person pulls the 1-piece bridge forward until the rollers can rest on the rails.
    CAUTION
    Potential component damage
    The rear wheels of the bridge may be damaged.
    Do not pull the front bridge out of the bore until one person has lifted the rear of the bridge and the rollers can enter the bore without damage.
  8. On the patient end, with two people, remove the bridge assembly and carefully put it on the floor away from the magnet.
