Installing the hoist

Install the hoist in order to replace components within the Integrated System Cabinet (ISC) and Integrated Cooling Cabinet (ICC).

Prerequisites

Personnel requirements
Required personsPreliminary requirementsProcedureFinalization
1-15 minutes-
Tools and test equipment

Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

About this task

The hoist service kit is used to remove and replace FRUs in the Integrated Cooling Cabinet (ICC) and Integrated System Cabinet (ISC). The service kit includes a winch with chains and a spreader bar.

Hoist beam mounting brackets are installed on top of the ICC and ISC during system installation. They may need to be repositioned depending on what component is being replaced.

Procedure

  1. Apply LOTO to the ISC cabinet. See Applying LOTO - ISC.
    The cabinet should not have any power as you are assembling and using the hoist kit.
  2. Remove the hoist beam from its storage location.
    The hoist beam is stored on top of the ISC cabinet. It is secured in place with two captive fasteners.
  3. Identify the hoist beam mounting bracket locations on the cabinet for the component that needs to be replaced.
    Refer to the labels on the top side of the ICC.
    PositionComponent
    1Cooling Cabinet Unit (CCU)
    2ICC Control Box (CB)
    3Gradient Cooling Unit (GCU)
    4Facility Plumbing Unit (FPU)
    PositionComponent
    1Cabinet bay 1, Power Supply (RPS) and Gradient Power Distribution Unit (PDU) Top Tray
    2Cabinet bay 2, Gradient Amp (RGA) and RF Amp
  4. If the hoist beam brackets need to be repositioned, loosen the mounting bolts and reposition the brackets in the required locations.
    For the ISC cabinet, only the front bracket needs to be repositioned.
    Make sure the front and back sides of the ICC hoist beam brackets are oriented correctly. The FRONT and BACK sides are marked on each bracket.
    1Back hoist bracket (ICC)
    2Front hoist bracket (ICC)
  5. Tighten the mounting bolts to secure the hoist beam brackets in place.
  6. Install the hoist beam on the hoist brackets.
    1. Slide the hoist beam hook plate into the rear bracket.
    2. Insert the hoist beam mounting pin into the center hole on the front bracket.
      1Hoist beam hook plate
      2Rear bracket (ISC)
      3Front bracket (ISC)
      4Hoist beam mounting pin
  7. Inspect the ball bearing in the locking pin to make sure it is operating correctly and is not worn.
  8. Slide the winch into the hoist beam and secure it with the locking pin so it does not slide out.
  9. Secure the locking pin ring with a tie wrap to keep it in position.
    Important: This step will make sure the locking pin cannot slide out while lifting a component.
