Installing the RF body coil

About this task

Unless otherwise specified, the UA/3TLC magnet is shown in each step. The installation procedure is the same for each type of upgrade.

Procedure

  1. Wipe the gradient coil bore clean using the provided alcohol wipes.
  2. Position the RF body coil in front of the bore on the patient end with the air flow connections closest to the bore.
    Important: Carefully monitor the clearance around the coil. Avoid rubbing the coil components on the gradient coil flange.
  3. With two people, partially insert the body coil into the bore.
  4. Move two-thirds of the full length of the body coil into the magnet.
    1. To move the body coil into the bore, one installer supports the patient end of the body coil while a second installer lifts the leading edge. The installer on the patient end then slowly and carefully eases the body coil into the bore.
    2. Once the body coil is in the bore, the installer on the patient end should slowly and carefully push the body coil 2/3 of the way into the bore.
  5. On the patient end of the body coil, attach the patient end mounting bracket (5776627) to the body coil using four M6 x 30 screws (1000-M6C030-33) and four G10 M6 Washers (5988407).
    Note: Do not overtighten the M6 screws.
  6. While one installer guides the body coil from service end of the magnet, the second person should slowly push the coil into the magnet bore from the patient end.
  7. Loosely secure the front of the mounting bracket with two M10 x 50 Socket Head Screws (1000-M10C050-33) and two M10 Stainless Steel Washers (2000-M10-17) (front locking screws).
  8. Thread an M10 Brass Nut (3000-M10C-40) midway onto two M10 x 45 Socket Head Screws (1000-M10C045-33) and thread a screw through the threaded holes under each side of the body coil bracket (bottom adjustment screws and bottom locking nuts) until it contacts the bracket surface.
  9. Adjust the patient end bracket height.
    1. Make sure the four top tray locking screws are tight.
    2. Adjust the patient end body coil bracket height to the middle adjustment line on each side of the patient end body coil bracket to the middle adjustment line using the two bottom adjustment screws.
      Note: 1 full clockwise turn → 1.5 mm up.
    3. Make sure both the left and right side are adjusted to the same height.
    4. Tighten the front locking screws.
    5. Make sure the top tray block screws are tight.
  10. Secure the service end of the body coil to the service end body coil bracket using four M6 x 30 screws (1000-M6C030-33) and four G10 M6 Washers (5988407).
    Note: Do not overtighten the M6 x 30 screws.
