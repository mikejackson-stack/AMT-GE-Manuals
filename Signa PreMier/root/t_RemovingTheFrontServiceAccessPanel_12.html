Removing the magnet front service access panel

Removes the front service access panel from the magnet to allow access to components mounted on the left-hand side of the magnet.

Prerequisites

Personnel requirements
Required personsPreliminary requirementsProcedureFinalization
1-5 minutes-
Tools and test equipment
ItemQuantityPart numberManufacturer
Nonmagnetic Titanium Service Tool Kit, Small Set15113258-
Required conditions
Service access trim is removed.

Procedure

  1. Remove three screws securing the front service access panel to the rear service access panel.
  2. Slide the panel forward until it stops.
  3. Lift the panel upward and remove it from the magnet enclosure.
