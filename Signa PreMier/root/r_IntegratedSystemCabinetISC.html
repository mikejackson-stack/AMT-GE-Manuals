Gradient field replaceable units (FRUs)

Note:
  • Return non-functional FRU(s) normally as a bad part. Clearly label part(s) and indicate it is non-functional.
  • If functional FRUs are replaced, return them with a red label similarly to non-functional parts. This will make sure the parts are not accidentally scrapped. Clearly indicate in the debrief notes and on the red label that the parts are operational: "Replaced due to compatibility issues. Part is fully operational."

All gradient components (power supplies and amplifiers) must be of the same generation (RGD or RG5). See Gradient FRU compatibility guidance for more information.

Gradient Amplifier (RGA) (5724205-2/6418000-2) cannot coexist with Gradient Amplifier (RGA 5) (6418005-2) or Gradient Power Supply (RPS 5) (6419005-2). Gradient Power Supply (RPS) (5851000-2) cannot coexist with Gradient Power Supply (RPS 5) (6419005-2) or Gradient Amplifier (RGA 5) (6418005-2).
