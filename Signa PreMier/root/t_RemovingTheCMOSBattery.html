Removing the CMOS battery - Dell T5810

Remove the CMOS battery from the host computer.

Procedure

  1. Lift up the computer cover release latch, lift the cover upward to a 45-degree angle, and remove the cover from the host computer.
  2. Put the computer on its side with the system board pointed up.
  3. Disconnect the data and power cables from the DVD drive.
  4. Disconnect the cable from the GOCAA and unthread the cables from the latches.
  5. Press on the clasp to release the latch holding the cables on the side of the cage.
  6. Lift the release latch on top of the cage and slide the cage out from the compartment.
  7. Push the tab and remove the long black cover.
  8. Find the CMOS battery on the Dell computer system board.
  9. Move the latch away from the battery to remove the battery from the socket.
    If necessary, you can use a small screwdriver to remove the battery.
  10. Let the computer sit for a minimum of 3 minutes to let the CMOS memory clear.
