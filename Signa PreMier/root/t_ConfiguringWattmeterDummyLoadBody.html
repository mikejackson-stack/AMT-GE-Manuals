Configuring the wattmeter and dummy load for body channel 1

Prerequisites

Notice
Possible component damage
If you connect or disconnect the wattmeter sensor while the meter is ON, component damage can occur.
Do not connect or disconnect the wattmeter sensor while the meter is ON. Always turn the meter OFF before plugging in or unplugging the sensor.

Procedure

  1. Make sure that the scanner is idle and that the RF ENB switch on the DTX1 exciter is set to the disabled (down) position.
  2. Configure the calibration hardware connections for body channel 1.
    Notice
    Equipment damage
    Damage to the Dual Drive Transmit/Receive Switch (DTRSW) can occur when the body coil is connected to the RF amplifier and the wrong channel is selected.
    Disconnect the unused body channel when you test and calibrate the RF amplifier.
    1. Disconnect both body channel 1 and 2 output cables (J4 and J22) from the front panel of the RF amplifier.
    2. Remove cable J21 (RF input for body channel 2).
    3. Attach the 3/16-wavelength stub cable (part of the RF Power Measurement Kit) to body channel output J22.
      Note: The stub cable is used as a safety measure to cover and prevent contact with an exposed RF connector. It is not required for successful calibration.
    4. Connect the input of the coupler to RF amplifier J4.
      Note: This arrangement will remain in place during UPM calibration and functional check for body channel 1 ONLY.
  3. Connect the output of the RF coupler to the dummy load.
  4. Plug the wattmeter power supply into a service outlet (batteries are often not charged). Connect one end of the wattmeter cable to the RS232 sensor connector. Connect the other end to the coupler.
  5. Plug the fan for the dummy load into a service outlet. Never use this dummy load without its fan running.
    Notice
    Possible equipment damage
    The dummy load must be cooled or it can overheat and become damaged.
    Do not apply RF power to the dummy load when its fan is not on.
  6. Set the RF ENB switch to the enabled (up) position.
