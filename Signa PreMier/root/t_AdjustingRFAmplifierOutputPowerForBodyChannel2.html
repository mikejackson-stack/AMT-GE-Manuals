Adjusting RF amplifier output power for body channel 2

Procedure

  1. Select values for the following settings within the tool:
    SettingValue
    Nuclei1H
    TaskLaunch RF Cal
    RF ChainBody Channel 2
    Note: The Power Type drop-down menu is inactive for this step.
  2. Click Start.
  3. Click Yes when the prompt asks if the hardware setup is correct.

    Result

    The tool reports the steps as they progress. In about 3 minutes, the Status field on the tool will show PASS or FAIL.
  4. When a dialog box shows, do not click Success or Failure at this time. Instead, navigate to the Scan Desktop.
  5. From the Scan Desktop, click on the patient icon to navigate to the Scan Workplace.
  6. In the Prescan Looping window, slowly move the Transmit Gain (TG) to 180.
    Note: For all the Transmit Gain (TG) adjustment steps given in this procedure, after the TG slider is set and released in the Prescan Looping window, make sure that the setting is retained by the system (for example the slider does not move back to some other TG setting) prior to executing the next step in the procedure. If the setting was not retained, move the TG slider to the desired setting again and make sure of the setting.
  7. Make sure that the wattmeter reading is more than 1 kW and less than 9.5 kW.
    • If the reading is zero or very low:
      1. Make sure of the correct center frequency value.
      2. Click Done to stop looping and recheck the test setup cabling.
    • If the reading exceeds 9.5 kW, slowly adjust the CH2 gain adjust on the front panel of the RF amp to 9.5 kW watts for body channel 2.
    • If the amplifier trips, reset TG to the highest setting it reached before tripping.
    Note: If the manual prescan must be stopped and restarted, you must relaunch the calibration sequence using the UPM/RAP tool. Click Failure in the calibration popup and then click Start on the UPM/RAP tool with the same menu selections to relaunch the calibration sequence.
  8. Slowly increase the TG to 200.
    Note: Allow 10 seconds for the meter reading to stabilize before making the next adjustment.
  9. Check the wattmeter to see if the reading is 15 kW ± 500 watts.
    • If yes, continue on to the next step.
    • If no, slowly adjust the CH2 gain on the front panel of the RF amp until the wattmeter reads 15 kW ± 500 watts.
      Note: If the RF amplifier trips before the Bird wattmeter reading reaches 14.5 kW, do Alternate calibration procedure for 3T Dual Drive RF Amplifier with Bird wattmeter.
    (For Troubleshooting) To isolate the issues between RF Amp or System Loading, refer to RF amplifier power check tool.
  10. After the RF output is calibrated to specification, reduce the TG to 0 (zero) and click Done.
  11. Click Success to make sure that the RF output calibration was successful.

    Result

    After a few seconds, PASS appears in the status area of the UPM/RF Amp & Power Monitor Calibration tool.

    This tool provides a quick check for gross deviations in RF power levels (inputs and outputs), Power Supply Unit (PSU) voltages, and Field Effect Transistor (FET) junction temperatures for the RF amplifier.
