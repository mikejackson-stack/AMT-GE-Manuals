Removing the rear endbell

Removes the rear endbell from the magnet enclosure.

Prerequisites

Personnel requirements
Required personsPreliminary requirementsProcedureFinalization
1-30 minutes-
Tools and test equipment
ItemQuantityPart numberManufacturer
Nonmagnetic Titanium Service Tool Kit, Small Set15113258-
Required conditions
Rear cable cover is removed.
Rear bridge inner cover is removed.
ISC LOTO is applied.

Procedure

  1. Disconnect the cooling air hoses from the body coil.
    1Patient cooling air connection
    2HEX temperature sensor connector
    3Body coil cooling air connections
  2. Disconnect the HEX temperature sensor connector.
    Notice
    Component damage
    The heat exchanger temperature sensor cable is highly susceptible to damage.
    Use caution when connecting, disconnecting, or working around the heat exchanger temperature sensor cable.
  3. Disconnect the cable connectors from the end of travel switch.
  4. Disconnect the patient cooling air hose.
  5. Disconnect the cable connector from the speaker.
  6. Remove six screws securing the rear endbell to the magnet.
  7. Remove the screws and washers securing the rear endbell to the magnet.
  8. Remove the rear endbell from the magnet.
