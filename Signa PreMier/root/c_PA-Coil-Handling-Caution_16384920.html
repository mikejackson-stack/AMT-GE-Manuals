Wrist strap connecting point

Notice
Equipment damage risk
Before plugging/unplugging the PA coil cables on Port-E, especially when doing the coil datapath diagnostics for TDI PA Port-E, make sure you do LOTO for the ISC.
Do not switch off/on the DPP breaker separately because this could cause impulse to the PA coil, which can damage the elements.

The TDI Posterior Array (PA) coil is a static-sensitive part, which can easily be destroyed by static electricity. If you are troubleshooting the TDI PA coil or replacing the TDI PA coil (TDI Posterior Array Coil Replacement), or doing the ICW coil datapath diagnostic (Doing the coil datapath diagnostic) for TDI PA Port-E and so on, a wrist strap is required to prevent static electricity.

The ground point for the antistatic wrist strap can be any location on the metal frame of table. See Figure 3 for the recommended ground point, due to its accessibility.
