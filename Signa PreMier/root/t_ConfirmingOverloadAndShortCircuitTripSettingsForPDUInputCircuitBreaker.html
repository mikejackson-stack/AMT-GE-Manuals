Doing a check of the overload and short circuit trip settings for the Gradient Power Distribution Unit (GPDU) input circuit breaker

Do a check of the GPDU settings for various voltages.

Procedure

  1. Open the GPDU input circuit breaker door.
  2. Lift open the transparent plastic cover on the lower portion of the circuit breaker to access the DIP switches controlling the circuit breaker.
  3. Make sure the DIP switches are configured correctly. The first illustration shows the location of the DIP switches and the second illustration provides the DIP switch settings for each input voltage.
  4. If the DIP switches are not configured correctly, make sure proper personnel correct the DIP switch settings.
  5. Restore the small cover plate and close the input circuit breaker door.
