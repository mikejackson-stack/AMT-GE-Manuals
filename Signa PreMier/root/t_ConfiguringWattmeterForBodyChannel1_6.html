        • Electrical Safety

          You must follow all recommended safety procedures when servicing and working near energy sources.

          Shutting down the host computer shuts down the system safely and turns off the power to the host computer.

        • Applying LOTO - ISC

          Apply LOTO to both the gradient and system Power Distribution Unit (PDU) breakers on the MDP when servicing either of these items in the ISC. Apply LOTO to make sure of a safe environment when working on these parts.
