Removing the Cabinet Cooling Unit (CCU)

Remove the CCU form the Integrated Cooling Cabinet (ICC)

Procedure

  1. Release the bands from the shipping base, flip the side plate down, and roll out the FRU box.
  2. Open CCU FRU box, turn the top cover upside down and set it aside for later use.
  3. Open the Integrated Cooling Cabinet (ICC) front doors far enough to remove the Facility Plumbing Unit (FPU).
    Note: The front doors can also be removed if there is not enough space to open it all the way. Disconnect the ground wire and lift the doors up off the hinges and away from the cabinet.
  4. Make sure the hoist is installed. See Installing the hoist.
  5. Prepare the ICC control unit for CCU removal.
    1. Disconnect the XJ34 power cable from the ICC control unit.
    2. Disconnect the XJ30 signal cable from the ICC control unit.
    3. Remove the retaining screws from each side of the control unit.
    4. Slowly pull on the control unit handle to pivot it down and out of the way.

      Result

      Once the control unit is tilted completely forward, the hose connections to the ISC manifold on top of the CCU are accessible.
  6. Prepare the supply and return hoses for removal.
    1. Close the valves on the red ISC return connections (B) and loosen them just enough to allow some air into the line.
    2. Using a 38 mm wrench, loosen the green ISC supply connections (A) just enough to allow some air into the line.
      Note: To avoid excess spillage, wait a few minutes to allow the hoses to drain back into the CCU before fully disconnecting.
  7. Disconnect the supply and return hoses from the CCU.
  8. Return the ICC control unit back to its original position and secure it in place with the retaining screws previously removed.
  9. Drain the coolant from the CCU.
    Note: The capacity of the CCU tank is 15L. There is also additional coolant inside the piping.
    1. Prepare a 20L draining tank and a draining hose with a quick disconnect (supplied with the ICC).
    2. Insert the quick disconnect to the pump drain valve.

      Result

      Once connected, the valve inside will open automatically and the tank will start to drain.
    3. Repeat for the dirt trap drain valve (B) and the heat exchanger drain valve (C).
  10. Disconnect the ground wire from the bottom-front of the CCU.
  11. Disconnect the cam and groove fittings by pushing the cam levers in (towards the back of the ICC).
  12. Remove the four retaining screws and carefully pull on the handle to withdraw the CCU from the chassis.
  13. Attach the hoist brackets to the CCU using the included hardware. The hoist brackets can be found in the CCU FRU crate.
  14. Hook the winch from the hoist kit to the hoist bracket.
    Note: Make sure the hook is seated in the hoist bracket. If not, the weight can shift causing harm to the equipment or engineer.
  15. Ratchet the winch to slightly tighten the chain just enough to take the weight off of the slide rails.
    Note: The hoist and bracket illustrations below are representative. SIGNA Premier components and parts will vary.
  16. Press the release tabs in on each side and push the outer slide rail back into the ICC.
    Note: Make sure the outer slide rails are completely inside the cabinet before lowering the cooling unit.
  17. Place the FRU box top cover in front of the ICC.
  18. Use the hoist to slowly lower the CCU onto the FRU box top cover.
  19. Remove the slide rails from CCU by removing the screws holding them in place.
    Note: The same slide rails will be used to install the replacement CCU.
  20. Slightly lift up the CCU, rotate the FRU box by 90 degrees, and lower the CCU into the FRU box.
  21. Remove the hoist brackets.
    Note: The same hoist brackets will be used to install the replacement CCU.
