Installing the gradient coil

About this task

Notice
Product Damage
Make sure the gradient coil manifold is not pinched or crushed while moving the gradient coil into the magnet bore.

Procedure

  1. On the service end of the magnet, install eight (UA/3TLC) or six (G3/W-WB) M10 x 280 stainless steel studs (5766679) at the locations indicated.
    1UA/3TLC series magnet
    2W-WB/G3 series magnet (side studs at 3 o'clock, 4 o'clock, 7 o'clock, and 8 o'clock)
  2. Position the top tube support plate spacer (5729471) at the 12 o'clock position.
  3. Attach a tube support plate spacer (5729022) on the studs at the 4 o'clock and 8 o'clock positions and secure with M10 nuts.
  4. Position the tube support plate assembly (2284928-2) on the spacers using the studs.
    Note: Make sure the spacers are on the correct side of the tube support assembly. If necessary, move the tube support plate spacers to the outside face.
  5. Secure the top with two M10 nuts.
  6. Secure the right side with an M10 nut.
  7. At the 8 o’clock position, if a spacer is used on the support plate attachment, secure the plate with an M10 x 80 bolt and nut. If the spacer is not used, secure the plate with an M12 x 35 bolt and nut.
  8. Adjust the tube support plate horizontally with the adjustment screws until the tube support bearing is centered left-to-right.
  9. Position the jack assembly housing in the cart with the notch over the plate in the cart.
  10. Raise the jack about 50 mm (2 inches) and position the jack assembly (5191132) in the housing. Position the cradle on the jack.
  11. Move the cart to the patient end of the magnet, and position it in front of the magnet with at least a 50.8 mm (2 inch) gap between the cart and the magnet interface ring (required for lower wedge removal).
  12. Center the cart left-to-right with respect to the magnet bore.
  13. Release the hand lever on the cart handle to set the brakes. This will keep the cart from moving after the cart is aligned with the bore.
  14. Raise or lower the cradle of the cart as required to vertically align the tube support plate at the magnet and the gradient mounting plates at the gradient coil.
  15. Slide the male insertion tube through the tube guide roller assemblies at both sides of the gradient coil. Make sure the end with the standoff and pilot shaft points towards the magnet.
  16. Note: In the next step, do not overtighten the tube connection, because it may be difficult to remove after the gradient coil is replaced.
    With one person holding the male insertion tube to keep it from rolling, two people should obtain the female insertion tube from the shipping crate and thread the two tubes together.
  17. Note: Do not force the threads. Instead, make vertical or horizontal adjustments to the cart or tube support bearing, as necessary, to make the threading easier.
    Push the complete insertion tube assembly into the magnet bore. Get the tube pilot shaft as close to, but not touching, the tube support plate as possible.
  18. Secure the pilot shaft.
    1. Align the support plate mounting bearing with the tube pilot shaft by adjusting the vertical height of the support plate bearing using the stainless steel lever, and/or raising or lowering the cart.
    2. Recheck the centering of the cart left-to-right with respect to the magnet bore. Adjust the cart longitudinal alignment if required.
    3. Push the pilot shaft through the support plate mounting bearing. Insert a nonmagnetic safety pin through the hole in the pilot shaft.
  19. Raise the tube jack and support plate mounting bearing to lift the gradient coil up. Keep the coil approximately level with the magnet bore during the lifting. The coil should be able to rotate freely when completely lifted off the cart.
  20. Rotate the gradient coil, if necessary, until the electrical connection terminals at the gradient coil's service end are oriented upwards.
  21. Slowly push the gradient coil into the magnet bore while watching the clearances to the left, right, top and bottom. Adjust the tube jack and support plate mounting bearing as required to keep the gradient coil from touching the magnet bore surface.
    Note: Be sure to keep hands and loose clothing away from bore as coil is being moved into the magnet bore.
  22. Slowly push the gradient coil until it is close to the tube support plate at magnet service end. Adjust the support plate bearing to gain more clearance and keep the gradient coil from touching the magnet bore surface.
  23. At the magnet patient end, attach the gradient alignment block and the gradient coil radial alignment tool as follows.
    1. Mount the gradient alignment block (UA/3TLC 5694574, W-WB/G3 5694574-2) to the gradient coil patient end at 12 o'clock. Secure using two M10 x 25 SS FHSCS (5389090-3) at ± 15 degree hole in the coil’s flange. This sets the gradient coil Z position.
    2. Mount the gradient coil alignment tool (5266408) to the magnet patient end, using two M10 x 25 mm SS bolts (5389090-3) at ± 15 degree hole in the magnet interface ring.
    1Gradient alignment block (UA/3TLC 5694574, W-WB/G3 5694574-2)
    2Gradient coil alignment tool (5266408)
  24. Carefully turn the gradient coil until the slot in the gradient coil alignment tool (5266408) aligns with the threaded hole in the gradient alignment block (UA/3TLC 5694574, W-WB/G3 5694574-2).
  25. Position the thumbscrew (indicated by the arrow) (part of the gradient rotational alignment tool kit) through both openings in the gradient coil alignment tool and gradient alignment block. Thread the thumbscrew into the rotational alignment block on the gradient coil.
    Note: This sets the axial position of the gradient coil in relation to the magnet.
  26. Make sure the gradient axial and radial alignment have not changed. Install the mat under the coil as follows:
    1. Before lowering the gradient into place, install the gradient isolation mat (UA/3TLC 5473683-3, W-WB/G3 5473683-4).
    2. Carefully position the end of the mat under the patient end of the gradient, and make sure that the mat is flat on the inside surface of the bore. Push in the mat partially under the gradient.
    3. Continue to move the mat through smoothly.
      Be careful not to damage the mat. You may need to push and pull to inch the mat through the bore.
    4. The mat should be even with the magnet edges on the front and back of the magnet, within 10 mm from each end of the bore, and centered axially.
  27. Make sure the gradient axial and radial alignment have not changed.
  28. Position two wedges (UA/3TLC 5566941, W-WB/G3 5771932) as follows:
    Note: Wedge appearance may differ depending on the upgrade type.
    Note: Coating the wedge with a water/soap mixture assists wedge insertion.
    1. As each wedge is installed, insert an M10 x 35 Brass Screw (5326293-5) with a G10 washer (5146169) and apply Loctite #243 (10 ml) (5415261-2) to the engagement portion of the threads.
    2. On the patient end of the magnet, position wedges at locations 1 and 2 in numerical order.
    3. On the service end of the magnet, position wedges at locations 1 and 2 in numerical order.
    4. Make sure the mat does not interfere with the wedges.
  29. Adjust the position of the wedges at locations 1 and 2.
    1. Position each wedge 14 mm from the outside edge of the gradient to inside edge of the wedge.
    2. Tighten the wedges until they are firmly held in place, but do not tighten to the point they are pulled inward causing the spacing to be less than 14 mm.
  30. Make sure there is no gap between the gradient coil and the gradient alignment block.
  31. Remove the radial alignment tool and gradient alignment block. Return them to the gradient insertion kit shipping crate.
  32. Slowly lower the support plate bearing and tube jack to lower the gradient coil onto the mat.
    Note: Make sure the gradient axial and radial alignment have not changed.
  33. Release pressure on the beam, and let the four mounting wedges support the coil.
  34. At the service end, remove the tube support plate.
  35. Install wedges 3 through 8 (UA/3TLC 5566941, W-WB/G3 5771932) in numerical order:
    1. As each wedge is installed, insert an M10 x 35 Brass Screw (5326293-5) with a G10 washer (5146169) and apply Loctite #243 (10 ml) (5415261-2) to the engagement portion of the threads.
    2. On the patient side, install a wedge at locations 3 through 8 in numerical order.
    3. On the service side, install a wedge at locations 3 through 8 in numerical order.
    4. In numerical order, torque the bolts to 10 ft-lb. (13.56 N m) on both the service end and patient end. Do not torque the bolts over 12 ft lb. If a bolt bottoms out before it is tight, use a second G10 washer.
  36. Install the two axial stop assemblies in the third hole from 6 o'clock on the patient and service end of the magnet.
    1. Apply Loctite #243 (10 ml) (5415261-2) to the engagement portion of the M10 x 35 Brass Screw threads and position the axial stops at the locations shown
    2. Tighten the M10 x 35 Brass Screws to 15 ft-lb (20.33 N m).
    3. Tighten the axial stop studs with your fingers until you feel resistance, and then add one full turn with a screwdriver.
    4. Tighten the first M10 brass nut on the coil side of the axial stop bracket with your fingers, and then tighten with a wrench. Tighten the second nut.
    Note: The axial stop (5766129) locations are the same for G3 upgrades, the third hole on each side of 6 o'clock (lowest point).
    1Patient end axial stop placement
    2Service end axial stop placement
    3Axial Block (5766129)
    4G10 washer (5146169)
    5M10 x 35 Brass Screw (5326293-5)
  37. Remove the gradient coil insertion and lift kit from HRMw and magnet.
  38. Remove all parts of the gradient insertion tool from the HRMw gradient coil and return to crate. Properly store all components.
  39. Return the HRMw with cart (originally stored in local stock) as it was shipped.
  40. Return the gradient coil cart (originally shipped with the FRU) following the return process.
  41. Install the empty shim trays into the gradient coil to prevent contamination.
