Removing and installing the magnet enclosure covers

Provides removal and installation instruction for all magnet enclosure covers.

About this task

  • Removing the laser lens
    Removes the lens covering the alignment laser from the front endbell.
  • Installing the laser lens
    Installs the lens covering the alignment laser onto the front endbell.
  • Removing the magnet left- and right-hand lower access panels
    Removes the left- or right-hand lower access panel from the magnet front cover.
  • Installing the magnet left- and right-hand lower access panels
    Installs the left- or right-hand lower access panel onto the magnet front cover.
  • Removing the magnet service access trim
    Removes the service access trim. The trim panel covers the screws securing the front and rear service access panels.
  • Installing the magnet service access trim
    Installs the service access trim. The trim panel covers the screws securing the front and rear service access panels.
  • Removing the magnet rear trim ring
    Removes the rear trim ring from the rear endbell. The trim ring covers the bolts securing the magnet rear enclosures.
  • Installing the magnet rear trim ring
    Installs the rear trim ring onto the rear endbell.
  • Removing the rear bridge cover
    Removes the table rear bridge cover from the magnet enclosure.
  • Installing the rear bridge cover
    Installs the table rear bridge cover onto the magnet enclosure.
  • Removing the bridge cover
    Removes the bridge cover from the front endbell. This task can be performed with the front endbell installed on the magnet or not.
  • Installing the bridge cover
    Installs the front bridge cover onto the front endbell. This task can be performed with the endbell installed on the magnet or not.
  • Removing the front endbell
    Removes the front endbell from the magnet.
  • Installing the front endbell
    Installs the front endbell onto the magnet.
  • Removing the table interface cover
    Removes the table interface cover from the magnet front cover.
  • Installing the table interface cover
    Installs the table interface cover onto the magnet front cover.
  • Removing the magnet left and right control panel covers
    Removes the left or right control panel cover from the magnet front cover.
  • Installing the magnet left and right control panel covers
    Installs the left or right control panel cover onto the magnet front cover.
  • Removing the magnet front cover
    Removes the front cover from the magnet.
  • Installing the magnet front cover
    Installs the front cover onto the magnet.
  • Removing the magnet front service access panel
    Removes the front service access panel from the magnet to allow access to components mounted on the left-hand side of the magnet.
  • Installing the magnet front service access panel
    Installs the front service access panel onto the magnet enclosure. This panel covers the components mounted on the left hand side of the magnet.
  • Removing the magnet rear service access panel
    Removes the rear service access panel from the magnet to allow access to components mounted on the left-hand side of the magnet.
  • Installing the magnet rear service access panel
    Installs the rear service access panel onto the magnet enclosure. This panel covers the components mounted on the left-hand side of the magnet.
  • Removing the magnet upper service access panel
    Removes the upper service access panel allowing access to components mounted on top of the magnet.
  • Installing the magnet upper service access panel
    Installs the upper service access panel. This panel covers components mounted on top of the magnet.
  • Removing the magnet service access vent
    Removes the service access vent from the magnet enclosure allowing a ladder to stand close to the magnet on the left-hand side.
  • Installing the magnet service access vent
    Installs the service access vent onto the magnet enclosure.
  • Removing the rear bridge inner cover
    Removes the rear bridge inner cover from the magnet enclosure.
  • Installing the rear bridge inner cover
    Installs the rear bridge inner cover onto the magnet enclosure.
  • Removing the magnet rear cable cover
    Removes the rear cable cover from the magnet enclosure.
  • Installing the magnet rear cable cover
    Installs the rear cable cover onto the magnet enclosure.
  • Removing the rear endbell
    Removes the rear endbell from the magnet enclosure.
  • Installing the rear endbell
    Installs the rear endbell onto the magnet.
  • Removing the magnet left-hand rear cover
    Removes the rear cover from the left-hand side of the magnet.
  • Installing the magnet left-hand rear cover
    Installs the rear cover onto the left-hand side of the magnet.
  • Removing the magnet right-hand rear cover
    Removes the rear cover from the right-hand side of the magnet.
  • Installing the magnet right-hand rear cover
    Installs the rear cover onto the right-hand side of the magnet.
  • Removing the magnet right-hand top cover
    Removes the cover from the top of the right-hand side of the magnet enclosure.
  • Installing the magnet right-hand top cover
    Installs the cover onto the top of the right-hand side of the magnet enclosure.
  • Removing the magnet right-hand center cover
    Removes the center cover from the right-hand side of the magnet.
  • Installing the magnet right-hand center cover
    Installs the center cover onto the right-hand side of the magnet.
  • Removing the magnet right-hand bottom cover
    Removes the bottom cover from the right-hand side of the magnet.
  • Installing the magnet right-hand bottom cover
    Installs the bottom cover onto the right-hand side of the magnet.
  • Removing the magnet left-hand lower rear cover
    Removes the lower cover from the left-hand rear corner of the magnet.
  • Installing the magnet left-hand lower rear cover
    Installs the lower cover onto the left-hand rear corner of the magnet.
  • Removing the magnet left-hand upper rear cover
    Removes the upper cover from the left-hand rear corner of the magnet.
  • Installing the magnet left-hand upper rear cover
    Installs the upper cover onto the left-hand rear corner of the magnet.
  • Removing the magnet left-hand upper front cover
    Removes the upper cover from the front left-hand corner of the magnet.
  • Installing the magnet left-hand upper front cover
    Installs the upper cover onto the front left-hand corner of the magnet.
  • Removing the magnet left-hand lower front cover
    Removes the lower cover from the left-hand front corner of the magnet.
  • Installing the magnet left-hand lower front cover
    Installs the lower cover onto the left-hand front corner of the magnet.
