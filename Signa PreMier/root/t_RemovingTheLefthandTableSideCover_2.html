Removing the left-hand table side cover

Removes the left-hand cover from the dockable table.

Prerequisites

Personnel requirements
Required personsPreliminary requirementsProcedureFinalization
1-5 minutes-
Tools and test equipment
ItemQuantityPart numberManufacturer
Nonmagnetic Titanium Service Tool Kit, Small Set15113258-
Required conditions
The right-hand table side cover is removed.

Procedure

  1. Remove the three screws securing the side cover to the table.
  2. Remove the three screws securing the side cover to the table.
  3. Remove the left-hand table side cover from the table.
    Note: The acrylic tube is supported by the left- and right-hand table side covers. Make sure the tube is still supported and does not drop when the cover is removed.
