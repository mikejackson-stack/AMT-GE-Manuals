Troubleshooting the E-Stop/Gradient contactor

This document describes troubleshooting steps if the E-Stop/Gradient contactor is de-energized or not functioning properly.

Procedure

  1. Make sure all the E-Stop buttons are disengaged and press EMO Reset.
    • If the equipment is operating correctly, the E-Stop/Gradient contactor will be energized and all connected subsystems will power on.
    • If the E-Stop/Gradient contactor does not become energized, continue on to Step 2.
  2. Observe the Cabinet Monitor 3 assembly E-Stop/Gradient CNT LED.
    • If the E-Stop/Gradient CNT LED is illuminated, the CabMon3 is ready to supply 120 VAC. Continue on to Step 4.
    • If the E-Stop/Gradient CNT LED is not illuminated, continue on to Step 3.
  3. Make sure the leak sensors are connected and operating correctly.
    • If the leak sensors are unconnected or failing, the E-Stop/Gradient contactor will not energize. See Troubleshooting leak detection sensors to resolve.
    • If the leak sensors are working properly and the E-Stop/Gradient CNT LED is illuminated, continue on to Step 4.
  4. Make sure 120 VAC is present at the System PDU connector J1 using a DMM set to AC voltage.
    1. Place the positive probe in connector J1 pin 7 (E-Stop/Gradient Contactor Coil).
    2. Place the negative probe in connector J1 pin 4 (E-Stop/Gradient Contactor Neutral).
    • If 120 VAC is measured, the problem is within the System PDU or Gradient PDU. For more information, see the System PDU Owners Manual (DOC2078322 ) or Gradient PDU Owners Manual (DOC2078325) available on MyWorkshop (MWS).
    • If 120 VAC is not measured, continue on to Step 5.
  5. Reconfigure PDU connections.
    1. Disconnect the System PDU connector J1.
    2. Connect the 120 VAC Contactor Cable (5774880) to the System PDU connector J1 that was just removed.
    3. Insert the power plug into one of the service outlets.
    • If the E-Stop/Gradient contactors energize and the subsystems connected power on proceed to Step 3.
    • If the E-Stop/Gradient contactors do not energize and the subsystems connected remain powered off, the problem is within the System PDU or Gradient PDU. For more information, see the System PDU Owners Manual (DOC2078322 ) or Gradient PDU Owners Manual (DOC2078325) available on MyWorkshop (MWS).
  6. Power down the CabMon3 and remove connector J2. Use a DMM to check resistance in the contactor power cable (5749299) to verify continuity.
    • If the contactor power cable is verified to be good, replace the CabMon3 (5724164 or 5789922).
    • If the contactor power cable is bad, replace the cable and repeat Step 1 through Step 6.

Finalization

After the issue is resolved and all assemblies are properly connected, test the E-Stop circuit by pressing one of the E-Stop buttons, and then recover by pressing the EMO Reset button. This verifies the E-Stop circuit is functioning properly.
