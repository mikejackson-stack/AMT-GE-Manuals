Removing the GOC hard drive - Dell T5820

Remove the solid state drive (SSD) from the host computer.

Prerequisites

Personnel requirements

1-15 minutes-

Required conditions

Use an antistatic wrist strap and mat while doing this procedure to prevent electrostatic discharge (ESD).

Access to both the front and rear of the operator console is required. Move the console away from walls and remove obstacles in the room.

About this task

The Dell T5820 has two SSDs and they have the same removal procedure.

Procedure

  1. Make sure that you have read and understood the electrical safety requirements before starting this procedure. See Electrical Safety - Global Operator Cabinet (GOC).
  2. Prepare the workstation for replacement. See Preparing the workstation for a computer replacement.
  3. Shut down and power off the host computer. See Shutting down the host computer.
  4. Apply GOC LOTO. See Applying LOTO - Global Operator Cabinet (GOC) and Operator Workspace.
  5. If installed, disconnect the optional uninterruptible power supply (UPS).
  6. For added protection, disconnect the twist-n-lock main power cable from the rear of the console.
  7. Remove the two screws that secure the left side panel of the GOC.
    Note The side panel has a short ground lead connecting it to the main chassis. When removing the side panel, do not strain this ground lead.
  8. Disconnect the short ground lead that connects the side panel to the GOC main chassis at the center of the lead.
  9. Remove the left side panel and put it in a safe place.
  10. Remove the side cover.
    1. Press the latch.
    2. Pull the latch upward and rotate it to release the cover.
  11. Remove the HDD bezel.
    1. Press the blue unlock button on the edge of ODD bay.
    2. Slide the latch to the unlock position, on the front I/O bezel.
    3. Rotate forward and lift the HDD bezel away from the system.
  12. Remove the HDD bracket.
    1. Press the release button to unlock the latch.
    2. Pull the latch to slide the bracket out of the HDD slot.
  13. Remove the HDD.
    1. Expand the bracket on both sides.
    2. Lift the hard drive out of the bracket.
  14. Do a disk wipe on all applicable hard drives in the old PC. See the latest revision of the Disk Management Tool Service Manual (5500610-1EN).
