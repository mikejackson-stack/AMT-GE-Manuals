Setting up for B0 Drift

Setting up for B0 Drift for calibration or functional check.

Procedure

  1. Put the body TLT phantom and loader in the foam support on the table. To achieve the best test measurement, the phantom must be located in the center (long direction) of the cradle.
  2. Move the table in until it is 1080 mm from the home position. Adjust the phantom position and landmark on the crosshairs of the body TL loader at 1080 mm from home position.
    Note This image is for reference only and may not be specific to your product.
  3. Press Advance to Scan.
