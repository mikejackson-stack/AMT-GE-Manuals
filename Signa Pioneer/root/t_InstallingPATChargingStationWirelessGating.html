Installing the Physiological Acquisition Transceiver (PAT) charging station

Install the Physiological Acquisition Transceiver (PAT) charging station.

About this task

The charging cradle supports the docking and charging of the PAT. It is intended for installing outside the magnet room, typically in the control room. The charging cradle can be put on a clean, level tabletop or mounted on the wall.
Note
  • Make sure of the mounting method and location of the charging station with the customer before installing.
  • Some sites may require their own facilities or contractor to drill and/or install the wall-mounted charging station.
  • The charging station must be put or mounted within 3000 mm (118 inches) of an electrical outlet.
  • The charging cradle has an IEC 60320 C8 receptacle for AC power. Starting in August 2025, charging stations are supplied with four AC power cords. If none of the supplied power cords are compatible with the on-site outlets, a site-compatible power cord may be required. The site-compatible power cord must be sourced locally if the existing power cord from the removed charging station is damaged and unusable.
