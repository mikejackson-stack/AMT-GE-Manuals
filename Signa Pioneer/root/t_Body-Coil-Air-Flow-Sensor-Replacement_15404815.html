About this task

This procedure describes the replacement of the body coil air flow sensor.

Procedure

  1. Perform LOTO for ISC. Refer to Loto for ISC.
  2. Remove Center Top Cover. Refer to Center Top Cover Removal.
  3. Remove Front Enddbell. Refer to Front Endbell Removal.
  4. Disconnect the RF body coil air flow sensor cable from SRI J24 and remove air flow sensor housing from Magnet side panel.
  5. Remove the air flow sensors with housing block on the body coil.
    Note Be careful not to touch the exposed sensor elements.
  6. Restore the air flow sensor assy in reverse order.
    Note Air flow sensor should be installed so that the sensor cable side faces the outside.
  7. Restore the center top cover and front end bell in reverse order.

Finalization

  1. Restore LOTO of ISC. Refer to Removing LOTO - Integrated System Cabinet (ISC).
  2. Ensure that sensors are operating correctly. See Checking the body coil air flow.
