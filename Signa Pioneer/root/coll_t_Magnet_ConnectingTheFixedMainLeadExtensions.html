coll_t_ConnectingTheFixedMainLeadExtensions

Connect the fixed main lead extensions to the main power supply, and insert them into the ramp ports on your series magnet.

Required conditions
The pre-ramp grounding circuit must be complete, and the main lead extensions grounded, before you insert or extract any main lead extensions from the magnet.

Procedure

  1. Connect the other end of the main power cables to the main lead extensions and secure them with the 25.4 mm (1 inch) brass bolts, washers, and nuts, as shown below.
  2. Make sure the connections are sufficiently tightened.
  3. Make sure that the gas flow holes in the main lead extension are not blocked.
  4. Remove the positive (+) ramp port cap located on the magnet's vertical stack. Make sure the O-ring inside the cap does not get lost.

  6. Do not excessively rotate the main lead extension after it is in the engaged position.
  7. Make sure that helium gas is exiting the gas flow holes in the main lead extension.
  8. Hand-tighten the compression nut onto the ramp lead port until no gas leaks except from the vent hole.
  9. While pressing down on the top of the lead, rotate it back and forth (about 15° in each direction from the initial position), raise it 0.5 inches up, re-engage it, and rotate again.
  10. Connect one end of the voltage sense lead to the positive main lead extension's voltage sense screw lug, directing the screw toward the edge of the service turret.

    Connect the other end of the voltage sense lead to a digital voltmeter (DVM) placed near the main coil power supply.

  11. Repeat Step 4 through Step 10 with the negative main lead extension and negative (-) ramp port.
  12. Make sure the current polarities are correct.
