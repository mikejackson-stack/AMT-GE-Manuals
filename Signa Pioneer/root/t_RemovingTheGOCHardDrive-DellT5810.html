Removing the GOC hard drive - Dell T5810

Remove the solid state drive (SSD) from the host computer.

Prerequisites

Required conditions

Use an antistatic wrist strap and mat while doing this procedure to prevent electrostatic discharge (ESD).

Access to both the front and rear of the operator console is required. Move the console away from walls and remove obstacles in the room.

About this task

The Dell T5810 has two SSDs and they have the same removal procedure.

Procedure

  1. Make sure that you have read and understood the electrical safety requirements before starting this procedure. See Electrical Safety - Global Operator Cabinet (GOC).
  2. Prepare the workstation for replacement. See Preparing the workstation for a computer replacement.
  3. Shut down and power off the host computer. See Shutting down the host computer.
  4. Apply GOC LOTO. See Applying LOTO - Global Operator Cabinet (GOC) and Operator Workspace.
  5. Remove the host computer. See Removing the host computer - Dell T5810 and HPZ420.
  6. Lift up the computer cover release latch, lift the cover upward to a 45-degree angle, and remove the cover from the host computer.
  7. Disconnect the power supply and data cables from the SSD.
  8. Press in gently on both sides of the bracket and slide the SSD carrier out of the slot.
  9. Remove the bracket from the SSD carrier.
  10. Press on the two tabs on one side of the SSD carrier to separate the pins from the SSD and remove the SSD from the carrier.
    Note You can twist the carrier to separate the pins more easily.
    Even though the pin is long and the tab is hard, it is possible to detach the pin from the SSD. The illustration below shows how to remove the pin.
  11. Do a disk wipe on all applicable hard drives in the old PC. See the latest revision of the Disk Management Tool Service Manual (5500610-1EN). This manual is available in the online documentation library.
