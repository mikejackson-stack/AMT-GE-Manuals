Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

About this task

This procedure replaces cable runs M3344 and M3394 and removes both bore light Bs. This procedure should only be performed when a bore light B is non-functional and the bore light B FRU is not available.

Procedure

  1. Position the cradle at the home position.
  2. Remove the front trim rings.
  3. Remove the dock interface cover:
    1. Remove the two screws and two washers securing the dock interface cover to the magnet.
    2. Remove the cover.
  4. Lower the table.
  5. Perform LOTO for ISC
  6. Remove the rear trim rings.
  7. Remove the rear cable cover:
    1. Remove the two screws securing the cover.
    2. Raise the rear cable cover to unhook and remove it.
  8. Remove the rear duct bar:
    1. Remove the four screws securing the duct bar.
    2. Remove the duct bar.
  9. Remove the rear center bridge cover:
    1. Remove two screws.
    2. Raise the cover to unhook and remove it.
  10. Remove the rear bridge bottom cover:
    1. Remove the two screws securing the cover.
    2. Raise the cover to unhook and remove it.
  11. Remove the left-hand rear bridge side cover:
    1. Disconnect cable run M3350 from the ambient temperature sensor connector at the bracket of the cover.
    2. Remove the three screws and three washers securing the cover.
    3. Remove the cover.
  12. Remove the right-hand rear bridge side cover:
    1. Remove the three screws and three washers securing the cover.
    2. Remove the cover.
  13. Remove the rear bridge:
    1. Disconnect cable 3507 from the end of travel switch.
    2. Disconnect the cable from the UTNS antenna.
    3. Remove the two plastic covers, two screws, and two washers securing the rear bridge to the magnet.
    4. Remove the four screws and four washers securing the rear bridge to the front bridge.
    5. Remove the rear bridge.
  14. Remove the rear endbell:
    1. Disconnect the two RF transmit cables.
    2. Disconnect the cooling air hose from the rear endbell.
    3. Disconnect the two body coil air hoses.
    4. Disconnect the speaker cable.
    5. Remove the five screws and five washers securing the rear endbell to the magnet.
    6. Remove the rear endbell from the magnet.
  15. Remove the front bridge:
    1. Remove the screws securing the bridge. (four in the front, two in the rear).
    2. Remove the front bridge.
  16. Remove the left-hand lower side cover assembly:
    1. Remove the nameplate cover and remove the two screws.
    2. Attach the side cover dolly to the lower side cover assembly by turning two knobs.
    3. Disconnect the connectors for the light.
    4. Pull to unlatch the hook and remove the lower side cover assembly.
  17. Remove the left-hand side access cover:
    1. Remove the two M16-1 x 12 mm philips pan-head screws.
    2. Unhook and remove the access cover.
  18. Remove the left-hand center top cover:
    1. Remove two screws.
    2. Unhook and remove the cover.
  19. Remove the left-hand side upper front cover:
    1. Remove two screws.
    2. Unhook and pull away the cover.
    3. Disconnect the illumination light switch cable.
    4. Remove the cover.
  20. Remove the left-hand side upper rear cover:
    1. Remove three screws.
    2. Unhook and remove the cover.
  21. Remove the left-hand rear cover:
    1. Remove the three screws and three washers securing the cover.
    2. Unlatch the three latches securing the cover.
    3. Remove the cover.
  22. Remove the left-hand front cover:
    1. Remove the four screws and four washers securing the cover to the magnet.
    2. Unlatch the latch.
    3. Remove the cover.
  23. Disconnect existing cable run M3394 (5549344) from both bore light Bs and both front bore light As.
  24. Disconnect M3394 P1 from cable run M3344 P3.
  25. Cut tie wraps as required and remove existing cable run M3394.
  26. Disconnect the following connectors of existing cable run M3344 (5476791):
    • P1 from SRI J22
    • P2 from M3393 P1
    • P4 from gradient coil HART ID
    • P5 from the Heat EXchanger (HEX) temperature sensor
  27. Cut tie wraps as required and remove existing cable run M3344.
  28. Remove one screw from each bore light B and remove them both.
  29. Reinstall the two bore light B screws into the body coil.
    Note The light pipes will remain installed.
  30. Connect the new cable run M3344 (5788763) as follows:
    • P1 to SRI J22
    • P2 to M3393 P1
    Note Gradient HART ID will not be connected.
  31. Route the M3344 heat exchanger temperature sensor cable towards the rear end of the magnet and secure it with tie wraps.
    Note If the temperature sensor on the heat exchanger has two pins, a new temperature sensor with four pin holes (5561373-3) will need to be ordered.
  32. Connect M3344 P5 to the HEX temperature sensor
  33. Connect the new cable run M3394 (5549343-2) P1 to run M3344 P3.
  34. Install the run M3394 (5549343-2) and secure both runs M3344 and M3394 with tie wraps.
  35. Connect run M3394 to both front bore light As.
  36. Restore the system by reverse order of removal.

Finalization

  1. Restore power. Refer to Removing LOTO - Integrated System Cabinet (ISC).
  2. Make sure the four bore light As are illuminated.
  3. Perform Doing a check scan.
