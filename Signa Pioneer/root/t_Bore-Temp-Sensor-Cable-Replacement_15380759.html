About this task

Overview

This procedure describes the removal and installation of the Bore Temp Sensor Cable (RUN M3350) cable which is coming from SRI J10. Since the cable is tie-wrapped on many places on the Magnet, it is necessary to remove the covers described in the procedure first to access and remove the cable assy.

Procedure

  1. Move the cradle at Home Position.
  2. Remove Dock Interface Cover.
    1. Remove front lower trim ring.
    2. Remove two screws.
    3. Unhook two points at bottom.
  3. Move the Patient Table at down position
  4. Follow Lockout/Tagout procedures per LOTO for ISC.
  5. Remove rear trim rings.
  6. Remove rear trim rings by removing poppers.
    1. Remove two screws.
    2. Bring up rear cable cover to unhook and remove it.
  7. Remove duct bar by removing four screws.
  8. Remove Lower Side Cover Assembly (Left).
    1. Remove Nameplate Cover and remove two screws.
    2. b.Fix Side Cover Dolly to the Lower Side Cover Assembly with turning two knobs.
    3. Disconnect connectors for light.
    4. Pull to unlatch the hook and move Lower Side Cover Assembly.
  9. Do the same procedure in Lower Side Cover Assembly (Left) for Lower Side Cover Assembly (Right).
  10. Remove Access Cover LH Side.
    1. Remove 2 screws.
    2. Unhook at 2 points.
  11. Remove Center Top Cover LH.
    1. Remove 2 screws.
    2. Unhook 4 points.
  12. Remove Side Cover LH Upper Front.
    1. Remove 2 screw.
    2. Unhook 3 points.
    3. Disconnect illumination light switch cable.
  13. Remove Side Cover LH Upper Rear.
    1. Remove 3 screws.
    2. Unhook 3 points.
  14. Remove Side Cover RH Upper Front.
    1. Remove 1 screw.
    2. Unhook 6 points.
  15. Remove right and left OCP Panels.
    1. Remove Trim Ring.
    2. Remove OCP Panel by removing three screws and 5 poppers.
    3. Disconnect data cable.
    Note Remove left and right OCP panels even though the illustration below only shows the removal of left OCP panel.
  16. Remove all fixing rubber stoppers and screws.
    1. Remove three rubber stoppers.
    2. Remove 6 screws.
  17. Access to the connector and disconnect it.
  18. Remove Front Upper Cover.
  19. Remove Front LH Lower Cover per following steps.
    1. Remove three rubber stoppers.
    2. Remove three screws and washers.
  20. Remove Rear Center Bridge Cover.
    1. Remove 2 screws.
    2. Bring up to unhook and remove Rear Center Bridge Cover.
  21. Remove Rear Center Bridge Cover.
    1. Remove 2 screws.
    2. Bring up to unhook and remove Rear Center Bridge Cover.
  22. Disconnect M3350 cable from the connector at the bracket of right Bridge Side Cover.
  23. Remove right Rear Bridge Side Covers by removing three screws.
  24. Remove Front LH Lower Cover per following steps.
    1. Remove three rubber stoppers.
    2. Remove two screws and washers.
  25. Remove alignment light cover by removing two screws and two poppers.
  26. Remove 8 screws and remove front end bell.
  27. Remove the Bore Temperature Sensor and cut tie wraps which are fixing the cable on Magnet front.
  28. Disconnect the RUN M3350 cable from SRI J10 and cut tie wraps which are fixing the cable on Magnet side and rear.
  29. Connect the new FRU cable and route the cable as it was.
  30. Installation is reverse order of removal.

Finalization

  1. Remove LOTO by referring to Removing LOTO - Integrated System Cabinet (ISC).
  2. Perform EPI White Pixel PM Mode.
  3. Perform check scan.
