About this task

Overview

This procedure requires disposal of coolant. Inform the customer that coolant disposal is needed and then follow the proper customer coolant disposal procedure.

XRMw coil water removal

Procedure

  1. Note Wear nitrile gloves when performing coolant removal.
    Remove the patient bridge. See .
  2. Remove the magnet rear endbell. See .
  3. Turn off the pumps at the Heat Exchange Cabinet (HEC) by pressing the appropriate key sequence for the blowers, gradient coil, and power electronics drives. See HEC control panel key sequence.
    Note Do not remove power from the VFDs (Variable Frequency Drive) without first powering down using the key sequences shown in HEC control panel key sequence. Failure to follow this power-down process may result in premature VFD failure.
    Table 2. HEC control panel key sequence
    Part Key Sequence
    Blowers ▲ F1
    Gradient Coil (GC) ▲ F2
    Power Electronics (PE) ▲ F3

    Press and hold ▲ while selecting the F-number key.

  4. Perform LOTO on the HEC. See .
  5. Perform LOTO on the PGR PDU/gradient subsystem.
  6. Turn off both PVC valves in the coolant supply and return lines at the service end of the XRMw coil.
  7. Place an empty five gallon bucket close to the magnet service end to hold discharge coolant.
  8. Carefully disconnect the manifold supply and return lines (one at a time) from the barbed connections below the closed valves. Direct one line (either the supply or return line) toward the bucket and empty as much coolant as possible into the bucket.
  9. Obtain a one inch barbed fitting from the Coolant Removal Kit and attach it to the other line. Connect the pump-to-manifold adapter to the other line through the barbed fitting.
  10. Attach the manual pump from the Coolant Removal Kit to the other end of the adapter (if not attached already).
  11. Start pumping air while watching the coolant discharge.
  12. Continue pumping until no more coolant comes out. This takes about 10 to 15 minutes.

Old manifold removal

Procedure

  1. With the magnet rear endbell and the rear pedestal removed, the XRMw manifolds are accessible.
  2. Ensure that both PVC valves in the coolant supply and return lines at the XRMw service end are turned off, and coolant has been purged from the XRMw coil.
  3. Remove the eight black anchor Velcro straps securing the manifolds to the XRMw coil.
  4. Notice
    Some manifold-to-coil connections have temperature sensors attached. Watch closely when cutting heat shrinks to avoid damaging the temperature sensors or wires.
    Using a non-magnetic side cutter, carefully cut open the heat shrinks at the manifold-to-inner coil connections, making sure not to cut the temperature sensor and wire when removing the heat shrink.
  5. After the heat shrink is removed, the stainless steel hose clamp is exposed. If it is a crimped clamp, it cannot be opened with a screwdriver. Use a combination of non-magnetic screwdriver and needle-nose pliers to push the clamp away from the coil and slide it towards the flexible hose
  6. Notice
    Do not apply force to the copper tube terminal. Bending the tube will cause severe damage and will greatly shorten the life of the XRMw coil.
    After the hose clamp is open or pushed off the copper tube terminal, disconnect the manifold hose from the tube terminal.
  7. Repeat Step 4 through Step 6 to disconnect all manifold hoses from XRMw inner coil.
  8. Disconnect the manifold tubes from the copper fittings.

New manifold installation

Procedure

  1. Use the Temperature Sensor Protector (5352243, included in FRU kit) to ensure the manifold will not damage the external temperature sensors when pushing the manifold extension hoses onto the copper terminals.
  2. Assemble the XRMw Manifold Coolant Return (5339472) on the coil. Corresponding hoses and coil locations are shown below.

    Insert a stainless steel hose clamp (46-252065P155) on each manifold leg before pushing the hoses into position, and make sure that all hose clamps are in a similar orientation.

  3. Make sure the manifold return assembly is in the correct position and sitting on the lower position of the manifold blocks.
  4. Assemble the XRMw Manifold Coolant Supply (5339471) on the coil. Corresponding hoses and coil locations are shown below.
  5. Make sure the XRMw manifold coolant supply is in the correct position and sitting on the upper position of the manifold blocks.
  6. Tighten all hose clamps. Use a nonferrous screwdriver to make sure all connections are tight.
  7. Tighten the Velcro straps.
  8. Apply rubber splicing tape (46-294167P91, provided with the manifold replacement kit) to the clamps and any exposed copper tube terminals.
  9. Connect the supply and return manifold to the barbed fitting of the main coolant lines on the magnet.
  10. Secure the connection with stainless steel hose clamps.
  11. Open the main coolant valve.

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Remove LOTO from the PGR PDU/gradient subsystem. See .
  2. See HEC Coolant Fill and Coolant Leak Check to add coolant to the heat exchanger reservoir, turn on the heat exchanger pumps, and check the coolant system for leaks. The volume added should be close to that of the discharged coolant.
  3. Restore the magnet rear endbell. See .
  4. See to restore the rear pedestal by attaching the rear pedestal to the magnet with the two bolts.
  5. Do a check scan to ensure the system is functioning correctly.
