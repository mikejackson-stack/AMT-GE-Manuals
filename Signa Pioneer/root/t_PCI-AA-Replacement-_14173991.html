Table 4. Safety
DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!
TO PREVENT POSSIBLE FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK, Shutdown the Host computer and perform Lockout/Tagout of the GOC BEFORE STARTING THIS PROCEDURE.
Notice
Potential component damage
Electrostatic discharge (ESD) can cause permanent damage to electronic components.
Observe the following ESD precautions:
  • Work on a static-free mat.
  • Wear a static strap to make sure that any accumulated electrostatic charge is discharged from your body to the ground.
  • Create a common ground for the equipment being worked on by connecting the static-free mat, static strap, and peripheral units to that piece of equipment.
  • For more information on ESD protection, see MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
Notice
For further information on ESD protection, see the MR Service Safety Manual, PN 5452735.

Procedure

  1. Remove the left side panel and set it is a safe place.
    1. Remove the two screws that secure the left side panel of the GOC.
    2. Lift up and remove the left side panel. (Be careful not to strain the ground cable) .
    3. Disconnect ground cable.
    Note The side panel has a short ground lead connecting it to the main chassis. When removing the side panel, do not strain this ground lead.
  2. Loosen the strap and remove the hook.
    1. Press release lever of buckle and loosen strap.
    2. Remove the hook.
  3. Remove a fixing screw of PC.
  4. Remove the access panel (left side) of the PC by pulling the latch out and sliding the cover back.
  5. Remove the cables from PCIAA Board and PCIAA Extended Board at PC rear panel.
  6. Remove Power connector from PCIAA Board.
  7. Remove the PCIAA Board and PCIAA Extended Board, and take away them.
  8. Install the PCIAA collector by the reverse order of removal.
  9. Restore PC and GOC by reverse order of removal.

Finalization

  1. Turn the system power on. Verify system boot up without any problem.
  2. Perform Emergency Stop and Off Check
  3. Perform Checking and adjusting the patient intercom functionality.
    Note GOC PCIAA is pre-adjusted at vendor. So, basically, intercom adjustment of GOC PCIAA is not required. If necessary, perform Intercom Adjustment as troubleshooting
  4. Perform check scan.
