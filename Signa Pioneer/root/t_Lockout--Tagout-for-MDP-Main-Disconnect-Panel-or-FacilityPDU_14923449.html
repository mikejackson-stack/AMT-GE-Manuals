Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

About this task

The power to the entire system can be removed by locking out/tagging out (LOTO) the Main Disconnect Panel (MDP) or Facility PDU. Prior to performing work on the PDU in System Cabinet, the steps in this Lock Out Tag Out Procedure must be performed by a LOTO Authorized GE Field Engineer. Completing all steps in this procedure will ensure a safe environment when working on these parts.

Power Down the computer subsystems

Procedure

  1. To prepare for shutdown of equipment, notify affected personnel working in the area the LOTO is being performed.
  2. From Common Service Desktop, click Utility > VRE > Power Control and select [POWER OFF VRE] to turn off the VRE.
  3. To shut down the system, click the arrow on the Tools High Level Access tab, select System Shutdown, and then click [Yes] to confirm the shut down.
  4. Confirm that the system is shut down.
Topic ID: id_SL28617-1083180

System Cabinet PDU Main Breaker OFF

Procedure

  1. At the front panel of the PDU, press the Power Off button.
  2. Rotate the larger main breaker labeled INPUT counter-clockwise to the green, OFF (O) position.
Topic ID: id_SL2889736-1083180

Lockout / Tagout for MDP (Main Disconnect Panel) or Facility PDU

Procedure

  1. Turn OFF the Main Breaker of MDP or Facility PDU.
  2. Lock Out/Tag Out the PDU breaker on the MDP or Facility PDU.
  3. Wait five minutes for dissipation of stored energy.
  4. Go to System Cabinet and discharge the stored energy by rotating the larger main breaker labeled INPUT counter-clockwise to the red, ON (I) position.
  5. At System Cabinet, check the AC Voltage between φA-B, φB-C, and φC-A (Primary) and verify that the voltage is under 1VAC.
  6. At System Cabinet, rotate the larger main breaker labeled INPUT counter-clockwise to the green, OFF (O) position.
Topic ID: id_SL28620-1083180

System Cabinet PDU - Restoring Power

Procedure

  1. Prepare for re-energizing the equipment. Notify affected personnel working in the area that LOTO will be removed.
  2. Remove personal LOTO device(s) from the Service Disconnect Panel.
  3. Remove the Lock and the Tag from the MDP or Facility PDU.
  4. Turn ON the Main Breaker of MDP or Facility PDU.
  5. Rotate the larger main breaker labeled INPUT clockwise to the Red, 1 ONposition.
  6. Boot Signa and log into scanning level

Finalization

  1. Insure all subsystems have powered up successfully.
  2. Do a test phantom scan to insure proper scanning.
