About this task

Overview

Use this procedure to replace the body coil mount and base plate (front).

Topic ID: id_SL15183319-1330661

Body Coil Mount Front Assembly Removal

Procedure

  1. Confirm that patient table is at the highest position.
  2. Remove the dock interface cover.
    1. Remove front lower trim ring.
    2. Remove two screws.
    3. Unhook two points at bottom.
  3. Move the patient table to the lowest position.
  4. Perform LOTO for ISC. Refer to Applying LOTO for ISC.
  5. Remove the front endbell. Refer to Front Endbell Removal.
  6. Remove the rear endbell. Refer to Rear Endbell Removal.
  7. Disconnect the two (2) cooling air ducts at the rear end of the body coil.
  8. Disconnect the HART ID cable at the rear end of the body coil.
  9. Disconnect the bore light cable connectors. (Front End: 4 connectors, Rear End: 2 cables).
  10. At rear end of the magnet, remove four M6 bolts and washers securing the RF body coil.
  11. On the front end of the magnet, remove two M6 bolts and washers from the bases of RF coil centering assembly..
  12. Remove the air flow sensors and tape them to the magnet enclosure in a position that will not interfere with the body coil removal.
  13. From the front of the magnet, pull the body coil forward until the front end is outside the bore.
    CAUTION
    Risk of injury!
    Space between VRMw and Body Coil is very narrow, creating a pinch point.
    Grasp the Body Coil at the proper grab points (handles) when positioning it in the bore.
  14. Remove the four M6 screws and large glass laminate washers. Remove the body coil mount front assembly.

Front Base Plate Replacement

Procedure

  1. Notice
    If the Front Base Plate is not damaged or body coil mount is not replaced from old type to new type, skip this section and go to Restoration for restoration.
    Confirm the front base plate is not damaged. If the front base plate is not damaged or body coil mount is not replaced from old type to new type, skip this section and go to Restoration.
  2. CAUTION
    Risk of injury!
    Space between VRMw and Body Coil is very narrow, creating a pinch point.
    Grasp the Body Coil at the proper grab points (handles) when positioning it in the bore.
    Move the body coil location back to create enough clearance to access the base plate.
    Note The following picture is an example of how to access the front base plate.
  3. Remove the lower mounting block by removing two screws and washers on the magnet front end.
  4. Mark the alignment lines for the base plate on the magnet.
  5. Remove the original base plate.
  6. On the new base plate, adjust the height of screw to make the height approximately equal to the original base plate. Then, tighten the lock nuts.
    Note This adjustment is the rough adjustment for centering the body coil in the magnet bore. Fine adjustment will be done in body coil isolation procedure later. There is no specification in this step.

  7. Align and install the new front base plate on the Magnet with four screws (1000-M10C45-33) and washers (2000-M10C-10).
  8. Attach the level block with two screws (1000-M10C35-33).
  9. Place the non-magnetic level on the level block and confirm that the level bubble is centered between the lines. If not, loosen the four screws and level the base plate position.
  10. Remove the level block and restore lower mounting block with two screws and washers.
  11. CAUTION
    Risk of injury!
    Space between VRMw and Body Coil is very narrow, creating a pinch point.
    Grasp the Body Coil at the proper grab points (handles) when positioning it in the bore.
    Move out the body coil slightly.

Restoration

Procedure

  1. Restore the new body coil mount front assembly.
    Note If the body coil was not replaced, reuse the original screw and washer.

    If the body coil is replaced, use the large glass laminate washers (5988407) and M6x30 screws (1000-M6C030-33) included in the body coil FRU.

  2. CAUTION
    Pinch Fingers!
    Space between VRMw and Body Coil is very narrow.
    Wear a pare of gloves and bring the Body Coil at the proper grab points.
    Restore the body coil to the original location.
  3. Restore the RF body coil:

    • On front end of the magnet, secure the body coil mount assembly and the base with two M6 bolts and washers.

    • On the rear end of the magnet, secure the RF body coil with four M6 bolts and washers.

    Note If body coil is not replaced, reuse the same washers and screws which were originally used for fixing the rear of the body coil.

    If body coil is replaced, use the large glass laminate washers (5988407) and M6x30 screws (1000-M6C030-33) which are included in the body coil FRU.

  4. Perform RF body coil alignment. Refer to Body Coil Alignment.
  5. Reconnect the bore light cable connectors. (Front End: 4 connectors, Rear End: 2 cables)
  6. Reconnect the Hart ID cable.
  7. Restore the air flow sensors.
  8. Restore the two (2) cooling air ducts at the rear end of the body coil.
  9. Reconnect two RF transmit cables cable to RF body coil.
  10. Restore the rear end bell and the rest of the rear covers.
  11. Restore the front end bell and bridge.

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Remove LOTO. Refer to Removing LOTO - Integrated System Cabinet (ISC).
  2. Move the table up and restore the dock interface cover.
  3. Perform Dual Drive Quadrature Calibration. Refer to Dual drive quadrature tool.
    Note

    For MR31.0 and later, Dual drive quadrature tool has been retired, please perform Calibrating for quadrature.
