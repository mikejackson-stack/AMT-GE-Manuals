Cryocooler (F-50/ F-50SH) setup

Shield/cryocooler installation

Procedure

  1. Open the Integrated Cooling Cabinet (ICC) cover and remove it.
  2. Remove the bottom reinforcement bar by removing 6 screws.
  3. Install the He line adaptors (long and short) and handle.
    1. Install the He line adaptor (long).
    2. Install the He line adaptor (short).
    3. Install the handle with 3 screws.
  4. Connect the E0011 and M0011 cables.
  5. Slide the F-50/ F-50SH shield/cryocooler into the lower opening of ICC.
  6. Do the following steps:
    1. Insert copper mesh in wave guide.
      Note (For remote (off-the-wall) siting configuration) insert the copper mesh into the penetration panel waveguide.
    2. Install elbow pipes.

  2. Note Make sure to route the hoses to avoid the hoist setup locations, which will be used for FRU replacement.
    Route the facility water hose on the tray and connect to the hose joint on the ICC.
  3. Install the Water Flow Control Adapter (orifice) (5335002) according to the following steps:
    Note The Water Flow Control Adapter (orifice) is shipped with the F-50 / F-50SH.
    1. Wrap the male end of the orifice fitting with Teflon tape to prevent leaks.
    2. Insert the water flow adaptor and thread the male end into the coolant inlet port of the compressor. Tighten as appropriate to prevent leaks.
    3. Connect hoses according to Step step 4.
  4. Connect the hoses per the following steps:
    Note Use the gray (5264039) and yellow (5264063) hoses only. DO NOT use the white hose supplied with flow (2333825).
    1. Connect two elbow hose nipples to the F50 water supply and return ports.
      Note Cut the gray hose to make two pieces for supply lines. One is for the line between the FPU and the flow/temp sensor. The other is for the line between the flow/temp sensor and the F-50 / F-50SH.
    2. Connect the water flow/temp sensor in between gray hoses with hose bands.
      Note Position the flow meter vertically as in 1/2-inch water hose connection. Do not lay the meter flat on the chassis, which will result in excessive impeller wear and degrade the performance of the unit over time.
      Note The mounting orientation must be with the axis of rotation (shaft) parallel to the ground. It is recommended that the radial surface of the sensor body points downward, or that the fluid ports are in a vertical position for easy air purge.
    3. Connect water hoses in between the FPU and F-50 / F-50SH and secure them by hose bands except FPU ends (use gray hose for supply line. Use yellow hose for return line).
    4. Note Secure the hose band firmly to avoid a water leak.
      Cover the hoses with insulator.
  5. Install the reinforcement bar with screws.
    Note (For remote (off the wall) siting configuration) The coldhead EMI filter should be de-installed from the ICC PW and installed on the Penetration Panel. Refer to Installing the coldhead EMI filter on the penetration panel.
  6. Route and connect the F-50 / F-50SH power cable (E0009) and sensor cable (Run 826).
    Note F-50 / F-50SH Power is provided by the MDP or facility PDU with a 30A breaker.
  7. (For standard (on the wall) siting configuration) In the scan room, connect the cables for the magnet monitor and MRU.

    (For remote (off the wall) siting configuration) route the cables from the magnet monitor and MRU to the scan room through the penetration panel (without routing through the ICC).

    Note (For remote (off the wall) siting configuration) The BNC/DB filter is removed from the ICC PW and installed on the penetration panel. These cables do not pass through the ICC, they connect to the same location on the penetration panel.

Topic ID: id_SL13415218-1212135

Filling ICC primary water circuit

Procedure

  1. Make sure the facility water hoses are connected to the ICC.
  2. Close the three valves with the red handle (two valves for emergency cooling and one valve for filter flushing).
  3. Open the shut-off valve for incoming coolant on module FPU. Leave the shut-off valves (for outgoing coolant) closed.
  4. Open the valves for in and out lines of the cryogen compressor.
  5. Connect a transparent hose onto the emergency valve. Install the other end of the flushing hose to a sufficient collecting vessel.
  6. Open the facility water valves and supply the facility slightly.
  7. Check for leakage.
  8. Open the emergency valve hose slightly and flush the water. If coolant with no additions of air (bubbles) appear, close the flushing line by closing the emergency valve.
  9. Open the shut-off valves. The residual air should leave by flushing the circuits while the system is running. Disconnect the flushing hose.
  10. Note Do not exceed the maximum pressure on the pressure gauge.
    Open valves of supply and return of the facility water as much as needed to read the gauge in between 50 L/min and 80 L/min (13.2 gpm and 21.1 gpm).
    Note Typical value of water flow is 60 L/min (15.8 gpm).
  11. Check for leakages.
