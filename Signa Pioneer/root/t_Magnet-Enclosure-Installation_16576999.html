Table 1. Safety
Warning
Strong magnetic field
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field that the magnet produces.
Do not bring any ferrous tools or equipment into the magnet room.
Warning
Strong magnetic field
The cart for the magnet enclosure package includes ferrous material, which can become a dangerous projectile in the presence of the magnetic field that the magnet produces.
Do not bring the cart into the magnet room after the magnet is ramped up.
Note Protective film is attached on the magnet enclosure from the forward production (Q3, 2016). It will be applied for both pre-attached enclosures on the magnet and the enclosures shipped for installation.

The purpose of protective film is to protect the enclosure surface from the scratch during transportation and system installation.

If the protective film is attached to the cover, please keep the following items in mind.

  • The film will be removed just before customer turnover. Please do not remove the film until then.
  • When fixing the cover with screws, peel off the film only for the fixing location.

Procedure

  1. (For AR magnet) Move the side top cover support from the shipping location to the installed location:
    1. Remove the side top cover support by removing two screws and washers.
    2. Install the side top cover support at the installed location and secure with two screws and washers.
  2. Install Side Cover Center Beam brackets (5534644) with four stainless steel hexagon M10 x 25 Socket Head Cap Screws and four large flat M10 Stainless Steel Washers (2001-M10-10) for both left and right side.
  3. Install Cover Lower Latch Assembly 2 (5620618 and 5620618-2) at four locations using two Flat M6 Stainless Steel Washers (2000-M6-10) and two Stainless Steel Hexagon Nuts (3000-M6C-33) at each location.
    Note 5620618 and 5620618-2 are symmetrical parts.
    AInstall Cover Lower Latch Assembly 2
    BThis stud is not used
    CFix the cover with a screw and washer

    Start at the front right bottom corner and proceed to the other three corners.

  4. Install the side cover right-hand upper rear with two M10 x 25 Socket Head Cap Screws (1000-M10C025-33) and two M10 x 50 Socket Head Screws (1000-M10C050-33).
    Note The magnet enclosure will be installed after magnet shimming; it is a task of Installation and Calibration wizard (ICW).
  5. (For magnets with right-hand side 90-degree vent pipes) Determine the center point of where to cut the space for the 90-degree vent pipe on the right-hand side magnet top cover.
    1. Locate the center point by measuring the distance from the center of the 90-degree vent pipe to either the front or rear edge of the right-hand side magnet top cover. As an alternative, using two to three people, you can hold the right-hand side magnet top cover up to the 90-degree vent pipe, and mark the center point.
    2. Match the measured center point of the lower side of the right-side magnet top cover with the center point of the upper half of the provided paper template. Mark the location, and cut the cover according to the paper template measurement to fit the 90-degree vent pipe.
  6. Install the side cover right-hand upper front with one M10 x 50 Socket Head Screw ( 1000-M10C050-33).
  7. Install the side cover left-hand upper rear with three M10 x 25 Socket Head Cap Screws (1000-M10C025-33) and three M10 Large Flat Washers (2001-M10-30).
  8. Install the side cover left-hand upper front with three M10 x 25 Socket Head Cap Screws (1000-M10C025-33) and three M10 Large Flat Washers (2001-M10-30).
  9. Notice
    Before installing the center top cover left-hand, make sure the three cables (M3341, M3386, and M3387) are connected to SRI and PAC.
    Install the center top cover left-hand with two M10 x 25 Socket Head Cap Screws (1000-M10C025-33) and two M10 Large Flat Washers (2001-M10-30).
  10. Install access cover left-hand side with two M6-1 x 12MM Phillips Pan Head Screws (1007-M6C012-31).
  11. Rear bridge side covers by fixing three M10 x 25 Socket Head Cap Screws (1000-M10C025-33) and three M10 Large Flat Washers (2001-M10-30) for both covers.
  12. Connect the M3350 cable to the connector at the bracket of right bridge side cover.
  13. Install rear bridge bottom cover with two M10 x 25 Socket Head Cap Screws (1000-M10C025-33) and two M10 Large Flat Washers (2001-M10-30).
  14. Install rear center bridge cover two M10 x 25 Socket Head Cap Screws (1000-M10C025-33) and two M10 Large Flat Washers (2001-M10-30).
  15. Install duct bar with four M10 x 25 Socket Head Cap Screws (1000-M10C025-33).
  16. Install rear cable cover with two M10 x 25 Socket Head Cap Screws (1000-M10C025-33).
  17. Note From Q3 2016, only one side cover dolly is shipped with the system because of VCP purpose. Use one dolly for both lower side covers.
    Install the lower side cover assembly (right) with two M10 x 25 Socket Head Cap Screws (1000-M10C025-33) and two M10 Large Flat Washers (2001-M10-30).
  18. Notice
    Before installing the lower side cover assembly (left), make sure the long coil cables (M1356 through M1365) are connected to the DPP.
    Do the same procedure in Lower side cover assembly (right) for the lower side cover assembly (left).
  19. Install the side nameplate cover for both side using poppers.
  20. If not yet done, install the alternate language laser light warning label over the English label at the location shown below for non-English country.
