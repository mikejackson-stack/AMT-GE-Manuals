Replacing the bridge trim

Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

DANGER
Strong Magnetic Field
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field produced by the magnet.
Do not bring any ferromagnetic tools or equipment into the magnet room.

Procedure

  1. Move the cradle to Home position.
  2. Lower the patient table to the down-limit, if not already done.
  3. Remove the rear center bridge cover.
    1. Remove two screws.
    2. Unhook the two connection points and remove the rear center bridge cover from the magnet enclosure.
  4. Remove the rear bridge bottom cover.
    1. Remove the two screws at the bridge side covers.
    2. Unhook the two connection points at the bottom and remove the bottom cover from the magnet enclosure.
  5. Remove the rear bridge side covers by removing three screws from both covers.
  6. Remove the front bridge.
    1. Remove the four front screws and two rear screws.
    2. Remove the rear bridge cover by removing the six caps and six bolts securing the front bridge (four in the front and two in the rear).
    3. With two people, pull the front bridge away from the magnet enclosure.
  7. Replace the bridge trim.
    Note The FRU part is one piece of trim. If both pieces of trim need to be replaced, order two pieces.
    1. Remove the bridge trim piece(s) that will be replaced.
    2. Insert the bridge trim into the slot on the side of the bridge. Align the trim end with the end of the slot.
  8. Restore the front bridge and covers in reverse order of removal.

Finalization

  1. Make sure that the cradle moves in and out smoothly.
  2. Do a cradle motion test. See Doing the cradle motion test.
