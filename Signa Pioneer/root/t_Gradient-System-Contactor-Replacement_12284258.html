DANGER
ELECTROCUTION HAZARD!
DANGEROUS AND OR FATAL VOLTAGES ARE PRESENT IN THE ENERGIZED CABINET.
Follow LOTO steps as given in first section of this document to disable and verify safe voltage levels.
DANGER
HOT SURFACE OF TRANSFORMER.
SURFACE TEMPERATURE OF TRANSFORMER WILL BE OVER 60°C (140°F) WHILE SYSTEM IS IN OPERATION.
EVEN THOUGH THE POWER IS TURNED OFF, THE HEAT WILL REMAIN FOR A LONG TIME. CONTACT WITH SURFACE WILL RESULT IN BURNS. DO NOT TOUCH TRANSFORMER.

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

PDU and Service Disconnect Panel (or Main Disconnect Panel) must be turned OFF. Refer to Lockout / Tagout for MDP(Main Disconnect Panel) or Facility PDU .

--

Procedure

  1. Open ISC.
  2. Remove PDU Front Panel.
  3. Disconnect all cables from the Gradient System Contactor terminal.
  4. Remove the contactor by pulling the lever to right direction with flat screwdriver.
  5. Restore contactor by the reverse order of the removal.

Finalization

  1. Restore the Power. Refer to Removing LOTO - Main Disconnect Panel (MDP).
  2. Perform check scan.
