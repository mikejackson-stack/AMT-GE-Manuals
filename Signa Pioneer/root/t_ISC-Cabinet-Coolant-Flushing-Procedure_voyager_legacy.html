System Power OFF and Confirmation

Procedure

  1. Perform LOTO for ISC. Refer to LOTO for ISC.
  2. Confirm the followings.
    1. CCU pressure meter (black meter) is at zero.
    2. ICC Control Unit TCU display is OFF.
Topic ID: id_SL15171600-1335737

Flushing Procedure for 3 axis SSSA and SSSPS

Procedure

  1. Connect Convert Coupler (BPV353 to NS6D10008) to DVMR pump.
  2. Remove RF Amp Leak Sensor tray per following steps.
    1. Disconnect two connectors for RF Amp leak sensor.
    2. Loosen two screws.
    3. Move up a little and remove RF Amp Leak sensor tray.
  3. Close Manifold top valve for supply coolant.
  4. Disconnect all supply hose per following steps.
    1. Notice
      Use towel to absorb any leakage of the coolant. Do not spill the coolant over the leak sensor.
      Notice
      When disconnect the hoses, disconnect quickly to avoid the excessive spilling of coolant.
      Hold a towel in one hand below the hose connector and disconnect supply hose for RF Amp.
    2. Disconnect the all supply hoses to the SSSD at the water manifold.
  5. Setup for flushing as following steps.
    1. Connect drain hose to the supply side of drain port.
    2. Insert drain hose to flexible tank.
  6. Setup for flushing as following steps.
    1. Connect Pump hose to Port 1.
    2. Open the Drain Valve.
  7. Use pump to push out coolant until there’s no coolant remaining inside (About 4 times).
  8. Close the draining valve which was opened in Step 6.2.
  9. Disconnect the hose and hand pump from the Port 1.
  10. Repeat Step 6 to Step 9 for the Port 2,3,4.
  11. Open Manifold top valve for 30 sec to fill the coolant in manifold. Then, close it again.
  12. Repeat Step 6 to Step 9. This time, the order is Port 4 -> 3 ->2 ->1.

Flushing Procedure for RF Amp

Procedure

  1. Remove Convert Coupler (BPV353 to NS6D10008) from DVMR pump.
  2. Setup as following steps.
    1. Notice
      Use towel to absorb any leakage of the coolant. Do not spill the coolant over the leak sensor.
      Notice
      When connect/disconnect the hoses, connect/disconnect quickly to avoid the excessive spilling of coolant.
      Hold a towel in one hand below the hose connector and connect supply hose for RF Amp.
    2. Disconnect return hose of RF Amp .
    3. Connect Pump hose to return port of RF Amp.
    4. Open Drain valve
  3. Use pump to push out coolant until there’s no coolant remaining inside (About 4 times).
  4. Close the draining valve which was opened in Step 2.4.
  5. Remove hose from drain port.
  6. Notice
    Use towel to absorb any leakage of the coolant. Do not spill the coolant over the leak sensor.
    Remove pump hose and connect return hose to return port of RF Amp.
Topic ID: id_SL15171625-1335737

Restoration

Procedure

  1. Restore the all supply hoses to the SSSD at the water manifold.
  2. Restore RF Amp Leak Sensor tray in reverse order of removal.
  3. Open Manifold top valve.
Topic ID: id_15802137

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Notice
    The RF Amp may be damaged if power is on without coolant inside. To avoid this situation, set the RF Amp Breaker to OFF Position before power ON for new FRU.
    Set the RF Amp Breaker to OFF Position.
  2. Restore the System power. Refer to Removing LOTO - Integrated System Cabinet (ISC).
  3. Wait till pressure meter (black meter) of CCU moves for about 40 seconds.
  4. Turn the RF Amp Breaker ON
  5. Refill Coolant. Refer to Refill Coolant .
    Note Reuse the drained coolant using the red funnel. Note that the funnel has a filter to exclude the unaccepted particles.
    Vent valve with transparent hose is removed from GCU/CCU after end of Q4, 2017 per VCP with engineering analysis. No operation is needed if not exist.
