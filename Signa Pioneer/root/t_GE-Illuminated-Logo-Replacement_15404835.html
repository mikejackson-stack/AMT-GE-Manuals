Projectile Hazard

Keep operator control panel away from front of the magnet bore.

About this task

Overview

This procedure describes the removal and installation of the Operator Displays, Control Panels, E-Stops and IRD Assembly.

Procedure

  1. Follow Lockout/Tagout procedures per LOTO for ISC.
  2. Remove Side Nameplate Cover for both side using poppers.
  3. Remove Lower Side Cover Assembly (Left).
    1. Remove 2 screws.
    2. Fix Side Cover Dolly with turning two knobs.
    3. Unlatch the two hook.
    4. Move Lower Side Cover Assembly.
  4. Do the same procedure in Lower Side Cover Assembly (Left) for Lower Side Cover Assembly (Right).
  5. Remove Access Cover LH Side.
    1. Remove 2 screws.
    2. Unhook at 2 points.
  6. Remove Center Top Cover LH.
    1. Remove 2 screws.
    2. Unhook 4 points.
  7. Remove Side Cover LH Upper Front.
    1. Remove 3 screws.
    2. Unhook 3 points.
  8. Remove Side Cover RH Upper Front.
    1. Remove 1 screw.
    2. Unhook 6 points.
  9. Remove right and left OCP Panels.
    1. Remove Trim Ring.
    2. Remove OCP Panel by removing three screws and 5 poppers.
    3. Disconnect data cable.
    Note

    Remove left and right OCP panels even though the illustration below only shows the removal of left OCP panel.

  10. Remove all fixing rubber stoppers and screws.
    1. Remove three rubber stoppers.
    2. Remove 6 screws.
  11. Access to the connector and disconnect it.
  12. Remove Front Upper Cover.
  13. Remove GE LOGO per following steps.
    1. Cut cable tie wrap.
    2. Unscrew 3 nuts and remove GE LOGO.
  14. Installation is reverse order of removal.

Finalization

  1. Remove LOTO by referring to Removing LOTO - Integrated System Cabinet (ISC).
  2. Press the EMO Reset button to return the system to normal operation.
  3. Check the functionality of the item replaced. Run the associated diagnostic, if necessary.

    • For the In-Room Display - from the Common Service Desktop, select Diagnostics, then Hardware Location, then Magnet Room, then In Room Display.

    • For the Control Panel - from the Common Service Desktop, select Diagnostics, then Hardware Location, then Magnet Room, then SRI Functional Tests.

  4. Perform check scan.
