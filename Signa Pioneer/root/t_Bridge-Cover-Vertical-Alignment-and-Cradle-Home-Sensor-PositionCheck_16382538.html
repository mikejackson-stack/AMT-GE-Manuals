About this task

This procedure describes Bridge Cover vertical alignment and Cradle home sensor position adjustment so that cradle home sensor switch works properly.

Topic ID: id_SL14453440-1267372

Bridge Cover vertical Alignment

Procedure

  1. Move the Table to the highest position and move the cradle to Home Position.
  2. Check the distance of Cradle end and bridge cover at upper position and lower position.
    1. Move the Table down a little so that the distance between Bridge Cover upper surface and the cradle end can be measured. Then, measure the distance at left/right center of the cradle and record it as A.
    2. Move the Table to down limit. Then, measure the distance between Bridge Cover lower surface and the cradle at left/right center of the cradle. Then, record it as B.
    Table 5. Bridge Cover vertical measurement
    Distance (A) mm
    Distance (B) mm
  3. Confirm that the difference between A and B is less than 1mm. ( |A-B|<1mm )

    • If the difference between A and B is less than 1mm ( |A-B|<1mm ), end of the functional check. Go to Cradle Home Sensor Position Check.

    • If the difference between A and B is equal or more than 1mm ( |A-B| ≥ 1mm ), perform the following adjustment until it satisfies the specification.

      1. Move the Table to uplimit.

      2. Remove the upper and lower trim rings.

      3. Remove Dock Interface cover by removing two screws and unhooking at the bottom.

      4. Remove a screw and washer of center screw as illustration below.

      5. Loosen a nut behind the front end bell.

      6. Adjust endbell bottom position (in/out) slightly by slowly turning the center screw of rod with non magnetic flat head driver.

        Note

        1 turn of center screw will move about 2.5mm to in/out position.

        • A<B: Turn rod to CCW direction to lengthen it.

        • A>B: Turn rod to CW direction to shorten it.

      7. Tighten nut. Then, restore washer and screw.

      8. Perform the Bridge Cover vertical alignment check again until it satisfies the specification.

Cradle Home Sensor Position Check

Procedure

  1. Note

    This procedure is applied for TCB2 or later. If your Table Control Box (TCB) is TCB1(5500081), perform Cradle Home Sensor Position Adjustment for TCB1 in stead of this procedure (4.2 Cradle Home Sensor Position Check). If you do not know the TCB type, identification method is described in the linked procedure either.

    Move the cradle to Home Position.
  2. Slowly move the cradle in to adjust the distance between cradle end and Table top end. And do these two tests:
    1. When the distance is larger than 3 mm, press down pedal and confirm the Table cannot move down.
    2. When the distance is larger than (or equal to) 2 mm and smaller than (or equal to) 3 mm, press down pedal and confirm the Table can move down.
    Note

    Since actual distance is important, use rulers instead of the display of IRD.

  3. If it passes 2.a and 2.b test, this is the end of functional check. If it fails, perform Cradle Home Sensor Adjustment
