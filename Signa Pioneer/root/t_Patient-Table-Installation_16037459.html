Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

DANGER
Strong Magnetic Field
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field produced by the magnet.
Do not bring any ferromagnetic tools or equipment into the magnet room.
Warning
Electric shock hazard
High voltages are present in the energized cabinet.
Follow the LOTO steps for the cabinet and verify safe voltage levels.
CAUTION
Mechanical damage
The Electro-Hydraulic Actuator (EHA) may be damaged with the force of the screw rod.
Do not try to move the table up using the screw rod.

Removal of shipping lock and cable protection plate

Procedure

  1. Notice
    If table is positioned without removing the shipping lock, you may scratch the bridge cover or bridge.
    Use the following steps to remove the shipping lock just before the table positioning:
    1. Remove the shipping lock just before table positioning.
    2. Restore two screws for the cover after the shipping lock removal.
    Note After removal of shipping lock, keep the cradle at the home position (backward limit) and do not push it forward until it’s finally installed. Otherwise, it may cause a problem of table up movement because home sensor becomes unlatched.
  2. Use the following steps to remove the cable protection plate prior to table positioning.
    1. Cut two tie wraps which are fixing the cables on the protective plate.
    2. Remove two screws and remove cable protective plate.
      Note Keep the two screws which were on the lower base. These two screws will be used to connect the dock and table.
    Note It is recommended to store the cable protection plate at the site for future use to protect the cables during table transportation.
Topic ID: id_SL14777817-1178810

Patient table installation

Procedure

  1. Note The patient table is installed after magnet shimming.
    Perform LOTO for ISC.
  2. Warning
    Ferrous material hazard
    The table control box contains a small amount of ferrous material.
    Holding this assembly too close to the magnet bore will forcibly attract it to the magnet.
    Remove the table control box with the bracket using the following steps to install cables at table down limit.
    1. Remove five screws.
    2. Remove the table control box with the bracket.
  3. Do the following:
    1. Move the table next to the dock structure.
    2. Check that the table frame is attached and centered to the dock frame.
  4. Temporarily connect cables at J1, J6, J7, J8, and J9 at the table control box first.
  5. Remove the screw rod.
  6. Connect one of the up/down foot pedal cable to the connector to J1 of table control box.
  7. Remove LOTO for ISC.
  8. Move the table to upmost position by up pedal.
  9. Perform LOTO for ISC.
  10. Disconnect the cables at J1, J6, J7, J8, and J9 at the table control box.
  11. Carefully route DPP cables (M1356 through M1365) under the table control box and the magnet to DPP, keeping the cables straight.
  12. Connect coil cables (M1356 through M1365) to the DPP.
    1. Loosen eight captive screws.
    2. Flip the DPP RRx to access the bottom.
    3. Connect the cables.
    4. After all cables are connected, restore DPP RRx position.
  13. Route and connect the three cables (M3341, M3386, and M3387) from the patient table to PAC/SRI per following steps.
    1. Note From Q1, 2017, the three cables (M3341, M3386, and M3387) are originating from patient table per VCP purpose. There are two considerations.
      • If the three cables are not present at the patient table, connect the cables at table IO to M3341, M3386, and M3387, which come from the PAC and SRI4.
      • There is an overlap period between magnet pre-attached cables and patient table update. If the cables (M3341, M3386, and M3387) are pre-attached at the PAC and SRI4, and the three cables are present at the patient table, remove the cables from the magnet (PAC/SRI) and route the cables from the patient table according to the step below.
      Remove three rubber stoppers off the front LH lower cover.
    2. Remove three screws and washers to remove the front LH lower cover.
    3. Route the following three cables and connect them to SRI4 and PAC.
      Note Route the following three cables straightly under the Table Control Box as much before routing on the magnet.
      • M3341: Touch and Go cable (To SRI4 J20)
      • M3386: PPG cable (To PAC J5/J6)
      • M3387: ECG Cable (To PAC I/O ECG)
    4. Restore the Front LH lower cover.
  14. Install the remaining cables at table IO and table control box.
    Note Make sure the filter (5485745) is connected to J10 prior to connecting M3505.
    Note Route the cables in parallel and neatly under the table control box and for the table control box. Then, route the cables to the direction for each cable. For example, local Tx and MEMS cables go to the right, the other cables go to the left.

    Ignore the carved connector numbers on the table I/O plate when routing.

    Note

    *For systems with Remote Siting only, a table I/O bracket must be installed during the Head Tx cable installation. Follow the instructions in Step 15.

    Note In SIGNA Pioneer Forward Productions Systems the M3379 system cable (magnet pre-attached) was revised to add mechanical latch power line for TCB J11. The J11 cable is only used for the patient table with the mechanical latch shipped after W22. With the introduction of mechanical latch, the TCB J12 cable from the mechanical latch is also added.

    For the J11 cable and J12 cable, please follow the actions outlined in the table below.

    TCB Type M3379 Type Mechanical Latch Action
    TCB2With cable for TCB J11Does not existProtect the cable connector for J11 with plastic bag and leave it at magnet front bottom.
    TCB2Without cable for TCB J11Does not existNo action is required.
    TCB3With cable for TCB J11Does not existConnect the cable to the J11 connector to protect the cable connector.
    TCB3Without cable for TCB J11Does not existNo action is required.
    TCB4With cable for TCB J11Exists (J12 cable also exists)
    • Connect the power cable to the J11 connector for mechanical lock power.
    • Connect mechanical latch cable to J12.
  15. For systems with remote siting only:
    1. On the patient end, at the location shown, check for a ground cable. Remove it if one is already connected.

    2. Mount the table IO and mounting plate to the right side magnet foot. Secure using a M10 x 16 Socket Head Screws (1000-M10C016-33) and M10 Stainless Steel Washers (2001-M10-17) on the left side and reuse the brass screw, washer, and ground cable on the right (ground) side.

      Note If the ground cable is not installed, the ground wire may be installed later. Leave the left side of the bracket loose.
    3. Connect the cable from the RF amplifier to J2 on the top of the table I/O bracket.
    4. Connect one end of the Head Tx extension cable (5775578-3) to J2 (bottom connection) on the table I/O bracket.
    5. Connect the other end of the Head Tx extension cable to J15 on the table I/O.
  16. Restore the table control box with bracket with five screws.
  17. Disconnect the up/down foot pedal cables to the connector from J1 of table control box and remove the table base cover.
  18. (For Telescopic Cover)
    1. Remove six screws from bottom telescopic cover.
    2. Move the lowest telescopic cover up slightly and secure it with masking tape.
  19. (For Bellows Cover)
    1. Remove the six screws securing the bottom of the bellows cover; three on the left-hand side and three on the right-hand side.
    2. Lift up the bellows cover and secure it in the up position with six strips of Velcro.
  20. Complete table leveling using the following steps.
    1. Put the non-ferrous level on one side of the table top.
    2. Loosen the six locknuts of the leveling pad with the spanner.
    3. Measure the height difference between the table top and bridge top at both left and right sides. (Spec: within 1 mm)
    4. Rotate the leveling pad stud using 16 mm spanner to adjust the Table top and Bridge top at both left and right sides (Spec: within 1 mm) by check the level at right and left of the table to center the bubble.
      Note To raise the table height, turn the pad stud counterclockwise. To reduce the table height, turn the pad stud clockwise.
    5. Make the level bubble centered between the lines by raising one end of the level. Measure the distance (in mm) between the table top and the level at 910 mm. Do this for both left and right of the table top. If the value is greater than 4 mm, level the table at egress handle end.
  21. Roughly adjust the table lateral center and longitudinal gap while the caster is attached to the patient table.
    1. Note Fine adjustment will be done later.
      Move the patient table laterally to align the table edge line and bridge edge line
    2. Use the following steps to adjust the longitudinal gap.
      1. Move the patient table longitudinally and adjust the gap using 6mm jig as follows for left and right.
      2. After the adjustment, make sure the 8 mm side cannot be inserted into the gap for left and right.
  22. Adjust the dock frame position to align to the table position.
    1. Loosen the anchor nut.
    2. Remove four fixing bolts.
    3. Align the dock position so that the patient table can be fixed. Insert the washer to fill the gap between magnet bracket and dock frame.
    4. Fix the dock frame by restoring the four screws of dock frame and the nut for floor stud.
  23. Warning
    Ferrous material hazard
    Components in this procedure contain ferrous material. Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles within the magnetic field.
    Handle the components with care, making sure they are secure at all times within the magnetic field.
    Remove four casters.
  24. Notice
    Make sure to retain the table casters, screw rod, relief valve opening tool, and table gap check tool for use with next service.
    Use the following steps to fix the table to the dock frame.
    1. Make sure the screw holes can be seen through the dock frame slotted hole. If not, lower the six leveling pads with the same turns so that the table screw holes are visible.
    2. Fix the table to the dock frame using two bolts and washers.
  25. Remove the caution labels from both sides of patient table.
