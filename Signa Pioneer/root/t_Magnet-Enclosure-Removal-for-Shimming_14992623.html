Magnet enclosure removal for shimming

Prerequisites

Personnel requirements
Required personsPreliminary requirementsProcedureFinalization
2-60 minutes-

Procedure

  1. Remove the front bridge.
    1. Remove bridge rubber caps and screws (front four screws, rear two screws).

    2. Remove front bridge.
  2. Remove front trim rings by removing poppers.
  3. Remove alignment light cover by removing two screws and two poppers.
  4. Remove two screws and remove bridge cover.
  5. Find service driver for body coil tuning (5421330) in shipment package and store it behind bridge cover.
    Note This process can be done anytime during magnet shimming before restoring the cover.
  6. Remove eight screws and remove front endbell.
  7. Remove rear trim rings by removing poppers.
  8. Setup mapping fixture or STET according to the magnet manual for shimming.
  9. After shimming is completed, restore cover by reverse order.
