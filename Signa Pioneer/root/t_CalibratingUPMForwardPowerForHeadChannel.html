Calibrating UPM forward power for Head channel

Procedure

  1. Proceed to Calibrating the UPM in Head mode.
  2. Select values for the following settings within the tool:
    SettingValue
    Nuclei1H
    TaskCalibrate UPM
    RF ChainHead
    Power TypeForward

  3. Click Start.
  4. Click Yes when the prompt asks if the hardware setup is correct.
    After approximately 1 minute, you are asked to do a check of the peak power value output from the amplifier.
    • If the power read from the power meter is within specification, click Yes.
    • Otherwise, click No and recheck the calibration of the RF amplifier.

    Result

    The tool reports the steps as they progress. In about 3 minutes, the Status field on the tool will show PASS or FAIL.
  5. Based on the status choose one of the following options:
    • FAIL – Refer to Troubleshooting the UPM.
      Note If there is no power output from the MNS amplifier during UPM Forward Power Calibration, then follow the steps below.
      1. Close the UPM tool and start a new exam in geservice mode with a patient weight of 50 kg.
      2. Create a landmark with the 31P TR switch.
      3. Select Service > MNS Setup, and then prescribe the 31P Functional Check series.
      4. Save and download the sequence with any acceptable scan parameters.
      5. Open Spectro Prescan, click Start, and let the sequence run for about 10 seconds.
      6. Stop the sequence, end the exam, and continue with the UPM calibration.
    • PASS – Calibrate UPM Reflected Power for Head Channel.
