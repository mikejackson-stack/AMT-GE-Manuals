Removing the CMOS battery - HP Z4 G5

Remove the CMOS battery from the HP Z4 G5 host computer.

Procedure

  1. Make sure that you have read and understood the electrical safety requirements before starting this procedure. See Electrical Safety.
  2. Prepare the system for replacement. See Preparing the workstation for a computer replacement.
  3. Apply GOC LOTO. See Applying LOTO - Global Operator Cabinet (GOC) and Operator Workspace.
  4. If installed, disconnect the optional Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS).
  5. For added protection, disconnect the twist-n-lock main power cable from the rear of the console.
  6. Remove the two screws that secure the left side panel of the GOC.
    Note The side panel has a short ground lead connecting it to the main chassis. When removing the side panel, do not strain this ground lead.
  7. Disconnect the short ground lead that connects the side panel to the GOC main chassis at the center of the lead.
  8. Remove the left side panel and put it in a safe place.
  9. Remove the straps from the host computer.
  10. Remove the side cover of the host computer.
    1. Pull the release latch to release the lock mechanism.
    2. Use the release latch to pull the side cover away from the computer.
    3. Lift the side cover straight up to remove it.
  11. Remove the RAM fan assembly cover.
    1. Pinch the two blue release latches.
    2. Lift the RAM fan assembly cover and remove it.
  12. Locate the coin-cell battery.
  13. Remove the coin-cell battery from the socket.
    1. Insert a small screwdriver into the battery socket slot.
    2. Lift the small screwdriver to release the battery.
  14. Lift the battery out of the system board.
  15. Let the computer sit for a minimum of three minutes to let the CMOS memory clear.
