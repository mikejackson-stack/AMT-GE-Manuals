Table 4. Safety
DANGER
Electrocution Injury!
Hazardous voltage is present when GOC is energized.
Before beginning any work, perform lock out/tag out (LOTO). See LOTO Global Operator Cabinet / Operator Workspace.
CAUTION
Potential Component Damage
The cooling fan is OFF only when the workstation is turned off or the power cable is disconnected. The cooling fan is always ON when the workstation is ON or in Standby mode.
Disconnect the power cord from the power source before opening the workstation.
Notice
Potential component damage
Electrostatic discharge (ESD) can cause permanent damage to electronic components.
Observe the following ESD precautions:
  • Work on a static-free mat.
  • Wear a static strap to make sure that any accumulated electrostatic charge is discharged from your body to the ground.
  • Create a common ground for the equipment being worked on by connecting the static-free mat, static strap, and peripheral units to that piece of equipment.
  • For more information on ESD protection, see MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).

Procedure

  1. Remove the left side panel and set it is a safe place.
    1. Remove the two screws that secure the left side panel of the GOC.
    2. Lift up and remove the left side panel. (Be careful not to strain the ground cable) .
    3. Disconnect ground cable.
    Note The side panel has a short ground lead connecting it to the main chassis. When removing the side panel, do not strain this ground lead.
  2. Loosen the strap and remove the hook.
    1. Press release lever of buckle and loosen strap.
    2. Remove the hook.
  3. Remove a fixing screw of PC.
  4. Note To prevent damage, do not remove the DVD drive from its antistatic packaging until you are ready to install.
    Remove the access panel (left side) of the PC by pulling the latch out and sliding the cover back.
  5. Remove the front panel on the computer by gently unsnapping the plastic locks located on the left side and pulling on the left side of the cover.
    1. Release plastic locks by pull the cover out slightly not to damage the hinges on the other side of the cover.
    2. Push the cover slightly to the right to disengage the hinges and pull out on the cover.
  6. Locate and unplug the power/data cable harness (D4) on the back of the DVD drive.
  7. Perform the following steps.
    1. Pull out the plastic lock located on the left side of the PC
    2. Slide out the DVD carefully.
  8. Remove the guide screws from the old DVD and install on the new unit.
  9. Install the new DVD drive by pulling out on the plastic lock and sliding the new drive into the open slot until it is securely seated.
  10. Reconnect the power/data cable harness to the new DVD drive.
  11. Restore PC and GOC by reverse order of removal.
    Note Confirm all cables and covers have been reinstalled and are secure.

Finalization

  1. Perform LOTO on the GOC to return power. Refer to LOTO for GOC.
  2. Verify that the host computer front panel power LED is illuminated as a steady blue signal.
  3. Run a goodbye scan (Check Scan).
  4. Confirm that all customer functions are operational (auto voice, filming, network, archive).
