Removing the Image Compute Node (ICN) Gen 5, 6, or 7

Remove the Image Compute Node (ICN) Gen 5, 6, or 7 to replace it or to replace the ICN power supply.

Procedure

  1. Make sure that you have read and understood the electrical safety requirements before starting this procedure. See Electrical Safety.
  2. Apply LOTO to the ISC. See Applying LOTO - Integrated System Cabinet (ISC).
  3. If the ISC is on the wall, loosen the attachment screws and lift the ISC PW cover a small distance to remove it, otherwise complete the next step.
    Note If the furnished closet door prevents removal, you can separate the PW cover into two pieces by removing six screws at the center.
  4. Remove the rear access cover.
  5. Disconnect all the cables from the rear of the ICN, including the AC power plug(s).
  6. Open the ISC front cover.
  7. Loosen two captive screws under the screw cover and slide out the ICN.
