Magnet Enclosure Alignment Troubleshooting

Notice
Brackets are pre-adjusted at the Magnet assembling process in manufacturing. This troubleshooting is used only when magnet enclosure is obviously misaligned.

This document describes various error conditions for the Magnet Enclosure Alignment and possible solutions.

Note Alignment is performed using the side beam throughout this document

Notice
Mark the original screw and bracket position before loosen the screws. These brackets have large flexibility and easy to be messed up ! In case the bracket position is messed up, refer to Reference: Position of side bracket.
Notice
Move the beam only one direction at each adjustment.

Go to the following sections to resolve issues:

  • Left Side: Large gap between side bottom cover and front/rear cover
  • Left Side: Misalignment of side beam
  • Left Side: Access Cover Interferes with side electronics
  • Left Side: Distorted Arc Cover
  • Left Side: Not able to fix side access cover
  • Right Side: Large gap between side bottom cover and front/rear cover
  • Right Side: Side upper covers are pulled by side beam at the center of magnet
  • Right Side: Misalignment of side beam to lower direction
  • Right Side: Misalignment of side beam to upper direction
  • Bottom latch is hard to engage even though there is no gap between side bottom cover and front cover

Left Side: Large gap between side bottom cover and front/rear cover

Topic ID: id_SL16102957-1361220

Observation

Large gap between left side bottom cover and front/rear cover.

Topic ID: id_SL16102959-1361220

Alignment

  1. Mark the screws and bracket location. Then, loosen four screws.

  2. Move the side beam inward to adjust the position.

  3. Tighten the four screws. Then, check the side bottom cover gap after installing it.

Reference: Position of side bracket

Default position of side bracket at manufacturing is as follows.

  • Measurement position: Upper screw hole to magnet (horizontal) 4 brackets.

  • Nominal distance: 103.2 mm

  • Tolerance: +/- 2 mm

Notice
Adjust only distance from the magnet body.
Do not adjust the height and Z direction at this moment. (Screw is at the center of the slot hole.)
Note The following illustration shows the jig at engineering. In the field, use the nonferrous tool if measurement is needed.
