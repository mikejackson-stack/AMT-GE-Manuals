Removing the hydraulic rotary pump drip pan

Removes the rotary pump oil drip pan from the dockable patient table.

Procedure

  1. Lift one side of the drip pan and gently push it inwards to flex the bottom of the pan.
  2. While pushing the side of the pan inward, push the pan down between the rails, and slide the pan out from under the table.
