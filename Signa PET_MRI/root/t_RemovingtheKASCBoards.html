Removing the KASC boards

Remove the defective KASC boards.

About this task

This procedure applies to all KASC boards; do the steps below for any KASC board that is being removed.

Procedure

  1. Make sure that you have read and understood the electrical safety requirements before starting this procedure. See Electrical Safety.
  2. Shut down and power off the host computer. See Shutting down the host computer.
  3. Apply PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem LOTO. See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
  4. In the PEN cabinet, turn off the circuit breaker to the driver module.
  5. Open the PGR cabinet doors.
  6. Disconnect the cables from the front panel of the board(s) you will remove.
  7. Loosen the 2 screws that hold the board in the chassis.
  8. Push the release tab.
  9. Open the extractor tab.
  10. Remove the board.
    Note Handle the circuit board only by the faceplate, ejectors, and the edges of the board.
  11. Remove the terminators (if any) from the defective board and retain for installation on replacement board.
    Note New boards do not come with terminators, so you must reuse them from the old boards.
  12. Put the defective board in an appropriate shipping package, or discard, if applicable.
