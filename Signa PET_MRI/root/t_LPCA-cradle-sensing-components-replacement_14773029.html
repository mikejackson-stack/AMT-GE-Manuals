Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

CAUTION
Potential crush or pinch hazard
Movement of the table or cradle can result in pinched or crushed fingers.
Keep fingers away from the gaps between the table, cradle, and the LPCA when docking or undocking the table or while connecting coils.

Procedure

  1. Make sure that you have read and understood the electrical safety requirements before starting this procedure. See Electrical Safety.
  2. Press Home on the Operator Control Panel (OCP) to move the LPCA and cradle to the end.
  3. Manually lift the LPCA hook and move the LPCA to the rear.
  4. Shut down and power off the host computer. See Shutting down the host computer.
  5. After the system is powered down, turn off the Power, Gradient, RF Cabinet (PGR) Power Distribution Unit (PDU) breaker at the Main Disconnect Panel (MDP).
  6. Apply PGR PDU/Gradient subsystem LOTO. See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem. Leave the main, Heat Exchanger Cabinet (HEC), and PET Cabinet (PET) PDU breakers on.
  7. Remove the screws that secure the cover to the LPCA base (see LPCA hook and cover), and remove the cover.
    Note The LPCA for Direct Sample Digitized Per Pin (DS DPP) systems uses a reduced profile and does not have an A-port.
  8. Remove the screws that secure the switch to the cradle sensing assembly, and remove the switch.
    1Cradle sensor
    2Cradle sensing assembly
  9. Remove the screws and washers that attach the cradle sensing assembly to the LPCA base.
  10. Remove the screws that secure the cap. Replace the spring and plunger and, if needed, the screws.
    Note The housing and cap are not FRU parts.
  11. Put the cradle sensing assembly in the rear position, so the plunger protrudes over the LPCA base edge, and secure it with the previously removed screws.
  12. Replace and secure the switch to the cradle sensing assembly with the screws that were removed earlier. (Do not replace the LPCA cover at this time.)
  13. Remove PGR PDU/Gradient subsystem LOTO. See Removing LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Start up the host computer/system. See Starting up the host computer.
  2. Continue to the Cradle to LPCA Latch Adjustment procedure.
  3. Do a check scan. See MR and PET Hello-Goodbye Scan.
