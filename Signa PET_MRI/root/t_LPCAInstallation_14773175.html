Installing the LPCA

This procedure describes how to install the LPCA.

Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

Warning
Ferrous material hazard
The motor and gear assembly contain parts made with ferrous components. Holding these components too close to the magnet bore will forcibly attract them to the magnet.
To prevent possible bodily injury or component damage, keep them as far from the magnetic field as possible when servicing.
CAUTION
Potential crush or pinch hazard
Movement of the table or cradle can result in pinched or crushed fingers.
Keep fingers away from the gaps between the table, cradle, and the LPCA when docking or undocking the table or while connecting coils.

About this task

Note The LPCA for DS DPP systems uses a reduced profile and does not have an A-port.

Procedure

  1. Carry the new LPCA into the magnet room and position it on top of the rear bridge assembly with the hook facing the patient end.
  2. Remove harnesses, cables, switches, and other components from the existing LPCA, and install them on the new LPCA.
  3. Pull the encoder line with the eyelet attachment block, and position the block in its socket with the screw head facing up and toward the rear of the LPCA.
  4. Apply LOCTITE™ 243 to the screws that were removed earlier, and secure the A-port mounting block to the LPCA base. (This will secure the encoder eyelet attachment block.)
  5. Route the SW3 sensor lead in the channel on the left rear of the LPCA base and underneath the left cable track.
  6. Secure the left and right port cable tracks to the LPCA with the screws that were removed earlier.
  7. (For RRx systems) Push the J1 plug of the A-port harness through the A-port mounting block.
  8. Lift and position the sub-D plate and the Excite III A-bezel cable harness assembly if removed earlier (RRx only), and secure the sub-D Plate to the A-port mounting block with the screws.
  9. Secure the P1 and P2 baluns to the LPCA base with these screws as shown:
    1. Three screws for each balun
    2. One screw and one self-locking cable tie per balun
  10. (For RRx systems) Connect the sensor cable leads to the red and green LEDs on the A-port Excite III bezel.
  11. (For RRx systems) Connect the sensor cable plug to the A-port cable harness.
    (For DS DPP systems) Connect the sensor cable plug to the P-port cable harness.
  12. Secure the Port 1 and Port 2 supports and harness assemblies to the stationary slides with the screws removed earlier.
  13. Snap the P1 and P2 quick disconnects into place.
  14. Lift and support the LPCA assembly.
  15. Replace the encoder housing, and secure it to the A-port mounting block with the previously removed screws.
  16. Thread the drive belts through the pulley assemblies.
    Note

    Route the drive belts over the pulleys. Do not route the drive belts over the spacer rods.

  17. Thread the ends of the drive belts under the LPCA and up through the LPCA drive belt openings.
  18. Leaving four drive belt teeth exposed at the front drive belt clamps, position the drive belt clamp plates over the drive belt ends, and secure them with the screws.
  19. Position the rear drive belt clamps over the drive belt ends, pull the drive belt as snug as possible, and secure the rear drive belt clamps with the screws.
  20. Adjust the belt tension on the left-hand pulley assembly, so that both belts have the same tension. See Checking and setting the PET/MR belt tension.
  21. Replace the rear pedestal covers, but do not replace the LPCA cover at this time.
  22. Remove LOTO from the Power, Gradient RF Cabinet (PGR) Power Distribution Unit (PDU) breaker on the Main Disconnect Panel (MDP), and turn the PDU breaker on.

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Start up the host computer/system. See Starting up the host computer.
  2. Continue to Cradle/LPCA Release Adjustment.
  3. Do a test scan to verify system operation. See MR and PET Hello-Goodbye Scan.
