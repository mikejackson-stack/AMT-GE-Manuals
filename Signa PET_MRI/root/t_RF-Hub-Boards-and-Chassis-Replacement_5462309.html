Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

CAUTION
Projectile hazard!
The power distribution board contains some ferrous material and can be attracted to the magnet.
Do not place the power distribution board on top of the rear pedestal or less than 2 feet from the magnet. Use caution when removing boards.

RF switch board

About this task

Multiple versions of the RF boards have been used, but the location in the RF hub chassis and the replacement procedures are the same.

Topic ID: id_SL1989572-1061484

Removing RF switch board

About this task

Notice
The scan room power supply (SRPS) must be powered OFF before removing the power distribution board.

Procedure

  1. Remove the side covers from the rear pedestal.
  2. Turn off the power switch on the power distribution board. See RF hub components.
  3. Remove the cables on the front of the RF switch board.
  4. Loosen the top and bottom screws on front of the RF switch board.
  5. Lift up on the top and bottom release levers to eject the RF switch board.
    Note

    The board will not eject unless both screws are thoroughly loosened.

  6. Slide out the RF switch board from the RF chassis.
  7. Place the board in an ESD bag and remove it from the magnet room.
Topic ID: id_SL1989579-1061484

Installing RF switch board

Procedure

  1. Slide the new RF switch board into the RF chassis. Make sure the board is in the track.
  2. Line up the mechanical lever groove with the chassis edge, and lower the levers to insert the board.
    Note

    Make sure the board is fully seated in the RF chassis.

  3. Tighten the top and bottom screws to secure the RF switch board to the RF chassis.
  4. Attach cables to the front of the RF switch board.

Power Distribution Board

Topic ID: id_SL1989607-1061484

Removing the Power Distribution Board (PDB, PDBv, or ePDB)

Procedure

  1. Remove the side covers from the rear pedestal.
  2. Turn off the power switch on the PDB in the RF Hub, Slot 12. See RF hub components.
  3. Turn off the power switch on the scan room power supply (SRPS).
  4. Remove the cables on the front of the PDB.
    Note If replacing the Power Distribution Board (PDB) with the PDBv and J50 dongle, see Power Distribution Board (PDB) cable connections.
    Table 1. Power Distribution Board (PDB) cable connections
    Cable connectorDescription
    J117W5 Connector
    J213W6 Connector
    J313W6 Connector with terminator for 32-channel systems (cable Run M3305 in place of terminator only present in 64- or 128-channel systems; these systems require replacement to the PDB board)
    J4DB25 Connector
    J521W4 Connector
    J69W4 Connector
    J7Backplane Interface
  5. Loosen the top and bottom screws on the front of the PDB.
  6. Lift up on the top and bottom release levers to eject the PDB.
  7. Slide out the PDB from the RF chassis.
    Note

    The board will not eject unless both screws are thoroughly loosened.

  8. If there is a terminator (shunt connector) installed on J3, remove and save for reinstallation only if FRU replacement is the PDB. Otherwise PDBv does not have J3 (PDBv only supports up to 32 channels) and therefore a terminator will not used.
    Note

    ePDB does not have a terminator (shunt connector).

  9. Place the board in an ESD bag and remove it from the magnet room.
Topic ID: task_mtr_54f_lvb

Installing the Power Distribution Board (PDB, PDBv, or ePDB)

About this task

  • For systems with a PDB (5250082), see Installing the PDBv and J50 dongle in place of the PDB.
  • For all other replacements, see Installing the PDB, PDBv, or ePDB.
Topic ID: task_yss_rqf_lvb

Installing the PDBv and J50 dongle in place of the PDB

Procedure

  1. Slide the new PDBv into the RF chassis.
  2. Line up the mechanical lever groove with the chassis edge and lower the levers to insert the board. See Groove on RF Board Lever.
    Note Make sure the board is fully seated in the RF chassis.
  3. Tighten the top and bottom screws to secure the PDBv to the RF chassis.
  4. Attach the cable to the front of the PDBv. See Power Distribution Board (PDBv) cable connections.
    Note Since the Power Distribution Board (PDBv) does not require -8.5V from the SPRS cable, the cable Run M3307 connecting PDB J5 to Penetration Panel J50 is no longer used. Apply a few turns of electrical tape around connectors at each end of the cable, J5 at the RF hub and J50 at the Penetration Panel. Using an indelible marker, label each end of the cable “Unused” and set each end out of the way and aside.
    Table 2. Power Distribution Board (PDBv) cable connections
    Cable connectorDescription
    J117W5 Connector
    J213W6 Connector
    J4DB25 Connector
    J69W4 Connector
    J7Backplane Interface
  5. At the Penetration Panel, connect and secure the SRPS dongle to J50 where cable Run M3307 was previously connected. Cable Run M3307 is no longer used.
  6. Turn on the power switch on the PDB.
  7. Turn on the power switch on the scan room power supply (SRPS).
Topic ID: id_SL1989615-1061484

Installing the PDB, PDBv, or ePDB

Procedure

  1. Slide the new PDB, PDBv, or ePDB into the RF chassis.
  2. Line up the mechanical lever groove with the chassis edge, and lower the levers to insert the board. See Groove on RF Board Lever.
    Note

    Make sure the board is fully seated in the RF chassis.

  3. Tighten the top and bottom screws to secure the PDB, PDBv, or ePDB to the RF chassis.
  4. Attach the cables to the front of the PDB, PDBv, or ePDB.
  5. For a PDB, attach the previously used terminator or cable Run M3305 to J3. The PDBv and ePDB do not have J3 or terminator.
  6. Turn on the SRPS.
Topic ID: id_SL1985001-1061484

RF hub chassis

Topic ID: id_SL1989689-1061484

Removing RF hub chassis

Procedure

  1. Remove the following RF hub boards:

    • RF switch board(s). See Removing RF switch board.

    • MC driver board(s). See Removing MC driver board.

    • RF control board. See Removing RF control board.

    • Power distribution board (PDB or ePDB). See Removing the Power Distribution Board (PDB, PDBv, or ePDB).

  2. Remove any cables connected to the back of the RF chassis.
  3. On the other side of the rear pedestal, locate the vent intake connected to the RF chassis.
  4. Remove the three screws directly on top of the vent intake.
  5. Pull the vent intake away from the RF chassis.
  6. Remove the seven bolts securing the RF chassis to the rear pedestal.
  7. Remove the RF chassis from the rear pedestal.
Topic ID: id_SL1989695-1061484

Installing RF hub chassis

Procedure

  1. Position the new RF chassis in the rear pedestal.
  2. To connect the venting intake to the RF chassis, seat the vent in the RF chassis lip and secure the three screws directly on top of the vent intake. See Vent intake screw locations.
  3. Attach any previously installed cables to back of the RF chassis.
  4. Secure the seven bolts attaching the RF chassis to the rear pedestal. See RF chassis - bolt locations.
  5. Install the following RF hub boards:

    • RF switch board(s). See Installing RF switch board.

    • MC driver board(s). See Installing MC driver board.

    • RF control board. See Installing RF control board.

    • Power distribution board (PDB or ePDB). See Installing the PDB, PDBv, or ePDB.

Topic ID: id_15253738

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Turn on the power switch on the PDB.
  2. Replace the side covers on the rear pedestal.
  3. Remove LOTO. See Removing LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
  4. Perform a TPS reset.
