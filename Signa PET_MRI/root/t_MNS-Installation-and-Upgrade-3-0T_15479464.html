Required conditions
Remove any twists and kinks from the cabling. When routing cables, avoid circular loop bundles. Use “figure eight” coils of less than 2m (6 ft) in diameter.
Allow 1m (3 ft) of cable slack at the entrance to the cabinets.
If the amplifier cabinet is an AMT/Herley, move the MNS cabinet door down using the adjustable hinges before positioning the cabinet. With an opening at the top of the cabinet, the cables can be routed up and out of the MNS cabinet.
Safety
DANGER
Fatal electric shock hazard
Lethal voltages are present in the power distribution unit (PDU) even if all PDU breakers are off.
Disconnect and complete LOTO on the power from the system at the facility's main disconnect. See the MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).

About this task

The 3.0T Multi-Nuclear Spectroscopy (MNS) Kit includes a broadband exciter, amplifier, cabling, T/R switch, transmit switch, upconverter, and service coil for 31P spectroscopy. Other MNS nuclei are supported by the kit, although separate T/R switches, coils, and phantoms are necessary.
Note MNS scans can only be executed on the curved table. The flat or GEM table is not compatible with the MNS option.

There are two individual amplifiers that are utilized for the spectroscopy option:

  • 8 kW AMT/Herley amplifier – This is the original amplifier and is easily identified by cables connecting to the rear of the cabinet.
  • CPC MNS amplifier – This is a new amplifier introduced in summer of 2014. Cables connect to the top of the cabinet for this amplifier.
    Note The CPC MNS amplifier is only compatible with DV systems MR750, MR750w and Architect T. It is not compatible with HD systems.

    Two hoist support brackets are in the bottom of the CPC cabinet crate. Install them on the top of the cabinet so that they do not get lost.

System power shutdown

Procedure

  1. DANGER
    Fatal electric shock hazard
    Lethal voltages are present in the power distribution unit (PDU) even if all PDU breakers are off.
    Disconnect and complete LOTO on the power from the system at the facility's main disconnect. See the MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
    Notice
    Before powering down the entire Power, Gradient, RF (PGR) cabinet, complete a shutdown of the system software. The Volume Recon Engine (VRE) system in the cabinet can become corrupted if it loses power while software applications are active.
    To prevent fatal electric shock, disconnect the power from the PDU before doing the following procedures.
  2. Apply PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem LOTO. See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.

Installing RF detector boards, broadband (BB) AIF board, and cables

About this task

Board Requirements:
  • Amp IF Board: A quantity of 1 BB Amp IF Board is required for the MNS option, either part 5250032 (non-RoHS) or 6250112 (RoHS).
  • RF Detector Boards: A quantity of 2 RF Detector boards is required for the MNS option. Systems with a Dual Drive RF Amplifier will already have the boards installed. Systems with a Single Drive RF Amplifier will require the installation of two additional RF Detector Boards. It is required and important that the part number of the RF Detector Boards being installed match the part number of the other RF Detector Boards already installed [for narrowband (NB)] in the CAM (or ASC) chassis. If the CAM (or ASC) chassis has RF Detector boards with 5250034, then the RF Detector Boards for MNS option must also be 5250034. If the CAM (or ASC) chassis has RF Detector boards with 6250264, then RF Detector Boards for MNS option must also be 6250264.

Procedure

  1. (For systems with CAM chassis) :
    1. At the front of the PGR cabinet, locate slots 19U and 19L on the UPM chassis. Remove the covers from the blank slots, remove the aluminum blanking insert panel if present, and insert one BB UPM RF detector board into each slot.
    2. Locate slot 17U/18U in the UPM chassis. Remove the covers from the blank slots, remove the aluminum blanking insert panel if present, and insert the BB amp IF board.
    3. Locate the pre-installed white sub-D cable, 5166354 or 5410020, coiled and secured in the right side of the cabinet. Uncoil the cable and attach it to BB AIF board J3 in slot 17U/18U.
  2. (For Systems with ASC chassis)
    1. At the front of the PGR cabinet, locate slots 10 and 13 on the UPM chassis. Remove the covers from the blank slots, remove the aluminum blanking insert panel if present, and insert one BB UPM RF detector board into each slot.
    2. Locate slot 6 in the UPM chassis. Remove the covers from the blank slots, remove the aluminum blanking insert panel if present, and insert the BB amp IF board.
    3. Locate the pre-installed white sub-D cable, 5722895, coiled and secured in the right side of the cabinet. Uncoil the cable and attach it to BB AIF board J3 in slot 6.

Installing MNS power connections

About this task

For systems without a power distribution module (PDM), skip to Step 2.

Procedure

  1. (For systems with PDM) Remove the PDM located in the top right corner of the PGR cabinet:
    1. Remove all cables connected to the front and top of the PDM.
    2. From the top of the PGR cabinet, remove the outer edge screws surrounding the PDM securing this unit to the rails. Remove all the screws from the PDM door.
    3. From the inside of the PDM, with the door open, unscrew the two cables locks (block retaining nuts) from the rear of the PDM, and disconnect the connections from the two terminal blocks. After the cables are disconnected, close the PDM door.
      Note It is important to note the orientation of the connections at the terminal blocks. Label the cables or record where to reconnect these cables later in the procedure.
    4. Slide the PDM out from the front of the PGR cabinet. Set it aside on a flat and safe surface.
  2. (For systems without PDM) Install the MNS breaker box in the top right corner of the PGR cabinet (for systems with PDM, proceed to Step 3):
    1. Remove the blank plate located in the top right corner of the PGR cabinet. Remove six screws from the top side of the cabinet.
    2. Attach the adapter plate to the MNS breaker box with one screw.
    3. Slide the adapter plate into position at the top of the PGR cabinet, secure it with screws (removed earlier from the top side of the cabinet), and attach the MNS breaker box to the right handrail in the PGR cabinet using two screws.
    4. Run the power cable from the MNS breaker box along the top edge of the PGR cabinet, and secure with cable ties as needed.
    5. Route the cable down the left side of the PGR cabinet to the terminal strip. [To access the terminal strip, the RF amplifier and CAM chassis (or ASC chassis in case of ICE configuration) must be rolled out from the PGR cabinet.]
    6. Roll the RF amplifier out from the PGR cabinet.
    7. (For systems with CAM chassis) Roll the CAM chassis out from the PGR cabinet. For details, see CAM chassis replacement.
    8. (For systems with ASC chassis) Roll the ASC chassis out of PGR Cabinet. For details, see Replacing the KASC chassis assembly.
    9. Note It may be necessary to lean over the pulled out modules in the next step. Be careful not to put too much pressure on them.
      With a 5/16 inch socket wrench, connect the five wires from the power cable to the terminal strip as follows:
      • Connect the ground wire to terminal 6.
      • Connect wire labeled “N” (neutral) to terminal 4.
      • Connect the L1, L2, and L3 wires to terminals 1, 2, and 3. (The specific order of these wires is not important.)
      • Install the terminal strip plastic cover.
    10. Make sure that the cables are securely fastened, and return the RF amplifier and the CAM or ASC chassis to their original positions.
  3. (For systems with or without PDM) At the PGR Input/Output (I/O) panel, remove the blank plate from the J204 connection.
  4. Attach cable.
    1. (For systems with CAM chassis) Attach cable 5271096 to J204 on the underside of the I/O panel. Run this cable along the side of the PGR cabinet between the cabinet wall and the recon components. Attach the other end of the cable to the BB AIF board at CAM SLOT 17/18FU J5.
    2. (For systems with ASC chassis) Attach cable 5271096-2 to J204 on the underside of the I/O panel. Run this cable along the side of the PGR cabinet between the cabinet wall and the recon components. Attach the other end of the cable to the BB AIF board at ASC SLOT 6 J5.
  5. Note

    Before attaching the BNC cables (J200-203) to the I/O panel, connect the adapter (46-208990P1) to the BNC connector.

    Attach BNC cables.
    1. (For systems with CAM chassis) Attach the BNC cables to J200-203 connections on the underside of the I/O panel. Run these cables along the side of the PGR cabinet between the cabinet wall and the recon components. Attach the other ends to CAM slot 19FU J4, CAM slot 19FU J5, CAM slot 19FL J4, and CAM slot 19FL J5 .
    2. (For systems with ASC chassis) Attach the BNC cables to J200-203 connections on the underside of the I/O panel. Run these cables along the side of the PGR cabinet between the cabinet wall and the recon components. Attach the other ends to ASC slot 10 J4, ASC slot 10 J5, ASC slot 13 J4, and ASC slot 13 J5 .
  6. Add a 50 ohm terminator (2329754 or equivalent) to any unused port(s) on the RF detector boards.
  7. Tie-wrap the MNS cables that run along the side of PGR cabinet. Make sure the cables are not in the way of other components that may require service.
  8. (For sites with PDM) After all cables are connected properly and tie-wrapped, slide the PDM into the PGR cabinet:
    1. From the top of the PGR cabinet, open the PDM door, and route the power cables through the opening at the rear of the PDM.
    2. From the inside of the PDM, connect the cables and screw on the black retainer nuts to secure the cables.
    3. Close the PDM door, attach the PDM to the rails with the surrounding screws, and screw the PDM door closed.
    4. Connect all cables to the top and front of the PDM as shown in Front of PDM and Top of PDM.

Ferrite installation

About this task

Inside the equipment room, install ferrites on the gradient cables connecting to the gradient filter on the equipment room side of the Secondary Penetration Wall (SPW).

Procedure

  1. Warning
    Projectile hazard
    Ferrites contain ferrous material and will be attracted to the magnet.
    Do not take ferrites into the magnet room. Install ferrites only in the equipment room.
    Open the ferrites using the tool provided (Ferrites may be opened and closed multiple times).
  2. Put one ferrite on each red and black gradient lead and squeeze the ferrite to lock it closed.

    Installation notes:

    • Ferrites fit loosely around the cables and do not require exact placement.
    • Ferrites may be put behind the bottom cover of the pen wall filter.
    • Ferrites do not require mechanical anchoring.
    • Do not put a ferrite around the smaller ground lead.

Topic ID: id_SL4210640-1054743

MNS exciter calibration

About this task

The nuclei that are calibrated by default are 13C and 31P. To guarantee a linear output from the MNS exciter at other frequencies, you can edit a config file to specify which other frequency or frequencies that the site needs calibrated. This config file controls which MNS frequencies are used when calibrating the transmit gain tables of the MNS exciter. To choose frequencies to calibrate, open the config file and change the value for the desired frequencies from 0 to 1. The calibration happens during the next TPS reset or system restart. This calibration uses the loopback path only. No RF is sent to the broadband amp. The system only needs to be configured for MNS with the standard MNS hardware (exciter, cabling, and so on). It is not necessary to connect an MNS TR module during this calibration.

Procedure

  1. Note Each additional frequency calibration will add time to the TPS reset. Calibrate only the nuclei that the site needs; do not calibrate them all.
    In a C Shell, go to the directory /w/config.
    Note The Shell window can only be launched when the EA3 user is included in the authorized EA3 group. Users not in this EA3 group will not have access to launch the Shell window. If you are not logged on as the proper logon user, log off and then log back on as the correct EA3 user with the authorized permissions.
  2. Type gedit mnsTgCal.cfg and Enter.
    The mnsTgCal.cfg file contains a list of available nuclei.
  3. For the nuclei that the site needs calibrated, change the value from 0 to 1.
  4. Save the file.
  5. Type more mnsTgCal.cfg and Enter, and make sure that the changes were saved.
    At the next TPS reset or system restart, the nuclei will be calibrated.
