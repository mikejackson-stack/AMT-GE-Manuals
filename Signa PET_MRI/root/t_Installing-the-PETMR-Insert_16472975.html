Remove the patient table. Store it where it will not interfere with this procedure.

Remove the rear end bell.

Extract and service the PET/MR insert.

--

Procedure

  1. LOTO the PDUs in both the PGR and PET cabinets.
    • See Applying LOTO - PET Power Distribution Unit.
    • See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
  2. Position one person at the service end of the magnet and another person at the patient end of the magnet.
  3. Loosen the screws that secure the PET/MR insert to the insertion tool (1).
  4. Loosen the holding bolts from the interface plate.
  5. Slowly, slide the PET insert into the magnet bore.

    As you slide the insert, make sure that it does not catch on any part of the bore. The most likely items to catch are:

    • The fiber-optic box

    • The DMOD fluid end, including cooling hoses and fiber-optic cables

    • The DMOD itself

    • Any power cable routing

  6. Adjust the installation tool as needed to avoid interference.
    Note

    It will be necessary to adjust the PET insert height on the insertion tool and/or the adjustment jack screw on the magnet interface place.

  7. At the service end, attach the PET/MR insert to the L-brackets.
    1. Tighten the M10 bolts (1) on the inside of the L-brackets first.
    2. Tighten the M10 bolts (2) on the outside of the L-brackets second.
  8. At the patient end, attach the two L-brackets (1) of the PET/MR insert (2) to the carbon mount (3).
  9. Perform the PET/MR insert mechanical alignment.
  10. Disconnect the service extension hoses, and attach the cooling hoses from the PET chiller to the manifold hoses with the quick disconnects.
  11. Connect the two I and Q RF transmit cables at the service end of the insert.
  12. Connect the air hoses and the temperature sensor at the patient end of the magnet.
  13. Install the leak sensor (2) at the service end of the insert.
    1. The fiber optic cables (1) should already have been reconnected.
    2. Slide and rotate the leak sensor to clear the fiber-optic breakout boxes.
  14. Install the DMOD covers (2) and screws (1) on the DMOD cable trays.
  15. Confirm that all CPDA cables are fastened in order on both sides of the magnet bore, and that the three clear shields are attached with their captive screws.

Finalization

  1. Remove LOTO from the PDUs in the PET and PGR cabinets, and turn the PET chiller on.
    1. See Removing LOTO - PET Power Distribution Unit.
    2. See Removing LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
  2. Place the PROP circuit breaker to the on position.
  3. Remove the PET/MR insert installation tool.
  4. Reinstall the front end bell.
  5. Reinstall the rear end bell.
  6. Reinstall the rear pedestal.
  7. Reinstall the magnet covers.
  8. Redock the patient table.
  9. Perform these calibration and functional checks:

    • PET Daily Quality Assurance (DQA) check

    • If PET DQA check fails, perform PET detector calibration.
