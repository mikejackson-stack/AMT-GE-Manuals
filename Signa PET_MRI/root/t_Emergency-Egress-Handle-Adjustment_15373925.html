Cradle Cover Removal

Procedure

  1. Notice
    The number in parentheses within an instruction corresponds to the numbered item in the referenced illustration.
    Undock the patient table and move it out of the magnet room.
  2. On the rear cradle cover, remove the four plastic M5 x 8 mm Phillips screws (two each in the front and rear) and lift the cover up and off.
  3. On the front cradle cover, remove the four plastic M5 x 8 mm Phillips screws (two each in the front and rear) and lift the cover up and off.
Topic ID: id_SL15379238-1343802

Cord Adjustment

Procedure

  1. Locate the spool (1) and top block (2).
  2. Align the slot of the spool (1) with the mark on the spool top block (2) as shown.
  3. Insert a flat-blade screwdriver into the slot of the spool (1) to prevent the spool from turning and simultaneously push and hold the release block (2) all the way down.
  4. At the handle end of the table, have an assistant carefully loosen the M6 x 12 mm brass button-head cap screw (1) and M6 anti-rotation brass washer (2).
    Note

    The end of the Kevlar cord may be tied in a knot. This knot is a precaution against accidentally losing the cord inside the table. Do not untie this knot. The cord end is extremely difficult to retrieve from inside of the table if it becomes disconnected.

  5. Pull the Kevlar cord until all the slack is removed where the cord enters the block.
  6. With an assistant holding the release block down, pull the Kevlar cord snug, and wrap it clockwise one turn (do not overlap) behind the M6 anti-rotation brass washer around the M6 x 12 mm brass button-head cap screw.
  7. Hand tighten the M6 x 12 mm brass button-head cap screw with the short end of an Allen wrench until pressure is felt, then turn the Allen wrench an additional 170 to 180°.
  8. With an assistant holding the release block down, adjust the M4 screw (1) to remove any slack in the cord.
  9. Tighten the lock nut.
  10. Return the patient table to the magnet room and dock it. Pull the emergency egress handle to confirm functionality.
    1. If the handle does not function properly, return to Step 2 to adjust cord length. Continue the procedure from that point.
    2. If the handle functions properly, replace and secure both cradle covers with the plastic M5 Phillips screws removed earlier.
