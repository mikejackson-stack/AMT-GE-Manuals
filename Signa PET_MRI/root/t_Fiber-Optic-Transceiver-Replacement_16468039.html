System Preparation

Procedure

  1. Turn off the PROP circuit breaker to remove power to the photon reconstruction organization processor (PROP), which turns off the low voltage power supply (LVPS) and the 80 volt power supply (80V PS).
  2. Press the On/Off button to turn the PET chiller off, and place the chiller breaker (CB12) in the off position.
  3. LOTO the power distribution unit (PDU) in the PET and PGR cabinets. See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
  4. Complete the remaining prerequisite steps for fiber-optic transceiver replacement identified in PET Insert Maintenance Matrix.
Topic ID: id_SL15609893-1350414

Fiber-Optic Transceiver Replacement

Procedure

  1. Tighten the screws to secure the PET/MR insert to the insertion tool.
  2. Notice
    Potential component damage
    Electrostatic discharge (ESD) can cause permanent damage to electronic components.
    Observe the following ESD precautions:
    • Work on a static-free mat.
    • Wear a static strap to make sure that any accumulated electrostatic charge is discharged from your body to the ground.
    • Create a common ground for the equipment being worked on by connecting the static-free mat, static strap, and peripheral units to that piece of equipment.
    • For more information on ESD protection, see MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
    Remove the fiber-optic cable from the transceiver. See Fiber Optic Cable Devices Installation and Replacement (SFP) for proper handling of fiber-optic devices.
    Note
    The clip on this cable faces the insert. To release the clip, position a strap or plastic tie wrap under the clip and pull up to release before removing the cable. You may also remove the DMOD, although this is not required.
  3. Depress the transceiver clip and remove the transceiver from the DMOD.
  4. Insert the new fiber-optic transceiver and reconnect the fiber-optic cable.

    The cable should click twice. Gently push and pull on the connection to confirm that it is secure.

Topic ID: id_15207526

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Confirm DMOD connectivity before reinstalling the PET/MR insert. See DMOD Connectivity Troubleshooting.
  2. Proceed with PET/MR insert replacement and the finalization steps identified in PET Insert Maintenance Matrix.
