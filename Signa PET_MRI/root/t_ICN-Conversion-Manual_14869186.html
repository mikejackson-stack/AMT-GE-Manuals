Replacing ICN 4170 with ICN 4100

Topic ID: id_SL3648521-1103493

Removing ICN 4170

Procedure

  1. Locate the ICN 4170 in the PGR cabinet.
    Note

    To open the PGR cabinet, turn the key to the right. The cabinet handle pops out. Turn the handle bar to the left and the cabinet door opens.

  2. Power down the host.
  3. Apply PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem LOTO. See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
  4. Loosen the thumbscrews on each side of the ICN.
  5. Slide out the ICN until the stop is reached at the end of the side rails.
  6. Remove all the connections at the back of the ICN.
  7. CAUTION
    Possible personal injury!
    The 4170 ICN weighs approximately 40 pounds and requires two people to lift it.
    Locate the two release tabs on the slide rails. Press both release tabs toward the center of the ICN while pulling the ICN forward until the rails release and the ICN is free. Place the ICN to the side in a safe area.
  8. Loosen the network switch and slide it down to access the mounting screws.
  9. Locate and remove the four mounting screws for each side-mount bracket.
Topic ID: id_SL3648522-1103493

Installing ICN 4100

Procedure

  1. Attach slide rails to the mounting brackets using 3 screws each.
  2. Locate the placement for the mounting bracket in the cabinet. Mount brackets for installation of ICN 4100 units. Be sure to orient the brackets with the slot on the bottom.
  3. Attach trays to mounting brackets as shown below.
  4. CAUTION
    Possible personal injury!
    The 4100 ICN weighs approximately 40 pounds and requires two people to lift it.
    Insert ICN 4100 units into the PGR cabinet. Slide the ICNs out until the stop is reached at the end of the slide rails. Make sure the release tabs engage and the ICNs are locked into the rails.
  5. Connect all cables listed in ICN 4100 Wiring Matrix for Infiniband. Correct Cable Orientation shows the orientation of cables when looking at the rear of the ICN. Green indicates ports that should have cable inputs; red indicates ports that should NOT have cable inputs.
    Note

    Ethernet connection should go to Net MGT, Eth 2, Eth 3 ports.

    Table 2. ICN 4100 Wiring Matrix for Infiniband
    Connection Item ICN 4170 ICN 4100
    32-Channel 16-Channel 32-Channel 16-Channel
    Power Cords2242
    Ethernet3363
    Duplex - Fiber Optic2121
    Single Fiber Optic2121
    Infiniband - JumperN/AN/A1N/A
    Fiber Optic - JumperN/AN/A1N/A
  6. Press the release tabs and push the ICNs into the PGR cabinet until the ICNs are completely inside.
  7. Tighten the thumbscrews on the front of the ICNs to secure.
Topic ID: id_SL3652094-1103493

Initializing ICN 4100

Procedure

  1. Remove PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem LOTO. See Removing LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
  2. Power on the host.
    Note

    For the first 3 or 4 minutes, LEDs turn on only at the rear of the ICN, next to the power cables. After 3 to 4 minutes, you will hear the ICN fans come on and then the LED at the front of the ICN starts flashing.

  3. Turn on the ICN per ICN On/Off Procedure.
  4. Configure the new ICN. See Configuring the SIP, ICN, or VRE.

Replacing ICN 4100 with ICN 4170

Topic ID: id_SL3652143-1103493

Removing ICN 4100

Procedure

  1. Locate the ICN 4100 in the PGR cabinet.
    Note

    To open the PGR cabinet, turn the key to the right. The cabinet handle pops out. Turn the handle bar to the left and the cabinet door opens.

  2. Power down the host.
  3. Apply PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem LOTO. See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
  4. Loosen the thumbscrews on each side of the ICN(s).
  5. Slide out the ICN(s) until the stop is reached at the end of the side rails.
  6. Remove all the connections at the back of the ICN(s).
  7. CAUTION
    Possible personal injury!
    The 4100 ICN weighs approximately 40 pounds and requires two people to lift it.
    Locate the two release tabs on the slide rails. Press both release tabs toward the center of the ICN while pulling the ICN forward until the rails release and the ICN is free. Place the ICN to the side in a safe area.
  8. Remove the ICN trays from the ICN mounting brackets.
  9. Remove the three screws securing each mounting bracket for the ICN.
Topic ID: id_SL3652144-1103493

Installing ICN 4170

Procedure

  1. Loosen the mounting screws for the network switch and slide the switch down to give access to the mounting location for the slide rails for the ICN 4170.
  2. Attach the slide rails for the ICN 4170 module to the mounting rails in the PGR cabinet using four screws each.
  3. CAUTION
    Possible personal injury!
    The 4170 ICN weighs approximately 40 pounds and requires two people to lift it.
    Insert the ICN 4170 unit into the PGR cabinet. Slide the ICNs out until the stop is reached at the end of the slide rails. Make sure the release tabs engage and the ICNs are locked into the rails.
  4. Connect all cables listed in ICN Wiring Matrix for Infiniband. Correct Cable Orientation shows the orientation of cables when looking at the rear of the ICN. Green indicates ports that should have cable inputs; red indicates ports that should NOT have cable inputs.
    Note

    Ethernet connection should go to Net MGT, Eth 2, Eth 3 ports.

    Table 3. ICN Wiring Matrix for Infiniband
    Connection Item ICN 4170 ICN 4100
    32-Channel 16-Channel 32-Channel 16-Channel
    Power Cords2242
    Ethernet3363
    Duplex - Fiber Optic2121
    Single Fiber Optic2121
    Infiniband - JumperN/AN/A1N/A
    Fiber Optic - JumperN/AN/A1N/A
  5. Press the rail release tabs and push the ICN module into the PGR cabinet until the ICN is completely inside.
  6. Tighten the thumbscrews on the front of the ICN to secure it to the cabinet mounting rails.
  7. Move the network switch back into position and secure it with its mounting screws.
Topic ID: id_SL3652162-1103493

Initializing ICN 4170

Procedure

  1. Remove PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem LOTO. See Removing LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
  2. Power on the host.
    Note

    For the first 3 or 4 minutes, LEDs come on only at the rear of the ICN, next to the power cables. After 3 or 4 minutes, the ICN fans come on and then the LED at the front of the ICN starts flashing.

  3. Turn on the ICN per ICN On/Off Procedure.
  4. Configure the new ICN. See Configuring the SIP, ICN, or VRE.
  5. Perform a software load. See Loading Host System Software (Quick Start).
    Note

    This upgrade requires at minimum 22.x software to be loaded. Make sure to obtain the latest version of the software.

    Sites installing 22.x for the first time must download any new option keys from the eLicense web site in order to ensure access to all system tools.
