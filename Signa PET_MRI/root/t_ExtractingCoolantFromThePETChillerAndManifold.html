Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

DANGER
Electrocution hazard
Dangerous voltages are present around the PET chiller.
Before servicing, do LOTO on the PET PDU. See MR Service Safety Manual, (5452735).

Procedure

  1. Retrieve the manual pump from the Heat Exchanger Cabinet (HEC).
  2. Remove power from the PET detectors. Turn the four switches on the Low Voltage Power Supply (LVPS) in the PET cabinet to the Off position.
  3. Press the On/Off button on the front of the PET chiller to turn the chiller off.
    Notice
    Do not power down the PET chiller with the circuit breaker on the back.
  4. Get the plastic container that will hold the coolant that drains from the chiller. Put the container under the drain.
  5. Set the PET chiller flow valve to the fully closed (horizontal) position.
  6. Set the three-way External Flow Control (EFC) valve to position 2, which is described in the following table. The EFC valve is on the back of the PET chiller.
    EFC valve positionDescription
    1The fluid flows from the PET chiller outlet to the PET detector hose loop.
    2The fluid flows from the PET chiller outlet to the PET chiller recirculation loop to the PET chiller inlet.
  7. Press and hold the enter button to enable the Maintenance mode, which turns the PET chiller back on.
  8. Use the up or down arrow to highlight SETTINGS on the menu and press enter.
  9. Use the down arrow to highlight Draining and press enter.
  10. Press enter twice to open the drain valve on the PET chiller.
  11. Put both inline shut-off valves on the supply and return hoses in the closed position.
  12. Using either a 10 mm open-end or adjustable wrench, remove the brass plug from the drain as shown in Drain at the back of the chiller.
    Note If only one container is used for coolant extraction, screw the plug back into the drain while emptying the container. Remove the plug again to continue the coolant extraction.
    Notice
    Follow local regulations and customer procedures for proper disposal of coolant.
  13. Wait until all of the fluid drains from the chiller (approximately 2 gallons), and replace the plug in the drain.
    Note Check the coolant window at the front of the chiller to determine the rate of coolant extraction. Be prepared to wipe any coolant leakage with a paper towel.
    Notice
    Chiller coolant is blue in color and can potentially stain any surface it contacts.
  14. Apply PFTE tape to the threads on the drain plug to prevent leaks.
  15. Reinstall the brass drain plug, using either a 10 mm open-end or adjustable wrench.
  16. Get the plastic pail and remove both the supply and return hoses from the PET chiller. Put the gray return hose into the plastic pail to drain the coolant from the system.
  17. Find the adaptor for the HEC pump at the back of the chiller.
  18. Attach the adaptor to the HEC manual pump, and attach the manual pump adaptor to the yellow supply hose.
  19. Open both inline shut-off valves as shown.
  20. Pump until approximately 90 to 95 percent of the coolant (5 to 8 gallons) is removed. Stop the pump when coolant spits from the hose.
  21. Close the two inline shut-off valves on the supply and return hoses as shown in Inline shut-off valves closed.
  22. Remove the manual pump from the supply hose, and reconnect both the yellow and gray hoses to the PET chiller.
  23. Follow local regulations and customer procedures for proper disposal of the coolant.
    CAUTION
    Possible equipment damage
    Without coolant in the reservoir, serious damage to the chiller could result.
    Do not turn on the chiller at this point.

Finalization

  1. To refill the PET chiller, see the Priming the PET chiller section of the Adding coolant and priming the PET chiller procedure.
  2. To restore power to the PET detectors, turn the four switches on the LVPS to the On position. The LVPS is in the PET cabinet. See LVPS switches.
  3. Do a PET/MR Hello Goodbye scan. See PET-MR Hello-Goodbye Scan.
