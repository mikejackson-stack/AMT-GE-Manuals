Installing the Waveguide sub-panel

Prerequisites

The original waveguide sub-panel must be removed and replaced with the custom waveguide to allow cabling into the magnet room.

DANGER
Fatal electric shock hazard
The PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU) or gradient cabinet has lethal voltages.
Perform a system shutdown, and then perform LOTO on the main PDU breaker and the penetration cabinet. The capacitors may have 800 volts. Wait at least five minutes after LOTO for the capacitors to drain before continuing this procedure.
DANGER
Fatal Electric Shock Hazard
Coldhead (cryocooler compressor) presents a shock hazard.
Turn off and perform LOTO on the coldhead before continuing this procedure. See the MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).

Procedure

  1. Perform LOTO on the PGR and penetration cabinets. See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem .
  2. Perform LOTO on the Heat Exchanger Cabinet (HEC) to remove power from the cryocooler compressor, which is necessary to complete this procedure. See Applying LOTO - Heat Exchanger Cabinet (HEC).
  3. Install the custom waveguide panel (2394034-10) in place of the standard panel.
    Note If the waveguide panel has preexisting (custom) modifications, they must be reapplied to the sub-panel before BrainWave can be installed.
