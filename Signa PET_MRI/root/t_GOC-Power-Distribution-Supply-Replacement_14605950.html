Powering Off Host and GOCAA

Topic ID: id_SL1890300-1057080

Shut Down Software

Procedure

  1. Click the Tools icon.
  2. On the Service Desktop Manager, click System Shutdown.
  3. Confirm the shutdown by clicking OK.
  4. Wait for the system to indicate on the monitor that it is safe to power off the computer before proceeding. It usually takes about 90 seconds for this message to display.
    Note

    Watch the screen and wait until the light turns yellow.

Topic ID: id_SL1890305-1057080

Remove Power to Operator Workspace

Procedure

  1. Verify that the power indicator on the Operator Workspace is off.
  2. Host circuit breakers are located on the front of the PDU.
Topic ID: id_SL1890311-1057080

GOC Power Distribution Supply Replacement

Procedure

  1. Shut down system applications. Perform LOTO on the GOC before continuing. See Applying LOTO - Global Operator Cabinet (GOC) and Operator Workspace.
    Note

    Take extra precaution if the site has installed the optional GOC UPS accessory.

  2. Remove the screws that secure the left and right side panels of the GOC.
  3. Disconnect the ground leads from both side panels.
  4. Remove both GOC side panels and set aside.
  5. Remove Strain Relief Plate (shown below) from the power cable.
  6. Disconnect the power cable and connector from the GOC power distribution supply, and remove the retaining screw from the plate.
  7. Remove two screws from the side of the GOC power distribution supply, and disconnect the AC power cables from the PDU.
  8. Remove the two screws at the top of the plate, and lift the plate up to remove.
  9. Carefully slide the GOC power distribution supply out of the GOC.
  10. Insert the replacement GOC power distribution supply. Reverse the preceding steps to properly install the replacement.
Topic ID: id_15210556

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Remove LOTO from the GOC power distribution supply. See Removing LOTO - Global Operator Cabinet (GOC) and Operator Workspace.
  2. Turn the host and PC circuit breakers on the PDU back to the On position.
  3. Start the host computer by pressing the On button, and log in.
    Note

    It is possible that the customer changed the default password. If you cannot log in, contact the customer for the correct password.

  4. When the system is fully booted, perform a scan to ensure proper operation of the system.
