Table 3. Safety
CAUTION
Possible Equipment Damage
The cooling fan is off only when the Host PC is turned off or the power cable is disconnected. If the power cord is not unplugged when installing the NIC, the card may be damaged or the system will not recognize the change.
Disconnect the power cord from the power source before opening the Host PC to avoid damage to a board or component.
Notice
Potential component damage
Electrostatic discharge (ESD) can cause permanent damage to electronic components.
Observe the following ESD precautions:
  • Work on a static-free mat.
  • Wear a static strap to make sure that any accumulated electrostatic charge is discharged from your body to the ground.
  • Create a common ground for the equipment being worked on by connecting the static-free mat, static strap, and peripheral units to that piece of equipment.
  • For more information on ESD protection, see MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).

About this task

Overview

This procedure details replacing or swapping the dual port Hewlett-Packard (HP) Network Interface Card (NIC) in a HP Z400 computer.

Procedure

  1. Shut down all system applications, and power down the GOC.
  2. Apply GOC LOTO. See Applying LOTO - Global Operator Cabinet (GOC) and Operator Workspace.
  3. Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the two screws (1) from the left side panel of the GOC, and set the screws aside; carefully lift the panel up and off to avoid straining the ground lead.
  4. Disconnect the ground lead that connects the side panel to the GOC main chassis at the center of the lead.
  5. Remove all cables connected to the computer, ensure they are properly labeled, and note their locations for reattachment.
  6. To loosen and remove the strap securing the computer:
    1. Press the release lever (1) on the buckle of the strap.
    2. Remove the hook (2) from the base of the computer.
  7. To remove the computer:
    1. Remove the two screws (1) securing the back bracket.
    2. Loosen the two screws securing the plate at the back (2), and slide the plate in the direction of the arrow (3).
    3. Loosen the two screws securing the plate on the side (4), and slide the plate in the direction of the arrow (5).
    4. Notice
      The computer weighs 50 lb. (22.7 kg), which requires two people to remove it from the GOC.
      Carefully remove the computer using the back and bottom brackets from the GOC, and position it on its side as shown.
  8. To access the inside of the computer:
    1. Remove the two screws (1) securing the left cover at the back of the computer, and set them aside.
    2. Pull out on the handle (2), slide the cover back (3), and set it aside in a safe location.
  9. Locate the installed dual port NIC between the two NVIDIA® cards in the Peripheral Component Interconnect (PCI) Slot 3 (1).
  10. Disconnect and remove the Desktop and mobile Architecture for System Hardware (DASH) Cable that connects the mother board and the NIC, and set it aside.
  11. Press down on both green levers, one on each side of the retention clamp, to release it.
  12. Gently pull the existing NIC straight up from the motherboard to remove it.
  13. Firmly seat the new dual port NIC into the open slot of the HP Z400 computer.
  14. Close the retention clamp by pressing down on it until the green levers click into place.
  15. Align the bottom groove of the left side panel with the bottom edge of the GOC chassis, secure with the two Phillips screws removed earlier, and reattach the ground lead as shown in Ground Lead.
  16. To replace the left cover on the HP Z400 computer, refer to Removing HP Z400 Left Cover.
    1. Slide the panel forward until it engages the top cover.
    2. Push the handle in to secure the cover with the latch.
    3. Secure the left cover with the two screws removed earlier.
  17. Reconnect the Ethernet cables to the computer as follows:
    Note

    The number in parentheses corresponds to the location of each in the illustration below.

    • ETH1 (1) = VRE Onboard Adapter

    • ETHO (2) = Hospital Internet

    • ETH2 (3) = PET Cabinet

  18. Connect one end of the Signal Cable to the dual port NIC in the HP Z400, and the other end to J6 on the I/O panel at the top of the PET Cabinet.
  19. Remove GOC LOTO. See Removing LOTO - Global Operator Cabinet (GOC) and Operator Workspace.
  20. Reconnect the power cord, and boot the Host PC.

Finalization

  1. Using Guided Install, obtain the Host ID for the NIC and create a new license explained in Generating licenses on eLicense portalCreate Licenses.
  2. Ensure the System Readiness Monitor is green, and perform PET Daily Quality Assurance (DQA) check to verify system operation.
