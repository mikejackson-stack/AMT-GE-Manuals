About this task

Overview

The dock contains three connector plates that pass data from the patient table to the Radio Frequency (RF) hub. Signals flow from the table to the system through several cables with connecting points. The most important connection is where the patient table connects to the magnet via the dock. This document describes replacing the dock side of this connection. When the patient table and dock are connected, data travels between two types of connectors: Coaxial for the RF signal and Pin/Socket for control.

Topic ID: id_SL13474323-1184686

Dock and Connector Removal

Procedure

  1. Disconnect the dock switch connector and the two cables from the Radio Frequency (RF) Hub on the rear pedestal.
  2. Notice
    Before removing the dock from its position, use pieces of tape to mark the points on the floor to replace it.
    Remove the dock and take it out of the Magnet Room. See Replacing the dock .
  3. With the dock outside of the Magnet Room, use a 3/4 in. wrench to remove the four bolts (two on each side) securing the dock cover to the dock base and remove the cover.
    Note

    The illustration below shows two bolts already removed from the left side of the dock.

  4. Lay a cloth on the floor, and turn the dock cover upside down, taking care not to scratch the top of the cover.
  5. Remove the two Allen screws (1) from the P4 connector, cut the cable tie securing the P4 dock harness (shown in Cable Tie on P4 Dock Harness ), and remove the P4 connector from the dock connector plate (2).
  6. To remove the P4 dock harness, route it through the opening inside the dock cover.
Topic ID: id_SL13474324-1184686

Dock and Connector Installation

Procedure

  1. Reroute the replacement P4 dock harness through the opening in the dock cover.
  2. Reattach the P4 connector to the dock connector plate.
  3. Secure the P4 harness with a new cable tie.
  4. Carefully turn the dock cover right side up.
  5. Reconnect the dock switch connector.
  6. Replace the dock cover on the dock base, and secure it with the four bolts removed earlier.
  7. Return the dock to the Magnet Room, and reinstall it using the tape that marked the location of the dock when it was removed. SeeReplacing the dock.
  8. Reconnect the two cables to the RF Hub on the rear pedestal.

Finalization

Finalization

  1. Dock the table and verify:

    • Proper up and down motion of table.

    • Table is level with bridge when fully raised.

    • LPCA automatically drives out to engage cradle when table is raised to full height.

    • LPCA and cradle are advanced normally into the bore when table is raised to full height.

    • Retraction of LPCA into the bridge when table is lowered.

    • Normal LPCA and cradle motion from Home position to end-of-travel on the bridge using In Fast and Out Fast buttons on the Operator Console

    • Emergency release and side latches work properly.

  2. Run the Doing the coil datapath diagnostic (RRx) on all P and A connectors to validate a successful cable signal flow.
