Aligning dual tables

Align two tables dedicated to one MR suite.

Safety
Warning
Strong magnetic field
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field that the magnet produces.
Do not bring any ferrous tools or equipment into the magnet room.

About this task

This procedure is for the alignment of two tables dedicated to one MR suite. You may need to adjust all four wheels on both tables.

Procedure

  1. Drive the LPCA through the magnet to the end of the rear pedestal.
  2. Undock the patient table and move to an open work area.
  3. Remove all lower table covers from all patient tables adjusted in this procedure (see Fastening the patient table upper bellows and removing the lower base side covers).
  4. Make sure of the following:
    1. Bridge height is 582 to 585 mm.
    2. Bridge is centered left/right and in/out.
    3. Bridge is level (left/right, front/back).
  5. Warning
    Ferrous material hazard
    The dock contains ferrous materials and can be pulled into the magnet bore if securing hardware is removed.
    Only loosen the hardware sufficiently to do adjustments in this procedure. Make sure the dock remains secured to prevent it from being pulled into the magnet bore.
    At the rear of the dock, loosen the top and bottom dock bracket nuts (two on each side).
  6. Get the table removed earlier (first table) and do a check of table alignment/levelness with the bridge to make sure of smooth travel. Adjust the height as needed according to Leveling the patient table.
  7. Adjust left/right according to Bridge alignments, dock centering, and adjustments).
  8. Mark the proper dock alignment (left/right) of the first table with a piece of tape.
  9. Undock the first table and get the second table.
  10. Dock the second table and repeat Step 6 and the dock hook centering procedure from Step 7, but do not do the dock centering.
  11. Mark the proper dock alignment (left/right) of the second table with a second piece of tape.
    1. Mark the center point between the two marks.
    2. Shift the dock to align with the center point mark.
  12. Secure the dock by tightening the dock bracket nuts at the rear of the dock. See Dock bracket nuts.

Finalization

  1. Dock each table, move the cradle into the bore, and make sure of smooth table travel.
  2. If necessary, adjust the table height (see Adjusting the table top height).
  3. Install all lower table covers from all patient tables adjusted in this procedure (see Installing the patient table lower base side covers and upper bellows).
