Electrostatic discharge (ESD) can cause permanent damage to electronic components.
Observe the following ESD precautions:
Wear a static wrist strap to ensure that any accumulated electrostatic charge is discharged from your body to the ground.
Create a common ground for the equipment being worked on by connecting the static-free mat, static wrist strap, and peripheral units to that piece of equipment.
Notice
Before powering down the entire CAM chassis, safely power down the ICNs from the VRE power control to prevent corruption of the ICN(s).

About this task

The Combined ASC/MGD (CAM) chassis includes the Multi-Generational Data (MGD) acquisition components and the Amplifier Support Chassis (ASC) components.

The MGD portion of the chassis contains the circuit boards for the pulse generation, system control, and DVMR fiber-optic link communication subsystems. These boards can be easily accessed from the front and rear of the chassis.

The ASC portion of the chassis contains the circuit boards for the amplifier and Universal Power Monitor (UPM) subsystems. These boards can be easily accessed from the front of the chassis.

The power supply modules located in the front of the chassis supply both the MGD and ASC components.

The following removal and insertion procedures are applicable for all the types of circuit boards located within the card rack. The following topics are found in this document:

Power down CAM chassis

About this task

Before powering down the entire CAM chassis, power down the ICNs from the VRE power control. The VRE system can become corrupted if it loses power when the application software is active.

Procedure

  1. At the host computer, open the Service Browser and select Utilities > Tool Box > VRE > Power Control.
  2. Select Power Off VRE.
  3. Apply PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem LOTO. See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.

Circuit board removal procedure

Procedure

  1. Attach an ESD grounding wrist strap.
  2. Remove the cables (PCI card, 10/100 BaseT, Com1, etc.) from the face of the board.
  3. Loosen the faceplate holding screws. These screws are captive to the faceplate and do not need to be completely removed.
    Note If the board is double wide, there are four faceplate holding screws to remove (SRF/TRF board and power supply boards).
  4. Using your thumb, push in the grey release tabs, and gently pull the extractor tabs located on the top and bottom of the circuit board. The lever action of the ejector disengages the circuit board connections from the chassis.
    Note The AGP and SCP boards do not have release tabs.
  5. Being careful not to bend or bind the card in any way, slide the circuit board all the way out of the card cage.
    Note Handle the circuit boards only by the faceplate, ejector, and board edges.
  6. Place the card in an appropriate shipping package, or discard if applicable.
Topic ID: id_SL1959230-1124338

Circuit board replacement

Procedure

  1. Remove the packing material from the replacement board, and gently slide the circuit board along the slot guides into the chassis.
  2. DANGER
    Possible equipment damage!
    Exercise extreme caution when removing and replacing circuit boards.
    All boards that have female connectors (shown in Board connections) must be carefully disengaged, and re-engaged to the male pins located on the backplane chassis. If any of the pins are damaged, the entire chassis needs to be replaced.
    Gently engage the circuit board connectors with the backplane.
    1. If the circuit board is not properly engaging the backplane, remove the board and check the connectors for bent or broken pins.
    2. After the board ejectors contact the CAM chassis faceplate, gently push the ejectors into the center of the board. This causes the ejectors to insert the board into the chassis. (Do not use anything other than the ejectors to insert or remove the board from the chassis.)
  3. Tighten, but do not overtighten, the faceplate holding screws to secure the circuit board to the card rack.
  4. Where applicable, reattach the cables to the face of the board.

Circuit board removal

Procedure

  1. Attach an ESD grounding wrist strap.
  2. Where applicable, remove the cables (sub D, SMB, etc.) from the face of the board.
  3. Loosen the faceplate holding screws. These screws are captive to the faceplate, and do not need to be completely removed.
  4. Using your thumb, push in the gray release tab, and gently pull the extractor tab located on the bottom of the circuit board. The lever action of the ejector disengages the circuit board connections from the chassis.
  5. Notice
    When replacing the detector board, the 50 ohm terminators must be taken from the old board and placed on the new board.
    Being careful not to bend or bind the card in any way, slide the circuit board all the way out of the card cage.
    Note Handle the circuit boards only by the faceplate, ejectors, and board edges.
  6. Place the card in an appropriate shipping package, or discard if applicable.

Circuit board replacement

Procedure

  1. Remove the packing material from the replacement board, and gently slide the circuit board along the slot guides into the chassis.
  2. Gently engage the circuit board connectors with the backplane.
    1. If the circuit board is not properly engaging the backplane, remove the board and check the connectors for bent or broken pins.
    2. After the board ejectors contact the CAM chassis faceplate, gently push the ejector into the center of the board. This causes the ejector to insert the board into the chassis. (Do not use anything other than the ejector to insert or remove the board from the chassis.)
  3. Tighten, but do not overtighten, the faceplate holding screws to secure the circuit board to the card rack.
  4. Where applicable, reattach the cables and terminators to the face of the board.

Power supply module removal/replacement

Topic ID: id_SL1959258-1124338

Power down CAM chassis

About this task

Before powering down the entire CAM chassis, power down the ICNs from the VRE power control. The VRE system can become corrupted if it loses power when the applications software is active.

Procedure

  1. Note Beginning in August 2009, the CAM chassis has a one power supply configuration in slots 14/15. Functionality is maintained, and the empty slot is covered with a front panel airflow blocker. If a new CAM chassis FRU is ordered, one power supply (slots 14/15) and an additional front panel and airflow blocker (slots 12/13) are installed in the new CAM unit.
    At the host computer, open the Service Browser and select Utilities > Tool Box > VRE > Power Control.
  2. Select Power Off VRE.
  3. Apply PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem LOTO. See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
Topic ID: id_SL1959262-1124338

Power supply module removal

Procedure

  1. Attach an ESD grounding wrist strap.
  2. Loosen the two faceplate holding screws at the top and bottom of the faceplate. These screws are captive to the faceplate, and do not need to be completely removed.
  3. Using your thumb, push in and gently pull the extractor tab located on the circuit board. The lever action of the ejector disengages the supply module connections from the chassis.
  4. Slide the power supply module all the way out of the card cage.
    Note Handle the modules only by the faceplate, ejectors, and board edges.
  5. Place the module in an appropriate shipping package.

Power supply module replacement

Procedure

  1. Remove the packing material from the replacement module, and gently slide the module into the chassis.
  2. Use the ejectors to insert the module into the chassis.
  3. Tighten, but do not overtighten, the faceplate holding screws to secure the power supply module to the card rack.

Fan blower removal/replacement

About this task

Before powering down the entire CAM chassis, power down the ICNs from the VRE power control. The VRE system can become corrupted if it loses power when the applications software is active.

Procedure

  1. At the host computer, open the Service Browser and select Utilities > Tool Box > VRE > Power Control.
  2. Select Power Off VRE.
  3. Apply PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem LOTO. See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
  4. On the front of the CAM chassis are two fan blower assemblies (each is held in place with two thumb screws). Loosen the two thumb screws securing the fan assembly that needs to be replaced.
  5. Pull out the defective fan(s).
    Note The fan is 'plug and play' with no cables for ease of removal or insertion.
  6. Slide the new fan into the same slot from which the defective fan was removed, and push it back into the slot until snug.
    Note The connector of the fan automatically aligns itself to the plug of the chassis.
  7. Tighten the two thumb screws to secure the fan assembly.
  8. Restore power to the CAM chassis.
  9. Refer to Finalization for checkout and proper operation.

CAM chassis removal

Procedure

  1. Attach an ESD grounding wrist strap.
  2. Remove all the circuit boards and store them in a static-safe location. (Refer to Circuit board removal procedure and Circuit board removal for removal instructions.)
  3. Remove the power supply modules. (Refer to Power supply module removal for removal instructions.)
  4. Remove the six screws that hold the CAM chassis to the cabinet frame.
  5. Disconnect all cables.
  6. Slide the CAM chassis out of the PGR cabinet.
  7. Remove the top tray on the CAM chassis by loosening the four screws.
  8. Disengage the slide rails, and safely place the CAM chassis on a flat surface.
  9. Remove the slide rails and the four screws to remove the bottom tray.
  10. Install the slide rails and bottom tray on the new chassis.
    Note Ensure that the switch on the back of the CAM chassis is ON.
Topic ID: id_SL1959399-1124338

CAM chassis installation

Procedure

  1. Attach an ESD grounding wrist strap.
  2. Slide the CAM chassis into the PGR cabinet.
  3. Install the top tray with the term server, and reconnect the cables.
  4. Replace the six screws that hold the CAM chassis to the cabinet frame.
  5. Install all boards. See Circuit board replacement and Circuit board replacement.
  6. Replace the power supply modules. See Power supply module replacement.
  7. Reattach all cables.

Finalization

Procedure

  1. If one of the narrow and (NB) or broad band (BB) interface/detector boards or the UPM processor board has been replaced or swapped, run UPM Calibration and UPM Functional Check – Body Mode and UPM Calibration and UPM Functional Check – Head Mode.
  2. Run a single iteration of Quick Diagnostics (15 minutes) to verify operations.
  3. Run a goodbye scan. See Doing a check scan.
