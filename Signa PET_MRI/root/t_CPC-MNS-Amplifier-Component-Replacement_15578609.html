Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

DANGER
Electrocution hazard
Hazardous voltage is present when the cabinet is energized.
Before beginning any work, do LOTO. See the MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
Warning
Possible personal injury or equipment damage
This unit could fall when using the hoist tool.
Before putting weight on the hoist chain, be sure that the links are aligned properly. This helps keep the chain from binding up or becoming kinked, which is nearly impossible to correct when the chain is under tension.
Warning
Possible personal injury
Lifting MR parts, such as FRUs, toolkits and parts of the system without assistance (mechanical or additional personnel), can result in personal injury.
The use of mechanical lift assistance devices or extra personnel are recommended to lift an object weighing more than 35 pounds and less than 50 pounds, but required for objects weighing more than 50 pounds.

The document describes the replacement procedures for the major components of the original Gen1 CPC cabinet (5411744) and the new Gen2 cabinet (5750811). Because all of the major components are the same dimensions (depth and width), the replacement instructions are the same, with the exception of the top module of the original cabinet (5411744).

The major components of the original CPC cabinet (5411744) are:

  1. Combiner module: 5411744-8
  2. Power amplifier #1 and #2: 5411744-6
  3. Driver chassis: 5411744-7
  4. Power supply: 5411744-9

The major components of the new CPC cabinet (5750811) are:

  1. Power amplifier: 5750811-8
  2. Power supply: 5750811-7

Preparation

Procedure

  1. Make sure that you have read and understood the electrical safety requirements before starting this procedure. See Electrical Safety.
  2. Shut down and power off the host computer. See Shutting down the host computer.
  3. Apply RF Subsystem LOTO. See Applying LOTO - RF Subsystem (magnet room electronics).
  4. Confirm that the hoist support brackets are installed.
  5. Warning
    Personal injury!
    The Hoist could disengage from the support tube and the load could fall.
    Make sure the safety pin is installed into the hoist support bracket after the hoist installation.
    Install the hoist. Use the instructions in Hoist Service Kit and Lifting Accessories to set up the hoist.

    Instead of the support tube (from the HEC cabinet) being attached to the top of the cabinet, the support tube is attached to the front hoist support bracket.

  6. Remove all cables at the rear of the component being replaced.
  7. Remove all screws that secure the front of the component to the cabinet.
  8. Follow the instructions for the type of component you are replacing.

    • To replace the combiner (top module), proceed to Replacing the combiner.

    • To replace any other component, proceed to Replacing the amplifier component.

Replacing the combiner

Procedure

  1. Notice
    The weight of the combiner module is 35 lb (15.9 kg). Due to the bulkiness of the unit, it is recommended to have two persons perform the replacement procedure for the combiner.
    Slide the top component out until the guide rails are locked into place.
  2. With two engineers, release the slide rails on both sides of the combiner. Support the combiner during this process.
  3. Attach the FRU replacement brackets to each side of the combiner module.

    Each bracket is held in place with three screws. Tighten each screw so that the bracket is securely fastened to the component.

  4. Warning
    Possible personal injury or equipment damage!
    This unit could fall when using The hoist tool.
    Before putting weight on the hoist chain, be sure that the links are aligned properly. This helps keep the chain from binding up, or becoming kinked, which is nearly impossible to correct when the chain is under tension.
    Attach the hoist hooks into the slots on each bracket.

    When removing the top module, there will be some slack in the hoist chain. Confirm that the chain links are aligned properly.

  5. Remove the module by having two FEs support both sides of the module, then slide the guide rail back into the cabinet.
  6. On the opposite side, slide the other support rail back into the cabinet. The module is now entirely support by the hoist.
  7. Lower the combiner module onto the top section of the FRU crate.
  8. Remove the FRU replacements brackets from the old component and install them on the new component.
  9. Attach the component to the hoist.
  10. With two persons, raise the new module to the height of the slide rail. Attach the module to the guide rails.
  11. Slide the component back into the cabinet. Reinstall the fasteners into the front of the component so it is secure in the cabinet.
  12. Reattach the cables per cable label identifiers.
  13. Proceed to Finalization.

Replacing the amplifier component

About this task

The original CPC cabinet has four lower components (power amplifier #1 and #2, driver chassis, and power supply) that all have the same dimensions (depth and width). The replacement instructions for these components are the same. The new CPC cabinet has two lower components (power amplifier and power supply). They also have the same dimensions and same replacement instructions.

Procedure

  1. Remove all cables from the rear of the component.
  2. Remove fasteners from the front of the component.
  3. Slide the component out until the guide rails are locked into place.
  4. Attach the FRU replacement brackets to each side of the combiner module.

    Each bracket is held in place with three screws. Tighten each screw so that the bracket is securely fastened to the component.

  5. Warning
    Bodily injury or equipment damage!
    The combined weight of the component and the hoist can shift, causing harm to the equipment or the engineer.
    confirm that the hook is properly seated in the FrU Replacement bracket.
    Attach the hoist hooks into the slots on each bracket. Install the spreader bar to the hoist to stabilize the chains. Confirm that the chain links are aligned properly.
  6. Raise the component using the hoist to take the weight of the FRU off of the cabinet guide rails.
  7. Retract the guide rails from each side. Push in the button on the guide rail to unlock the rail and slide the rail back into the cabinet.

    The hoist is now supporting the component.

  8. Lower the component onto the top section of the FRU crate. Move the defective component out of the way and move the new component into position.
  9. Remove the FRU replacement brackets from the old component and install them on the new component.
  10. Attach the hoist hooks to the slots in the FRU replacement brackets and raise the component so that the rail on the component is level with the rail in the cabinet.
  11. Slide the cabinet rails onto the component.
  12. When the component is secured to the guide rails, remove the hoist and the FRU replacement brackets.
  13. Slide the component into the cabinet and confirm that the guide rail is locked.
  14. Install the fasteners into the front of the cabinet.
  15. Install the cables
  16. Proceed to Finalization.
Topic ID: id_15196467

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Return the FRU replacement brackets with the failed component.
  2. Repack the hoist service kit. See Hoist Service Kit and Lifting Accessories.
  3. Remove RF Subsystem and/or PEN Cabinet LOTO. See Removing LOTO - RF Subsystem and/or PEN Cabinet.
  4. Run:
    1. Calibrating RF Amplifier and UPM.
    2. Calibrating RF Amplifier and UPM.
    3. Doing Multi-Nuclear Spectroscopy (MNS) functional checks.
  5. Start up the host computer/system. See Starting up the host computer.
  6. Do a check scan. See MR and PET Hello-Goodbye Scan.
