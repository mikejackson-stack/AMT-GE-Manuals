3.0T split-top head coil FRUs and replacement

Topic ID: id_SL2069874-1062866

Personnel Requirements

Required Persons Procedure Timing
130 minutes
Topic ID: id_SL2069875-1062866

Procedure Overview

This document contains replacement procedures and lists the FRUs for the 3.0T Split-Top Head Coil by GE BGL. Review the list of coil FRUs and other accessories before replacing the external cable and mechanical hardware of the coil. Run SNR check after any FRU replacement to confirm the coil performs per specifications.

Procedure

Topic ID: id_SL2069884-1062866

External Cable Replacement (P/N 5269301 for Rev 1 through Rev 11)

  1. Remove the head coil top from the head coil bottom.

  2. Unscrew the five brass screws (No. 45) and nine nylon screws (No. 29) with nylon washers (No. 32) from the head coil bottom cover plate.

  3. Remove the head coil bottom cover plate from the bottom of the head coil.

  4. Unscrew the two M3 x 8 screws (No. 51) from the TR board secured to the head coil bottom.

  5. Lift the TR board and while holding it outside of the coil, disconnect the cable from the board.

    1. Remove the two SMA straight crimp-type plugs from the RG58 cable using the appropriate spanner.

    2. Unplug the SMB RG174 straight cable crimp and female DC connector on the cable assembly.

  6. Remove the sealing nut from the cable strain relief (P/N 5248402) using the appropriate spanner.

  7. Pull and remove the cable from the head coil, and reposition the TR board.

  8. To reconnect the cable assembly to the TR board:

    1. Insert the cable through the opening in the housing.

    2. Lift the TR board, and hold it outside of the coil.

    3. Connect the two SMA straight crimp-type plugs for the RG58 cable to the TR board. Tighten until snug, but do not overtighten.

    4. Insert the SMB RG174 straight cable crimp and the female DC connector on the cable assembly into the TR board.

  9. Insert the TR board into the head coil, and secure it to the head coil bottom with two M3 x 8 screws (No. 51 in Connectors on TR Board). Tighten the screws until snug, but do not tighten too much.

  10. Using the appropriate spanner, tighten the sealing nut of the cable strain relief until it is snug, but do not tighten too much.

  11. Replace the head coil bottom cover plate (No. 44).

    1. Secure it with the five brass screws (No. 45) and nine nylon screws (No. 29) with nylon washers (No. 32) shown in 3.0T Split-Top Head Coil Bottom Cover Plate.

    2. Tighten the screws until snug, but do not tighten too much.

  12. Reattach the head coil top to the head coil bottom.

  13. Perform a test scan with the head coil to verify operation.

Topic ID: id_SL2069898-1062866

TR Board Replacement (P/N 5366299 for Rev 7 through Rev 11)

  1. Remove the head coil top from the head coil bottom.

  2. From the head coil bottom, unscrew five brass screws (No. 45) and nine nylon screws (No. 29) with nylon washers (No. 32) shown in 3.0T Split-Top Head Coil Bottom Cover Plate.

  3. Remove the head coil bottom cover plate from the bottom of the head coil.

  4. Unscrew the two M3 x 8 screws (No. 51) from the TR board secured to the head coil bottom shown in Connectors on TR Board.

  5. Disconnect the J1 and J2 cables from the TR board.

  6. Lift the TR board out, and while holding it outside of the coil, disconnect the cable from the board.

    1. Remove the two SMA straight crimp-type plugs from the RG58 cable using the appropriate spanner.

    2. Unplug the SMB RG174 straight cable crimp and the female DC connector on the cable assembly from the TR board. (See Connectors on TR Board.)

  7. To reinstall the TR board:

    1. Reconnect the two SMA straight crimp-type plugs for the RG58 cable to the TR board.

    2. Plug the SMB RG174 straight cable crimp and the female DC connector on the cable assembly (P/N 5182567) into the TR board.

    3. Tighten the screws until snug, but do not tighten too much.

  8. Connect the J1 and J2 cables to the TR board.

  9. Insert the TR board into the head coil bottom, and secure using two M3 x 8 screws (No. 51) shown in Connectors on TR Board. Tighten the screws until snug, but do not tighten too much.

  10. Position the head coil bottom cover plate onto the bottom of the head coil with five brass screws (No. 45) and nine nylon screws (No. 29) with nylon washers (No. 32) shown in Connectors on TR Board. Tighten the screws until snug, but do not tighten too much.

  11. Reattach the head coil top to the head coil bottom.

  12. Perform a test scan with the coil to verify operation.

Preamplifier Replacement (P/N 5433509)

Topic ID: id_SL12066557-1062866

Replace Preamplifier in QHTR Interface Box (Rev 12 onwards)

  1. Remove the top cover of the head coil QHTR interface box.

  2. To remove the defective preamplifier, cut the tie wrap securing the preamplifier.

  3. Use a flat-blade screwdriver to lift the preamplifier from QHTR interface board.

  4. Install the new preamplifier on the QHTR interface board. Secure it with a tie wrap. Cut and remove the excess length of the tie wrap.

  5. Replace the top cover of the head coil QHTR interface box.

  6. Perform a test scan with the coil to verify operation.

Topic ID: id_SL12066567-1062866

Replace Preamplifier in Head Coil TR (Rev 7 through Rev 11)

  1. Remove the head coil top from the head coil bottom.

  2. From the head coil bottom, unscrew 5 brass screws (No. 45) and 9 nylon screws (No. 29) with nylon washers (No. 32) shown in 3.0T Split-Top Head Coil Bottom Cover Plate.

  3. Remove the head coil bottom cover plate from the bottom of the head coil.

  4. Unscrew the two M3 x 8 screws (No. 51) from the TR board secured to the head coil bottom shown in Connectors on TR Board

  5. Disconnect the J1 and J2 cables from the TR board (all shown in Cable Assembly on 3.0T Split-Top Head Coil).

  6. Lift the TR board out, and while holding it outside of the coil, disconnect the cable from the TR board.

    1. Remove the two SMA straight crimp-type plugs for the RG58 cable using the appropriate spanner.

    2. Unplug the SMB RG174 straight cable crimp and the female DC connector on the cable assembly from the TR board. (See Connectors on TR Board.)

    3. Remove the TR board outside of the coil.

    4. Make sure the health of pin diodes (D1, D2, D3, D4, D5) using a digital multi-meter (in Diode Test mode). In forward bias, the DMM should show values between 400 and 700; in reverse bias, it should show open.

      • If the DMM shows any short (beep sound or very low value, <400, in forward bias), the pin diodes are damaged. Replace the TR board assembly (P/N 5366299).

      • If all pin diodes are good (both in forward and reverse bias), replace only the preamplifier (P/N 5433509).

  7. To remove the defective preamplifier, cut and remove the tie wrap securing the preamplifier to the TR board.
  8. Use a flat-blade screwdriver to lift the preamplifier from the TR board.

  9. Install the new preamplifier in the same location. Secure it with a tie wrap. Cut and remove the excess length of the tie wrap.

  10. Reconnect the two SMA straight crimp-type plug for the RG58 cable to the TR board.

  11. Plug the SMB RG174 straight cable crimp and the female DC connector on the cable assembly (P/N 5182567) into the TR board.

  12. Tighten the screws until snug, but do not tighten too much.

  13. Connect the J1 and J2 cables to the TR board.

  14. Insert the TR board into the head coil bottom, and secure using two M3 x 8 screws (No. 51). Tighten the screws until snug, but do not tighten too much. (See Connectors on TR Board.)

  15. Set the head coil bottom cover plate onto the bottom of the head coil with five brass screws (No. 45) and nine nylon screws (No. 29) with nylon washers (No. 32) shown in Connectors on TR Board. Tighten the screws until snug, but do not tighten too much.

  16. Reattach the head coil top to the head coil bottom.

  17. Perform a test scan with the coil to verify operation.

Head Coil Latching FRU Kit P/N 5191687 (Head Coil Rev 12 onwards)

The contents of Head Coil Latching FRU Kit are:

P/N Description
2347569Nylon Screw Pan Head M4 x 8, Qty 10
2308715Bottom Cover Plate, Qty 1
2382710Pivot Block, Qty 4
2347569-2 Nylon Screw Pan Head M4 x 12, Qty 4
2358535-3 Slotted Flat Head Screw Brass M4 x 10, Qty 4
46-214941P2Nylon Plane Washer, Qty 10

The only tool required in these procedures is a slot-head screwdriver.

  1. Remove the bottom cover plate of the head coil by removing 10 nylon screws and 4 brass screws. (Discard the disassembled bottom cover plate)
  2. Remove the head coil latch and pivot blocks by removing the nylon screws. (Discard the disassembled pivot blocks.)
  3. Insert two new pivot blocks (2382710, supplied in FRU kit) on the latch and mount the screws.

  4. Repeat the steps above on the second head coil latch.

  5. Fix the new bottom cover plate (2380715, supplied in FRU kit) with nylon screws and brass screws. Ensure free movement of the latch assembly.

Spring Clip Replacement

  1. The four spring clip connectors are located in the head coil baseplate, under the plastic caps (marked A in the illustration below).
  2. Remove the plastic caps by removing the two screws (marked B in the illustration below).
  3. Remove the gold spring clip and red gasket.
  4. Install the new spring clip.

  5. Insert the red gasket and replace the plastic cap.
