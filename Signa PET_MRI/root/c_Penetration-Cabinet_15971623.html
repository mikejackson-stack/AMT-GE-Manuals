Penetration (PEN) Cabinet

Power supply configurations

The configuration of the PEN Cabinet is based on power supplies:
  • EcoPower Scan Room Power Supply (eSRPS)
  • Exciter and RxDist/IRD DC Power Supplies (DCPSs)
The FRUs for each configuration are listed in a separate table.
