Wheel Housing Assembly Replacement

Procedure

  1. Pry the wheel housing assembly out of the roller rail with a thin flat-blade screwdriver.
  2. Push the new wheel housing assembly into the roller rail until it is firmly seated.
  3. If it is necessary to replace more than one wheel housing assembly in a roller rail, remove the entire roller rail or roller module assembly.
    1. For roller module assemblies on the rear bridge, proceed to Rear Bridge Roller Module Assembly Replacement
    2. For roller module assemblies on the front bridge, proceed to Front Bridge Roller Module Assembly Replacement
    3. For roller module assemblies on the middle bridge, proceed to Middle Bridge Roller Module Assembly Replacement
    4. For roller rails on the table, proceed to Table Roller Rail Replacement
Topic ID: id_SL12716697-1191726

Rear Bridge Roller Module Assembly Replacement

Procedure

  1. Notice
    The number in parentheses within an instruction corresponds to the numbered item in the referenced illustration.
    Drive the LPCA forward into the patient bore to access the rear bridge roller module assemblies. Refer to the illustration below for roller module assembly identification.
  2. Using a flat-blade screwdriver in the recessed access area, pry the roller assembly up and lift it out.
  3. With the roller assembly removed, push against the defective wheel housing assembly from the bottom to remove it from the roller rail.
  4. Push the new wheel housing assembly into the roller rail until it is firmly seated.
  5. Reattach the roller module to the bridge and push it back into its location.
Topic ID: id_SL12716698-1191726

Front Bridge Roller Module Assembly Replacement

Procedure

  1. Drive the LPCA all the way to the rear to access the front bridge roller module assemblies (1).
  2. Undock the table and move it away from the magnet to allow easier access to the front bridge.
  3. Using a flat-blade screwdriver in the recessed access area to pry the roller assembly up, and lift it out. (See Roller Module Access Area.)
  4. With the roller assembly removed, push against the wheel housing assembly from the bottom to remove it from the roller rail. (See Wheel Housing Assembly Removal.)
  5. Reattach the roller module to the bridge and push it back into its location.
  6. Push the new wheel housing assembly into the roller rail until it is firmly seated.
Topic ID: id_SL12716699-1191726

Middle Bridge Roller Module Assembly Replacement

Procedure

  1. Drive the LPCA all the way to the rear to access the middle bridge roller module assemblies (1).
  2. Using a flat-blade screwdriver in the recessed access area, pry the roller assembly up and lift it out. (See Roller Module Access Area.)
  3. With the roller assembly removed, push against the wheel housing assembly from the bottom to remove it from the roller rail. (See Wheel Housing Assembly Removal.)
  4. Push the new wheel housing assembly into the roller rail until it is firmly seated.
  5. Reattach the roller module to the bridge and push it back into its location.
Topic ID: id_SL12716700-1191726

Table Roller Rail Replacement

Procedure

  1. Dock the patient table.
    1. Run the cradle completely into the magnet bore.
    2. Proceed to Step 3.

    OR

  2. Undock the table.
    1. Depress the dock pedal.
    2. Simultaneously, push in the secondary latch actuator pin and the vertical latch actuator to release the secondary latch.
    3. Pull the cradle forward far enough to clear the cradle guides at the rear of the table.
    4. Lift and prop the cradle up to access the table roller rails; proceed to the next step.
  3. Remove the M4 x 25 mm brass socket-head cap screws and washers securing the long table roller rail (1) and short table roller rails (2), and lift the table roller rail up and out.
  4. With the table roller rail removed, push against the wheel housing assembly from the bottom to remove it from the roller rail. (See Wheel Housing Assembly Removal.)
  5. Push the new wheel housing assembly into the roller rail until it is firmly seated.
  6. Apply Loctite to the previously removed M4 x 25 mm brass socket-head cap screws and attach the table roller rail to the table, using the previously removed washers.
