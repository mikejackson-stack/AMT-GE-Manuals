Replacing AMT system control module

About this task

Refer to MNS cabinet front view and MNS cabinet rear view for FRU locations.

Procedure

  1. Install anti-tip legs on both sides of the MNS cabinet in MNS Anti-Tip Legs Installed. The anti-tip legs are strapped inside the rear of the cabinet on the left side. Remove the anti-tip legs from the cabinet, and align them with the screw openings at the bottom of the cabinet. Screw one leg on each side.
  2. Apply LOTO to the XFRD amplifier and the PEN cabinet. Applying LOTO - RF Subsystem (magnet room electronics) and Applying LOTO - PEN Cabinet (magnet room electronics).
  3. Open the rear rack cabinet door.
  4. Disconnect the system power cable at J12 on the power supply.
  5. Disconnect cable J3 (RF IN) on the rear of the system control module.
  6. Disconnect the system interface cable at J19.
  7. Disconnect the RF interface cable at J1.
  8. Working from the front of the system control module, carefully remove the four large mounting screws that attach the module to the rack. Set the screws aside in a safe place.
  9. Working from the front, carefully slide the control module out of the front of the rack.
  10. Set the removed unit aside in a safe place.
  11. Remove the FRU system control module from its packaging and inspect it for damage.
  12. Working from the front of the rack, align the system control module with the slides and carefully slide it into the rack.
  13. Reattach the system control module to the rack using the four large screws removed in Step 8.
  14. Working from the rear, reconnect the RF interface cable at J1.
  15. Reconnect the system interface cable at J19.
  16. Reconnect cable at J3 (RF IN).
  17. Working from the front of the rack, reconnect the system power cable at J12 on the power supply.
  18. Remove LOTO from the XRFD amplifier and the PEN cabinet. See Removing LOTO - RF Subsystem and/or PEN Cabinet.

Replacing driver module

About this task

Refer to MNS cabinet front view and MNS cabinet rear view for FRU locations.

Procedure

  1. Install anti-tip legs on both sides of the MNS cabinet in MNS Anti-Tip Legs Installed. The anti-tip legs are strapped inside the rear of the cabinet on the left side. Remove the anti-tip legs from the cabinet, and align them with the screw openings at the bottom of the cabinet. Screw one leg onto each side.
  2. Apply LOTO to the XFRD amplifier and the PEN cabinet. Applying LOTO - RF Subsystem (magnet room electronics) and Applying LOTO - PEN Cabinet (magnet room electronics).
  3. Working from the front of the rack, carefully remove the two mounting screws that attach the driver module to the rack. The screws are located below the driver module air intake vents. Set the screws aside in a safe place.
  4. Open the rear rack cabinet door.
  5. Disconnect the system power cable at J12 on the power supply in AMT system control module - cabinet connections.
  6. Disconnect the fan AC power cable at J17 at the rear of the cabinet.
  7. Loosen the 20 thumbscrews that attach the fan panel to the rack.
  8. Carefully allow the top of the fan panel to pivot away from the cabinet, then carefully lift the fan panel up and away from the rack.
  9. Use a 5/16 inch wrench to remove the RF cable at J25.
  10. Remove J26 RF interface cable.
  11. Carefully remove the RF output cables 1 through 5 from J27-1 through J27-5 on the driver module.
  12. Loosen the two thumbscrews that attach the driver module to the rack.
  13. Carefully slide the driver module out of the rear of the rack.
  14. Set the removed unit aside in a safe place.
  15. Remove the FRU driver module from its packaging and inspect it for damage.
  16. Align the driver module with the slides and carefully slide it into the rack.
  17. Tighten the two thumbscrews that attach the driver module to the rack.
  18. Reattach RF output cables 1 through 5 to J27-1 through J27-5 on the driver module.
  19. Reattach the RF interface cable to J26.
  20. Use a 5/16 in. wrench to reattach the RF cable to J25.
  21. Carefully lift the fan panel into place and position it on the rack.
  22. Tighten the 20 thumbscrews that attach the fan panel to the rack.
  23. Reconnect the fan AC power cable to J17 at the rear of the cabinet in MNS cabinet - AC power cable.
  24. Reconnect the system power cable to J12 on the power supply in AMT system control module - cabinet connections.
  25. Insert the two mounting screws that attach the driver module to the rack. The screws are located on the front of the cabinet and below the driver module air intake vents in Driver module mounting screws.
  26. Remove LOTO from the XRFD amplifier and the PEN cabinet. See Removing LOTO - RF Subsystem and/or PEN Cabinet.
    Notice: After LOTO is removed from the PEN cabinet, the exciter board requires 20 minutes of power-up time before scanning.

Replacing power amplifier module(s)

About this task

Refer to MNS cabinet front view and MNS cabinet rear view for FRU locations.

Procedure

  1. Install anti-tip legs on both sides of the MNS cabinet MNS Anti-Tip Legs Installed. The anti-tip legs are strapped inside the rear of the cabinet on the left side. Remove the anti-tip legs from the cabinet, and align them with the screw openings at the bottom of the cabinet. Screw one leg onto each side.
  2. Apply LOTO to the XFRD amplifier and the PEN cabinet. Applying LOTO - RF Subsystem (magnet room electronics) and Applying LOTO - PEN Cabinet (magnet room electronics).
  3. Open the rear rack cabinet door.
  4. DANGER
    Electric shock hazard
    Capacitors in the power amplifier modules store enough energy to cause a dangerous shock.
    Allow capacitors to drain for at least 10 minutes after AC power has been turned off.
    Disconnect the system power cable at J12 on the power supply AMT system control module - cabinet connections.
  5. Disconnect the fan AC power cable at J17 at the rear of the cabinet MNS cabinet - AC power cable.
  6. Loosen the 20 thumbscrews that attach the fan panel to the rack.
  7. Carefully allow the top of the fan panel to pivot away from the cabinet, then carefully lift the fan panel up and away from the rack.
  8. Disconnect all the power amplifier interface cables at connectors J29-A through J29-E or, for an individual module replacement, disconnect only that module's connectors.
  9. Disconnect the RF input cables at J28-A through J28-E or, for an individual module replacement, disconnect only that module's cables with a 5/16 inch wrench.
  10. Disconnect the RF output cables at J30-A through J30-E or, for an individual module replacement, disconnect only that module's RF output cables.
  11. Working from the front of the rack, remove the two corresponding screws that attach the power amplifier module to the front panel. The screws are located next to the air intake vents. Keep the screws.
  12. At the rear of the cabinet, loosen the two thumbscrews that attach the power amplifier.
  13. Working from the rear of the rack, carefully slide the power amplifier module out of the rack.
  14. Set the removed unit aside in a safe place.
  15. Remove the FRU amplifier module from its packaging and inspect it for damage.
  16. Working from the rear of the rack, align the power amplifier module with the slides and carefully slide it into the rack.
  17. Working from the front of the rack, reattach the power amplifier module to the rack using the two screws removed earlier.
  18. Reattach the RF output cables at J30-A through J30-E.
  19. Reattach the RF input cables at J28-A through J28-E.
  20. Reconnect the power amplifier interface cables at J29-A through J29-E.
  21. CAUTION
    Possible equipment damage
    The fan panel is heavy.
    Use safe lifting methods. Do not allow the fan panel to fall.
    Carefully lift the fan panel into place and position it on the rack.
  22. Tighten the 20 thumbscrews that attach the fan panel to the rack.
  23. Reconnect the fan AC power cable to J17 at the rear of the cabinet MNS cabinet - AC power cable.
  24. Reconnect the system power cable to J12 on the power supply AMT system control module - cabinet connections.
  25. Remove LOTO from the XRFD amplifier and the PEN cabinet. See Removing LOTO - RF Subsystem and/or PEN Cabinet.
    Notice: After LOTO is removed from the PEN cabinet, the exciter board requires 20 minutes of power-up time before scanning.
  26. Do Calibrating RF Amplifier and UPM.

Replacing combiner

About this task

Refer to MNS cabinet front view and MNS cabinet rear view for FRU locations.

Procedure

  1. Install anti-tip legs on both sides of the MNS cabinet in MNS Anti-Tip Legs Installed. The anti-tip legs are strapped inside the rear of the cabinet on the left side. Remove the anti-tip legs from the cabinet, and align them with the screw openings at the bottom of the cabinet. Screw one leg onto each side.
  2. Apply LOTO to the XFRD amplifier and the PEN cabinet. Applying LOTO - RF Subsystem (magnet room electronics) and Applying LOTO - PEN Cabinet (magnet room electronics).
  3. Open the rear rack cabinet door.
  4. Disconnect the system power cable at J12 on the power supply in AMT system control module - cabinet connections.
  5. Disconnect the fan AC power cable at J17 at the rear of the cabinet in MNS cabinet - AC power cable.
  6. Loosen the 20 thumbscrews that attach the fan panel to the rack.
  7. Carefully allow the top of the fan panel to pivot away from the cabinet, then carefully lift the fan panel up and away from the rack.
  8. Disconnect the thermostat cable at J35.
  9. Use a 5/16 inch wrench to remove the RFL RF cable from J33.
  10. Use a 5/16 inch wrench to remove the FWD RF cable from J34.
  11. Disconnect the RF output cable from J32.
  12. Disconnect RF input cables 1 through 5 from J31-1 through J31-5.
  13. Loosen the two thumbscrews that attach the combiner to the cabinet.
  14. Working from the rear of the rack, carefully slide the combiner out of the rear of the rack.
  15. Set the removed unit aside in a safe place.
  16. Remove the FRU combiner from its packaging and inspect it for damage.
  17. Working from the rear of the rack, align the combiner with the slides and carefully slide it into the rack.
  18. Working from the front of the rack, tighten the two thumbscrews that attach the combiner to the cabinet.
  19. Reconnect RF input cables 1 through 5 to connectors J31-1 through J31-5.
  20. Reconnect the RF output cable to J32.
  21. Use a 5/16 inch wrench to reconnect the FWD RF cable to J34.
  22. Use a 5/16 inch wrench to reconnect the RFL RF cable to J33.
  23. Reconnect the thermostat cable to J35.
  24. Tighten the 20 thumbscrews that attach the fan panel to the rack.
  25. Reconnect the fan AC power cable to J17 at the rear of the cabinet in MNS cabinet - AC power cable.
  26. Reconnect the system power cable to J12 on the power supply in AMT system control module - cabinet connections.
  27. Remove LOTO from the XRFD amplifier and the PEN cabinet. See Removing LOTO - RF Subsystem and/or PEN Cabinet.
    Notice: After LOTO is removed from the PEN cabinet, the exciter board requires 20 minutes of power-up time before scanning.

Replacing power supply

About this task

Refer to MNS cabinet front view and MNS cabinet rear view for FRU locations.

CAUTION
Potential personal injury
The power supply has sharp edges.
Wear cut-resistant gloves while handling the power supply.

Procedure

  1. Install anti-tip legs on both sides of the MNS cabinet in MNS Anti-Tip Legs Installed. The anti-tip legs are strapped inside the rear of the cabinet on the left side. Remove the anti-tip legs from the cabinet, and align them with the screw openings at the bottom of the cabinet. Screw one leg onto each side.
  2. Apply LOTO to the XFRD amplifier and the PEN cabinet. Applying LOTO - RF Subsystem (magnet room electronics) and Applying LOTO - PEN Cabinet (magnet room electronics).
  3. Open the rear rack cabinet door.
  4. Disconnect the system power cable at J12 on the power supply.
  5. Remove the power supply low-voltage interconnect cables from J13 and J15.
  6. Remove the protective earth grounding cable from the grounding stud.
  7. Remove the two screws that attach the power supply to the rack. The screws are located in the lower left and right corners of the power supply. Keep the screws.
  8. On the front of the MNS cabinet, remove the eight large screws that attach the power supply to the rack. Set the screws aside in a safe place.
  9. Remove 16 screws securing front cover of power supply.
  10. Warning
    Heavy component
    The power supply is heavy. Moving it without assistance can result in personal injury.
    Four people are required to slide the power supply out of the cabinet.
    Working from the front, with the help of three additional field engineers, carefully slide the power supply out of the front of the rack.
    Note

    It may be easier to partially remove the power supply before setting it on the floor, as shown above.

  11. With additional personnel, set the removed unit aside in a safe place.
  12. With additional personnel, remove the FRU power supply from its packaging and inspect it for damage.
  13. Working from the front, with the help of additional personnel, carefully slide the replacement power supply into the front of the rack.
  14. Reattach the two screws, removed earlier, that attach the power supply to the rack. The screws are located in the lower left and right corners of the power supply on the back side of the MNS cabinet. Be sure that the power supply FRU is pushed in from the front.
  15. Reattach the front cover of the power supply using the 16 screws previously removed.
  16. Reattach the eight large screws, removed earlier, that attach the power supply to the front of the rack.
  17. Reattach the protective earth grounding cable to the grounding stud.
  18. Reattach the power supply low-voltage interconnect cables to J13 and J15.
  19. Reconnect the system power cable to J12 on the power supply.
  20. Close the rear rack cabinet door.
  21. Remove LOTO from the XRFD amplifier and the PEN cabinet. See Removing LOTO - RF Subsystem and/or PEN Cabinet.
    Notice: After LOTO is removed from the PEN cabinet, the exciter board requires 20 minutes of power-up time before scanning.

Replacing fuses

About this task

The fan module and the internal power supply are equipped with replaceable fuses. The fans are equipped with two 250 volt, 8 amp, time-delay type 8AT fuses. The internal power supply is equipped with two 250 volt, 5 amp, time-delay type 5AT fuses. See MNS cabinet rear view for details.
