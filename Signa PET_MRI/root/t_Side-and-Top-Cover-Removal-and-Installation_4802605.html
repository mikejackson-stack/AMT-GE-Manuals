Side and top cover removal and installation

Safety
DANGER
Strong Magnetic Field
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field produced by the magnet.
Do not bring any ferromagnetic tools or equipment into the magnet room.

About this task

For some of the side and top covers, other covers must be removed first. This diagram shows the names of the GEM-style covers, which covers can be removed individually and which need other covers removed first, and the estimated removal time for each cover. Installation time is estimated to be the same as the removal time.

Removing side covers

About this task

This sub-procedure is written for removal of the covers on the left side of the scanner. The procedure for the right side is similar. On each side, the side middle cover must be removed before the side rear cover or side front cover can be removed.
Topic ID: id_SL3852174-1138026

Removing the left side middle cover

Procedure

  1. (For left side middle cover only) Label locations as needed and disconnect all cables except for the top PG cable.
    Note The PG cable is fiber optic and should be handled with care.
  2. Remove the M6 Allen screw at the bottom of the left side middle cover.
  3. CAUTION
    Possible equipment damage
    When removing the left side middle cover, carefully thread the top PG cable through the cable opening in the cover. The PG cable is fiber optic and should be handled with care.
    Holding the cover on each side, lift the cover up and off its two support brackets.
Topic ID: id_SL3852178-1138026

Removing the left side rear cover

Procedure

  1. If not already done, remove the M6 screw fastening the front and rear covers together.
  2. Remove the three M10 screws and three nylon washers securing the left side rear cover.
  3. Slide the cover toward the rear and lift the cover up and off its two support brackets.
Topic ID: id_SL3852181-1138026

Removing the left side front cover

Procedure

  1. If not already done, remove the M6 screw fastening the front and rear covers together.
  2. Remove the three M10 screws and three nylon washers securing the left side front cover.
  3. Slide the cover toward the front and lift the cover up and off its two support brackets.
Topic ID: id_SL3834972-1138026

Removing top covers

About this task

This sub-procedure is written for removal of the left top cover of the scanner. The procedure for the right top cover is similar.

  • Before the left (or right) top cover can be removed, the left side (or right side) middle and rear covers must be removed. In addition, the cable cover must be removed. For instructions on removing the cable cover, see Rear Cover Removal and Installation.
  • Before the front top cover can be removed, either all side covers and both left and right side top covers must be removed or the left and right front covers must be removed. It is highly recommended that you remove the side and left and right side top covers rather than removing the front covers due to the complexity of front cover removal.
  • Use a side shield to protect magnet components.
Topic ID: id_SL3852188-1138026

Removing left top cover

Procedure

  1. Make sure that the left side middle and rear covers have been removed as well as the cable cover.
  2. Remove the five M10 screws at the base of the left top cover.
  3. Remove the left top cover.
Topic ID: id_SL3852192-1138026

Removing front top cover if side covers and both left and right side top covers are removed (recommended)

Procedure

  1. Remove the four M10 screws on each side of the front top cover.
  2. With one person holding each side, slide the front top cover toward the rear far enough to lift it off.
Topic ID: id_SL3852196-1138026

Removing front top cover if the front covers are removed (optional)

Procedure

  1. Remove the four M10 screws on each side of the front top cover.
  2. With one person holding each side, slide the front top cover toward the front and lift it off.
Topic ID: id_SL3836207-1138026

Installing top covers

Topic ID: id_SL3852199-1138026

Installing the front top cover if side covers and both left and right side top covers are removed (recommended)

Procedure

  1. With one person holding each side, lift the front top cover and slide it toward the front until it is in position under the front cover.
  2. Install the four M10 Allen screws on each side of the front top cover.
Topic ID: id_SL3852202-1138026

Installing the front top cover if the front covers are removed (optional)

Procedure

  1. With one person holding each side, lift the front top cover and slide it toward the rear until it is in position.
  2. Install the four M10 screws on each side of the front top cover.
Topic ID: id_SL3852205-1138026

Installing the left top cover

Procedure

  1. Lift the left top cover into position, fitting the tabs into the slots of its brackets.
  2. Install the four M10 Allen screws at the base of the cover.
Topic ID: id_SL3836217-1138026

Installing side covers

About this task

This sub-procedure is written for installation of the covers on the left side of the scanner. The procedure for the right side is similar. On each side, the side front and rear covers must be installed before the side middle cover can be installed.

Topic ID: id_SL3852208-1138026

Installing the left side front cover

Procedure

  1. Lift the cover tabs at the top over its two support brackets.
  2. Slide the cover toward the rear until it is seated. Make sure its tabs on the front edge fit into the slots.
  3. Install the three M10 screws and three nylon washers securing the left side rear cover.
  4. When both front and rear side covers are mounted, install the M6 Allen screw to fasten front and rear side covers together.
Topic ID: id_SL3852212-1138026

Installing the left side rear cover

Procedure

  1. Lift the cover tabs at the top over its two support brackets.
  2. Slide the cover toward the front until it is seated. Make sure its tabs on the rear edge fit into the slots.
  3. Install the three M10 screws and three nylon washers securing the left side rear cover.
  4. When both front and rear side covers are mounted, install the M6 Allen screw to fasten front and rear side covers together.
Topic ID: id_SL3852216-1138026

Installing the left side middle cover

Procedure

  1. Move the cover close to position.
  2. Carefully thread the top PG cable through the cable opening in the cover to the outside of the cover.
    Note The PG cable is fiber optic and should be handled with care.
  3. Lift the cover onto its two support brackets.
  4. Install the M6 Allen screw at the bottom of the cover.
  5. Reconnect all cables.
