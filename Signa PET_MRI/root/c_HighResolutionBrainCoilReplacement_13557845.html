PET/MR 8-channel high resolution brain coil superior or inferior mirror replacement

The process to replace certain PET/MR brain coil FRUs are provided below.

Topic ID: id_SL13401666-1243982

Superior or inferior mirror replacement

Two mirrors, superior and inferior, are included with the 3.0T PET/MR 8-channel high resolution brain array coil. To replace either mirror, push the button and slide the mirror off its base.
