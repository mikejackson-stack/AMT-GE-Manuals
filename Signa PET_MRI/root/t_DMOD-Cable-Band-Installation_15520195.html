Procedure

  1. Note

    Three cable bands secure the DMOD power cables to the PET insert. The cable bands reduce DMOD cable vibrations, which may cause white pixel artifacts. The cable bands may need to be removed to permit DMOD filter cable replacement and then reinstalled. Use this procedure to replace an existing cable band or to install a new one.

    Remove the existing cable band, if present.
    1. Insert the tip of a flat-blade screwdriver in the buckle and twist the screwdriver to disengage the lock.
    2. Pull the cable band out of the buckle.
    3. (For cable band replacements) Discard the damaged cable band.
  2. Place the replacement cable band over the top of the DMOD power cables on the PET insert with the buckle facing up.
  3. Wrap the loose end of the cable band around the insert.
    Note

    The strap must be flat against the PET insert and free of kinks.

  4. Insert the loose end of the cable band into the buckle. Use one hand to pull the loose end through the buckle while using the other hand to push against the buckle.
  5. Pull the loose end until taut.
    Note

    Do not overtighten the cable band. The cable band may break if too much force is applied.

  6. Apply Gorilla tape to the loose end of the strap.
  7. Repeat this procedure as needed for the remaining cable bands, as shown. Two cable bands should be spaced evenly between the DMOD cable guides. One cable band should be spaced evenly between the DMOD cable guide and the DMOD power cable connector.

Finalization

  1. Remove LOTO from the PDUs in the PET and PGR cabinets, and turn the PET chiller on.

  2. Place the PROP circuit breaker to the on position.

  3. Connect the fiber-optic lines to the insert (2). The quick-disconnect sleeves (1) will click into place.

  4. .

  5. Install the PET/MR insert.

  6. Reinstall the front end bell.

  7. Reinstall the rear end bell.

  8. Reinstall the rear pedestal.

  9. Reinstall the magnet covers.

  10. Perform these calibrations and functional checks:

    • PET Daily Quality Assurance (DQA) check

    • If PET DQA check fails, perform PET detector calibration.
