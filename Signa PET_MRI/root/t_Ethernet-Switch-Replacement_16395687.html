About this task

The Ethernet switch transfers data between the host computer and the PGR cabinet components. The switch is part of the recon subsystem.

Topic ID: id_SL2001580-1061849

Removal of Ethernet Switch

Procedure

  1. Perform LOTO on the PGR PDU/gradient subsystem. See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
  2. Locate the Ethernet switch in the right side of the PGR cabinet, under the ICN.
  3. Remove the Ethernet cables from the front of the switch.
  4. Remove the four screws on the front of the switch bracket.
  5. Pull the switch forward. Disconnect the power plug from the rear of the chassis.
  6. If the switch is a returnable FRU, repack the defective switch into the FRU packaging and send it back to its destination.
Topic ID: id_s4-4-1061849

Installation and Power-up

Procedure

  1. Plug in the power cable to the rear of the switch.
  2. Slide the network switch into the original position in the PGR cabinet.
  3. Secure the switch to the cabinet with four screws.
  4. D-link switches with hardware version G3 or H1 on the rating plate have DIP switches on the front. Set the DIP switches according to DIP Switch Settings.
    1DIP switches
    2Power LED
    3Connection LEDs
    Table 5. DIP Switch Settings
    DIP Switch Setting
    EEEOFF
    Flow ControlOFF
    Port Isolation & Storm ControlOFF
  5. Reconnect all Ethernet cables to the ports on front of the switch as marked.
  6. Remove LOTO and power up the unit. See Removing LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
  7. Check that the green power LED is on. Also check the connection LEDs for each connected port (solid green indicates a good connection, and flashing green indicates network activity).
Topic ID: id_14953662

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Reboot the host computer.
  2. Perform a verification scan.
