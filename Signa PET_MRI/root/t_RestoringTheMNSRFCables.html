Restoring the Multi-Nuclear Spectroscopy (MNS) RF cables

Restoring the RF cables for Multi-Nuclear Spectroscopy (MNS).

Prerequisites

Notice
Possible component damage
If you connect or disconnect the wattmeter sensor while the meter is ON, component damage can occur.
Do not connect or disconnect the wattmeter sensor while the meter is ON. Always turn the meter OFF before plugging in or unplugging the sensor.

Procedure

  1. (For AMT/Herley amplifiers)
    1. Apply RF Subsystem LOTO. See Applying LOTO - RF Subsystem (magnet room electronics).
    2. Apply PEN Cabinet LOTO. See Applying LOTO - PEN Cabinet (magnet room electronics).
    3. Remove the top cover of the controller module in the MNS amplifier cabinet.
    4. Remove the top panel of the MNS amplifier cabinet.
    5. Connect the SMA cable connections to J7 and J8 on the controller module PCB.
    6. Replace the top cover of the controller module in the MNS amplifier cabinet.
    7. Replace the top panel of the MNS amplifier cabinet.
    8. Remove RF Subsystem and/or PEN Cabinet LOTO. See Removing LOTO - RF Subsystem and/or PEN Cabinet.
  2. (For CPC Gen 1 and CPC Gen 2 amplifiers)
    1. On the front of the exciter, set the RF enable S2 switch to the disabled (down) position.
    2. Remove the cables and power measurement equipment from the MNS amplifier.
    3. Reattach the RF OUT cable at the rear of the cabinet.
    4. Restore the MNS RF cables.
    5. On the driver module, set the SW2 switch to TR enable (up).
    6. On the front of the exciter, set the RF enable S2 switch to the enable (up) position.
