Electric shock hazard
High voltages are present in the PET cabinet.
LOTO the PDU in the PET cabinet before proceeding. See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.

Procedure

  1. LOTO the PDU in the PET Cabinet. See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
  2. Shut down the PET Cabinet.
  3. Remove all cables (J101, J102, J103, J104, J105, J106, power, and fiber-optic) from the front and rear of the PROP in the PET Cabinet.
  4. Remove the four front panel screws from the PROP.
  5. Slide the PROP out and off the L-brackets and set it aside.
  6. Slide the new PROP onto the L-brackets and push it in until the front is flush with the PET Cabinet.
  7. Reinstall the four screws to the front panel of the PROP to ensure the unit is securely in place.
  8. Reattach all the cables to the front and rear of the PROP.
  9. Proceed to PROP Functional Check.

Finalization

  1. Remove LOTO from the PDU in the PET cabinet. See Removing LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
  2. Turn the PET cabinet on.
