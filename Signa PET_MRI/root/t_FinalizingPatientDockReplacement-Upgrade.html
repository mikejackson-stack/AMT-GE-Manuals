Finalizing patient table dock replacement

Procedure

  1. Do all finalization steps required in other referenced procedures.
  2. Make sure that the dock motor on switch operates properly when the table UP pedal is pressed.
  3. Do dock adjustments. See Bridge alignments, dock centering, and adjustments. Make sure that the dock and table are properly aligned and centered.
  4. Make sure that cradle travel into the bore is smooth.
  5. Do Laser light alignment.
  6. Do Doing DQA calibration with the DQA III phantom.
  7. Do a check scan. See MR and PET Hello-Goodbye Scan.
  8. Dispose of the old component(s) per local requirements.
