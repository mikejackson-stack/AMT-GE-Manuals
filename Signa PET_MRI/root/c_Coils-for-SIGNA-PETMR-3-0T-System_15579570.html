Coils Suite for the SIGNA PET/MR 3.0T System

This topic provides an overview of the SIGNA PET/MR 3.0T Coils Suite (M8000RG) available for use with this system.

The PET/MR 3.0T coils are receive-only coils, made by GE HealthCare. The coils listed below, and in the Coil information section of the manual, are supported for use with the SIGNA PET/MR system.

The SIGNA PET/MR 3.0T Coils Suite (M8000RG) includes:

  • The head neck unit (HNU) coil (HNU coil)
  • The lower anterior array (LAA) coil (LAA coil)
  • The upper anterior array (UAA) coil (UAA coil)
  • The central matrix array (CMA) coil and CMA proxy (CMA coil and proxy)

Some of the coils for the Discovery MR750w system are also compatible with the PET/MR 3.0T system. See Coil information.

The performance of any modifications and/or repairs/materials that were not evaluated and approved by GE HealthCare Coils Engineering may cause the device to be adulterated within the meaning of Section 501(h) of the Food, Drug and Cosmetic Act, 21 U.S.C. § 351(h) and is strictly prohibited.

Introduction to the SIGNA PET/MR 3.0T Coils Suite

Topic ID: id_SL15579816-1212227

HNU coil

The HNU coil consists of four parts: Anterior (face bill), posterior, adapter block (AB), and AB bridge. To identify the HNU coil parts, see the illustrations below. For the HNU FRUs, see 3.0T PET/MR Head Neck Unit (HNU) FRUs.

Topic ID: id_SL15579819-1212227

LAA coil

To identify the LAA coil, see the illustrations below. The LAA coil should be stored on top of the coil positioner to maintain the curved shape of the LAA coil. For the LAA coil FRU, see 3.0T PET/MR Anterior Array (AA) FRUs.

Topic ID: id_SL15579822-1212227

UAA coil

To identify the UAA coil, see the illustrations below. Put the UAA coil on top of the coil positioner to hold the curved shape of the UAA coil. For the UAA coil FRU, see 3.0T PET/MR Anterior Array (AA) FRUs.

Topic ID: id_SL15579824-1212227

CMA coil and proxy

To identify the CMA coil and CMA proxy, see the illustrations below. The CMA proxy and CMA phantom positioner for the MCQA test will ship with the system. To install or replace the coil or proxy, see Replacing or installing the CMA coil/proxy. For the CMA coil FRUs, see 3.0T PET/MR Central Matrix Array (CMA) FRUs.
