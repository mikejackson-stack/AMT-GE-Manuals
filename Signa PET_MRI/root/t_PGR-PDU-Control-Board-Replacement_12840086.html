Notice
Potential component damage
Electrostatic discharge (ESD) can cause permanent damage to electronic components.
Observe the following ESD precautions:
  • Work on a static-free mat.
  • Wear a static strap to make sure that any accumulated electrostatic charge is discharged from your body to the ground.
  • Create a common ground for the equipment being worked on by connecting the static-free mat, static strap, and peripheral units to that piece of equipment.
  • For more information on ESD protection, see MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).

Disabling Power

Procedure

  1. Notice
    The number in parentheses within an instruction corresponds to the numbered item in the referenced illustration.
    Turn the PGR PDU main input circuit breaker (1) to the Off position.
  2. LOTO the PGR Cabinet PDU breaker on the MDP. See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
  3. Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the four 6-32 x 3/8 in. screws (1) from the circuit breaker guard on the PDU, and set the screws aside.
  4. Wait five minutes for the capacitors to discharge.
  5. Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the twelve 10-32 x 1/2 in. screws, shown as (2) in Input Circuit Breaker on PDU (Shown in On Position), from the front panel and set them aside.
    Note

    There are two screws located behind the circuit breaker guard that are not shown.

  6. Carefully pull the front panel out and lean it against the side of the PDU.
  7. To disconnect SW1 (1) andSW2 (2) switches:
    1. Remove SW1 by turning the collar (1) and pulling it out.
    2. Remove SW2 by squeezing the tabs (1) on it to release from the connection slot (2).
  8. To disconnect the P8 and J8 15-pin housing connector, press the locking tab and pull the connectors apart as shown.

Replacement Procedure

About this task

The control board is mounted on the inside of the side panel. Wear an ESD wrist strap and follow proper ESD steps when handling static sensitive devices. Before removing the board, remove the connectors from front to back.

Procedure

  1. Place masking tape on each cable at P1, P2, P3, A1-P5, A1-P6, A1-P7, A1-P8.
    1. Record the corresponding ID for each cable on the tape with a marker or pen.
    2. Disconnect each of these cables.
  2. Insert the control board tool (1) onto each of the four nylon posts (2) to collapse them, steadily pull the board away from the posts and remove it.
  3. Gently slide the control board out of the rear holders as shown.
  4. To configure the replacement PDU control board for the eXtreme Gradient Driver (XGD) II Cabinet:

    • If the board is horizontal, turn the orange LED 3 switch to the up position (1).

    • If the board is vertical, the switch should be in the left position (2).

    Note

    All boards ship with the switch set to the AUX position.

  5. Attach the previously removed cables, beginning with the connections at the rear and working forward.
    Note

    The front orange connector at J6 (1) is not keyed and can be connected incorrectly. Make sure the cable is attached properly and not offset. If set incorrectly, the PDU will not function.

  6. Align the control board over the nylon posts (1), and gently push the board onto them until it snaps into place.
Topic ID: id_SL5534207-1187886

Reassembly

Procedure

  1. Reconnect the following:

    • Switches SW1 and SW2

    • P8/J8 Connector

    Note

    Make sure SW2 is attached at the third slot as shown in Tabs on SW2 Switch.

  2. Use the 12 Phillips screws removed earlier to reattach the front cover on the PDU.
  3. Use the four Phillips screws removed earlier to reattach the circuit breaker guard.
Topic ID: id_15244693

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Notice
    If this is a service-related replacement:
    Remove LOTO from the PGR PDU main input circuit breaker on the front of the MDP. See Removing LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
  2. Turn the PGR PDU main circuit breaker on the MDP to the green On position.
  3. Remove LOTO from the main breaker on the PDU in the PGR Cabinet. See Removing LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
