Preliminary

Procedure

  1. Notice
    The number in parentheses within an instruction corresponds to the numbered item in the referenced illustration.
    Turn off the PET chiller.
  2. LOTO the PDUs in both the PET and PGR cabinets. See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.

PET/MR Insert Removal

Procedure

  1. Disconnect the two RF transmit cables at the service end of the insert.
  2. Disconnect the air hoses and the temperature sensor at the patient end of the magnet.
  3. Remove the five fiber-optic patch cables (1) at the service end of the magnet (only two are shown).
  4. Remove the leak sensor (2) from the service end of the Insert:
    1. Use an 8 mm wrench to remove the screw.
    2. Pull the leak sensor out slightly, then slide and rotate it to the left to clear the fiber-optic breakout boxes.
    3. Pull the leak sensor out completely.
  5. Remove the DMOD covers from the DMOD cable trays by loosening the six screws (1) securing each cover and sliding them (2) off. Remove the DMOD cables from the slots in the DMOD cable trays.
  6. Remove the bolts securing the PET/MR insert:
    1. At the patient end, use an 8 mm combination wrench to remove the four M5 bolts and washers (1) from the base of the L-bracket insert support assembly, and set the hardware aside.
      Note

      The M5 bolts connect to a bolt capture fixture assembly (2) under the mount (3). Grip the handle of the fixture assembly when removing the bolts to prevent it from sliding under the mount.

    2. At the service end, use a 17 mm combination wrench to remove the four M10 bolts (1) and tube supports (2) from the L-bracket insert support assembly attached to the PET/MR insert, and set the hardware aside.
      Note

      It is necessary to lift the insert off the tube supports by raising the installation tool.

  7. To lower or raise the tool at the service end, use the jack screw handle (1) to rotate the screw jack (2) on the magnet interface.
  8. To lower or raise the tool at the patient end, use the jack screw handle (1) to rotate the screw jack (2) on the tripod. The M10 socket-head cap screw (3) is used for left/right adjustment.
  9. Ensure that the clearance around the insert is the same around the circumference of the insert.
  10. Roll the PET/MR insert out of the magnet bore for service.
    Note

    When the PET/MR insert is removed from the bore, make sure that cables and hoses do not get caught, tangled, or kinked. Avoid bringing the insert to a point that may cause stress on any cables or hoses.

  11. Once the insert is outside the bore, tighten the holding bolts.
Topic ID: id_15244176

Finalization

Procedure

  1. (For a new PET/MR insert) : Remove the detector modules from the insert. The detector modules will be placed on the new insert. See PET/MR Insert Replacement.
  2. Perform any necessary service and tests.
  3. Install the PET/MR insert.
  4. Remove LOTO from the PDUs in the PET and PGR cabinets and restore power. See Removing LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
