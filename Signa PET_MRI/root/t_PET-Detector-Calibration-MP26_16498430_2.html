Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

DANGER
Radiation exposure
The phantoms used for calibrating the SIGNA PET/MR system contain a radioactive substance.
Special training and compliance with facility guidelines are required to handle these phantoms.
Warning
Thermal stability
PET calibration requires system voltage and thermal stability.
Wait 30 minutes following PET ring power-up from being off more than 1 hour. Wait at least 1 hour after PET chiller power-up, especially from a warm equipment room.

About this task

This procedure calibrates the entire PET detector module ring, or integrates a single detector module. A sequence of tasks (described below) is done during this calibration procedure to bring all detectors into uniform specification.

Even though the user interface allows calibration of individual PET detector modules, all modules must be selected for initial calibration.

  • Map (also known as crystal maps) makes sure distinct matrices exist for every crystal block (9 x 4) in the system.
  • Energy sets thresholds for each crystal’s energy spectrum and is updated automatically during Silicon PhotoMultiplier SiPM gain.
  • Bias or Gain makes sure of uniform (SiPM) gain within a detector module and also across all detector modules in the ring.
  • Source Position accounts for any off-centeredness of the phantom in the timing calibration.
  • Timing makes sure of uniform time measurement for each crystal’s coincidence events.

Annulus phantom setup

Procedure

  1. Press Home to send the patient cradle to the home position, then lower or undock the table and detach the cradle from the LPCA.
  2. Hold down Table-In on the operator control panel for about 3 seconds. The LPCA will start traveling to the back of the bridge. When the LPCA starts its travel, the user can release the table-in button and LPCA travel will continue to the back. The LPCA travel to the back of the bridge takes more than 3 seconds.
    Note The operator control panel is white, not black as shown.
  3. Insert the posts on the annulus phantom holder into the holes on the LPCA, and push the holder until it clicks into place.
  4. Put the annulus phantom in the phantom holder.

PET detector calibration

Procedure

  1. From the header area of the screen, click the Tools icon .
  2. Click the Service Desktop Manager tab.
  3. From the service desktop manager, click Service Browser.
  4. From the MR Service Desktop, select PET.
  5. From the PET common service desktop browser, select Detector Calibration under the Calibrations tab.
  6. Click each View Detail box in these sections of the screen: Current Selected Cal Files, Module Selection, and Calibration Information to show the summary.
    Note To refine the calibration files selected:
    • Click the tabs (crystal map cal, gain cal, energy cal, or ctc cal) to view the results of the currently selected calibration.
    • In the Module Selection section, all modules should already be selected for calibration and show as green by default.
  7. If the calibration is for a new system or if one or more modules have been replaced, a cathode search is required before proceeding. To do a cathode search, use the Select Action menu in the Module Selection section, choose Cathode Search, and then follow the prompts.
  8. In the Calibration Information section, select Detector Cal from the Calibration Type menu, then click the With Review radio button.
    • Without Review (default): Suggested for new users. Takes care of when to complete the sequence and save calibration data.
    • With Review: Requires monitoring of the intermediate calibration results and deciding whether to continue or abort the scan. Also, manually saves the calibration data.
    Note For multiple iterations, click the Without Review button. If Without Review is selected, the calibration steps continue automatically.
  9. Select the Stop on Counts acquisition type for one iteration.
    • Stop on Counts (default): Suggested for new users. Accounts for phantom life and acquires for as long as needed.
    • Stop on Time: Can be selected from the menu. Click Up or Down to set the Acquisition Time in Minutes.
      • 1 to 2 minutes for a new phantom (6 months or less)
      • 4 minutes for phantoms 6 months to 1 year
      • 6 to 8 minutes for phantoms older than 1 year
    Note Stop on counts is only available for software version 26 and later.
  10. Type the number for the Iteration:
    • 8 for a new system, not yet calibrated
    • 1 for a previously calibrated system
  11. Click the Start button.
  12. Make sure all personnel have left the scan room.
  13. When the Take PET Calibration Instructions screen shows, click Start PET Scan.
  14. The PET scan begins.
    • The Detector Calibration Process flowchart shows the system progress.
    • The Acquisition Status area updates in real-time and the system shows messages in the status area.
    • When the calibration is complete, a message shows whether the scan completed correctly.
    • The system automatically saves the new Crystal map, Gain, Energy, and CTC cal file.
  15. After the PET scan is completed, review the data.
    1. From the Current Selected Cal Files area, select View Detail.
      • Crystal map
      • SiPM gain
      • Energy
      • Coincidence Timing Calibration (CTC)

Crystal map

Procedure

  1. A new Crystal Map calibration file is generated. Select the Crystal Map tab to review the data.
    The slide bar can be dragged to scroll to the desired module and block.
  2. Review the calibration log in the Cal UI message section. There should be 560 blocks calibrated; if not, another iteration is required.
    Note To automatically cycle through all maps sequentially, select the Browse check box. The numbered slider under Browse selects the speed of the automatic review.
  3. The crystal alignment should have a "peaks" + symbol around the circles as shown in the figure. Otherwise, more iterations of the detector cal are needed.
    • The block is roughly divided into 36 areas in a 9 x 4 matrix or crystal block.
    • Each area should have a blue crosshair (+) positioned over the area of peak response.

SiPM gain

Procedure

Select the SiPM Gain tab at the top of the screen. The min-max Delta Anode at the module level should be ± 4. If not, another iteration is required.
  • The min-max of five unit-midrange anode DAC values should be about 120.
  • The min-max Delta Anode DAC at Module Level should be within ± 4 range if the gain calibration has converged. Otherwise, more iterations of the detector cal are needed.

Energy

Procedure

  1. Select the Energy tab at the top of the screen. After SiPM gain calibration is converged, the data peak should be between 180 and 240, depending on each individual crystal.
  2. Click Continue, and calibration proceeds for the final iteration of Crystal Map and Energy/Bias calibrations.
    Note The new Crystal Map calibration file will be downloaded to the PET detector(s).
  3. Click Continue to begin Coincidence Timing Calibration (CTC).

Coincidence Timing Calibration (CTC)

About this task

CTC applies only to software versions 26 and later.

Procedure

  1. Select the CTC tab at the top of the screen, and review the CTC results. The mean value of CTC Delta T should be within ± 0.01 and the standard deviation within ± 0.7. If not, another iteration of CTC is required.
    • The timing difference delta should be centered at 0 with a standard deviation of 1.0 or less.
    • The time difference distribution line should be straight.
  2. Click Save to save all calibration files as default.
  3. Click Exit to close the PET Calibration screen.

    Result

    If gain convergence has not been reached:
    1. In the Calibration Information pane, select Detector Cal under Calibration Type.
    2. Click Without Review and enter 8 iterations.
    3. Click Start and follow the prompts.

Doing the Coincidence Timing Calibration (CTC)

About this task

Use this procedure to correct for timing differences in the detector electronics. The CTC runs automatically during the Detector Calibration sequence. If the mean and standard deviation of timing differences from the CTC are not satisfied, or if PET Daily Quality Assurance shows the Timing change failed, a new standalone CTC is needed.
Note When replacing one or more modules, a more uniform CTC calibration will result if CTC Reset is applied prior to execution of CTC calibration. Do a CTC Reset by selecting Reset CTC on the Select Action menu under the Module Selection section.

Procedure

  1. Load the PET Annulus Phantom. See Annulus Phantom Setup.
  2. From the header area of the screen, click the Tools icon.
  3. Click the Service Desktop Manager tab.
  4. Click Service Browser.
  5. From the MR Service Desktop, select PET.
  6. From the PET Service Desktop, click Calibration.
  7. Select Detector Calibration.
  8. From the PET Calibration screen, in the Calibration Information area, select the View Detail box.
    • (For software version 30.1 and later) The system automatically defaults to Stop on counts, Without Review, and an Iteration of 1. A previously calibrated system should only require 1 iteration to converge to a delta of ± 0.7 or less.
    • (For software version 26) The system automatically defaults to an 8-minute, 1-iteration acquisition.
  9. Change to a 1-to-2 acquisition time and reevaluate the timing delta. Repeat this process until the delta is ± 0.7 or less, making sure to click Set as Default between each re-run.

    • 1 to 2 minutes for a new phantom (6 months or less)

    • 4 minutes for phantoms 6 months to 1 year

    • 6 to 8 minutes for phantoms older than 1 year

  10. Select CTC from the menu for the Calibration Type.
  11. (For software version MP26 and later) With Stop On Counts selected under Acquisition Type, enter 3 in the Iteration field.
    Note Stop on counts is only available for software version 26 and later.
  12. Make sure all personnel have left the scan room.
  13. Click Start.
    • The Timing box shows green during the CTC scan.
    • When the scan is complete, the system status area shows Scan completed normally, then processes and shows the CTC data.
  14. Make sure the status area shows Crystals not meeting EDFC criteria: 0.0% and Number of Blocks failed temperature check: 0.
    • The calibration data is automatically saved to the new cal files.
    • CTC limits are (mean, SD) ± at 0.01 ± 0.7.
    • To reject the data, click Start to re-run the CTC and try again.
  15. Click Exit to close the PET Calibration screen.
    • A message prompt shows Do you want to delete prior calibration files?
    • Follow your facility guidelines on the deletion of the files.
  16. Click Close to close the PET Service Desktop.
    Note PET can only do one task (Calibration, DQA, or Scan) at a time. Close all other open PET processes to start a new one.
  17. If the UI does not report that CTC has reached convergence, repeat Step 11 above.

  2. Continue to PET daily quality assurance check. See Calibration Sequence for PET/MR for the complete PET calibrations workflow.
