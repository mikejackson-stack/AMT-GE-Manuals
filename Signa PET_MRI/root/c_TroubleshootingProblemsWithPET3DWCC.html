Troubleshooting problems with PET 3D Well Counter Correction (WCC)

Troubleshooting problems that might occur when doing the PET 3D Well Counter Correction (WCC) procedure.

If the calculated Well Counter Correction (WCC) activity factor is outside of the 4.75 to 6.00 range, a warning message will appear. The WCC activity factor directly scales image activity units, including SUV. The Calibration errors would lead to incorrect image quantitation.

Potential causes related to the calibration procedure include:
  • Incorrect entry of activity or measurement times.
  • Incorrect or missing post-injection activity measurement entry.
  • Incorrect phantom volume entry.
  • Spilled activity that remains at high activity concentration on the outside of the phantom (which could affect AC map alignment).
  • Other spilled activity while filling (which would be omitted from the post-injection measurement).
  • Phantom inserted in bore in the incorrect direction.
Potential causes related to the dose calibrator settings include:
  • Inconsistent time reference between the dose calibrator and the scanner.
  • Incorrect isotope selection on the dose calibrator.
  • Atypical dose calibrator settings for F18.

Issues related to activity or time entry can be corrected with Edit Tracer. Other issues require re-filling the phantom and repeating the calibration.

If repeated WCC measurements produce activity factors that are consistent, but slightly out of range, it might indicate an atypical dose calibrator setting for F18. In this case, either (a) the dose calibrator should be adjusted, or (b) the activity factor should be accepted to account for potential error in the dose calibrator settings. The decision to override the out-of-range message should only be made with input from the site physicist.
