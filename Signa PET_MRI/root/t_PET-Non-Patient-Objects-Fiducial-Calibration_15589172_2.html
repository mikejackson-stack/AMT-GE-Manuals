The PET non-patient objects fiducial (NPOF) calibration checks the PET imaging.

Required conditions
If the Sphere Volumetric Quality Control (VQC) Phantoms are 2.5 to 4.0 years old, have the customer order a new phantom.
Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

DANGER
Radiation exposure
Exposure to radiation will occur when handling the VQC phantom.
Follow facility guidelines to handle the phantom.
Note The phantoms are a source of low-dose radiation (0.7 MBq Ge-68 per sphere for a total of 2.1 MBq when using three).
CAUTION
Crush/Pinch Hazard
Moving the LPCA or cradle top when exchanging a CMA coil with a CMA proxy can crush/pinch the user’s hand.
Do not move the LPCA or cradle while exchanging a CMA coil or proxy.

About this task

The PET non-patient objects fiducial (NPOF) calibration checks the PET imaging. This calibration uses theSphere Volumetric Quality Control (VQC) Phantoms and requires four calibration tasks for:

  • Cradle and HNU coil

  • CMA coil and CMA proxy

  • HNU and LPCA

Note If the site does not have a CMA proxy, then the CMA Proxy test can be skipped.
Topic ID: id_SL15589351-1192088

PET scan for NPOF calibration

Procedure

  1. Retrieve the CMA coil and proxy from their storage location.
  2. Click the new patient icon and enter the patient information.
    1. Type NPOF for Patient Name.
    2. Type NPOF Cal for the Patient ID.
      Note If this is a new installation without IIS passed, use the patient ID geservice.
    3. Click Show All Protocols....
  3. Click Serviceon the Protocol Selector screen.
  4. Select MR-PET NPOF Calibration from the alphabetical list on the Protocol Selector screen, then click Accept.
  5. Click Start Exam on the patient information screen.
  6. Select 1st level mode for both dB/dt and SAR on the head SAR window.
  7. Choose to image a patient without implants.
  8. The NPOF Calibration Task screen displays with the first task, HNU-far end side, highlighted.
Topic ID: id_SL15589356-1192088

HNU-far end side calibration

About this task

Follow the protocol notes to set up the HNU-far end side calibration.

Procedure

  1. Set the landmark on the adapter.
  2. Retrieve the Sphere Volumetric Quality Control (VQC) Phantoms from their storage location. Set the spheres into the three holes at the top of the adapter.
  3. Click SaveRx, then Proceed, and then select Start PET from the pulldown next to Scan.
  4. Wait for the scan to complete.
    Note Scan time will depend on the age of the activity in the phantoms.
Topic ID: id_SL15589360-1192088

CMA calibration

About this task

Follow the protocol notes for the CMA calibration to place the phantoms on the CMA coil.

Procedure

  1. Select CMA from the list, then click Setup.
  2. Manually disengage the cradle from the LPCA.
  3. Pull the cradle to the rear of the table.
  4. Install the CMA coil.
  5. Place three Sphere Volumetric Quality Control (VQC) Phantoms in the coil holes. Do not place the phantoms on the phantom holder.
  6. Click Save Rx, then select Start PET from the drop-down next to Scan.
  7. Wait for the scan to complete.
    Note

    Scan time will depend on the age of the activity in the phantoms.

Topic ID: id_SL15589364-1192088

CMA Proxy calibration

About this task

Follow the protocol notes for the CMA calibration to set the PET spheres on the CMA proxy.

Note If the site does not have a CMA proxy, then the CMA Proxy test can be skipped.

Procedure

  1. Select CMA Proxy from the list, then click Setup.
  2. Remove the CMA coil and install the CMA proxy.
  3. Place three phantoms on the CMA proxy as shown in Sphere Volumetric Quality Control (VQC) Phantoms in CMA coil holes.
  4. Click Save Rx, then select Start PET from the drop-down next to Scan.
  5. Wait for the PET scan to complete.
    Note

    Scan time will depend on the age of the activity in the phantoms.

Topic ID: id_SL12024131-1192088

HNU-LPCA Side calibration

About this task

Follow the protocol notes for the HNU-LPCA side calibration.

Procedure

  1. Select HNU-LPCA Side from the list, then click Setup.
  2. Remove the CMA proxy and set the CMA coil.
  3. Press Home on the operator control panel to move the LPCA to the home position.
  4. Position the Sphere Volumetric Quality Control (VQC) Phantoms in the three holes on the head end of the cradle, and landmark as shown.
  5. Click Save Rx, then Scan.
    Note

    Scan time will depend on the age of the activity in the PET spheres.

  6. Return the phantoms, CMA coil, and CMA proxy to their storage location.
Topic ID: id_SL12024163-1192088

NPOF calibration tool

Procedure

  1. From Service Desktop Manager, click the service tools icon and then Service Browser to display the MR service desktop.
  2. Click the PET radio button on the MR service desktop.
  3. Select the Calibration tab.
  4. On the Common Service Desktop, select NPOF Position Cal to launch the NPOF calibration tool.
  5. From the Service Desktop Manager, click the Patient Browser icon.
  6. Select a PET image series from the list.
  7. Click LOAD IMAGE SERIES on the NPOF Calibration Tool screen.
  8. Click RUN CALIBRATION.
  9. When the calibration is completed, click SAVE RESULT.
  10. Repeat Step 6 through Step 9 for each remaining PET image series to complete the NPOF calibration.
  11. Click EXIT when all PET image series are processed.
