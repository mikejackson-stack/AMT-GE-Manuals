Adjusting the cradle home table down interlock

This procedure describes how to adjust the mechanical interlock, which prevents the patient table from being undocked or lowered while the cradle is in the magnet bore.

Prerequisites

About this task

This adjustment is for the mechanical interlock that prevents the patient table from being undocked or lowered while the cradle is in the magnet bore.

Procedure

  1. Undock the table.
  2. Fasten the upper bellows in the up position and remove both the lower base side cover and the front base cover from the patient table. See Fastening the patient table upper bellows and removing the lower base side covers.
  3. Raise the table to its maximum height.
  4. Warning
    Risk of personal injury
    Severe injury can occur if the table is accidentally lowered during maintenance.
    When doing maintenance with the table in the Up position, make sure the lock bar is safely engaged.
    Lower the table lock safety bar.
  5. Lower the table and make sure that all of the table top's weight is resting on the safety bar.
  6. With the cradle in the NOT HOME position (cradle off the flipper and in the up position), do a check of the interlock mechanisms for tolerances.
  7. To make adjustments, do one of the following:
    1. 0.79 to 1.59 mm (1/32 to 1/16 inch) gap - Adjust the angle between the link and the flipper with set screws.
      13.18 to 6.35 mm (1/8 inch to 1/4 inch) gap
      20.79 to 1.59 mm (1/32 inch to 1/16 inch) gap
      3Set screws
      4Flipper
      5Link
    2. 3.18 to 6.35 mm (1/8 inch to 1/4 inch) gap - Loosen the lock nut and tighten or loosen the cable adjustment screw to achieve the proper gap. Tighten the lock nut.
      1Fine adjustment screw
      2Lock nut
      3Coarse adjustment cable clamp screw
      Note The cable wraps twice through the clamp hole.
    Note If the adjustment screw does not provide enough range for adjustment, coarse adjustment may be made to the cable length where the cable end is clamped into the actuator bracket. Fine adjustment will be required after doing a coarse adjustment.
  8. Raise the table lock safety bar.

Finalization

With the cradle fully into the bore, make sure that the table down pedals do not let the table go down. Use both the table down pedal from the table and the dock. If the table does go down, do the adjustment procedures again.
