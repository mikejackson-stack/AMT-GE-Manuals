Setting up the Adaptive Image Receive (AIR) Anterior Array (AA) for MCQA

Set up the Adaptive Image Receive (AIR) Anterior Array (AA) for the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance (MCQA) check.

Prerequisites

The AIR AA coils for the MCQA use the 3.0T phantoms, which are pink in color.
Note Coils are not shipped with phantoms. Phantoms come in a unified phantom set with the system.

Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

Procedure

  1. Move the empty cradle from the home position until the display shows 1080 mm.
  2. Put two TL unified phantoms on the table with the laser light crosshair centered between the two phantoms. Make sure that there is no gap between the phantoms.
    ItemDescription
    11080 mm
    2Landmark position
  3. Put the PA phantom positioner on the two TL unified phantoms with the notch in the direction of the bore. Make sure that there are two patient straps on each side of the table.
  4. Set up the AIR AA coil:
    1. Put the AIR AA coil on top of the phantom positioner for the PA. Align the laser light with the crosshairs at the center (or GE logo) of the coil, to the existing landmark at 1080 mm.
    2. Secure the coil to the phantom positioner with Velcro straps. Put the straps over the tick marks on the coil or approximately 190 mm offset from the centerline.
      Note AIR 2.0 does not have tick marks on the coil.
    3. Connect the coil to port P4.
      Note Do not connect the coil to port P2. Running MCQA with the coil connected to P2 will produce false test failures.
  5. If the In-Room Display (IRD) prompts, set the center position of the AIR AA coil connected on port P4 with the Touch-and-Go strips on the side of the table.
  6. Landmark the AIR AA coil at the crosshair mark.
  7. Press Advance to Scan.
  8. Run the MCQA check.
    Note Select 3.0T_30Ch_AirAA_by_GE (or equivalent platform option) from the dropdown in the Current Coil field.
