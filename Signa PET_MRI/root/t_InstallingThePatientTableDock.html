Installing the patient table dock

Installs the magnet-side dock.

About this task

Warning
Back injury
The dock is heavy, weighing more than 50 pounds, and it contains ferrous material, making it strongly attracted to the magnet.
Two people are necessary to move the dock out of the magnet room to do maintenance. From this point in the procedure, downward pressure must be continuously applied to the dock to prevent it from being pulled into the magnet bore.
Warning
Possible personal injury
To oppose any pull from the magnet, field engineers must apply continuous downward pressure to the top of the dock, even when lifting the dock in this step.
Do not place any part of the human body in the path between the dock and the magnet.
Warning
Possible personal injury
The dock contains highly ferrous material.
Never lift the front of the dock (the portion of the dock farthest from the magnet) more than 7.6 cm (3 inches) from the floor. The rear of the dock (the portion of the dock closest to the magnet) should always remain in contact with the floor. Never lift the rear of the dock off the floor in the magnet room.
Warning
Possible personal injury
This procedure requires movement of ferrous material in close proximity to the magnet.
Two field engineers are required at all times.
Note When the dock is removed and reinstalled on the magnet, make sure the dock is level and aligned with the patient table.

Procedure

  1. With two field engineers (FEs), one holding each side of the dock, enter the magnet room and put the dock on the floor where the foot of the patient table would normally be located.
  2. Put the dock cables below the magnet, toward the service side of the magnet.
  3. With two field engineers (FEs), one holding each side of the dock, slowly and deliberately slide the dock toward the magnet front, keeping downward pressure on the top of the dock assembly.
  4. Put the dock in position to clear the floor stud. There are two ways to do this:
    1. If the site has followed the Pre-Installation Manual (PIM), then when reinstalling the dock it should be possible to remove the top portion of the stud. If this is the case, then continue to maintain pressure on the top of the dock and carefully slide the dock against the base of the magnet. Reinstall the top portion of the anchor stud.
    2. If the site has an anchor stud that protrudes vertically from the floor and cannot be removed, follow these instructions per the reverse directions shown in Positioning the dock when the anchor stud cannot be removed.
      1. While maintaining pressure on the top of the dock, position the dock against the base of the magnet to the side of the floor stud at the front of the dock (labeled as 3).
      2. Lift the dock just enough to clear the top of the stud in the floor (labeled as 2).
      3. While rotating the dock, align the hole at the base of the dock with the stud and put the dock down into position and re-anchor the dock (labeled as 1).
  5. Reattach the nuts and washers to the dock bracket.
    Note Take care with the washers that are put between the dock and the bracket. Make sure that the same number of washers is installed behind the dock on each bolt.
  6. Reconnect all cables from the rear of the dock to the magnet input/output (I/O) panels and the rear pedestal.
    Table 1. Cable connections (For MP24, MP26, and software version 30)
    Dock cableMagnet cableLocation
    MAG SRIJ3, J20Magnet side electronics plate
    MAG-DCK-LJ1, J2, J3, J4Front left magnet foot
    MAG-I/O DOCK PWRJ5M3364
    RFCBJ4Rear pedestal, slot 11
    RFSB2P4Rear pedestal, slot 1 and slot 2
    Table-Dock Hart IDJ57 RxDistMagnet side electronics plate
    Table 2. Cable connections (For software version 30.1 or later)
    Dock cableMagnet cableLocation
    DPP3J1, J2, J3Coil P3
    DPP4J1, J2, J3Coil P4
    MAG SRIJ3, J20Magnet side electronics plate
    MAG-DCK-LJ1, J2, J3, J4Front left magnet foot
    MAG-I/O DOCK PWRJ5M3364
    Table-Dock Hart IDJ57 RxDistMagnet side electronics plate
  7. Replace the front, bridge, and side covers in reverse order of removal.
  8. Remove the masking tape, which marks the end of the table and the position of the dock, from the floor.
  9. Dock the patient table.
  10. Remove RF Subsystem and/or PEN Cabinet LOTO. See Removing LOTO - RF Subsystem and/or PEN Cabinet.
