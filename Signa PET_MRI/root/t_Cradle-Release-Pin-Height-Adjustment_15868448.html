Adjusting the cradle release pin height

Adjust the cradle release pin to the correct height.

Prerequisites

Personnel requirements
Required personsPreliminary requirementsProcedureFinalization
1-25 minutes5 minutes
Tools and test equipment
ItemQuantityPart numberManufacturer
Nonferrous Medium Slotted Screwdriver1--
Safety
Warning
Strong magnetic field
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field that the magnet produces.
If you are working on the table in the magnet room, do not bring any ferrous tools or equipment into the magnet room.
Note It is recommended that you remove the table from the magnet room before you do any service tasks to the table.

About this task

This procedure provides instructions on how to adjust the cradle release pin to the correct height.

Procedure

  1. Undock patient table and remove it from the magnet room.
  2. Remove upper and lower base covers from the table. See Fastening the patient table upper bellows and removing the lower base side covers.
  3. Raise the table to its maximum height.
  4. Warning
    Risk of personal injury
    Severe injury can occur if the table is accidentally lowered during maintenance.
    When doing maintenance with the table in the Up position, make sure the lock bar is safely engaged.
    Lower the table lock safety bar.
  5. Lower the table and make sure that all of the table top's weight is resting on the safety bar.
  6. Remove the front base cover.
  7. At the same time, press the cradle latch block and push in the cradle release button at the front of the cradle.
  8. While holding down the latch block and pressing the cradle release button, slide the cradle forward enough so that the cradle covers the top area of the cradle latch block. Release the latch block and cradle release button.
  9. CAUTION
    Potential pinch hazard
    The forward movement of the cradle can pinch your fingers while you hold down the cradle latch block.
    Move the cradle forward slowly and deliberately. Move it until the bottom edge covers the top of the cradle latch block far enough that you can remove your fingers.
    Push in the right actuator on the table front base.
  10. Note

    Pressing the actuator button engages the secondary cradle latch cable and causes the pin stop to move down flush with the table top surface, letting the cradle slide forward.

    Prop up the cradle or remove the cradle from the patient table to do desired maintenance.
  11. Adjust the cable so that when the push rod button is pushed in 9.6 mm (0.375 inches), the pin stop is flush with the pin housing. Use the threaded cable adjuster for fine adjustment. Tighten the lock nut after the adjustment is completed, and then cut off the excess length of cable.
  12. Raise the table lock safety bar.
  13. Replace the cradle, front base cover, and upper and lower base covers.
  14. Return the table to the magnet room and dock the table.
  15. Drive the cradle into the magnet to expose the cradle release pin. Make sure that the release pin is flush to 2 mm (1/16 inch) below flush when the table is docked.

Finalization

  1. Dock the table.
  2. Make sure that the cradle release functions properly.
  3. Make sure that the LPCA connects to the cradle and that the cradle can move into the bore.
