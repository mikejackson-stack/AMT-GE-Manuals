E-stop bypass and communication check

Prerequisites

Table 1. Personnel requirements
Required personsPreliminary requirementsProcedureFinalization
1-2 minutes-
Table 2. Required conditions
ConditionReferenceEffectivity

The system must not be in an E-Stop condition, and power must be On to the PGR and PET Cabinets.

--

About this task

Overview

Pinging ensures the system can communicate with the PET Detector Distributed Modules (DMODs).

Topic ID: id_SL12616811-1200963

Bypassing the E-stop (if required)

Topic ID: id_SL12616812-1200963

PGR cabinet

Procedure

  1. Power OFF the PDU in the PGR cabinet.
  2. Disconnect Cable E3002 from connector J103 located on the connector panel on top of the PGR cabinet.
    Note 3002 and E3006 do not exist on systems with Cabmon 3.
  3. Connect the 15-pin sub-D female jumper to J103 on the PGR cabinet connector panel to bypass all external components (with the exception of the auxiliary module or power equipment monitor from the E-stop circuit).
    Note

    Jumpers are normally stored in the PGR cabinet. These come with the system and are not FRUs.

  4. Power On the PDU in the PGR cabinet.
  5. Press the green EMO Reset button on the front of the PDU to reset the E-stop circuit.
  6. Verify operation of the E-stop circuit, and confirm the RF amplifier and Patient Handling Power Supply (PHPS) are On.
Topic ID: id_SL12616821-1200963

PET cabinet

Procedure

  1. Refer to E-stop bypass to bypass the E-stop in the PET cabinet.
  2. Make sure that the leak detectors are connected to J1 and J2 on the leak detection circuit.
Topic ID: id_SL12616825-1200963

Communication check

Procedure

  1. Check that the PET detector indicator is green on the PET System Readiness Monitor screen.
  2. Open a C-shell prompt, and type telnet dfe 1727.
  3. Type ping (1) in the window that displays, and check that all DMODs are present (2).
Topic ID: id_15203199

Finalization

Finalization

Restore the E-stop circuit to the PGR and PET cabinets.
