Replacing the PAC and SRI-4

This procedure explains replacement of the physiological acquisition controller (PAC) assembly and the scan room interface (SRI-4) on a fixed site system.

Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or performing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

DANGER
Electrocution Hazard!
Dangerous voltages are present around the magnet enclosure and rear pedestal electronics.
Perform LOTO on the magnet enclosure. See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.

About this task

This procedure explains replacement of the physiological acquisition controller (PAC) assembly and the scan room interface (SRI-4) on a fixed site system.

The systems may have either a PAC-2BV or a PAC-5. The PAC-5 is backward compatible with the PAC-2BV. There is no impact with PAC hardware configuration in Guided Install.

Removing the PAC

Procedure

  1. Do LOTO on the RF amplifier and PEN cabinet. Refer to the MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  2. The PAC is behind the left side middle cover.
  3. Disconnect all cables from the I/O panel except the white PPG cable, which is permanently attached.
    1. Remove the left side covers to access the PAC. See Side and Top Cover Removal and Installation.
    2. Carefully pass the PPG cable through the opening in the left side middle cover. Avoid damage to the cable while removing the cover.

Installing the new PAC

Procedure

  1. Note

    If replacing a PAC-2BV with a PAC-5, use existing cables. Do not order a PAC-5 Y cable.

    Put the PAC in the proper location and install it in reverse order from how it was removed.
  2. Attach all previously removed cables to the replacement PAC.
  3. Replace all previously removed covers and enclosure trim panels.
  4. Check the alignment of the PAC with the enclosure in all three planes.

    • There are adjustment screws that allow for adjustment up/down, left/right, and in/out.

    • Adjust the PAC I/O panel so that it lines up with the slot in the enclosure in the left/right and up/down plane, and adjust it in/out so the gap between the I/O panel and the enclosure is less than 1 mm.

  5. Remove LOTO and restore power to the system. See Removing LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
Topic ID: id_SL2857025-1093049

Removing the SRI

Procedure

  1. Notice
    Before removing the SRI, the PDU must be powered down completely to prevent damage to system electronics.
    Do LOTO on the PGR PDU/gradient subsystem. Refer to the MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  2. The SRI is located under the left side of the magnet enclosure.

    • (For RRx receive chain systems) Toward the rear of the magnet. See General location of PAC and SRI (RRx systems).

    • (For DS-DPP receive chain systems) Toward the front of the magnet. See Location of PAC and SRI (DS-DPP systems).

  3. Remove the left side covers and expose the SRI. See Side Cover Removal and Installation.
  4. Remove the temperature sensor box attached to the SRI. (It will be reinstalled on the replacement SRI.)
  5. Disconnect the cables attached to the SRI.
  6. Remove the four screws securing the SRI to the electronics rack. Unstrap and remove the SRI.
  7. Remove the straps from the SRI. They will be used when installing the new SRI.
Topic ID: id_SL2857029-1093049

Installing the new SRI

Procedure

  1. Put the new SRI in the proper location, and install in the reverse order of how it was removed.
  2. Mount the removed temperature sensor box on the replacement SRI.
  3. Reconnect the cables and replace the enclosure panels.
    Note Improper cable routing can result in intermittent receive chain faults and noise cross-talk. Make sure the microphone cable from SRI (J21, J32) is not to close or touching the DS-DPP power cable (J7) or the DS-DPP itself. See Tx induced RxC fault caused by microphone cable.
  4. Remove LOTO and restore power to the system. Refer to the MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
Topic ID: id_15238359

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Do a TPS reset.
  2. Make sure that the reported problem has been resolved by running PAC or SRI diagnostics. In Service Methods, instructions for these diagnostics can be found in Troubleshooting > Diagnostics > Hardware Location > Magnet Room > PAC Diagnostic Tests and SRI Functional Diagnostics.
  3. Do a Check Scan.
