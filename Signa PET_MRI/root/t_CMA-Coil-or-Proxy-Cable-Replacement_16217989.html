About this task

Overview

Check the cable connections inside the CMA Coil and Proxy and from the Coil and Proxy to the system prior to performing this task.

Procedure

  1. Remove the four screws from the access cover on the CMA Coil or Proxy with a flat-blade screwdriver.
  2. Disconnect these connectors:

    • Labeled J1 through J18 inside the CMA Coil (shown below)

    • One connector for the CMA Proxy (shown in Connector for CMA Proxy)

  3. Remove the four screws from the strain relief that connects the system cable to the CMA Coil or Proxy.
  4. Remove the two screws that connect the strain relief to the CMA Coil or Proxy cable.
  5. Reinstall the cable to the CMA Coil or Proxy in the reverse order of the above steps, referring to Connectors J1 through J18 on CMA Coil.
  6. To reinsert the cable on the CMA Coil or Proxy, lift up on the heat shrink (so it does not catch on the boards inside), and plug the cable into the coil or proxy.

Finalization

(For CMA Coil) Run the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance Tool to verify the CMA Coil operates properly.

(For CMA Proxy) Plug the CMA Proxy into the system to ensure the Proxy ID is recognized.
