Procedure

  1. LOTO the PDUs in the PGR and PET cabinets. See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
  2. Notice
    Several cables are hidden behind the rear end bell. Be careful when removing the end bell to avoid inadvertently pulling on any cables.
    Remove the two 2-inch blower hoses from the insert.
  3. Pull back the quick-disconnect sleeves (1) of the fiber-optic lines (2) to remove the fiber-optic lines from the insert.
  4. Remove the leak sensor.
    1. Trace the leak sensor cable from the insert to the leak sensor box on the rear pedestal.
    2. Remove the screw.
    3. Extract the leak sensor from the bore, maneuvering the leak sensor up and around the fiber optics.
      Note

      It is not necessary to fully disconnect the leak sensor to remove the end bell.

  5. Remove the four screws that secure the rear end bell to the arm brackets.
  6. Tip the end bell out of the scanner.
  7. Disconnect the four-inch patient air hose (1), the bore light cables (2), and the speaker cable (3) from the end bell.
  8. Rotate the end bell 90° clockwise.
  9. Pivot the rear end bell so that the open end is clear of the two rear end bell stand-offs.
  10. Remove the rear end bell.

Finalization

  1. Proceed to Rear End Bell Installation.

    Confirm that the following procedures are completed during rear end bell installation:

    • Reinstall the middle bridge

    • Reinstall the rear pedestal with LPCA

    • Reinstall the covers

  2. Remove LOTO from PDUs in the PGR and PET cabinets. See Removing LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
  3. Restore power.
