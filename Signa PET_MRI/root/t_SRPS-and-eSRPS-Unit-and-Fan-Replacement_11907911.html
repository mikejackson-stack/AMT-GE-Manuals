About this task

Overview

This document describes the procedure for replacing the Scan Room Power Supply (SRPS) and EcoPower Scan Room Power Supply (eSRPS).

Because of its weight, removing the SRPS from the PEN cabinet requires you to use the hoist service tool. The hoist service tool allows one person to perform this procedure. This is the preferred method to avoid injury.

The eSRPS is light enough to be removed by one person without the use of a hoist.

This procedure also describes fan replacement for both the SRPS and eSRPS.

SRPS Removal

Procedure

  1. Apply LOTO to the PEN cabinet. See Applying LOTO - PEN Cabinet (magnet room electronics).
  2. Remove the cables from the front of the SRPS.
  3. Loosen the 8 thumb screws on the front of the SRPS.
  4. Set up the hoist service tool according to Hoist Service Kit and Lifting Accessories.
  5. Pull the SRPS out from the PEN cabinet until the slide rails lock into position.
  6. Remove the cables from the rear of the SRPS.
  7. Attach a lifting bracket from the FRU container to each side of the SRPS.
  8. Attach the spreader bar (hoist kit) to the lifting brackets. Attach the spreader bar to the hoist chain.
  9. Ratchet the winch to tighten the hoist chain.
    Note Before putting weight on the hoist chain, be sure that the links are aligned properly. This helps keep the chain from binding up, or becoming kinked, which is nearly impossible to correct when the chain is under tension.
    Warning
    Potential crush hazard
    If the replacement part accidentally falls, it can cause serious injury.
    Do not put any part of the body below the replacement part.
  10. Press the release tab and pull out the SRPS to release it from the rail.
  11. Release the PEN cabinet outer slide rails and slide them back into the cabinet.

    You may want to use a screwdriver to release the outer slide rail.

  12. Lower the SRPS onto the FRU packaging platform for removal.
  13. Loosen the hoist chain and remove the spreader bar.
  14. Remove the lifting brackets.
  15. Remove both slide rails from the SRPS.
  16. If present, remove the dongle from J50 of the failed SRPS unit to transfer to the new SRPS unit.
Topic ID: id_SL4476946-1060200

SRPS Replacement

Procedure

  1. Note

    After the SRPS is replaced, the final system configuration should have a dongle (5172838-2) on the new SRPS at J50, or on the PEN panel at J50 from the magnet room side.

    Attach both slide rails to the new SRPS.
  2. Attach both lifting brackets to the new SRPS. See Attach Lifting Brackets.
  3. Attach the spreader bar (hoist kit) to the lifting brackets. Attach the spreader bar to the hoist chain. See Spreader Bar - Hoist Kit.
  4. Ratchet the winch to tighten the hoist chain and begin lifting the SRPS.
  5. Slide out the PEN cabinet slide rails a short distance to see how high to lift the module.
  6. Notice
    The slide rails for both the SRPS and PEN cabinet must be aligned for proper insertion. Take care not to damage the roller bearings on the mounted slide rails.
    After the SRPS is aligned with the outer rails, pull out each PEN cabinet outer slide rail fully, until each locks into position.
  7. Attach the cables to the rear of the SRPS. See SRPS Rear with J51 and J52 Highlighted. Ensure that J51 and J52 are installed correctly.
  8. Loosen the chain tension with the winch.
  9. Remove the spreader bar from the lifting brackets.
  10. Remove the lifting brackets.
  11. Press the release tab on each rail and carefully slide the SRPS completely into the PEN cabinet slide rails until they lock into position.
  12. Tighten the 8 thumb screws on the front of the SRPS.
  13. Refer to the Finalization section for checkout and proper operations.

SRPS Fan Replacement

Procedure

  1. Apply LOTO to the RF and PEN cabinets. Applying LOTO - RF Subsystem (magnet room electronics) and Applying LOTO - PEN Cabinet (magnet room electronics).
  2. Determine which fan you are replacing (see SRPS Front Fans and SRPS Rear Fans).

    • If replacing fan 5 or 6 (front fans), see Replacing Front Fans.

    • If replacing fan 1, 2, 3, or 4 (rear fans), see Replacing Rear Fans.

      Note

      If replacing fan 1, 2, 3, or 4, use the lifting fixture and remove the SRPS from the cabinet. Service access to fans is restricted when the power supply is left on the rails; fan replacement is possible but difficult.

Topic ID: id_SL2077083-1060200

Replacing Front Fans

Procedure

  1. Remove the top 2 screws from the fan cover.
  2. Disconnect the appropriate cable using quick release. If you are unable to access the cable, first disconnect all cabling routed to the front of the SRPS. Then, remove the top cover by sliding the SRPS out of the cabinet and removing 3 screws on each side.
  3. Remove the fan assembly from the fan cover by removing 2 screws.
    Note

    Pay careful attention to the label orientation on the fan. When installing the new fan, verify that the label is in the same orientation.

  4. Install the new fan.
  5. Reconnect all cabling appropriately.
  6. Replace all covers.
  7. Restore power. See Removing LOTO - RF Subsystem and/or PEN Cabinet.
  8. Check the Fault light on the SRPS to verify that it is not lit.

    If the Fault light is lit, check all cable connections.

  9. Refer to the Finalization section for checkout and proper operation.
Topic ID: id_SL2077089-1060200

Replacing Rear Fans

Procedure

  1. Disconnect all cables routed to the front of the SRPS. See SRPS Front.
  2. Slide the SRPS completely out of the PEN cabinet by removing 8 thumb screws.
  3. Note

    When replacing fan 1, 2, 3, or 4, use the lifting fixture and remove the SRPS from the cabinet. Service access to fans is restricted when the power supply is left on the rails; fan replacement is possible but difficult.

    Disconnect the cable connections in the rear to access the fans.
  4. Remove the top 2 screws from the fan cover.
  5. Disconnect the appropriate cable using quick release. If you are unable to access the cable, remove the top cover by loosening a screw on each side.
  6. Remove the fan assembly from the fan cover by removing 2 screws. An example is shown in Removing Fan Assembly.

    Pay careful attention to the label orientation on the fan. When installing the new fan, verify that the label is in the same orientation.

  7. Install the new fan.
  8. Reconnect all cabling appropriately
  9. Replace all covers.
  10. Restore power and remove LOTO from RF/PEN cabinets. See Removing LOTO - RF Subsystem and/or PEN Cabinet.
  11. Check the Fault light on the SRPS to verify that it is not lit.

    If the Fault light is lit, check all cable connections.

  12. Refer to the Finalization section for checkout and proper operation.

eSRPS Removal

Procedure

  1. Apply LOTO to the PEN cabinet. See Applying LOTO - PEN Cabinet (magnet room electronics).
  2. Remove the cables from the front of the eSRPS.
  3. Loosen the 6 thumb screws on the front of the SRPS.
  4. Pull out the eSRPS from the PEN cabinet until the slide rails lock into position.
  5. Press the release tabs and pull out the eSRPS to release it from the rails.
  6. Remove both slide rails from the eSRPS.
Topic ID: id_SL4476969-1060200

eSRPS Replacement

Procedure

  1. Install the slide rails on the new eSRPS unit.
  2. Connect the eSRPS rails to the mounting rails in the PEN cabinet, and slide the eSRPS into the cabinet.
  3. Use the thumbscrews on the front of the eSRPS to secure the unit to the cabinet mounting rails.
  4. Connect the cables to the front of the eSRPS.
  5. Restore power to the PEN cabinet. See Removing LOTO - RF Subsystem and/or PEN Cabinet.
Topic ID: id_SL4611912-1060200

eSRPS Fan Replacement

Procedure

  1. Apply LOTO to the PEN cabinet. See Applying LOTO - PEN Cabinet (magnet room electronics).
  2. Remove the eSRPS from the PEN cabinet. See eSRPS Removal.
  3. Remove the top cover of the eSRPS by removing the screws securing it to the power supply.
  4. Disconnect the fan cable.
  5. Remove the screws securing the fan to the rear of the eSRPS.
  6. Attach the new fan to the inside rear of the eSRPS.
  7. Connect the fan cable. See Step 4.
  8. Attach the top cover of the eSRPS with screws.
  9. Install the eSRPS in the PEN cabinet. See eSRPS Replacement.
  10. Restore power to the PEN cabinet. See Removing LOTO - RF Subsystem and/or PEN Cabinet.
Topic ID: id_15259687

Finalization

Procedure

  1. After replacing the unit, perform a quick scan to ensure proper functionality.
  2. After fan replacement, check the error log to verify that there is no longer a fan failure.
