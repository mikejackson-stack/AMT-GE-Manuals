About this task

Overview

This procedure describes how to align the bridges, dock, and patient table on a SIGNA PET/MR System. Proper bridge alignment is critical to image quality and reliability of the table components.

Topic ID: id_SL11938256-1192084

Aligning the front bridge

Procedure

  1. Notice
    The number in parentheses within an instruction corresponds to the numbered item in the referenced illustration.
    Use a 10 mm Allen wrench to bolt each bridge into place with two M12 x 80 mm Long Brass Socket-Head Cap Screws and two M12 x 40 mm Long Brass Hex Socket-Head Cap Screws for each bridge. Torque screws to 24 ± 2 Nm (Reference only).
  2. Remove the alignment block between the front and middle bridge sections, and return the Shoulder Screws to the Bridge Alignment Block Assembly Kit.
Topic ID: id_SL11938260-1192084

Aligning the rear bridge

Procedure

  1. Turn the four rear pedestal feet to raise or lower the rear bridge until it is even with the middle bridge, and use a straight edge to make sure the two bridges are level.
  2. Use a level to make sure the bridge on the rear pedestal is level in the Z-direction, adjusting the rear pedestal feet as necessary to level the bridge.
  3. Connect the middle and rear bridges by fitting the pins on the middle bridge into the holes in the rear bridge, making sure there is no gap between them.
  4. Use an 8 mm Allen wrench to attach the two Bridge Connecting Plates at the bottom of the rear and middle bridges with four M10 x 25 mm Socket-Head Allen Screws and M10 SS Washers (Item 1 in illustration below). Torque screws to 13 ± 1 Nm (Reference only).
Topic ID: id_SL11938370-1192084

Dock adjustments

About this task

This procedure provides the longitudinal (in and out) and lateral (left to right) adjustments to center the dock. For proper patient table docking, the dock assembly must be adjusted to meet these criteria:

  • Gap between the patient table top and front of bridge magnet must be 1.22 in., ± 0.24 in. (31.0 mm, ± 6 mm). This ensures the gap is narrow enough to allow for smooth travel, but large enough to avoid rubbing on the covers or bridge, as well as avoid any pinch hazard. It also ensures the Low Profile Carriage Assembly (LPCA) hook can travel to its designated latch on the cradle.

  • Dock assembly must be square to the bridge, so the axis of cradle travel is the same for both the patient table and bridge.

  • Bosses of dock and patient table align to ensure proper rotary pump alignment.

Topic ID: id_SL11938665-1192084

Longitudinal adjustment

Procedure

  1. Dock the patient table.
  2. Measure the distance from the patient table top to the front bridge, which should be 1.22 in., ± 0.24 in. (31.0 mm, ± 6 mm). (Distance should be equal to each other so the patient table top is aligned with the bridge.)
  3. If adjustment is required, move the dock assembly either towards or away from the magnet, and re-measure until the proper gap is achieved.
Topic ID: id_SL11938672-1192084

Lateral adjustment

Procedure

  1. Use the table up (↑) pedal to raise the patient table to maximum height.
  2. Dock the patient table.
  3. Drive the cradle all the way into the magnet bore, and perform dock lateral adjustments until the vertical wheel tracks are aligned right and left.
  4. Align the patient table with the front bridge (left to right) using the vertical wheel tracks and the inner corner edges of the table and bridge as alignment points.
Topic ID: id_SL12453619-1192084

Dock hook tension adjustment

Procedure

  1. Dock the patient table by depressing the dock pedal, focusing on the amount of force required to engage the docking mechanism. (Initial docking should not require significant force to depress dock pedal.)
  2. CAUTION
    Do not attempt to fully engage the dock pedal if a strong force is needed.
    The hook can become overstressed and bend, requiring its replacement.
    When depressing the dock pedal, if the force needed was other than a crisp snap at the bottom of the stroke, then one of two conditions exist and action is required:

    • Too Loose - Minimal or no force was needed to reach the bottom of the stroke.

    • Too Tight - Considerable to significant force was needed to reach the bottom of the stroke, resulting in a strong snap at the end.

      Note

      Both conditions described above require the same action of loosening the set screw.

  3. Undock the patient table before making any hook adjustments.
  4. Adjust the dock hook tension:
    1. Loosen the lock nut at the rear of the hook.
    2. Loosen the set screw shown below.
    3. Adjust the hook by turning the hook clockwise to tighten or counterclockwise to loosen. (One full turn is equal to 1/16 in.)
    4. Repeat the required adjustment until a crisp snap of the hook is achieved when docking the table, and retighten the set screw.
    Note

    The hook always has to face down to the floor after adjustment, otherwise it will not engage at the dock.

  5. Record the number of full turns of the dock hook in the Installation and Calibration Wizard.
