Centering the dock and adjusting the dock hook tension

This dock adjustment procedure is required to properly align the cradle to the bridge in both the Z and X directions so that the cradle travels smoothly and protects table components from premature wear.

Safety
DANGER
Fatal electric shock hazard
To prevent fatal electric shock, disconnect power from the Power Distribution Unit (PDU) before you do any procedure that requires you to remove the dock.
See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
Warning
Possible personal injury
This procedure requires movement of ferrous material in close proximity to the magnet.
Two field engineers are required at all times.
Warning
Personal injury and equipment damage
Strong magnetic field.
When servicing any magnetic equipment, it is critically important that the service engineer consciously plan the path to take when moving highly ferrous devices in the magnet environment. The path should be as far from the magnet as practical and avoid high flux-density fields.

Safety Requirements

  • The service path must allow for the dock to be moved along the ground a distance equal to that of the distance between the magnet and the foot-end of the patient table prior to being lifted vertically.
  • Two (2) MR safety trained personnel must be present at all times when servicing highly ferrous devices in the areas of magnetic fields.
  • When planning a service path, it is critical that the path be clear and sufficiently wide. Make sure that there are no trip hazards, obstacles, clutter, slippery surfaces, or other items even partially restricting the path. If there are portable obstacles in a path, remove them from the area and replace them after the service action is completed. It is required to walk the path prior to beginning service to make sure that there is sufficient space through which to pass for you and the object being serviced.

About this task

This dock adjustment procedure is required to properly align the cradle to the bridge in both the Z and X directions. Proper dock alignment makes sure that the cradle travels smoothly and protects table components from premature wear.

Centering the dock

About this task

This procedure describes the longitudinal adjustment (in and out) and the lateral adjustment (left to right) to center the dock. For proper patient table docking, the dock assembly must be adjusted to meet these criteria:
  • The gap between the leading edge of the cradle top and the front of the bridge must be 19.0 mm ± 6.4 mm (0.75 inch ± 0.25 inch). This makes sure that the gap is narrow enough for smooth travel, but large enough to avoid rubbing on covers or the bridge and to avoid a pinch hazard. See Table dock measurement.
  • The dock assembly must be square to the bridge, so the axis of the cradle travel is the same for both the patient table and bridge.
  • The bosses of the dock and the patient table must align to make sure that the rotary pump is properly aligned.
Topic ID: id_SL1766530-1045946

Longitudinal adjustment

Procedure

  1. With the patient table docked, measure the distance from the patient table top to the front of the magnet bridge. The distance should be 19.0 mm ± 6.4 mm (0.75 inch ± 0.25 inch). Also, the distance from the patient table top to the front of the magnet bridge should be equal so the patient table top is colinear with the bridge.
  2. If adjustment is required:
    1. Follow the precautions listed in Replacing the dock when you are working with the dock.
    2. Add or remove washers at the dock assembly and measure until the proper gap is achieved.
Topic ID: id_SL1766533-1045946

Lateral adjustment

Procedure

  1. Raise the table to maximum height using the UP foot pedal.
  2. Dock the patient table.
  3. Drive the cradle all the way into the bore. Do the dock lateral adjustment until the vertical wheel tracks are aligned right and left.
  4. Check the alignment of the patient table against the bridge (left-to-right alignment only). Use the vertical wheel tracks to line up the table with the magnet bridge. Use the inner edge corners of the table and magnet bridge as alignment points. Height is adjusted using the Adjusting the table top height procedure.
  5. If adjustment is required, loosen the nuts on the dock bracket (shown in Dock positioning adjustment) to the rear of the dock (shown in Rear of dock and lateral bolt holes). Move the dock left or right to the proper position so the wheel tracks line up.

Boss alignment

About this task

The goal for the boss alignment is to have the center of each boss be the same between the dock and the table. This alignment makes sure that the rotary pump and the dock connector are properly aligned.

Procedure

  1. Dock the patient table.
  2. Inspect the bosses on both sides of the table and dock for concentric alignment.
    Note A missing front table cover, as shown in Inspecting the bosses for concentric alignment may result in 16-channel systems reporting a 32-channel electrical connector not attached. When adjustment is complete, replace the front cover to prevent these errors.
  3. Adjust the table casters as necessary to make sure that alignment in the vertical direction is concentric. The final distance between the dock boss and the table boss should be ± 1.0 mm. (See Leveling the patient table.)

Adjusting the dock hook tension

Procedure

  1. Dock the patient table using the DOCK pedal, paying close attention to the force required to engage the docking mechanism. Initial docking should not require significant force to depress the dock pedal.
  2. Note the following dock pedal conditions:

    When you depress the dock pedal, if the force needed is other than a crisp snap at the bottom of the pedal stroke, then one of two conditions are in effect and action is required:

    • Too loose - Minimal or no force at all is needed to reach the bottom of the stroke.
    • Too tight - Considerable to significant force is needed to reach the bottom of the stroke, resulting in a hard, strong snap at the end.
      Note Both conditions (too loose and too tight) require the same action of loosening the set screw.
  3. Notice
    Potential equipment damage
    The hook can become overstressed and bend, requiring replacement.
    Do not attempt to fully engage the dock pedal if the force needed is too strong.
    Loosen the set screw. (See Dock hook tension adjustment.)
  4. Notice
    Count the number of turns made in this step. Tightening (turning the hook clockwise) is a positive number; loosening (turning the hook counterclockwise) is a negative number.
    Adjust the hook by loosening the lock nut at the rear of the hook, and turning the hook clockwise (to tighten) or counterclockwise (to loosen). One full turn is equal to 1/16 inch.
    Note The patient table must be undocked for all hook adjustments. Make the appropriate number of turns on the hook, retighten the lock nut, and then retest the force needed on the dock pedal when docking the table.
  5. Repeat Step 4 until a crisp snap of the hook is achieved when docking the patient table.
  6. Retighten the set screw.

Finalization

Finalization

  1. Replace all covers.
  2. Dock the table.
  3. Make sure that the cradle release function works properly.
  4. Make sure that the LPCA connects to the cradle and that the cradle can move into the bore.
  5. Do Verifying the patient table movement.
