About this task

Overview

This procedure details installing a PET/MR Insert when:

  • Upgrading from an MR750w to a SIGNA PET/MR System

  • Installing a new SIGNA PET/MR System

Topic ID: id_SL11881337-1191725

Installation

Procedure

  1. Locate and unpack the PET/MR Insert from the Transport Cart outside of the Magnet Room, referring to the instructions (SIGNA PET/MR Transport Cart User Guide, Direction 5567849) supplied with the cart.
  2. LOTO the PDUs in the PGR and PET Cabinets, referring to the MR Service Safety Manual, Direction 5452735.
  3. Refer to ‘PET/MR Installation Tool Setup’ found under Replacements > System > PET/MR insert System on the SIGNA PET/MR 3.0T System Service Methods Direction 5490579-x.
  4. Notice
    The number in parentheses within an instruction corresponds to the numbered item in the referenced illustration.
    Make sure the scissor jack (1) on the cart is located opposite of the magnet bore and on the patient end of the Insert once it is brought into the Magnet Room.
  5. Warning
    Possible Personal Injury or Equipment Damage
    Unsecured hardware may interfere with PET/MR Insert Installation Tool operation, which could result in personal injury or equipment damage.
    Make sure all bolts and the cotter pin are installed and secured.
    Position the transport cart with the PET/MR Installation Tool in front of the magnet, insert the guide tube assembly into the bore, then insert the pivot pin on the guide tube into the magnet interface plate.
    Note

    It will be necessary to make adjustments to the cart height and/or the adjustment jack screw on the magnet interface plate.

  6. When the PET/MR Installation Tool is in place and secured:
    1. Remove the transport cart from the PET/MR Insert by turning the handle on the scissor jack counterclockwise to lower the frame from the Insert.
    2. Remove the cart from the Magnet Room.
Topic ID: id_SL11881338-1191725

Cable attachment

Procedure

  1. Make sure all DMOD cables are fastened into the holders (1) on the PET/MR Insert.
  2. Make sure that the DMOD cable bands are in the correct position. Two cable bands should be spaced evenly between the DMOD cable guides. One cable band should be spaced evenly between the DMOD cable guide and the DMOD power cable connector.
  3. Attach each DMOD power cable from the detector on the PET/MR Insert to the CPDA on the magnet as detailed in Table 4.
    Note

    Each CPDA cable has two DMOD numbers. Use the lower number (1) when connecting to the right CPDA and the higher number (2) when connecting to the left CPDA.

  4. Disconnect the RF Drive Cables from the Dynamic Transmit/Receive Switch (DTRSW) and attach the cables to J2 and J3 on the PET/MR Insert.
Topic ID: id_SL11881339-1191725

Positioning insert inside magnet bore

Procedure

  1. Position the PET/MR Insert on the Installation Tool in front of the magnet, and check for potential interference before proceeding. These areas provide the most opportunity for interference between the bore and the Insert:
    1. Fiber-optic box
    2. DMOD fluid end, including cooling hoses and fiber-optic cables
    3. DMOD itself
    4. Power cable routing
  2. Notice
    Rolling the PET/MR Insert into the magnet bore too quickly risks damaging the components, cables, and/or hoses. The magnet will attempt to pull the insert into the bore.
    Rotate and adjust the Installation Tool as needed to avoid interference, such as scraping the PET Detectors against the magnet or catching/pinching the coils or hoses.
  3. Notice
    One person should be at the service end of the magnet and the other at the patient end to watch as the Insert is positioned.
    Loosen the holding bolts from the interface plate, and slowly slide the PET/MR Insert into the magnet bore.
  4. At the service end, attach the PET/MR Insert to the L-brackets:
    1. Tighten the M10 bolts (1) on the inside of the L-brackets first.
    2. Tighten the M10 bolts (2) on the outside of the L-brackets second.
  5. At the patient end, attach the two L-brackets (1) of the PET/MR Insert (2) to the Carbon Mount (3).
Topic ID: id_SL11881341-1191725

Alignment and final tasks

Procedure

  1. Perform the PET/MR Insert Mechanical Alignment.
  2. Reattach the RF drive cables to the DTRSW.

    • Channel 1 on the DTRSW connects to J2 on the Insert.

    • Channel 2 connects to J3 on the Insert.

  3. Disconnect the service extension hoses, and attach the cooling hoses from the PET Chiller to the manifold hoses with the quick disconnects.
  4. Make sure all CPDA cables are fastened in order on both sides of the magnet bore, and the three clear shields are attached with their captive screws.
    Note

    Pay attention to the following illustration for correct cable placement.

Finalization

Finalization

Remove LOTO from the PDUs in the PGR and PET Cabinets and restore power.
