About this task

This document contains the procedures for front cover removal and installation. The front covers consist of two halves and contain the emergency stop and control panels for the system.

Instructions for replacing the light strips are also included.

Topic ID: id_SL3847945-1095035

Preliminary

Procedure

  1. Make sure that you have read and understood the electrical safety requirements before starting this procedure. See Electrical Safety.
  2. Apply RF Subsystem LOTO. See Applying LOTO - RF Subsystem (magnet room electronics).
  3. Apply PEN Cabinet LOTO. See Applying LOTO - PEN Cabinet (magnet room electronics).
  4. Undock the patient table and position the table away from the magnet.
Topic ID: id_SL3847977-1095035

Removing the front cover

About this task

The type and order of the side cover removal depends on the components accessed. For left or right front cover removal, remove the corresponding side middle and side front covers first.

Procedure

  1. Remove the side covers (see Side and top cover removal and installation).
    Note Remove screws in the order listed. Screw locations shown above are approximate and may be hidden under unremoved components.
  2. Remove the left and right trim rings.
  3. Remove the bridge cover (4 screws).
  4. Remove the screw holding both front covers together and the bottom screw of each front cover from its bottom mounting.
  5. Remove the 2-paired covers (4 screws) on the back of the IRD to access a front cover screw behind the display (see Replacing the In-Room Display (IRD) - 12.1 inch IRD).
  6. Remove the screw located on top by the top cover (above the IRD) and the screw located below the IRD.
  7. Disassemble the operator control panel (OCP) and disconnect the cables (see Replacing the Operator Control Panel (OCP)).
  8. Remove the 2 screws located behind the OCP.
  9. Note

    Use care when moving the front covers:

    • Both left and right covers have the vertical light strips attached. Disconnect the power cord from each light before moving or replacing. See Replacing the vertical light strip.

    • The left front cover has the ambient room temperature sensor attached. Be careful of the sensor cable when setting cover aside. When replacing the left front cover, remove and reattach the ambient room temperature sensor. See Room Ambient and Bore Temperature Sensor Replacement.

    Remove the 2 screws holding the left or right front cover to the bracket mounted on the magnet, and set the covers aside.
  10. Reverse the instructions when replacing or reattaching the front covers. Ensure proper fit.

Replacing the vertical light strip

About this task

For left or right light strip removal, remove the corresponding side middle and side front covers.

Procedure

  1. Verify that LOTO is still in place and the patient table is undocked (see Preliminary).
  2. Remove the corresponding side covers. See Side and top cover removal and installation.
  3. Disconnect the power cord from the corresponding light strip.
  4. Remove the translucent light cover.
  5. Remove the clips holding the lights to the cover. Cut the wire ties on the back side holding the light strip wire and remove the light strip.
    Note If the current LEDs in the cover are already mounted to adhesive mounts, proceed to Step 10.
  6. CAUTION Nitrile gloves must be worn when using alcohol wipes and glue.
    Assemble the glue gun applicator using the 1:1 plunger, following the manufacturer's instructions included in the box. Make sure to connect the glue and mixing nozzle to the applicator assembly.
  7. Clean the location where the adhesive mounts will be installed using the alcohol wipes provided in the FRU kit.
  8. Install the adhesive mounts into the LED groove. Make sure to install one at the top and one at the bottom of the cover, with the remaining 9 spaced approximately 200 mm apart in the LED groove. Placement is not critical, but should span the whole groove.
  9. Using the gun applicator, liberally apply glue to the vertical sides of the adhesive mounts. Wait until the glue is no longer tacky (approximately 10 minutes) before continuing to the next step.
    Note It is critical that glue coverage does not extend outside of the LED groove, and that glue does not interfere with the slots for cable tie installation.
  10. Install the LED strip starting from the bottom to the top of the panel, pealing the backing off the LED strip (approximately 12 inches) to expose the adhesive tape, and continuing up the side as the LED strip is placed. Between adhesive mounts, make sure to press the LED onto the cover to eliminate any gaps.
  11. Loop the cable ties through the adhesive mounts to lock the LEDs in place.
  12. Clip the excess from the cable ties.
  13. Replace and reconnect the light strip by reversing the above instructions. Ensure proper fit.
  14. Remove RF Subsystem and/or PEN Cabinet LOTO. See Removing LOTO - RF Subsystem and/or PEN Cabinet.
Topic ID: id_15367902

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Whenever the IRD or trackball control panels are disconnected and then reconnected, a TPS reset must be completed. (This is not necessary if the host computer is rebooted.)
  2. Examine the functionality of the item replaced. Run the associated diagnostic, if necessary:
    • (For the IRD) From the Common Service Desktop, select Diagnostics > Hardware Location > Magnet Room > In Room Display.
    • (For the control panel) From the Common Service Desktop, select Diagnostics > Hardware Location > Magnet Room > SRI Functional Tests.
