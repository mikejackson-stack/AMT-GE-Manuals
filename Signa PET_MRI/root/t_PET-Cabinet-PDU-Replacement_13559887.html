About this task

Overview

The PET Power Distribution Unit (PDU) weighs 267 lb. (121 kg). It is located at the bottom of the PET Cabinet, so a hoist kit is required to safely remove and replace the PET PDU.

Procedure

  1. LOTO both the PET and PGR Cabinets.
    • See Applying LOTO - PET Power Distribution Unit.
    • See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
  2. Notice
    The number in parentheses within an instruction corresponds to the numbered item in the referenced illustration.
    Before setting up the hoist kit, unscrew the J9 connector from the XPS, and remove the main lifting beam from the PGR Cabinet.
  3. Retrieve the support tube from the HEC.
  4. Set up and secure the hoist, referring to Hoist Service Kit and Lifting Accessories.
  5. Disconnect all the cables and power cords from the back of the existing PET PDU.
  6. Remove the INPUT cables and eight front panel screws, securing the existing PDU in the PET Cabinet.
    Note

    Breaker should already be in the POWER OFF position on the PDU.

  7. Slide the PDU out of the PET Cabinet until it is fully extended.
    1. Install the two eyelet rings on the top of the PET PDU.
      Note

      If the eyelets are missing for the existing PDU, use those provided with the new PDU.

    2. Install the lifting bracket hooks onto the eyelet rings.
  8. Remove the five screws securing each side rail to the PET PDU, and set them aside in a safe location.
  9. CAUTION
    Possible Equipment Damage or Personal Injury
    Make sure the hook is properly seated in the lifting bracket.
    If not seated properly, the weight of the PDU can shift and cause equipment damage or personal injury.
    Hook the winch from the Hoist Kit to the lifting bracket, and ratchet the winch to tighten the chain.
  10. Ratchet the winch to lower the existing PDU to the floor so it is out of the way.
  11. Bring the new PDU in the wooden shipping crate to the front of the PET Cabinet.
    1. Remove the top from the crate and move it out of the way.
    2. Attach the hoist to the new PDU, and ratchet the winch to lift it out of the crate base.
  12. Reattach the side rails to the new PDU with the 10 screws that were set aside.
  13. Remove the hook and lifting bracket from the new PDU, slide the PDU into the PET Cabinet, and secure with the front panel screws removed earlier.
  14. Reconnect the power cables to the front and back of the PDU, and ensure the new PDU is set at the same voltage as the one replaced.
  15. Attach the hoist to the removed PET PDU, and ratchet the winch to raise it off the floor and lower it into the empty wooden crate.
  16. Disassemble the Hoist Kit, and return the main lifting beam to the PGR Cabinet.
  17. Once the beam is secured, screw the J9 connector into the XPS.
  18. Return the support tube to the HEC.

Finalization

  1. Remove LOTO from the PET and PGR Cabinets, and turn the PET Cabinet On.
    1. See Removing LOTO - PET Power Distribution Unit.
    2. See Removing LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
  2. Perform a PET Daily Quality Assurance (DQA) check.
  3. Attach the top to the wooden crate containing the removed PDU.
