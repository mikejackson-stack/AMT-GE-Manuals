Removing the LPCA

This procedure describes how to remove the LPCA.

Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

Warning
Ferrous material hazard
The motor and gear assembly contain parts made with ferrous components. Holding these components too close to the magnet bore will forcibly attract them to the magnet.
To prevent possible bodily injury or component damage, keep them as far from the magnetic field as possible when servicing.
CAUTION
Potential crush or pinch hazard
Movement of the table or cradle can result in pinched or crushed fingers.
Keep fingers away from the gaps between the table, cradle, and the LPCA when docking or undocking the table or while connecting coils.

About this task

Note The LPCA for DS DPP systems uses a reduced profile and does not have an A-port.

Procedure

  1. Press Home on the Operator Control Panel (OCP) to drive the LPCA and cradle to the patient end.
  2. Manually lift the LPCA hook, and drive the LPCA to the rear.
  3. Shut down and power off the host computer. See Shutting down the host computer.
  4. After the system is powered down, turn off the Power, Gradient RF Cabinet (PGR) Power Distribution Unit (PDU) breaker at the Main Disconnect Panel (MDP).
  5. Apply LOTO to the PGR PDU breaker. See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem. Leave the main, Heat Exchanger Cabinet (HEC), and PET Cabinet PDU breakers on.
  6. Remove the screws that secure the cover to the LPCA base. Remove the cover.
  7. Remove the rear pedestal covers.
  8. Loosen the screws securing the tensioner on both pulley assemblies, then release the belt tension by loosening the bolt until the tensioner rests against the block.

    Do not remove the bolt.

  9. Remove the screws that secure the four belt clamp plates to the LPCA base and remove the clamps.
  10. Pull the belts from under both ends of the LPCA, but do not remove the belts from the front or middle bridges.
  11. Remove the screws that secure the encoder housing to the A-port mounting block, and remove the encoder housing.
  12. Remove the P1 and P2 quick disconnects.
  13. Remove the screws securing the P1 and P2 baluns.
  14. Remove the screws securing the Port 1 and Port 2 support to the stationary slides, and set the ports with their respective baluns and cables aside.
  15. (For RRx systems) Disconnect the sensor cable plug from the A-port cable harness.
    (For DS DPP systems) Disconnect the sensor cable plug from the P-port cable harness.
  16. (For RRx systems) Carefully disconnect the sensor cable leads from the red and green LEDs on the A-port Excite III bezel, and avoid pulling the LEDs out of their socket.
  17. Remove the screws that secure the sub-D plate to the A-port mounting block.
  18. (For RRx systems) Disconnect J1 from the bottom of the sub-D plate, then feed the J1 plug through the A-port mounting block.
  19. (For RRx systems) Lift the sub-D plate and the Excite III A-bezel cable harness assembly, and put the cables and assembly to the side.
    (For DS DPP systems) Lift the sub-D plate and put it to the side.
  20. Notice
    In the next step, it is important to avoid disturbing the encoder eyelet attachment assembly.
    Remove the screws that secure the A-port mounting block to the LPCA base, and remove the A-port mounting block.
  21. Notice
    Possible equipment damage
    Pressure must be kept on the encoder cable while it retracts.
    Failure to do so will result in damage to the encoder.
    Lift the attachment eyelet block away from its slot, remove the encoder cable from the block, and let the cable slowly retract.
  22. Remove the screws securing the left and right cable tracks to the LPCA. Lift the ports and cables away from the LPCA.
  23. Lift the LPCA away from the rear bridge.
