About this task

Overview

The PARC HP Z820 computer holds four 2.5 in. hard disk drives (HDD) housed in tray-mounted assemblies. The HDDs are configured for RAID 1 with one each (master and slave) assigned for petRDFS and petLists patient scan data storage. A single HDD failure will not cause a hard failure of the PARC computer, but requires replacement of the HDD.

Topic ID: id_SL13456844-1241651

HDD Removal

Procedure

  1. Notice
    Make sure the HDDs are transferred or replaced to the same locations in the new PARC computer.
    Locate the HDD to be replaced or transferred.
    RAID 1 Assignment
    Item Master (Primary) Item Slave (Secondary)
    1Disk 0 for petRDFS2Disk 1 for petRDFS
    3Disk 0 for petLists4Disk 1 for petLists
  2. To remove the defective HDD from the PARC computer:
    1. Press the release lever on the left side of the tray holding the HDD.
    2. Swing the tray panel out to disengage it from the HDD.
    3. Gently remove the tray with the HDD from the computer.
Topic ID: id_SL13456847-1241651

HDD Installation

Procedure

  1. (For replacing an HDD) Place the tray with the defective HDD on a flat surface, and remove the four Phillips screws securing the HDD in the tray.
  2. (For replacing an HDD) Remove the defective HDD, correctly position the new one into the tray, and secure with the previously removed Phillips screws.
  3. Gently insert the tray containing the new HDD into the PARC computer open slot.
  4. Close the tray panel and gently press it until the tray assembly clicks into place.
Topic ID: id_15238943

Finalization

Finalization

After insertion of the new HDD, the RAID Controller will automatically begin to synchronize or rebuild RAID 1, which can be time consuming depending on the size of the HDD. If necessary, the synchronization process can be stopped and executed at a later time when the system is not in use. Refer to Resynchronization of Transferred or Replaced HDD in PARC.
