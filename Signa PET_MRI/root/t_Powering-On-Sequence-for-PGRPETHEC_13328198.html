Powering On Sequence for PGR/PET/HEC

Prerequisites

Table 1. Personnel requirements
Required personsPreliminary requirementsProcedureFinalization
1Not Applicable15 to 60 hrs minutesNot Applicable

About this task

Overview

The Main Disconnect Panel (MDP) and the Heat Exchange Cabinet (HEC) are required to power the Cryogen Cooler Compressor (CRY) and the Magnet Coldhead (flowchart shown below). This procedure provides an installation checklist for powering on the system and an overview of each main component: MDP, HEC, CRY, and the Power Distribution Units (PDUs) in this powering on sequence.

NOTICE: For sites with a different customer-supplied voltage, the control power transformer wiring will need to be changed on the MDP, HEC, and PDUs.

The checklist items covered here should be done prior to starting the Installation Calibration Wizard. This checklist is to confirm proper setup.

Main Components

  • MDP

    The MDP contains a control power transformer manufactured by Teal, and is set to 480 VAC by default. The MDP also contains a temporary jumper used to power up the system, which will need to be removed.

    Note The temporary jumper is used until the remote Emergency Off buttons are installed.
    Important GE Field Engineers cannot install, service, or repair the MDP. Only a licensed electrician is qualified to complete those tasks. Refer to the MR Service Safety Manual (5452735) for further safety information. To service the MDP, the customer must provide a qualified electrician or other appropriate facility personnel who meets the electrical safety codes for their area.

  • HEC

    The HEC contains a control power transformer that is set to 480 VAC. The HEC contains the power interface to the CRY. To power the coldhead after magnet delivery, the power cabling will need to be wired from the MDP to the HEC and from the HEC to the Cryocooler. The power cable between the MDP and the HEC will be customer supplied and installed.

  • CRY

    The CRY receives power from the HEC. The coldhead power cable will need to be installed to power the coldhead.

  • MR and PET PDUs

    Each PDU is set up for 480 VAC by default. The power cable between the MDP and the PDU will be customer supplied and installed. The overload and short-circuit settings are dependent upon the voltage input and may need to be changed if the input voltage is changed. Not all PDUs need the overload and short-circuit current settings adjusted. Refer to the manual for the installed PDU to determine adjustment requirements.

  • Power, Gradient, and Radio Frequency (PGR) Cabinet

    The PGR Cabinet houses the XG2 Gradient Amplifier, XPS2 Power Supplies, and the PDU. The gradient cable connections to the XG2 for each axis are accessed in this cabinet.

For the powering on sequence, confirm:

  • MDP
    • Selection of MDP Voltage
    • Removal of MDP Jumper Interlock
      Important GE Field Engineers cannot install, service, or repair the MDP. Only a licensed electrician is qualified to complete those tasks. Refer to the MR Service Safety Manual (5452735) for further safety information. To service the MDP, the customer must provide a qualified electrician or other appropriate facility personnel who meets the electrical safety codes for their area.
  • Selection of HEC Voltage
  • Completion of Internal Power Wiring to PDUs
  • Overload and Short Circuit Trip Settings on PDU Input Circuit Breaker
  • Connection of Gradient Cable at PGR Cabinet

Procedure

  1. Ensure all components are installed, operating properly, and LOTO removed.
  2. Notify the field service and other upgrade personnel, who are working at the site, that LOTO will be removed from the PDUs in the PGR and PET Cabinets so power can be restored.
    Note

    This restores power to the PDUs, Heat Exchanger Cabinet, and the PGR and PET Cabinets.

  3. Press the FULL ON button on the PGR Cabinet PDU front control panel to turn on the PGR PDU.
  4. Press the FULL ON button on the PET Cabinet PDU front control panel to turn on the PET PDU.
  5. To ‘System Shut Down and Start Up’, refer to the SIGNA PET/MR 3.0T System Service Methods DVD, Direction 5490579-x, found under Installation and Upgrade > PET-MR.

Finalization

No finalization steps.
