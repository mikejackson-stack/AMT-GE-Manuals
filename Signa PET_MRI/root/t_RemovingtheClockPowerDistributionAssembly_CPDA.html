Removing the Clock Power Distribution Assembly (CPDA)

Remove the Clock Power Distribution Assembly (CPDA) from the magnet enclosure.

About this task

CPDAs that are installed low on the sides of the magnet are mounted onto a plate that swings away from the magnet to make connecting and disconnecting the cables easier.

Procedure

  1. Make sure that you have read and understood the electrical safety requirements before starting this procedure. See Electrical Safety.
  2. Shut down and power off the host computer. See Shutting down the host computer.
  3. Apply LOTO to the following components:
    1. Apply PET Power Distribution Unit LOTO. See Applying LOTO - PET Power Distribution Unit.
    2. Apply PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem LOTO. See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
  4. Remove the appropriate side cover. See Removing the magnet covers.
  5. (For CPDAs with a mounting plate) Swing the CPDA away from the magnet as follows:
    Note Make sure that the hinge pins are properly connected and in good order before removing the thumb screws. If hinge pins are not installed, disconnect the cables first and then hold the CPDA while all the screws are removed to prevent possible equipment damage.
    1. Loosen the top two captive fasteners on the mounting plate.
    2. Hold the CPDA while loosening the bottom two captive fasteners on the mounting plate.
    3. Allow the CPDA to swing away from the magnet.
  6. Remove the 14 Detector Module (DMOD) cables.
  7. Remove the four grey CPDA power cables and the white 80V power cable.
  8. (For CPDAs on a mounting plate) Lift the plate to remove the hinge pins from the brackets and remove the CPDA.
  9. (For CPDAs without a mounting plate) Remove the four M5 hex nuts installed in the CPDA and remove the CPDA:
  10. While carrying the CPDA out of the magnet room, make sure to stay as far away from the magnet as possible.
  11. (For CPDAs with a mounting plate) Remove the CPDA chassis from the mounting plate by removing the four M5 nuts securing the chassis to the plate.
