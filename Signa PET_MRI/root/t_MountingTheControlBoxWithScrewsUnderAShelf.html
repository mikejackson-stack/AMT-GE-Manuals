Mounting the control box with screws under a shelf

Mounts the control box under a horizontal surface using screws.

Procedure

  1. Choose the mounting location for the control box near the operator console.
    Important The control box should be mounted within sight of the operator.
  2. Mark front mounting hole position on the shelf.
  3. Mark the rear mounting hole position on the shelf.
  4. Drill 1/16 inch pilot holes at both locations if furnished screws are used.
  5. Install the screws to a depth that allows the base of the control box to fit between the head and mounting surface.
  6. Center the control box keyhole slots over the screws, push in on box, and then push inward to seat screws within slots.
  7. Remove the control box, tighten or loosen screws, and reinstall the box onto screws as necessary to obtain a snug fit of the box against the shelf.
