Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

DANGER
Electric shock hazard
Contact with the connectors leading to an energized power supply can cause electrical shock.
Follow lockout/tagout (LOTO) procedures while performing services to electrical connection components.
CAUTION
Fluid pressure
Cooling fluid can cause damage to the electronics if the PET chiller pumps fluid with the hoses disconnected.
To avoid fluid contact with the electronics, magnet, or personnel, turn the PET chiller off.

About this task

This procedure covers the replacement of the PET detectors.

Topic ID: id_gmn_f3q_twb

System preparation

Procedure

  1. Turn off the PROP circuit breaker to remove power to the photon reconstruction organization processor (PROP), which turns off the low voltage power supply (LVPS) and the 80 volt power supply (80V PS).
  2. Press the On/Off button to turn the PET chiller off, and put the chiller breaker (CB12) in the off position.
  3. LOTO the power distribution unit (PDU) in the PET and PGR cabinets. See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
    Note When the PET/MR insert is removed from the bore, make sure that cables and hoses do not get caught, tangled, or kinked. Avoid bringing the insert to a point that may cause stress on any cables or hoses.
  4. Complete the remaining prerequisite steps for PET detector replacement identified in PET Insert Maintenance Matrix.
Topic ID: id_SL5543049-1189666

Removing PET detectors

Procedure

  1. Tighten the screws to secure the PET/MR insert to the insertion tool.
  2. Notice
    Possible equipment damage
    Cooling fluid can cause damage to the electronics if it remains in the hoses connected to the PET detectors.
    To avoid fluid contact with electronics, turn the PET/MR insert so the hose connections are at zero degrees at the top of the insert.
    To minimize potential fluid leakage, turn the insertion tool using the handle (1) so that the PET detector hose connections at the manifold (2) are at the top of the PET/MR insert.
  3. The following steps are to remove the Gen2 detector module (DMOD) cable. To remove the Gen3 DMOD cable, continue to Step 4.
    Notice
    Screws are ferrous and secured with Loctite. Use a stainless steel (ferrous) screwdriver and Allen wrench to remove the screws and loosen the setscrews. Be careful not to lose the ferrous screws.
    1. Remove the two screws (1) with a Phillips screwdriver.
    2. Remove the two screws (2) with an Allen wrench.
    3. Remove the clamp (3).
  4. Remove the Gen3 DMOD cable (3):
    1. Remove the two screws (1) with a flat head screwdriver.
    2. Remove the clamp (2).
  5. Disconnect the cable from the PET detector, being careful not to lose the small gasket.
  6. Remove the DMOD cable bands if necessary by inserting the tip of a flat blade screwdriver in the buckle and twist the screwdriver to disengage the lock, then pulling the cable band out of the buckle.
    Note If a cable breaks, see DMOD Cable Band Installation.
  7. Put clean lint-free towels below the hose connections at the manifold to contain any fluid leakage.
  8. Use the crimping tool to remove the hose clamps at the manifold end of the hoses.
    1. Put the tool over the crimp in the clamp, and rotate 120° clockwise, and then counterclockwise back to 0°, counterclockwise another 120°, and then clockwise back to 0°. This should break the crimp; if not, repeat.
    2. Open the crimper and slip the edge of one side under the lip of the hose clamp. The other edge should be on the other side of the crimp, as shown.
    3. Gently pull the hose clamp, which will come apart and remain in one piece.
  9. Notice
    Have a plastic bucket handy to drop the end of the hose into the bucket when it is disconnected from the fitting. Let the fluid drain before removing the PET detector. When removing the detector, lift the hose end up first to prevent fluid from spilling onto the PET/MR insert and electronics.
    Using the hose removal tool:
    1. Pinch the blue hose, pull it straight back from the manifold fitting, and put it into a plastic bucket to drain.
    2. Pinch the red hose, pull it straight back from the manifold fitting, and put it into a plastic bucket to drain.
  10. Use a flat blade screwdriver to remove the four pan head screws from the detector, and save for later use.
  11. Lift the manifold end up first, then remove the detector from the PET/MR insert.
  12. Disconnect the fiber-optic data cable (1) from the detector.

Installing PET detectors

Procedure

  1. Insert the new PET detector into the cavity (1) vacated by the defective detector.
  2. Fasten the new detector (2) with the four pan head screws removed earlier.
  3. Reconnect the fiber-optic data cable to the new detector.
  4. Insert new clamps (1) onto the beaded fittings (2) on the manifold.
  5. Size the hoses (3) to reach the manifold from the new detector. (Hose end should extend 6.4 mm (0.25 inches) past the bead (4) on the beaded fittings.)
  6. Use the hose cutter to cut the hoses to the correct length.
  7. Slide the red hose and blue hose for the PET detector onto the manifold tube extensions, placing the red hose on the inner ring and the blue hose on the outer ring.
    Note

    All red and blue hoses are shown connected, but there is only one each per PET detector.

  8. Crimp the clamp around each hose with the crimping tool.
Topic ID: id_15243113

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Remove LOTO from the PDUs in the PET and PGR cabinets. See Removing LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.. Turn the PET chiller on.
  2. Confirm DMOD connectivity before reinstalling the PET/MR insert. See DMOD Connectivity Troubleshooting.
  3. Do the finalization steps identified in PET Insert Maintenance Matrix.
  4. Do the complete PET detector calibration process specified in PET detector calibration.
    Note Grad cal should be done following VQC.
  5. Do a hello/goodbye scan.
