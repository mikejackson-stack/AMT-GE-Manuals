About this task

Overview

Follow this procedure to replace the leak sensors and leak sensor detector box, which prevents the detectors on a PET/MR system from getting wet in the event of a leak.

Topic ID: id_SL12672733-1241586

Preliminary

Procedure

  1. If a leak exists (for example, standing fluid), LOTO the PDU in the PGR Cabinet.
  2. Remove the appropriate rear pedestal covers depending on what is being replaced.
  3. Remove the four screws holding the rear pedestal to the middle bridge.
  4. Slide the rear pedestal away from the magnet to provide clearance to the leak sensors.
Topic ID: id_SL12672738-1241586

Bore Leak Sensor Removal

Procedure

  1. Remove the fiber-optic cable (1) going through the bore leak sensor.
  2. Remove the 8 mm bolt (2) securing the leak sensor to the PET/MR insert.
  3. Remove the two fiber-optic cables (3) to the left of the bore leak sensor, and slide the leak sensor around the fiber-optic breakout box and out from the magnet bore.
  4. Reverse steps above for replacement of bore leak sensor or bore leak sensor mount.
Topic ID: id_SL12725051-1241586

Rear Leak Sensor Removal

Procedure

  1. Remove the leak sensor rear assembly from the center of the carbon fiber mount.
  2. Replace the rear leak sensor assembly or leak sensor cable as required.
  3. Install a new rear leak sensor assembly to the center of the carbon fiber mount.
Topic ID: id_SL12672748-1241586

Leak Sensor Detector Box Removal

Procedure

  1. Remove the leak sensor cables and Scan Room Interface (SRI) cable from the Leak Sensor Detector Box.
  2. Remove the Leak Sensor Detector Box from the rear pedestal.
  3. Install the new Leak Sensor Detector Box onto the rear pedestal.
  4. Connect the leak sensor cables and the SRI cable to the Leak Sensor Detector Box.
  5. Remove LOTO from the PGR and the PET Cabinets at the MDP.
Topic ID: id_15225227

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Reset the PDU E-Stop in the PET Cabinet if tripped.
  2. Reattach the rear pedestal to the middle bridge.
  3. Reinstall all removed covers.
