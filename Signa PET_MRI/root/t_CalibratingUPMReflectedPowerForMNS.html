Calibrating UPM reflected power for MNS

Calibrate UPM reflected power for MNS.

About this task

After adjusting RF amplifier output power, follow the procedure below to calibrate UPM forward power.

Procedure

  1. On the exciter, set the RF enable S2 switch to the disabled (down) position.
  2. Disconnect the coupler from the RF amplifier:
    1. (For AMT amplifier) Disconnect the cable from J4 on the MNS output at the rear of the amplifier. Leave J4 open.
    2. (For CPC Gen 1/2 amplifier (5411744)) Disconnect the RF output cable from J3 on the combiner module in the CPC cabinet. Leave J3 open.
    3. (For CPC Gen 1/2 amplifier (5750811)) Disconnect the RF output cable from J2 of the power output in the CPC cabinet. Leave J2 open.
  3. On the front of the exciter, set the RF enable S2 switch to the enabled (up) position.
  4. Based on the status choose one of the following options:
  5. Click Start.
  6. Click Yes when the prompt asks if the hardware setup is correct.

    Result

    The tool reports the steps as they progress. In about 3 minutes, the Status field on the tool will show PASS or FAIL.
  7. Based on the status choose one of the following options:
    • PASS – Continue on to the next step.
    • FAIL – Refer to Troubleshooting the UPM.
      Note If there is no power output from the MNS amplifier during UPM Forward Power Calibration, then follow the steps below.
      1. Close the UPM tool and start a new exam in geservice mode with a patient weight of 50 kg.
      2. Create a landmark with the 31P TR switch.
      3. Select Service > MNS Setup, and then prescribe the 31P Functional Check series.
      4. Save and download the sequence with any acceptable scan parameters.
      5. Open Spectro Prescan, click Start, and let the sequence run for about 10 seconds.
      6. Stop the sequence, end the exam, and continue with the UPM calibration.
  8. When the MNS 31P channel is calibrated (PASS) for reflected power, set the RF ENB switch on the front of the exciter to the disabled (down) position.
  9. Reconnect the RF coupler/dummy load to the RF output connector on the rear of the MNS amplifier.
    Note The stub cable should remain connected to J22.
  10. On the front of the exciter, set the RF enable S2 switch to the enabled (up) position.
