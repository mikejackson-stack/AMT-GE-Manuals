Replacing the PET/MR insert

This procedure includes the steps necessary to replace the entire PET/MR insert, including instructions for removing the detector modules from an old PET/MR insert and putting the detector modules on a new PET/MR insert.

Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

DANGER
Electric shock hazard
Contact with the connectors leading to an energized power supply can cause electrical shock.
Follow lockout/tagout (LOTO) procedures while performing services to electrical connection components.
Warning
Possible personal injury
When moving the PET/MR insert, which weighs approximately 249.5 kg (550 lb), there is a risk of back strain or personal injury.
Two people are required to remove the insert.
CAUTION
Fluid pressure
Cooling fluid can cause damage to the electronics if the PET chiller pumps fluid with the hoses disconnected.
To avoid fluid contact with the electronics, magnet, or personnel, turn the PET chiller and chiller breaker (CB12) off to stop the PET chiller from pumping fluids.

About this task

This procedure describes how to replace the PET/MR insert using the PET/MR insert installation tool and transportation cart. Refer to the SIGNA PET/MR Transport Cart User Guide (5567849) for instructions on the cart.

This procedure includes the steps necessary to replace the entire PET/MR insert. See PET Detector Replacement for specific information regarding DMOD replacement.

The transportation cart for the PET/MR insert and installation tool is only intended to be used by authorized personnel as described in this procedure, and used only to transport the PET/MR insert inside the magnet room.

System preparation

Procedure

  1. Turn off the PROP circuit breaker to remove power to the photon reconstruction organization processor (PROP), which turns off the low voltage power supply (LVPS) and the 80 volt power supply (80V PS).
  2. Press the On/Off button to turn the PET chiller off, and put the chiller breaker (CB12) in the off position.
  3. LOTO the power distribution unit (PDU) in the PET and PGR cabinets. See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
    Note When the PET/MR insert is removed from the bore, make sure that cables and hoses do not get caught, tangled, or kinked. Avoid bringing the insert to a point that may cause stress on any cables or hoses.
  4. Complete the remaining prerequisite steps for PET detector replacement identified in PET Insert Maintenance Matrix.

PET/MR insert preparation

Procedure

  1. CAUTION
    Possible equipment damage
    Cooling fluid can cause damage to the electronics if it remains in the hoses connected to the PET detectors.
    To avoid fluid contact with electronics, turn the PET/MR insert so hose connections are at zero degrees at the top of the insert.
    Position the PET/MR insert transport cart below the extracted PET/MR insert.
    Note

    Position the transport cart on the patient end of the insert, opposite of the magnet bore, within the magnet room.

  2. Remove the DMOD power cables from the Clock Power Distribution Assembly (CPDA) side only.
  3. The following steps are to remove the Gen2 detector module (DMOD) cable. To remove the Gen3 DMOD cable, continue to Step 4.
    Notice
    Screws are ferrous and secured with Loctite. Use a stainless steel (ferrous) screwdriver and Allen wrench to remove the screws and loosen the setscrews. Be careful not to lose the ferrous screws.
    1. Remove the two screws (1) with a Phillips screwdriver.
    2. Remove the two screws (2) with an Allen wrench.
    3. Remove the clamp (3).
  4. Remove the Gen3 DMOD cable (3):
    1. Remove the two screws (1) with a flat head screwdriver.
    2. Remove the clamp (2).
  5. Use the crimping tool to remove the hose clamps at the manifold end of the hoses.
    1. Put the tool over the crimp in the clamp, and rotate 120° clockwise, and then counterclockwise back to 0°, counterclockwise another 120°, and then clockwise back to 0°. This should break the crimp; if not, repeat.
    2. Open the crimper and slip the edge of one side under the lip of the hose clamp. The other edge should be on the other side of the crimp, as shown.
    3. Gently pull the hose clamp, which will come apart and remain in one piece.
  6. Remove the DMOD cable bands if necessary by inserting the tip of a flat blade screwdriver in the buckle and twist the screwdriver to disengage the lock, then pulling the cable band out of the buckle.
    Note If a cable breaks, see DMOD Cable Band Installation.
  7. Pull each cable off the PET detector, being careful not to lose the small gaskets that are on each DMOD power cable.
  8. Remove two of the four holding screws from each DMOD securing it to the PET/MR insert. Choose screws that are opposite from each other.
    Note

    Tightly fasten the screws that secure the PET/MR insert to the insertion tool. Failure to do so will cause the insert to slip when it is rotated to access the DMODs.

Removing PET detectors

Procedure

  1. Put lint-free towels below the hose connections at the manifold to absorb any coolant leakage.
  2. Notice
    Retrieve a plastic bucket before disconnecting the fitting from the hose and beginning the draining process. The fluid must be drained before removing any DMODs.
    Remove the hoses, one DMOD at a time:
    1. Use the DMOD hose removal tool to slide the red cooling hose off of the manifold.
    2. Drain any remaining coolant into the plastic bucket.
    3. Remove the blue cooling hose from the manifold.
    4. Drain any remaining coolant into the plastic bucket.
  3. Remove the two remaining screws from each DMOD.
  4. Remove the DMODs.
    1. Note

      DMODs are highly electrostatic discharge (ESD) sensitive. Use all appropriate ESD protection to protect the DMODs.

      Lift the manifold end of the DMOD from the insert first. Keep the manifold end positioned up to prevent coolant from spilling onto the insert and electronics.
    2. Disconnect the fiber-optic cable (1) from each detector.
  5. If the original DMOD shipping crate is available, put each DMOD in the shipping crate for safekeeping.

    If the DMOD shipping crate is not available, put the DMODs on the patient table on a clean, lint-free towel.

    Locate the shipping crate and pack DMODs that have been replaced for return shipment.

  6. Rotate the PET/MR insert back 120° the other way to do the same steps on DMODs 15, 16, 17, and 18.
  7. Continue four DMODs at a time, balancing each side, to work toward DMODs 0 and 27.

Original PET/MR insert shipping preparation

Procedure

  1. Use the extraction tool to lower the PET/MR insert onto the transport cart.
  2. Secure the bolts that attach the PET/MR insert to the transport cart.
  3. Remove the PET/MR extraction tool. See PET/MR Insert Installation Tool Removal.

New PET/MR insert preparation

About this task

You may do these steps either in or outside the magnet room with both inserts next to each other.

Procedure

  1. Remove the manifold from the original PET/MR insert and install it on the new insert.
  2. Put a nonmagnetic straight edge (1) against the cooling manifold support block (2), and measure the gap (3) between the straight edge and the inside of the PET/MR insert.

    • The gap along the entire length between the straight edge and the insert must be no more than 6 mm.

    • Adjust the manifold support block as needed to get the proper gap.

  3. Remove the fiber-optic boxes from the existing PET/MR insert and install them on the new insert.

Installing PET detectors

Procedure

  1. Bring the new PET/MR insert into the magnet room.
  2. Set up the PET/MR insertion tool.
  3. Rotate the PET/MR insertion tool so that the bottom of the insert is accessible.
  4. Drape and tape each fiber-optic cable to the insert.
    Note

    This image shows a fully assembled PET/MR insert, including cooling hoses. The cooling lines and DMODs will not yet be in place during this procedure. Also, the PET/MR insert will be inverted.

  5. Rotate the PET/MR insert so it is upright.
  6. Beginning with DMOD 0, connect the fiber-optic cable.

    The cable should click twice. Gently push and pull on the connection to make sure that it is secure.

  7. Put the DMOD in the appropriate cavity on the PET/MR insert (1).
  8. Secure the DMOD to the insert with the previously removed screws (2).
  9. Repeat Step 6, Step 7, and Step 8 for the remaining DMODs.
  10. Return to DMOD 0.
    Note

    The red hoses connect to the inner ring (1). The blue hoses connect to the outer ring (2).

  11. Put a hose clamp (1) on the inner ring manifold tube extension (2) for the red hose.
  12. Push the red hose (3) over the beaded fitting on the inner ring (4). The hose should extend 6.4 mm (1/4 inch) beyond the bead.
    Note

    Use the hose cutter to trim the hoses on new DMODs to fit the beaded fitting.

  13. Slide the hose clamp over the red hose, up to the bead.
  14. Use the crimping tool to crimp the clamp.
  15. Repeat Step 11 through Step 14 for the blue hose.
  16. Repeat Step 11 through Step 15 for the remaining DMODs.

    Alternate sides of the insert as you progress. Start with DMOD 0, and then connect DMOD 27, followed by DMOD 26, then DMOD 2. Follow this pattern until all DMODs are connected.

  17. Transfer the patient bore temperature sensor on the original PET/MR insert to the new PET/MR insert. See Room Ambient and Bore Temperature Sensor Replacement.
Topic ID: id_15244394

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Remove LOTO from the PDUs in the PET and PGR cabinets. See Removing LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
  2. Turn the PET chiller on.
  3. Confirm DMOD connectivity before reinstalling the PET/MR insert. See DMOD Connectivity Troubleshooting.
  4. Do the finalization steps identified in PET Insert Maintenance Matrix.
  5. Do the complete PET detector calibration process specified in PET detector calibration.
    Note

    Grad cal should be done following VQC.

  6. Do a hello/goodbye scan.
