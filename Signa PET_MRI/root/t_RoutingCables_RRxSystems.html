Routing cables - RRx systems

Route cables for RRx systems.

Procedure

  1. Route:
    1. P1/P2 cables through the left side of the P3-port housing.
    2. A-port cables through the right side of the P3-port housing.
  2. Route the A-port cables to:
    1. RF hub slot 1–A1 through the blue cable restrainer.
    2. RF hub slot 11–J6 through the lower left front of the pedestal frame.
    3. PED Load A and PED TIF–J1 cable along the upper left inner rail of the pedestal frame.
  3. Coil the PED J2 cable and tie-wrap it to the pedestal frame leg above the RF connector, leaving enough slack in the unconnected end. Connect the PED J2 cable to the RF P2 connector, and connect the cable.
  4. Route the P1-port cables to:
    1. RF hub slot 1-P1 and to RF hub slot 2-P1 through the blue cable restrainer.
    2. RF hub slot 11-J1 through the lower left front of the pedestal frame.
    3. PED load B cable along the upper left inner rail of the pedestal frame.
  5. Coil the PED-J1 cable and tie-wrap it to the pedestal frame leg above the RF connector, leaving enough slack in the unconnected end. Connect the PED-J1 cable to the RF-P1 connector, and connect the cable.
  6. Route the P2-port cables to:
    1. RF hub slot 1-P2 and RF hub slot 2-P2 through the blue cable restrainer.
    2. RF hub slot 11-J2 through the lower left front of the pedestal frame.
  7. Route the P3-port cables to:
    1. RF hub slot 1-P3 and RF hub slot 2-P3 through the blue cable restrainer.
    2. RF hub slot 11-J3 through the lower left front of the pedestal frame.
  8. Route cable (5191650) from RF hub slot 11-J7 to PED TIF-J6 along the upper left inner rail of the pedestal frame.
  9. Secure the cables routed on the inside of the upper left rail of the pedestal with tie-wraps to the hold- downs at the end of the rail.
  10. Apply LOCTITE to the two M6 x 20 mm socket-head cap screws removed earlier, and secure the encoder side mount and assembly to the rear bridge.
  11. Connect the encoder cable to the Table InterFace (TIF).
  12. Install the LPCA. See Installing the LPCA.
