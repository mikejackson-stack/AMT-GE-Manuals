Preparing the system

Instructions to prepare the system for detector module (DMOD) cable replacement.

Procedure

  1. Do a check of detector module (DMOD) connectivity. See DMOD Connectivity Troubleshooting.
  2. Undock the patient table.
  3. Shut down and power off the host computer. See Shutting down the host computer.
  4. Turn off the PET chiller.
  5. Turn off the PROP circuit breaker to remove power to the photon reconstruction organization processor (PROP), which turns off the low voltage power supply (LVPS) and the 80 volt power supply (80V PS).
  6. Press the On/Off button to turn the PET chiller off, and put the chiller breaker (CB12) in the off position.
  7. LOTO the power distribution unit (PDU) in the PET and Power, Gradient, RF (PGR) cabinets:
    1. Apply PET Power Distribution Unit LOTO. See Applying LOTO - PET Power Distribution Unit.
    2. Apply PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem LOTO. See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
    Note When the PET/MR insert is removed from the bore, make sure that cables and hoses do not get caught, tangled, or kinked. Avoid bringing the insert to a point that may cause stress on any cables or hoses.
  8. Remove the magnet covers. See Removing the magnet covers.
  9. Remove the rear pedestal and the rear bridge. See Removing the rear pedestal.
  10. Remove the front and middle bridges. See Front and Middle Bridge Removal.
  11. Remove the rear endbell. See Rear End Bell Removal for PET/MR.
  12. Remove the front endbell. See Front End Bell Removal for PET/MR.
  13. Replace the leak sensors. See Leak Sensor and Leak Detector Box Replacement.
  14. Assemble the PET/MR insert installation tool. See PET/MR insert installation tool setup.
  15. Remove the PET/MR insert from the bore. See PET/MR Insert Removal.
