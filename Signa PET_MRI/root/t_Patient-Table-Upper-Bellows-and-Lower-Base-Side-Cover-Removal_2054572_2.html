Fastening the patient table upper bellows and removing the lower base side covers

Fasten the upper bellows in the up position and remove one or both of the lower base side covers.

Prerequisites

About this task

In this procedure, there are two types of actions. For the lower base side covers, you remove one or both of them. However, for the upper bellows, you fasten it in an up position.

The upper bellows, an accordion style cover, can be positioned up or down depending on the type of servicing needed. The lower base side cover is a solid single cover that runs the length of the lower chassis from the front caster assembly to the rear caster assembly on each side.

Topic ID: id_SL1911158-1058185

Fastening the upper bellows

Procedure

  1. Depending on servicing needs, the patient table may be docked or undocked.
  2. Remove four screws (two at front, two at rear) that connect the bottom end of the upper bellows to the top of the lower base side cover.
  3. To fasten the upper bellows in an up position, lift the bellows from the bottom of both sides and use the Velcro straps located on the inside of the bellows to secure the cover in place.
Topic ID: id_SL1911162-1058185

Removing the lower base side cover

Procedure

  1. Remove four screws (two at front, two at rear) that connect the bottom end of the upper bellows to the top of the lower base side cover.
    Note Once you remove the four screws from the bottom of the upper bellows, you can fasten the upper bellows in an up position with the Velcro straps.
  2. On the left or right side of the table, remove two screws from the lower base side cover (see item 5 below).
  3. Lift the lower base side cover off of front and rear guide studs on the front and rear base covers.
