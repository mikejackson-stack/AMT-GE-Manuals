Movement of the table or cradle can result in pinched or crushed fingers.
Keep fingers away from the gaps between the table, cradle, and the Low Profile Carriage Assembly (LPCA) when docking/undocking the table.
Topic ID: id_SL13719881-1276449

Removal

Procedure

  1. Press the Home button on the Operator Control Panel (OCP) to drive the LPCA and cradle to the patient end.
  2. Manually lift the LPCA hook (1) and drive the LPCA to the rear.
  3. Undock the patient table and move it out of the magnet room.
  4. Lift the front end of the cradle no more than 2.54 cm (1 in), and gently slide the cradle forward until the secondary pin obstructs further movement.
  5. Push in the right actuator button on the front of the table base.
    Note

    Pressing the actuator button engages the secondary cradle latch cable and causes the secondary latch pin stop to move down flush with the table top surface, allowing the cradle to slide forward.

  6. Slide the cradle out approximately 20 cm (8 in) from the rear of the table to clear the cradle vertical stopper from the table top rails at the back.
  7. Prop up each end of the cradle from the patient table.
  8. Remove the cover at each end of the cradle.
  9. Remove the tie wraps securing the P4 connector cable.
  10. Remove the P4 Connector cable from the mounting block by removing the screws and the brass RoHs fastener.
  11. Remove the cable carrier mounting bracket by removing the screws that secure it.
  12. Remove the cable carrier from the mounting bracket.
  13. Remove the P4 connector through the opening.
Topic ID: id_SL13719896-1276449

Installation

Procedure

  1. Place the new cradle assembly on the table, and insert the P4 connector from the bottom of the cradle through the opening.
  2. Position two tie wraps for the P4 connector cable.
  3. Reassemble the cable carrier mounting bracket as shown in Cable carrier.
  4. Reattach the cable carrier mounting bracket as shown in Cable carrier mounting bracket.
  5. Reattach the P4 connector cable to the mounting block with the screws and the brass RoHs fastener as shown in P4 connector cable mounting block.
  6. Secure the P4 connector cable to the mounting block with the tie wraps positioned earlier.
  7. Reattach the covers to each end of the cradle.
  8. Loosen the screws on the vertical latch, and move the cradle to the rear of the table so it engages the flipper.
  9. Engage the V-plunger (1) on the vertical latch with the table top guide block (2) so cradle motion is restricted to 2 mm (0.08 in) in either direction, then retighten the mounting screws on the vertical latch.
  10. Remove the screws (1) that secure the cover to the LPCA base, and remove the cover.
  11. Release the lock screws (2) on both right (4) and left (1) plungers, then turn the plungers until they are at the service end of the LPCA.
  12. Loosen the mounting screws on the cradle presence sensor (3) block and push it toward the LPCA.
  13. Return the patient table to the magnet room, redock it, and move the LPCA forward until the LPCA hook latches with the cradle. (It may be necessary to adjust the table height to accomplish this.)

    When the LPCA hook latches properly with cradle (4), a visible gap (1) should exist between the plungers, cradle presence sensor, and H-plunger on the vertical latch (2), and the spool top block (3).

  14. Notice
    In the next step, if the vertical latch is not pressed, the cradle cannot be moved out of table.
    To ensure the LPCA hook remains latched to the cradle, move the cradle all the way from the front to the back of the patient table by pressing the vertical latch horizontal plunger until the cradle enters the bridge. (If not pressed, the plunger will hit the bridge and stop cradle motion.) Proceed as follows:
    1. Move the right plunger forward to touch spool top block (4).
    2. Move the left plunger forward to the H-plunger of the vertical latch (3) until it pushes the V-plunger flush with the bottom of the cradle.
    3. Adjust the position of the cradle presence sensor (5) until it touches the spool top block.
  15. Reattach the LPCA cover, front cradle cover, and rear cradle cover.
Topic ID: id_15196989

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Confirm the cradle release functions properly. If not, proceed to Cradle Release Pin Height Adjustment.
  2. Confirm that the LPCA connects to the cradle and that the cradle can move in and out of the magnet bore.
