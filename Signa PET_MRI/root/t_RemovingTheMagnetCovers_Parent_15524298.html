Removing the magnet covers

Remove the front, rear (two halves), and side cover. For some of the side and top covers, other covers must be removed first.

Prerequisites

Required conditions
Detach the patient table from the scanner. Store the patient table where it will not interfere with this procedure.
Remove the trim rings, except the ring on the bridge cover. Store the trim rings with the patient table.
Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

DANGER
Strong Magnetic Field
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field produced by the magnet.
Do not bring any ferromagnetic tools or equipment into the magnet room.

Removing the rear pedestal covers

About this task

The rear pedestal covers do not attach to the magnet. You do not need to remove the rear pedestal covers to access the magnet.

Procedure

  1. Remove the screws and washers from the top of each cover and from the lower back (2 at the top of each cover and 1 holding both covers at the lower back). Remove the rear pedestal covers.
  2. Remove the side pedestal covers.
Topic ID: id_SL5486187-1188046

Removing the rear cable cover

Procedure

  1. Remove the rear pedestal covers. See Remove the rear pedestal covers.
  2. Remove the rear cable cover screws.
  3. Slide the cable cover straight up to disengage it from the upper hooks.

Removing the In-Room Display (IRD) pivot covers

Procedure

  1. Remove the 4 screws from the IRD bracket that hold the front pivot covers.
  2. Remove the IRD front pivot covers.
  3. Remove the 4 screws from the IRD bracket that hold the rear pivot covers.
  4. Remove the IRD rear pivot covers.

Removing the side covers

About this task

You must remove the center cover on each side before you remove the side rear or side front covers.

Procedure

  1. Remove the screw that holds the left center side cover in position.
  2. Remove the left center side cover.
    1. Guide the patient alert squeeze bulb and peripheral gating cable through the opening as you remove the cover.
    2. Put the patient alert squeeze bulb and peripheral gating cable on the floor, along the system.
  3. Remove the screws that hold the left-side covers in position.
  4. Remove the left-side covers.
  5. Remove the screw that holds the right center side cover in position.
  6. Remove the right center side cover.
  7. Remove the screws that hold the right-side covers in position.
  8. Remove the right-side covers.

Removing the bridge cover

Procedure

  1. Remove the front trim rings.
  2. Remove the screws that secure the bridge cover to the system.
  3. Disengage the trim ring and bridge cover from the system.
  4. Lift the bridge cover and trim ring straight up.

Removing the laser light cover

Procedure

  1. Remove the screw that holds the laser light cover in position.
  2. Remove the laser light cover from the system.

Removing the front and rear covers

Procedure

  1. Remove the screws that hold the left front cover to the system.
    Note The two Operator Control Panels (OCPs) each hide two front cover installation screws. Remove the OCPs, remove the screws, and replace the OCPs. Reconnect the OCP cables.
  2. Remove the left front cover. Lean it against the side of the system.
    Note Do not stretch the OCP cables.
  3. Remove the screws that hold the right front cover to the system.
  4. Remove the right front cover. Lean it against the side of the system.
  5. Remove the screws that hold the left rear cover to the rear end bell.
  6. Remove the screws that hold the left rear cover to the system.
  7. Remove the left rear cover.
  8. Remove the screws that hold the right rear panel to the rear end bell.
  9. Remove the screws that hold the right rear cover to the system.
  10. Remove the right rear cover.
Topic ID: id_SL5486191-1188046

Removing the top covers

About this task

Remove the covers in the following order:
  • Front top cover
  • Left top cover
  • Right top cover

Procedure

  1. Remove the screws that secure the front top cover to the system.
  2. Remove the front top cover.
  3. Remove the screws that secure the left top cover to the system.
  4. Remove the left top cover.
  5. Remove the screws that secure the right top cover to the system.
  6. Remove the right top cover.
