PET chiller setup

Procedure

  1. Notice
    The number in parentheses within an instruction corresponds to the numbered item in the referenced illustration.
    Unpack the PET chiller from the pallet in the equipment room.
  2. Position the PET chiller next to the PET cabinet and as close as possible to the SPW in the equipment room. Consult the PET chiller manual for proper spacing to the adjacent equipment.
  3. Note The PET chiller arrives with the voltage and frequency switches set in the SHIP position, and will not operate unless the switches are set.
    Locate the switches on the back of the PET chiller, and set the voltage switch and the frequency switch as follows:

    • For the United States, set the switches to 208 to 230 V and 60 Hz.

    • For other countries, set the switches for the appropriate voltage, 208 to 230 V or 200 V and frequency, 60 Hz or 50 Hz for that local area.

Mounting the PET chiller filter

About this task

Notice
PREFERRED MOUNTING LOCATION
  • Option 1: On a wall near the PETMR Chiller
  • Option 2: On Chiller side panel if Option 1 is not possible

Filter hardware is included as part of the Coolant Filter Service Kit (5549454). The installation process documented below is for sites where the filter assembly can be mounted to the PETMR Chiller cabinet, using the provided hardware. If you cannot mount the filter assembly to the PETMR Chiller cabinet, follow applicable building codes to have the customer properly mount the filter on the wall near the Chiller (preferred) per Cutting the male end of green hose. The filter assembly can be mounted on either side of the PETMR Chiller.

Note The Filter assembly should be mounted high enough so that a bucket can be placed under the filter when changing cartridges.

Procedure

  1. If attaching the filter to the PETMR Chiller, it should be located near the top center of the PETMR Chiller cabinet in the right-left direction or near the front for easy filter access during replacement. See Filter mounting on chiller If attaching at a different location, use applicable building codes to mount the filter, see Cutting the male end of green hose.
  2. Locate the mounting bracket from filter kit as shown. Use four holes on bracket shown. After marking, use center punch to put the first pilot hole in the PETMR chiller side panel, see Tape beneath the mounting bracket to catch drill fragments.
    Note One screw will be used to attach the bracket to the cabinet and then the remaining hole will be used to locate the other screw.
  3. Put tape in position to catch filings/metal chips on the outside and inside of the PETMR chiller. Tape beneath the mounting bracket to catch drill fragments.
  4. Using a variable speed drill, start drilling the self-tapping screw into the PETMR chiller cabinet. See Start drilling. When the self tapping screw goes through cabinet, remove the screw, then install the bracket and install the screw. See First hole to secure the bracket.
    Note Use the screws with full threads for mounting the bracket onto the chiller cabinet. Use the proper screws for fastening to chiller, see Proper screws for fastening to the chiller.
  5. Put level on bracket to ensure that it is parallel to the PETMR chiller cabinet.
  6. Center punch a hole in the 2nd location, then drill the 2nd location.
  7. When the bracket is installed on the PETMR chiller or other location, install the filter on the bracket using 4 partially threaded screws as shown in Filter mounting on chiller. Use a ½-inch wrench or socket to secure the filter to the bracket. Make sure that the red button is orientated as shown in the figure.

PET chiller hose connections

About this task

Plan the placement of the yellow supply hose and gray return hose from the PET chiller to the Magnet Room, and connect each hose to the manifold at the base of the PET/MR insert. Assemble the parts in the following order:

  • One External Flow Control (EFC) chiller priming kit (5494714)
  • Two parker stainless steel fittings (5462477)
  • 12 parker male push-lok brass hose fittings (5270174)
  • Two parker female swivel push-lok brass hose fittings (5263572)
  • Lengths of yellow supply hose (5458706) and Gray Return Hose (5457192) cut to fit from the PET chiller, through the SPW slots, and to the service end of the magnet. Total length of the hoses should not exceed 108 ft (33m).
  • Four 1 inch Shut-Off Valves (5481367), two placed near the PET chiller and two near the manifold at the service end of the magnet
  • Quick Disconnects (QD) on each hose (Male: 5443761, Female: 5453935)
  • Two check valve assemblies (5338545)
  • Filter kit hose from PET Chiller to filter assembly (5561397)

Attaching EFC pump priming kit

About this task

The EFC Pump Priming Kit should arrive with thread seal tape around the threads of the inlet and outlet connectors. Thread seal tape will need to be reapplied to both connectors before assembly.

Procedure

  1. Locate the EFC Pump Priming Kit for the PET chiller, and remove the outlet connector as shown.
  2. Wrap thread seal tape in the direction of the threads on the inlet connector. (Refer to Step 1 for tape application.)
  3. Attach the EFC Kit to the inlet port on the PET chiller.
  4. Tighten with the bypass hose positioned at a right downward angle to prevent any kinks or bends.
  5. Wrap thread seal tape on the outlet connector in the direction of the threads that connect to the PET chiller.
  6. Attach the outlet connector to the outlet port on the PET chiller.
  7. Reapply thread seal tape to the threads of the outlet connector, and reattach the hose to the valve assembly.

Preventing fluid loss

About this task

Instructions are provided below to prevent the loss of PET chiller coolant using one of the following for connectors and fittings:

  • Preferred – PTFE Thread Seal Tape (5476736)

    Note

    PTFE thread seal tape is designed for one-time use. If a connection with thread seal tape is disassembled, remove the existing tape, and apply new tape before reassembly.

  • Optional – Loctite 565 or 567, especially when replacing parts in the Chiller line or reusing fittings.

Procedure

  1. Properly apply the thread seal tape:
    1. Wind the thread seal tape clockwise (as viewed from end of fitting) around the mating components, ensuring it does not unwind.
    2. Ensure the tape is applied uniformly without bulges, and cut or tear the end neatly. Reapply new tape if any threads are exposed.
  2. If using a Loctite seal, make sure threads are thoroughly clean of contaminants or residual thread seal tape.
  3. CAUTION
    Possible Equipment Damage
    Overtightening connectors may result in stripped threads or cracked fittings.
    Tighten all fittings according to the manufacturer’s specifications.
    Tighten each straight or 37° connector enough to prevent any leakage.
  4. If leakage persists or occurs, check for damaged threads and/or total number of threads engaged.
    1. Notice
      To avoid damage to the threads, do not connect/disconnect fittings more than necessary.
      If the threads on the fitting are badly nicked or galled, replace the fitting.
    2. Ensure thread engagement is 3-1/2 to six threads.

      • If less than 3-1/2 threads, tighten the connector further, but not more than one full turn.

      • If greater than six threads, check both parts and replace any part with out-of-tolerance threads.

  5. Keep the PET chiller reservoir’s cap tightly closed to prevent evaporation and contamination of the coolant.
Topic ID: id_SL12108128-1188134

Routing hoses

Procedure

  1. Notice
    Total hose length from the PET chiller to the magnet should be no more than 108 ft (33m).
    Route a yellow hose from the PET chiller through J300 on the SPW, and end just above the magnet bore.
  2. Route a gray hose from the PET chiller through J301 on the SPW, and end just above the magnet bore.
    Note

    These hoses will be attached to the manifold later in this procedure.

Topic ID: id_SL5559730-1188134

Connecting hoses with fittings

Procedure

  1. Notice
    If thread seal tape is used, ensure the tape does not overhang the first thread as it could shred and get into the fluid.
    Make sure each hose has a clean cut at the PET chiller.
  2. Wrap thread seal tape around the threads on the nontapered end (1) of the two stainless steel (SS) fittings.
    Note

    Do not put thread seal tape on the flared end of the fittings as it will affect the integrity of the seal.

  3. Attach the two SS fittings to the outlet and inlet connectors on the EFC Kit.
  4. Cut the male end of the green hose off as that is not needed. See Cutting the male end of green hose.
    Note All of the green hose included will be used. Do not cut in the middle of the hose.
  5. Connect the stripped end of yellow hose to the output connection of the filter at the barb fitting. Make sure that there are no sharp bends in the hose so that the coolant will properly flow. Push the hose on tightly. See Chiller filter hose connections.
    Note DO NOT use hose clamps, they are not needed.
  6. Connect the stripped end of green hose to the input connection on the filter at the barb fitting. Make sure that there are no sharp bends in the hose so that the coolant will properly flow. Push the hose on tightly. See Chiller filter hose connections.
    Note DO NOT use hose clamps, they are not needed.
  7. Connect the female fitting end of the green hose to the supply of the PETMR chiller. Make sure the hose is seated all the way on the fitting before connecting to chiller. See Chiller filter hose connections.
    ItemDescription
    1 Filter input connection
    2Filter output connection
    3Chiller supply connection
  8. Attach a female brass fitting to the inlet SS fitting, and connect the fitting on the gray hose to the inlet port of the PET chiller.
  9. Make a clean cut on each hose from about one to 1-1/2 ft (0.5m) from the Chiller.
  10. Slide the other end of the yellow and gray hoses over the ribs of the fittings on the check valves.
    Note

    The check valves have an arrow engraved on their housing to indicate flow direction. If the check valves are reversed, coolant will not flow to the system. Make sure the arrow points in the correct flow direction as indicated:

    • Yellow hose – Arrow should point away from the Chiller.

    • Gray hose – Arrow should point towards the Chiller.

  11. Make a clean cut on the hose approximately 2 to 3 ft (61.0 to 91.4 cm) from the Chiller to attach the shut-off valves.
  12. Wrap thread seal tape around the threads of four Male Brass Hose Fittings as shown in Attaching hoses to check valves.
  13. Attach a male brass hose fitting to each end of two Shut-Off Valves.
  14. Insert a male brass hose fitting with the shut-off valve into each hose until the barb is inside the hose.
  15. While gripping each hose approximately one inch from the end, push each hose with a steady force until the bottom of the hose fitting is covered by the yellow plastic cap on the shut-off valve as shown.
    Note PET chiller filter connections are not shown.

Preparing hoses for manifold

About this task

Follow these instructions to attach the fittings to the supply and return coolant hoses near the manifold on the PET/MR insert.

Procedure

  1. Make sure the ends of the yellow and gray hoses have a clean cut at the magnet.
  2. While gripping each hose approximately one inch from the end, push each hose with a steady force until the bottom of the hose fitting is covered by the yellow plastic cap on the shut-off valve as shown in EFC priming kit.
  3. Attach the male brass fitting on the 5 ft gray hose to a female QD.
  4. Attach the 5 ft gray hose with the male brass fitting to the shut-off valve connected to the gray hose from the Chiller.
  5. Repeat above steps in this section for yellow hose connections to manifold.

Electrical connections

Procedure

  1. Using a Phillips screwdriver, remove the two cover screws that secure the electrical safety cover to the back of the PET chiller, and remove the cover.
  2. To attach the strain relief assembly to the PET chiller:
    1. Slide the strain relief assembly (shown below) into the opening for the power wires so the locking mechanism is at the bottom.
    2. Position the strain relief lock nut on top of the assembly and carefully tighten with channel lock pliers.
    3. Feed the power wires through the strain relief assembly.
    4. Twist the locking mechanism on the strain relief assembly (Item 3 in Strain relief assembly) one turn to secure the power wires.
  3. Use a flat-blade screwdriver to fasten the brown and blue wires with the terminal block screws, and use an adjustable wrench to attach the ground wire to the ground post.
  4. Reattach the electrical safety cover to the back of the chiller with the two Phillips screws removed earlier.
  5. Coil the excess cable and store it in the cable tray between the PET chiller and the PET cabinet.
  6. Assemble the yellow and gray service extension hoses and PET/MR insert drain hose, utilizing the parts from the PET/MR cooling hose kit (G8500PS).
    1. Cut both service extension hoses to a length of 13.25 ft (4.03m), and attach the appropriate fittings to each hose end.
    2. Attach the applicable fittings and the quick-disconnect to the drain hose for the PET/MR insert.
    3. Attach the parker hose pump adapter for purging to the PET chiller as shown.
      Note PET chiller filter connections are not shown.
