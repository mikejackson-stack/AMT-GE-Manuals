Setting up Well Counter Correction (WCC) calibration

Steps on how to set up a PET 3D Well Counter Correction (WCC) calibration.

Prerequisites

Consumables
ItemQuantityPart numberManufacturer

Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

DANGER
Radiation exposure
The phantoms used for calibrating the SIGNA PET/MR system contain a radioactive substance.
Special training and compliance with facility guidelines are required to handle these phantoms.

Procedure

  1. Get the pre- and post-injection values for the flood phantom from the customer. Note these values for future reference.
    Note

    For the best precision in the 3D WCC procedure, the measured volume of the actual phantom imaged must be entered in the processing software.

  2. Start a new exam and enter PETWCC3D for both Patient ID and Patient Name. Enter 111 for Weight and 5’0” for Height.
    Note The number in parentheses in an instruction corresponds to the numbered item in the referenced illustration.
    Note If this is a new installation without Install In Spec (IIS) passed, start a new exam and do the scan with the Patient ID set to geservice. Use the Edit patient information procedure after the exam is complete to change the Patient ID to PETWCC3D.
  3. On the Protocol Selector screen, select PET WCC Calibration, then click Accept.
  4. Set a landmark at approximately 0.5 meters from the end of the cradle and push Advance to Scan on the magnet. (This will move the LPCA to the service end of the magnet.)
  5. Unlatch the cradle from the LPCA and slide it through the magnet to expose the CMA coil.
  6. From the rear (bridge side) of the magnet, unplug and remove the CMA coil in order to access the rails.
  7. Slide the right-hand lip of the phantom assembly inside the rails.
  8. Unscrew the left-hand side of the phantom assembly locking screws to adjust the left-hand side lip. Slide the lip into the phantom assembly so that the assembly lays flat in the bed.
  9. Slide the lip out inside the left-hand side rails and tighten the locking screws.
  10. Adjust the slider to select the flood phantom.
  11. Put the phantom into the phantom holder assembly.
  12. From the rear (bridge side) of the magnet, slide the assembly into the bore over the center of the space created by the removal of the CMA coil and gap filler until the assembly stops.
    Note The phantom will not be correctly in position and the calibration will fail if the assembly is installed from the front (patient table side) of the magnet.
  13. Select the Radioactive Dose tab and click Dose.
    1. Enter the dose values gotten from the customer into their correct fields.
      Notice
      For the best accuracy, enter the measured volume of the actual phantom imaged in the processing software.
    2. Type 5640 for Tracer Volume (mL).
      Note All fields involving tracer activity require date and time.
    3. Click Nuclide/Tracer.
    4. Use Prior or Next to select 18F and FDG – fluorodeoxyglucose from the list and click OK.
    5. Click N / Y for MRAC/phantom shown in WCC calibration parameters. Click Prior or Next, select Fillable FDG WCC Flood, and click OK.
      Note If you select Other, attenuation correction will be disabled. If this is done in error, start a new scan and repeat this step.
  14. Click Save Rx.
  15. Click Proceed when prompted to continue the PET scan despite the missing PET tracer information.
  16. Click Scan.
  17. Wait for the PET scan to complete. The PET images require approximately 3 minutes to reconstruct.
  18. End the exam.
  19. Click the tools icon, then Service Browser in the service desktop manager section to show the MR service desktop.
  20. Click the PET radio button, then select the Calibration tab, and 3D WCC Calculation from the list.
  21. Select WCC Recon FDG ... from the list, and click OK.
  22. Click Accept on the Sensitivity and Activity Correction Generation screen.
    Note

    The typical range for the WCC activity correction factor is 4.75 to 6.00. If it is outside of this range, see Troubleshooting problems with PET 3D Well Counter Correction (WCC).

  23. On the common service desktop, click the PET radio button, then select the Calibration tab and Cal Manager from the list (see 3D WCC Calculation).
