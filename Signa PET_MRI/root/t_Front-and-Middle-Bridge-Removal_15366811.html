Table 5. Safety
Warning
FERROUS MATERIAL HAZARD
THE BRIDGE SUPPORT AND PULLEY ASSEMBLIES CONTAIN PARTS MADE WITH FERROUS COMPONENTS. HOLDING THESE COMPONENTS TOO CLOSE TO THE MAGNET BORE WILL FORCIBLY ATTRACT THEM TO THE MAGNET.
TO PREVENT POSSIBLE BODILY INJURY OR DAMAGE TO THE COMPONENTS, KEEP THEM AS FAR FROM THE MAGNETIC FIELD AS POSSIBLE.
Warning
POSSIBLE BACK INJURY
THE BRIDGES ARE HEAVY, WEIGH MORE THAN 50 POUNDS (22.7 KG), AND CONTAIN FERROUS MATERIAL, MAKING THEM STRONGLY ATTRACTED TO THE MAGNET.
TWO PEOPLE ARE NECESSARY TO MOVE THE BRIDGES INTO THE MAGNET ROOM AND POSITION WITHIN THE MAGNET BORE.
CAUTION
Crush/Pinch Hazard
Movement of the table or cradle can result in pinched or crushed fingers.
Keep fingers away from the gaps between the table, cradle, and the low profile carriage assembly (LPCA) when docking/undocking the table or while connecting coils.

Procedure

  1. Notice
    The number in parentheses within an instruction corresponds to the numbered item in the referenced illustration.
    Press Home on the operator control panel (OCP) to drive the LPCA and cradle all the way to the patient end.
  2. Manually lift the LPCA hook (1) and drive the LPCA all the way to the rear.
  3. Shut down the system (see System Startup and Shutdown).
  4. After the system is powered down, turn off the PGR PDU breaker at the MDP.
  5. LOTO the PGR PDU breaker. See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem. Leave the main, HEC, and PET PDU breakers on.
  6. Remove the four M6 brass button-head screws (two each in the front and rear) that secure the cover to the LPCA base (2). Lift the cover up and off of the LPCA.
  7. Remove the drive belts from the front and middle bridges:
    1. Remove the M5 brass socket-head cap screws (1) that secure the two front belt clamp plates (2) to the LPCA base and remove the clamps.
    2. Pull the belts (3) from under both ends of the LPCA and from the front and middle bridges.
  8. Step Remove the CMA Coil or CMA Proxy from the magnet.
  9. Remove the CMA support gap filler (3) from between the front (1) and middle (2) bridges.
  10. On each side of each bridge, use a flat-blade screwdriver in the recessed access area to pry the roller assembly up and remove the roller assemblies.
  11. Use a 10 mm Allen wrench to remove the four M12 Allen bolts that hold each bridge in place, which includes two M12 x 80 mm long brass socket-head cap screws (1) and two M12 x 40 mm long brass hex socket-head cap screws (2) for each bridge.
  12. Use an 8 mm Allen wrench to remove the four M10 x 25 socket-head Allen screws from the two bridge connecting plates (1) that hold the rear and middle bridges together. The connecting plates are located on the underside of the rear and middle bridges.
  13. Remove each bridge from the magnet bore. Store them in a safe location.
    Note

    Move one bridge at a time, with one person on each end.

Finalization

Remove LOTO from the PDU in the PGR cabinet. See Removing LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
