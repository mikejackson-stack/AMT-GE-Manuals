Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

DANGER
Fatal electric shock hazard
The gradient amplifiers act as constant load sources and will send maximum current to any load, including any human who gets across the outputs.
To prevent fatal electric shock, follow LOTO steps to disable and make sure of safe voltage levels.
Warning
Strong magnetic field
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field that the magnet produces.
Do not bring any ferrous tools or equipment into the magnet room.

About this task

The gradient filter attaches to the secondary pen wall (SPW) from the equipment room. The thumbscrews on the filter attach to PEM nuts on the SPW.
Topic ID: id_SL2018500-1062451

Removing the GEN1 gradient filter

Procedure

  1. Do LOTO on the PGR cabinet. See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
  2. From the scan room side of the gradient filter, remove the plastic gradient filter terminal covers.
    Note

    Two wrench technique: Use two wrenches when removing the cables from the gradient input/output connectors on each side of the SPW. One wrench prevents the post from turning, while the other loosens the nut. If you try to loosen the nut with only one wrench, the post itself could turn and damage the gradient filter.

  3. Remove the cables attached to the terminals in the scan room.
  4. From the equipment room side of the gradient filter, remove the plastic gradient filter terminal covers and remove the terminal cables. Use the two wrench technique to remove the cables.
  5. While in the equipment room at the SPW, remove the screws securing the gradient filter assembly to the SPW panel.

    Remove the gradient filter and set it aside.

Topic ID: id_SL2018510-1062451

Installing the GEN1 gradient filter

Procedure

  1. Lift the gradient filter onto the guide pins on the SPW and tighten the thumbscrews. When done, use a screwdriver to tighten.
  2. Attach the cables to the gradient filter connections on both sides of the filter. Start with the GND, Z-axis, Y-axis, and X-axis cables and ground wires. Snugly tighten each cap nut to attach the correct axis cable to the correct gradient filter connection. The lug connections between axes and polarities should not be touching.
    Note

    Red cable indicates positive (+) wires, black cables indicate negative (-) wires.

    Two wrench technique: Use two wrenches when attaching the cables from the gradient input/output connectors on each side of the SPW. One wrench prevents the post from turning, while the other tightens the nut. If you try to tighten the nut with only one wrench, the post itself could turn and damage the gradient filter.

    Note

    Make sure to tighten each cable as you attach it, because you cannot reach all cables after attaching them.

  3. Tighten the cap nuts and make sure that nuts are not loose.

    Make sure that all connections are tight, correct, and not touching each other. Make sure each screw is fixed, each nut is tight, and each cable is not detached from terminal.

  4. Replace the protective shield on the equipment room side and scan room side of the gradient filter.
  5. Remove LOTO from the PGR cabinet. See Removing LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
Topic ID: task_mq3_5ff_lsb

Removing the GEN2 gradient filter

Procedure

  1. Do LOTO on the PGR cabinet. See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
  2. From the scan room side, remove the gradient filter cover screws and cover.
  3. Starting on the left side of the gradient filter at the Z cable, remove the cable bracket screws and cable brackets.
    Note Cables are not shown for clarity.
  4. Remove the cable bolts and screws.
    Note Do not remove the cable hardware with the torque wrench.
    Note Cables are not shown for clarity.
  5. Remove the cable from the filter.
  6. Repeat the steps to remove the remaining two cables (Y and X).
  7. From the equipment room side, remove the gradient filter cover screws and cover.
  8. Starting on the left side of the gradient filter at the X cable, remove the cable bracket screws and cable brackets.
  9. Remove the cable bolts and screws.
    Note Do not remove the cable hardware with the torque wrench.
  10. Remove the cable from the filter.
  11. Repeat the steps to remove the remaining two cables (Y and Z).
  12. Remove the screws surrounding the filter mounting plate from the penetration wall panel.
  13. Remove the gradient filter.
Topic ID: task_c2b_5ff_lsb

Installing the GEN2 gradient filter

Procedure

  1. Put the gradient filter in the opening of the penetration wall using the guide pegs for support and positioning.
  2. Put the filter frame over the filter on the filter plate and secure it to the plate.
  3. Secure the gradient filter assembly to the penetration wall of the penetration panel.

    For new installations, use the supplied hardware.

  4. From the scan room side, remove the gradient filter cover and screws.
  5. Put the gradient cables on the corresponding clamps on the gradient filter. The black insulation should be under the top clamp, braid under the bottom clamp. Loosely secure the cables with the clamps.
    Note The lower clamps are reversible to provide a uniform contact surface around the braid for EMC. The smaller side of the clamps (1 AWG) should face inward the X and Y (1 AWG) cables. The larger side of the clamp should face inward on the Z (2/0 AWG) cable. The upper clamps are used as a strain relief and are sized to work with either cable.
    Note

    This image represents the equipment room side only.

  6. Connect each ground cable (blue lugs) to the ground pegs on the gradient filter. Do not overtighten the GND studs.
  7. Attach and secure the X,Y (pink lugs), and Z axis (orange lugs) to the corresponding posts.
    Note Single posts are positive (red wire) and double posts are negative (black wire)
  8. Torque each socket head cap screw (SHCS) to 45 N m, using the 3/8 inch hex adapter.
  9. Tighten the upper and lower clamps until each gradient cable is secured. Do not pinch or compress the gradient cable.
    Note The lower clamps are reversible to provide a uniform contact surface around the braid for EMC. The smaller side of the clamps (1 AWG) should face inward the X and Y (1 AWG) cables. The larger side of the clamp should face inward on the Z (2/0 AWG) cable. The upper clamps are used as a strain relief and are sized to work with either cable.
  10. Repeat the above steps to install the gradient cables for the equipment room side. See Securing the gradient filter to the PW.
  11. Install the two gradient filter covers.
  12. Remove LOTO from the PGR cabinet. See Removing LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
Topic ID: id_15211136

Finalization

Procedure

Run the DQA II tool to make sure of proper gradient polarities. See Doing DQA calibration with the DQA III phantom.
