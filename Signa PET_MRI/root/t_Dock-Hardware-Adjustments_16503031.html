Adjusting dock hardware

The Motor On actuation switch in the dock assembly should be adjusted so it is activated when the table Up foot pedal is pressed approximately three-fourths of its total length of travel.

Safety
DANGER
Fatal electric shock hazard
To prevent fatal electric shock, disconnect power from the Power Distribution Unit (PDU) before you do the following procedures.
Do lockout/tagout per GE OSHA lockout/tagout requirements listed in the Field Service Environmental Health and Safety Manual & Training 2000 (2135387-200).
Warning
Possible personal injury
This procedure requires movement of ferrous material in close proximity to the magnet.
Two field engineers are required at all times.
Warning
Personal injury and equipment damage
Strong magnetic field.
When servicing any magnetic equipment, it is critically important that the service engineer consciously plan the path to take when moving highly ferrous devices in the magnet environment. The path should be as far from the magnet as practical and avoid high flux-density fields.

Safety Requirements

  • The service path must allow for the dock to be moved along the ground a distance equal to that of the distance between the magnet and the foot-end of the patient table prior to being lifted vertically.
  • Two (2) MR safety trained personnel must be present at all times when servicing highly ferrous devices in the areas of magnetic fields.
  • When planning a service path, it is critical that the path be clear and sufficiently wide. Make sure that there are no trip hazards, obstacles, clutter, slippery surfaces, or other items even partially restricting the path. If there are portable obstacles in a path, remove them from the area and replace them after the service action is completed. It is required to walk the path prior to beginning service to make sure that there is sufficient space through which to pass for you and the object being serviced.

About this task

The Motor On actuation switch in the dock assembly should be adjusted so it is activated when the table Up foot pedal is pressed approximately three-fourths of its total length of travel. This is factory adjusted; however, it is possible to adjust the position of the Motor On switch in the field to maintain the proper actuation level.

Procedure

  1. Remove the dock from the magnet room. See Replacing the dock.
  2. Using a 3/4 inch wrench, remove four bolts (two on each side) to unfasten the dock cover from the dock base. See the illustration below.
  3. While removing the dock cover, disconnect the switch connector.
  4. Carefully remove the dock cover from the dock base and then turn the dock cover upside down.
    Note

    Use care to make sure that the top of the cover is not scratched. Put down a cloth before setting the cover on the floor.

  5. Loosen two mounting screws securing the Motor On switch to the side of the dock assembly.
  6. Adjust the Motor On switch position so it is activated (clicks) when the table Up foot pedal is pressed approximately three-fourths of its total length of travel.
  7. After adjustment is complete, tighten the two mounting screws.
  8. Connect the switch connector inside the main dock cover.
  9. Replace the main dock cover by securing the four mounting bolts.
  10. Reinstall the dock. See Replacing the dock.

Finalization

  1. Make sure that the dock Motor On switch operates properly when the table Up pedal is pressed.
  2. Make sure that the cradle travel into the bore is still okay. If not, see Bridge alignments, dock centering, and adjustments and do the recentering procedure for the dock/table.
  3. Do a “good-bye” or validation scan.
