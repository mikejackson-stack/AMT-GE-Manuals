Removing the MNS exciter

Procedure

  1. Make sure that you have read and understood the electrical safety requirements before starting this procedure. See Electrical Safety.
  2. Shut down and power off the host computer. See Shutting down the host computer.
  3. Perform LOTO on the RF amplifier and the PEN cabinet (magnet room electronics). Applying LOTO - RF Subsystem (magnet room electronics) and Applying LOTO - PEN Cabinet (magnet room electronics).
  4. Remove the thumbscrews that secure the MNS exciter to the PEN cabinet.
  5. Disconnect all the cables to the MNS exciter. Be sure to note cable locations.
  6. Carefully slide the MNS exciter out of the PEN cabinet.
