Warning
Possible personal injury or equipment damage!
The module could fall when using The hoist tool.
Before putting weight on the hoist chain, be sure that the links are aligned properly. This helps keep the chain from binding up, or becoming kinked, which is nearly impossible to correct when the chain is under tension.
Notice
Before removing power from the PEN cabinet, verify that the ICNs are properly shut down. Perform LOTO on the PDM-RF power on the PDU to remove power from the PEN cabinet and driver module. See Applying LOTO - PEN Cabinet (magnet room electronics).

Potential crush hazard

Do not put any part of the body below the replacement part.

About this task

Overview

This document describes the procedure for replacing the driver module. The driver module weighs approximately 50 pounds, so a hoist service tool is required to remove it from the PEN cabinet. The hoist service tool allows one person to safely perform this procedure.

Removing Driver Module

Procedure

  1. Notice
    PDM - recon power can remain on.
    Perform LOTO on the PEN cabinet. See Applying LOTO - PEN Cabinet (magnet room electronics).
  2. Remove the four screws securing the driver module to the PEN cabinet frame.
  3. Loosen the 16 thumbs screws securing the penetration panel to the magnet room wall. This allows access to the rear of the driver module.
  4. Carefully pull the penetration panel away from the wall, leaving all cables attached to the panel.
  5. Remove the cables from the rear of the driver module.
    Note

    Remove screws to access the PWR cable. Be careful not to drop them, they are very small.

    Put the screws back in when the PWR CBL is removed.

  6. Push the driver module forward a little, from the pen wall side.
  7. Set up the hoist service tool according to Hoist Service Kit and Lifting Accessories.
  8. Pull the driver module out from the cabinet until the slide rails lock into position.
  9. Attach lifting brackets to each side of the driver module.
  10. Attach the lifting bar (hoist kit) to the lifting brackets. Attach the lifting bar to the hoist chain.
  11. Ratchet the winch to tighten the hoist chain.
  12. Press in the release tabs on each side of the driver module, and pull it out further from the cabinet.
    Warning
    Potential crush hazard
    If the replacement part accidentally falls, it can cause serious injury.
    Do not put any part of the body below the replacement part.
  13. From the magnet room side, unlatch the locking lever on each rail to remove the driver module from the PEN cabinet slide rails.
  14. Release the PEN cabinet slide rails and slide them back into the cabinet.
  15. Remove both slide rails from the driver module. See Slide Rails, Release Tabs.
  16. Lower the driver module onto the FRU packaging platform for removal.

Installing Driver Module

Procedure

  1. Attach both slide rails to the new driver module. See Slide Rails, Release Tabs.
  2. Attach both lifting brackets to the new driver module. See Attach Lifting Brackets.
  3. Attach the lifting bar (hoist kit) to the lifting brackets; attach the lifting bar to the hoist chain. See Lifting Bar - Hoist Kit.
  4. Ratchet the winch to tighten the hoist chain and begin lifting the module.
  5. Slide out the PEN cabinet slide rails a short distance to see how high to lift the module.
    Note

    The slide rails for both the driver module and PEN cabinet must be aligned for proper insertion.

  6. Finish pulling out each PEN cabinet slide rail until each rail locks into position.
  7. Carefully slide the driver module slide rails into the PEN cabinet slide rails until they lock into position.
  8. Loosen the chain tension with the winch.
  9. Remove the lifting bar from the lifting brackets.
  10. Remove the lifting brackets from the driver module.
  11. From the magnet room side, unlatch the locking lever on each rail. See Locking Lever.
  12. Finish pushing the driver module into the PEN cabinet.
  13. Tighten the four screws to secure the driver module to the PEN cabinet frame. See Driver Module - Rear.
  14. Attach the cables to the rear of the driver module. See Driver Module - Rear.
  15. Tighten the 16 thumb screws to secure the penetration panel to the magnet room wall. See Removing the Penetration Panel.
Topic ID: id_15201729

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Remove LOTO. See Removing LOTO - RF Subsystem and/or PEN Cabinet.
  2. Perform TR Dynamic Disable Calibration.
  3. Complete a head and body test scan to ensure the system is functional.
