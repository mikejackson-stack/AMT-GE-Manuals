Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

Warning
Possible personal injury
The coolant fluid in the pet chiller is treated for bacteria and corrosion and not intended for human consumption.
Avoid getting the coolant in the eyes or ingesting it.
CAUTION
Possible Equipment Damage
If the PET Chiller is turned on without the wire mesh diffuser and filter, fluid could overflow, resulting in an overflow warning message (audio and displayed). PET detectors could also be damaged.
Do not operate the PET Chiller without the diffuser and filter in place.
CAUTION
Possible Equipment Damage
If the PET Chiller and the process and supply/return lines are not completely filled, the unit will shut down on a fault condition. The PET Chiller is designed to shut down if not properly primed.
Upon initial startup, continue adding fluid until the PET Chiller and supply/return lines are completely filled.
CAUTION
Possible Equipment Damage
Do not overfill the PET Chiller above the maximum (Max) level.
Filling it more than the maximum indicated will cause an overflow.

About this task

Overview

Details are provided for adding the coolant, setting the parameters on the PET chiller, and doing a brief test of the system. The chiller must be primed and all air exhumed from the system for proper operation. The preferred method is mounting the EFC Chiller Pump Priming Kit (P/N G8500RZ) to the rear of the PET chiller.

Preparation for priming

About this task

Preparation with the service extension hoses is only required if the PET/MR Insert is outside of the magnet bore. If the PET/MR Insert is inside the magnet bore, go to Priming the PET chiller. Service extension hoses are not needed.

Procedure

  1. Set up the PET/MR insert installation tool setup.
  2. Connect the service extension hoses to the supply and return hoses on the PET chiller:

    • Yellow Service Extension Hose (P/N 5498854) to the yellow hose from the chiller

    • Gray Service Extension Hose (P/N 5498855) to the gray hose from the chiller

  3. Connect the service extension hoses to the manifold hoses on the PET/MR insert:

    • Yellow service extension hose to the yellow manifold hose

    • Gray service extension hose to the gray manifold hose

  4. In the equipment room, turn PET chiller hose inline shut off valves to ON position as shown in PET chiller hose valves to on position.

Priming the PET chiller

Topic ID: id_SL12268578-1212878

Using External Flow Control (EFC) kit – preferred method

Procedure

  1. Notice
    The number in parentheses within an instruction corresponds to the numbered item in the referenced illustration.
    Make sure that the inline shut-off valves on the supply and return hoses are fully open as shown in PET chiller hose valves to on position.
  2. Fill the reservoir (1) to the three-quarter level.
  3. Set the flow valve on rear of PET chiller fully open as shown.
  4. Set the EFC Valve to the open position as shown.
  5. Add fluid to the reservoir (Item 1 in PET chiller) to ensure the PET Chiller is filled approximately halfway between the minimum (Min) and maximum (Max) levels.
  6. Start the PET Chiller. The chiller firmware will begin the priming process. As the pump is on, the coolant level in the reservoir will go down. At this time, the chiller will turn off automatically on the low coolant level and will prompt to ‘add coolant’ on the display. Add coolant into the reservoir until the chiller turns ON by itself. The chiller will continue to turn OFF and ON as the coolant inside the reservoir is consumed and refilled. This cycle will repeat until the coolant in the reservoir is stable and the chiller functions without turning OFF itself. Do not manually turn OFF the chiller any time during priming.
  7. Notice
    While coolant is being supplied to the system in the next step, ensure the fluid level in the Chiller reservoir is maintained. It may be necessary to remove the filter to prevent a coolant backup.
    Check the coolant level in the reservoir after 20 minutes to ensure it is maintained at midpoint and no air exists in the system.
  8. If the PET Chiller parameters need to be set at this time, refer to PET chiller configuration.
Topic ID: id_SL12268596-1212878

Leak and filter inspection

Procedure

  1. Inspect all hose fittings and valves for leaks. If necessary, retighten them with a wrench.
  2. Inspect all connections on the PET/MR Insert manifold and PET Detector connections for leaks. Let the PET Chiller run overnight if possible.
  3. If there are no apparent leaks, but a low flow rate is evident, inspect the wire mesh diffuser and filter. (A clogged diffuser or filter will reduce/impair fluid flow through the PET Chiller.)
    1. Remove the clogged component, and clean it with either deionized water or the chiller fluid only.
    2. After replacing the diffuser and filter in the Chiller, add additional fluid if required.
