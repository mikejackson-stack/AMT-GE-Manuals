About this task

Overview

Before beginning the SPW installation to upgrade an MR750w to a SIGNA PET/MR system:

  • Check the gradient coil connections at each stud location on the magnet.
  • Inspect the gradient coil water lines for signs of leaks.

Preliminary Tasks

About this task

Before beginning any work on the SPW:

  • Retrieve the record of any optional equipment removed from the former SPW.
  • Make sure the Magnet Monitor in still in Service mode. (The monitor will return to Normal mode after 8 hours if not done manually.)

Procedure

  1. Notice
    Two people are needed to install the SPW panel. Both need to set the panel gently into the wall. One must hold and support the panel on the equipment room side while the other installs the bolts that hold the panel at the frame.
    With one Field Engineer (FE) each in the magnet and equipment rooms, lift and put the SPW into position.
    1. While one FE holds the panel in place, the second FE uses an 8 mm socket wrench to attach four bolts at each of the four corners to secure the SPW panel on the magnet room side.
    2. Pull the cryogen lines through the SPW and into the equipment room.
    3. Wind the excess lines and fasten with tie wraps.
  2. In the equipment room, attach the cryogen return line (Run 622) first and cryogen supply line (Run 621) next, through J110 and J111 on the SPW to the cryogen cooler compressor.
  3. On the magnet room side, use an 8 mm socket wrench to attach the remainder of the 20 bolts to secure the SPW.
    Note

    The FE on the equipment room side may need to shift and adjust the SPW to align the holes in the SPW to the frame for the bolts.

  4. On the magnet room side, use a 10 mm wrench to attach the ground cables to the SPW with the nuts from the removed SPW.

Magnet Room Side

About this task

The Magnet Room gradient cables, Runs M3317, M3318, and M3419, are delivered to the site preterminated at one end for connection to the rear of the magnet. The other ends of the gradient cables are routed to the SPW cut to length, and terminated.

Procedure

  1. In the magnet room:
    1. Use a 7 mm wrench to remove the coldhead power filter cover from the SPW.
    2. Use a 9 mm open-end wrench to attach the wiring harness to the caps and post on the new SPW in this arrangement:
      • F1: Blue
      • F2: Brown
      • F3: Black
      • F4: Green
      Note

      The wire insulation (1) may require trimming to fit the new alignment. Avoid cutting the wires.

    3. Replace the coldhead filter and cover. The strain relief on the wiring harness may need to be attached with a cover screw.
  2. Install all cables, hoses, and so on as described in GE3.0T LC Magnet and Cryogen Subsystem (5670009) (for Discovery MR750w).
  3. Attach the:
    • Green and yellow ground cables at the bottom of the SPW.
    • J116 and J117 cables (black) to the location labeled on the SPW.
  4. Power up the cryogen cooler compressor at the HEC. (Reading should be between the 16.5 to 17.0 bar on the compressor.)
  5. Return the Magnet Monitor to Normal mode.
  6. Using two 7/16 inch or 11 mm open-end wrenches:
    • One FE holds each bolt on an RF cable on the magnet room side of the SPW.
    • Second FE attaches the nuts, washers, and lock washers onto each bolt on the equipment room side.
  7. Use a flat-blade screwdriver to reattach the DB9 connection at J123 at the coaxial switch.
  8. Fasten the following RF cables to the coaxial switch: J120, J121 (other end attached to J128), and J122.
  9. With an Allen wrench, attach the three blue ground cables to the two grounding pegs on the GEN2 gradient filter.
  10. Attach the six cable sets to the GEN2 gradient filter; single posts are positive and double posts are negative. Torque each socket head cap screw (SHCS) to 45 N m, using a 3/8 inch hex adapter.
  11. Store excess gradient cables in accordance with Storing excess gradient cables.
  12. Reattach the blue J100 and black J101 hoses to their corresponding coupler.
  13. Reinstall the gradient filter covers.
  14. Use a large flat-blade screwdriver to attach the clamp onto the hose at J106.
  15. Notice
    Clean the air filter with compressed air or shop vacuum if the filter is dirty before replacing it in the hose in the next step.
    Inspect and clean (with a vacuum) or replace the air filter in the hose that connects to J105, and use a flat-blade screwdriver to attach the clamp onto the hose at J105.
  16. Run the PET Chiller hoses (gray, J301 and yellow, J302) through the SPW to the shutoff valve at the rear of the magnet.

Equipment Room Side

About this task

The Equipment Room gradient cables (Runs E3317, E3318, and E3419) are delivered to the site preterminated at one end for connection to the PGR cabinet. The other ends of the gradient cables are routed to the SPW (Secondary Pen Wall), cut to length, and terminated. If the equipment room gradient cables are pre-terminated only on the PGR end, refer to Stripping gradient cables and Crimping gradient cables depending on the gradient filter supplied.

Procedure

  1. Attach J128 and J129 RF cables on the SPW:
    1. Rotate the collar to insert the bolts before installing the RF cables.
    2. Make sure the pins on the SPW cables mesh with the cables from the other side.
  2. Attach the gradient filter ground and power cables.
  3. Attach the six cable sets to the GEN2 gradient filter, single posts are positive and double posts are negative. Torque each SHCS (Socket Head Cap Screw) to 45 Nm, using a 3/8 in. hex adapter.
  4. Reattach the gradient filter covers.
  5. Reconnect air ducts J105 and J104.
  6. Run the PET Chiller hoses (gray, J301 and yellow, J302) to the shutoff valves at the PET Chiller.

What to do next

Finalization

Make sure all breakers on the HEC are OFF except the one for the cryogen cooler compressor, and remove LOTO from the HEC.
