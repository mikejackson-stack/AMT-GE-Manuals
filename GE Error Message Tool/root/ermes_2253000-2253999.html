ERROR: EM_PEARL_DOORBELL_DP_FAILED_ACK_OF_INT_CLEAR
Bridge Datapath Diagnostic - Doorbell Interrupt test failed when the master processor failed to acknowledge the interrupt was cleared by the slave processor. Master Proc = %s Slave Proc = %s Doorbells = 0x%08x Poll Attempts = %d
Extended Error:
The interrupt was issued to the slave processor side of the Bridge module. An ISR on the slave processor should have been called when the doorbell was issued and then detected by the slave processor. The ISR clears the corresponding doorbell interrupt. Meanwhile, the master processor is polling the doorbell interrupt's status bit, checking for a cleared interrupt. After several polling attempts, the master processor failed to detect the interrupt was cleared, resulting in an error.
