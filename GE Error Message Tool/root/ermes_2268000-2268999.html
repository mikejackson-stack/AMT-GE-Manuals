2268225

ERROR: EM_PDU_INT_FAIL
The Aux Board is reporting a distortion of one or more of the incoming AC line phases to the PDU or a 48V undervoltage event.
Extended Error:
Check for messages reported at same time describing 48V undervoltage or overvoltage events. If undervoltage or overvoltage events reported confirm Cronus GMC Chassis boards and Auxiliary Module, which receive PS1 power, show active power LED indicators. If power LEDs inactive then cycle Gradient 48V Control circuit breaker on front of PDU. If cycling required or if LEDs are OK but undervoltage and overvoltage messages are seen then the 48V PDU PS1 power supply is exhibiting degraded performance. Replace at next earliest convenience. If LEDs are OK and no undervoltage or overvoltage messages seen then check quality of each incoming power line phase to PDU.
