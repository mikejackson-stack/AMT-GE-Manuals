2245043

ERROR: EM_ACGDT_SGAPS_POWER_OFF_FAULT
GRADIENT DRIVER TESTS - The GP is reporting SGA-PS Power Supply Power Off Fault. %b
Extended Error:
Please check MRconfig.cfg file to determine the amplifier type, 8915-ACGD, 8916-HFD. ACGD MESSAGE: Check the ACGD Power (or ACGD 420V Power) breaker on the PDU. If breaker is tripped, replace the SGA Power Supply. Notice: Do not attempt to reset the breaker! Press the EMO Reset Button on the PDU. Check J2 on the rear of the PDU for continuity between pins 7 and 8. Check phase rotation by confirming that the gradient fan is rotating correctly, or that the Phase OK" light is lit for newer PDUs. Else, replace the SGA Power Supply. HFD MESSAGE: Check the ACGD 420V Power breaker on the PDU. If breaker is tripped, replace the SGA Power Supply. Notice: Do not attempt to reset the breaker! Press the EMO Reset Button on the PDU. Check J2 on the rear of the PDU for continuity between pins 7 and 8. Check the "Phase OK" light on the front of the PDU. Else, replace the SGA Power Supply."

Extended Error:
Check tuning in gradient_tune.dat file. Swap amplifier axis by swapping cables between the GP and HFA (between the failing axis and a passing axis), and also swapping gradient output leads on the same two Gradient Amplifiers. If the problem moves, replace the HFA or HFA Control Board (Boards can be swapped with another axis to isolate HFA vs. Control Board). Else, check the gradient coil resistance to ground or replace the GP. While this error could indicate a hardware failure, please also check that HFA Control Boards are installed in the front of each gradient amplifier. Using SGA Control Boards instead of HFA Control Boards will cause this error.
