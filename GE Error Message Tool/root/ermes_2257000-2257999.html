2257273

ERROR: EM_UPM_PREDICT_SAR_TEMP_SAR_ERROR
The predicted SAR downloaded was above the temperature de-rated SAR limit. Please lower the scan room ambient temperature Predicted SAR = %d.%d SAR Limit = %d.%d Scan Room Temp = %d.%d degrees C
Extended Error:
The SAR limits are adjusted based on the temperature read by the scan room temperature sensor. For each degree above 23.5 degrees C, the SAR limit is reduced by 0.25. If the predicted SAR of a given scan is expected to exceed the SAR limit imposed by the scan room ambient temperature, the scan will be inhibited. The most likely cause of this error is the scan room air conditioning not being set to a low enough temperature. This could also be caused by a faulty scan room air temperature sensor.
