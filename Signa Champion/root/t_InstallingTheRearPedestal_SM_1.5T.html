Installing the rear pedestal

About this task

Procedure

  1. Notice
    If without dollies, the weight of the rear pedestal requires at least two people.
    Move the rear pedestal near to the magnet.
    Note Do NOT handle the bar on both sides.
  2. Fix the rear pedestal to the front bridge by installing four M10 hex nuts (2109877-3) and washers (2228717-2)
  3. Lock the rear pedestal to the the magnet by installing screws and washers on both left and right sides.
  4. Insert the flexible cables into the the hole of flexible cable holder and the hole of front bridge, then install the screws 1 > 2 > 3to fix the flexible cable holder and the flexible cable onto the front broidge.
  5. Route the drive belt and fix by two screws (1107-M4C016-40).
  6. Check belt tension by closing tension lever.
    Note Tension lever should lock crisply into position without excessive force (which could cause later problems). If adjustment is necessary, loosen clamp and adjust position of belt one tooth at a time until desired tension is obtained.
  7. Instal the belt cover by 4 screws (1107-M4C020-40) and trim excess belt length..
  8. (For new rear pedestal installation) Remove the sensor flag from the defective part and install on new rear pedestal.
    1Sensor Flag
  9. Connect the blue cable with label (MAG LOAD 5872437-2) to Dummy load on right side of rear pedestal.
  10. Connect all cables on the left side of rear pedestal.
