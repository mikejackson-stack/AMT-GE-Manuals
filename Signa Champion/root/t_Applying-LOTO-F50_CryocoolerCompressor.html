Applying LOTO - Cryocooler Compressor

Apply LOTO to the cryocooler compressor breaker on the MDP when servicing this item. Applying LOTO ensures a safe environment when servicing the F50 cryocooler compressor.

Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.
Warning
Electrocution hazard
Dangerous and/or fatal voltages are present in the energized cabinet.
Follow LOTO steps to disable and make sure voltage levels are safe.

Procedure

  1. Notify affected personnel that LOTO will be applied.
  2. Turn off the Internal Switch and Main Power Switch on the cryocooler compressor.
  3. On the MDP, turn off the cryocooler compressor breaker.
  4. Make sure the Cryocooler Compressor indicator light is off.
  5. Apply LOTO to the cryocooler compressor breaker on the MDP.
  6. Wait 5 minutes for any stored energy to dissipate.
  7. Remove the screws that secure the cryocooler compressor power cable cover and slide it off of the cryocooler compressor.
  8. Make sure power is not reaching the cryocooler compressor by measuring the voltage at the test points on the cryocooler compressor.
  • Removing LOTO - Cryocooler Compressor
    Remove LOTO from the cryocooler compressor breaker on the Main Disconnect Panel (MDP) before restoring cryocooler compressor power.
