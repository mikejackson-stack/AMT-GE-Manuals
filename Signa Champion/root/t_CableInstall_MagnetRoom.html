Installing cables in the magnet room

Topic ID: id_SL2125439-1178811

Installation of Integrated System Cabinet (ISC) cables

Procedure

  1. Make sure the cables are already routed.
  2. Route and clamp the cables for ISC.
  3. For P5001 cable harness at ISC side, there is a 1m length of protection cover for shipment. Remove this cover.
  4. Optical cable (P2011) to PHPS 2 J37/38 to PHPS 2.
    1. Loosen 2 captive screws under the screw cover of ICN.
      Note This step is needed to make cable access space at rear side.
    2. Slowly move forward the ICN approximately 1 inch (2.5 cm) not to force the rear cable.
    3. At magnet room side, remove ICN rear access cover by loosening the screws.
    4. Remove four screws at wave guide.
    5. Tilt the wave guide and route optical cable (P2011) through wave guide
    6. Connect optical cable (P2011) to PHPS2 J37/38.
      Note If the space is too narrow, the ICN can be moved forward.
    7. Restore the wave guide, ICN rear access cover, and ICN front screws.
  5. Install the cables to ISC by referring to cable map.
