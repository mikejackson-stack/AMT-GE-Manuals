Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

Warning
ELECTROCUTION HAZARD!
Dangerous and or fatal voltages are present in the energized cabinet.
Follow LOTO steps as given in first section of this document to disable and verify safe voltage levels.
Required conditions
ISC and and F-50SH Cryocooler Compressor Power must be turned OFF. Refer to Applying LOTO - Main Disconnect Panel (MDP)

Procedure

  1. Confirm that ISC cabinet and Cryocooler power is OFF.
  2. Apply LOTO to the ISC. See Applying LOTO - Integrated System Cabinet (ISC).
  3. Read Notice for Servicing Penetration Panel Above the ISC Cabinet carefully, if the Penetration Panel is installed above the ISC cabinet.
  4. Remove 2 protect covers by removing the screws in Equipment Room and Scan Room.
  5. Using two wrenches to disconnect cables Run 624 and Run 623 on penetration panel carefully.
    Note The small filter service wrench is available in the EMI filter FRU package.
  6. Remove 4 Hexagon nuts and washers on the filter connect board and remove the EMI filter assembly from Equipment room.
  7. Install the new Cold Head EMI filter assembly onto the Penetration Panel.
  8. Install back the cables Run 624 and Run 623 carefully with the two wrenches, make sure the cables are connected correctly.
    1Ground cable (Green/Yellow)
    2Scan room connections (cable Run 624)
    3Equipment room connections (cable Run 623)
  9. Install back the protect covers in Equipment room and Scan room.
  10. Remove ISC LOTO. See Removing LOTO - Integrated System Cabinet (ISC).

Finalization

  1. Turn on the MDP Power. Refer to Removing Removing LOTO - Main Disconnect Panel (MDP).
  2. Turn on the Cryocooler power and confirm that Cold Head is operating normally.
  3. Perform Doing a check scan.
