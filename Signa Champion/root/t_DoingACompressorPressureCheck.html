Doing a compressor pressure check

Do a check of the cryocooler compressor supply pressure.

Prerequisites

Personnel requirements
Required personsPreliminary requirementsProcedureFinalization
1-5 minutes-
Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

Procedure

  1. Make sure the cryocooler system is at equilibrium (coldhead at approximately 4.2 K from Magnet Monitor 3).
    Note Do not do a check of the operating (dynamic high-side) pressure if the cryocooler system is not at equilibrium (coldhead is not at approximately 4.2 K). It will not be accurate.
  2. Do a check of the operating (dynamic high-side) pressure of the cryocooler system and compare the pressure to the specifications below.
    Note The supply pressure gauge shows static pressure when the compressor is off and high-side dynamic pressure when the compressor is on. The table below shows the applicable compressor operating (dynamic high-side) pressure range for the compressor and coldhead combination.
    Table 1. Acceptable compressor operating pressure range
    Compressor modelCompressor operating (dynamic high-side) pressure
    F50H and F50SH1.90 to 2.20 MPa (approximate)

    Result

    If the dynamic high-side pressure is within the acceptable range, the task is completed.

  3. If the dynamic high-side pressure is outside the acceptable range:
    1. Turn the cryocooler system off.
    2. Let the system stabilize.
    3. Do a check of the static pressure and compare the pressure to the specifications below.
      Table 2. Compressor pressure specifications
      Coldhead modelCompressor static gas pressure at 20°C (68°F)
      RRDK-408 4K two-stage coldhead1.60 to 1.65 MPa (approximate)
      RDK-408, RDK-408A2, RDK-408A3 4K coldhead1.60 to 1.65 MPa (approximate)
      RDK-408T, RDK-4001.45 to 1.50 MPa (approximate)

      Result

      • If both the dynamic and static pressures are within the acceptable range, the task is completed.
      • If both the dynamic and static pressures are out of range, continue on with the next steps for Adjusting the static pressure on the compressor.
