Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.
DANGER
FATAL SHOCK HAZARD!!
LETHAL VOLTAGES EXIST AT GRADIENT CONNECTIONS. FOLLOW THE STEPS BELOW EXACTLY.
FAILURE TO DO SO COULD RESULT IN SEVERE INJURY OR DEATH.
DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!
TO PREVENT FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK, DISCONNECT POWER FROM THE PDU BEFORE YOU PERFORM THE FOLLOWING PROCEDURES.
PERFORM LOCKOUT/TAGOUT PROCEDURE PER GE OSHA LOCKOUT/TAGOUT REQUIREMENTS 29 CFR 1910.147. DO THIS BY SECURING THE PDU CIRCUIT BREAKER FOR THE SCALABLE GRADIENT CABINET.

About this task

It is recommended that checking Gradient cables connections internal to the System Cabinet be performed one time, approximately 1 year after system installation. The gradient cabling at the System Cabinet and the rear of the magnet should be checked on a yearly basis.

Procedure

  1. System Power must be turned OFF. Refer to Applying LOTO - Integrated System Cabinet (ISC).
  2. Open system cabinet covers by rotating the handlebar.
  3. Check the AC Voltage between PRI-A, PRI-B, and PRI-C (Primary) on PDU front panel and verify that the voltage is under 1VAC.
  4. Check the connections of AC Input at SBPS front panel are tight at all terminal strips.
  5. Remove the system cabinet rear cover (9 screws per each panel, and 6 screws to connect the two panels).
  6. Remove Gradient Cable Cover per following steps.
    1. Loosen 6 screws.
    2. Lift up a little and remove the Gradient Cable Cover #1.
  7. Remove three Gradient Filter Covers by removing two screws per each cover.
  8. Check gradient connections at Gradient Filter Boxes and inspect for arcing. If there is any arcing, replace the Gradient Filter.
  9. Check the gradient cables at the rear of the magnet. Look for cables that may be slapping against the magnet. Also verify that the cable insulation has not started to tear. See Cable Rubbing Against Magnet and Example Of Insulation Issue.
  10. Close the system cabinet covers.
  11. Turn on the system power. Refer to Removing LOTO - Integrated System Cabinet (ISC).
  12. Verify that the system boot up without any fail.
