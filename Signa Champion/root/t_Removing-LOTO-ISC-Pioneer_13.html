Removing LOTO - Integrated System Cabinet (ISC)

Removing locks and tags, and restoring power to the ISC (Integrated System Cabinet) Power Distribution Unit (PDU).

About this task

Notice
Potential B0 drift interference
B0 drift can be affected if the gradient coil temperature is not cooled down.
You must wait 1 hour after restoring power before doing certain calibrations and/or a customer scan.
Note You must wear Arc Flash Suit based on Zero Energy assessment requirement if you need to open the PDU front panel to finish the test.
Topic ID: task_cmd_vcj_g1c

Removing LOTO – ISC

Procedure

  1. Alert necessary personnel that power is being restored.
  2. Remove the lock and tag.
  3. Go to the MDP and discharge the stored energy by rotating the INTEGRATED SYSTEM CABINET main breaker labeled INPUT counter-clockwise to the red On (I) position.

    Then Press the green ISC POWER On button.

  4. Go to the PDU in ISC, press the green EMO RESET button.
    Note Make sure the Main Breaker is on the red ON (I) position.
  5. Boot Signa and log on to scanning level.
