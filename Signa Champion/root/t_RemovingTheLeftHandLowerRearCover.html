Removing the magnet left-hand lower rear cover

Removes the lower cover from the left-hand rear corner of the magnet.

Prerequisites

Personnel requirements
Required personsPreliminary requirementsProcedureFinalization
1-10 minutes-
Tools and test equipment
ItemQuantityPart numberManufacturer
Nonmagnetic Titanium Service Tool Kit, Small Set15113258-
Notice
Magnetic Field
If the patient fan is installed on left hand lower rear cover, keep the cover away from the magnet to avoid part failure.

Procedure

  1. Remove six screws securing the left-hand lower rear cover to the magnet.
  2. Remove the cover from the magnet.
