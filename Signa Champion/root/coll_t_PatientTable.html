Required conditions
Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

About this task

Note To raise the table height, turn the pad stud to anticlockwise direction. To reduce the Table Height, turn the pad stud to clockwise direction.

Procedure

  1. Remove the support pad.
  2. Remove cross ribs across the length of the cradle.
  3. Remove cross ribs fixing the cable.
  4. Remove the P1 cable.
  5. Remove the P3 cable:
    1. Cut the cable ties securing the P3 cable in place on the cradle section.
    2. Remove four screws and remove the P3 port connector.
  6. Do the following steps to remove the egress mechanism.
    1. Remove the swivel link by removing three screws.
    2. Remove the O-ring.
    3. Remove the guide block by removing four screws.
      1Swivel link
      2Guide block
      3O-ring
  7. Remove the P4 cable alignment block to release the PG cable and respiratory tube, which are both part of P1/P3 cable track.
  8. Do the following steps to align the cable, and remove the o-ring and shoulder screw.
    1. Move the link rod up so that the P3 cable and air tubes can pass through.
    2. Align the P3 cable and air tubes with the other cables.
    3. Remove the O-ring.
    4. Remove the shoulder screw.
      1Link rod
      2Shoulder screw
      3O-ring
  9. Move the egress mechanism as needed to allow for P1/P3 cable track removal.
    1. Move the assembly to make space for cable removal.
    2. Move the group 1 cables to the back.
      1Assembly
      2Group 1 cables
      The turns of the O-ring for removal are shown below.
  10. Remove the P2 cable.
  11. Remove the PE cables set.
  12. Remove the P3 cable connector, if not removed.
  13. Remove the P4 cable guide, if not removed.
  14. Remove the P4 cable connector.
  1. Loosen the belt tension with belt tension lever.
  2. Mark on the drive belt at the edge of the clamp with white pen so that the belt tension can be restored after the replacement.
  3. Remove the clamps by removing 4 screws.
ISR Plumbing
  1. Remove two screws securing the ISR plumbing to the magnet rear panel.
  2. Locate ISR plumbing to the ground, disconnect main water in and water out hoses from the top of ISR plumbing.
    Note Put a tank under the water hose when disconnect water hose, as some water will fluid out.
  3. Locate ISR plumbing to the ground, disconnect water hoses of BRM2 from the ISR plumbing.
    Note Put a tank under the water hose when disconnect water hose, as some water will fluid out.
  4. Disconnect the water hoses of BRM2 from the ISR plumbing

  6. Undock the table and move it away from the magnet.
  7. Press and hold the dock pedal to override the primary latch and push the cradle approximately 80 mm off the tabletop surface.
  8. Push and hold the rod button and pull the cradle approximately 150 mm off the tabletop surface to expose the tabletop rear cover.
Raising Bellows

    1. Remove the six screws securing the bottom of the bellows cover; three on the left-hand side and three on the right-hand side.
    2. Lift up the bellows cover and secure it in the up position with six strips of Velcro.
