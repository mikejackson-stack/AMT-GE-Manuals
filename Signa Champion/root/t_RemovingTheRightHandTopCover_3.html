Removing the magnet right-hand top cover

Removes the cover from the top of the right-hand side of the magnet enclosure.

Prerequisites

Personnel requirements
Required personsPreliminary requirementsProcedureFinalization
2-10 minutes-
Tools and test equipment
ItemQuantityPart numberManufacturer
Nonmagnetic Titanium Service Tool Kit, Small Set15113258

Procedure

  1. Remove the two screws (#6, #7) securing the right-hand top cover to the magnet.
  2. Lift the cover upward to remove it from the magnet.
    1. The hook of the right hand top cover (#5) is hung on the T-shape bracket.
    2. The other brackets (#1~#4) is plugged into the covers.
