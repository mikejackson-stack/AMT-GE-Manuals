Removing LOTO - Global Operator Cabinet (GOC) and Operator Workspace

Remove LOTO from the GOC and operator workspace before restoring power to these items.

Procedure

  1. Alert necessary personnel that power is being restored.
  2. Remove the lock and tag.
  3. Open plastic door over the front of the PDU breakers and switch on HOST Breaker on the PDU.
