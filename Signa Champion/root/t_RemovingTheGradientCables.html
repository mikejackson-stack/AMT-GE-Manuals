Removing the gradient cables

Remove the gradient cables in the scan room and equipment room.

Topic ID: task_k41_srr_vfc

Remove the gradient cables (off the wall systems)

Procedure

  1. Make sure that you have read and understood the electrical safety requirements before starting this procedure. See Electrical Safety.
  2. Shut down and power off the host computer. See Shutting down/powering off the host computer.
  3. Apply LOTO to the ISC. See Applying LOTO - Integrated System Cabinet (ISC).
  4. From the scan room side, remove the gradient filter cover screws and cover.
  5. Loosen the cable braid and insulation brackets to remove the gradient cable from the filter.
  6. From the equipment room side of the gradient filter, remove the plastic gradient filter terminal covers and remove the terminal cables. Use the two wrench technique to remove the cables.
  7. Loosen the clamp and remove the gradient cable from the top of the magnet busbar.
  8. If the replacement cable is in the equipment room, loosen the cable braid and insulation brackets to remove the gradient cable from the filter.
  9. Loosen the clamp and remove the gradient cable from the top of the ISC.
  10. Use the nonmagnetic ladder to reach the overhead tray and remove the cable ties securing the gradient cable.
  11. Remove the gradient cable from the tray and discard.
