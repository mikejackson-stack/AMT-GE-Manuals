System Power OFF and Confirmation

Procedure

  1. Perform LOTO for ISC. Refer to LOTO for ISC.
  2. Confirm the followings.
    1. ICC Control box display is OFF.
Topic ID: task_abr_5cf_1dc

Flushing Procedure

Procedure

  1. Open ISC cabinet left door.
  2. Pull off the Velcro strips, and remove the Hoist Beam.
  3. Remove the Leak Sensors, refer to Leak Sensor Replacement.
    Note Just removing water tray is ok for Leak Sensor (Tray1) and (Tray2).
  4. Disconnect all the quick disconnect connectors to the water manifold.
    Notice
    Use towel to absorb any leakage of the coolant. Do not spill the coolant over the leak sensor.
    Notice
    When disconnect the hoses, disconnect quickly to avoid the excessive spilling of coolant.
  5. Slide out Water Manifold Plate.
    1. Loosen screws.
    2. Slide out Water Manifold assy.
  6. Setup for flushing as following steps.
    1. Connect drain hose to the right side quick disconnect connector of SSSA (Z).
    2. Insert drain hose to flexible tank.
  7. Repeat flushing setup for the left side quick disconnect connector of SSSA (Z).
  8. Drain SSSA and SSSPS module, refer to Draining Operation before SSSA or SSSPS Replacement.
  9. Disconnect the hose from SSSA (Z) quick disconnect connector.
Topic ID: task_pbn_zvg_1dc

Restoration

Procedure

  1. After Replacement is done, restore the water manifold.
    1. Restore Water Manifold Plate.
    2. Restore Quick Disconnect connectors.
  2. Restore Leak Sensors. Do not forget to connect sensor cables.
Topic ID: id_15802137

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Restore the System power. Refer to Removing LOTO - Integrated System Cabinet (ISC).
  2. Checking coolant level and filling.
  3. Wait till pressure gauge of ICC moves and stay stable.
