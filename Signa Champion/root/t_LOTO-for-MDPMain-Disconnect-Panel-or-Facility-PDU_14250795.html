Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.
Warning
Electrocution hazard
Dangerous and/or fatal voltages are present in the energized cabinet.
Follow LOTO steps to disable and make sure voltage levels are safe.

About this task

The power to the entire system can be removed by locking out/tagging out (LOTO) the Main Disconnect Panel (MDP) or Facility MDP. Prior to performing work on the MDP, the steps in this LOTO procedure must be completed by a LOTO Authorized GE Field Engineer (FE). Completing all steps in this procedure will ensure a safe environment when working on these parts.

Power down before turning off main breaker

About this task

System Power Off, under Access tab, is for customer use since it will require one hour at the most to power down the system to cool down gradient coil temperature, and reduce B0 drift in customer scan level. For service LOTO, use system shutdown for immediate shutdown. However, note that it will be required to wait 1 hour after restoring power for certain calibration and/or customer scan.
Topic ID: id_SL14015128-1191264

System shutdown when system is in operator level

Procedure

  1. To prepare for shutdown of equipment, notify affected personnel working in the area the LOTO is being performed.
  2. From the Common Service Desktop, select Utility > System Shutdown and click Shutdown to shutdown the PC and ICN.
Topic ID: id_SL14015130-1191264

System shutdown when system is in root level

Procedure

  1. Notify affected personnel that LOTO will be applied.
  2. Right-click on the Linux desktop to access the Root Menu and then select Logout.
  3. Select Menu > Shutdown on the Welcome login screen to shutdown the system.
  4. Make sure the system is shut down.
Topic ID: id_SL2889736-1191264

Lockout/tagout for MDP or facility PDU

About this task

Note You must wear Arc Flash Suit based on Zero Energy assessment requirement if you need to open the PDU front panel to finish the test.

Procedure

  1. Notify affected personnel that LOTO will be applied.
  2. Make sure the host computer is shut down.
  3. Open ISC door.
  4. Use DVM check the voltage between each two of the three contact points (for both E-STOP & GRADIENT CONTACTOR) on PDU, make sure the voltage of GRADIENT contactor is around 420V, and E-STOP contactor is around 208V.
    Note This step is to guarantee that secondary input is functioning.
    Note Make sure the Main Breaker is in the Red ON (I) position.
  5. Make sure you turn off the Internal Switch and Main Power Switch on the cryocooler compressor.
  6. Go to the MDP, rotate the INTEGRATERD SYSTEM CABINET & Cryocooler Compressor main breakers labeled INPUT counter-clockwise to the green Off (O) position. Then make sure the INTEGRATERD SYSTEM CABINET & Cryocooler Compressor indicator lights are off.
  7. Turn off the system power.
  8. Turn off the main breaker and apply LOTO to the MDP main breaker or facility disconnect.
  9. Wait 5 minutes for any stored energy to dissipate.
  10. Remove the screws that secure the cryocooler compressor power cable cover and slide it off of the cryocooler compressor.
  11. Make sure power is not reaching the cryocooler compressor by measuring the voltage at the test points on the cryocooler compressor.
  12. Restore the cryocooler compressor power cable cover.
  13. Go to the PDU in ISC, use DVM check the voltage between each two of the three contact points (for both E- STOP & GRADIENT CONTACTOR) on PDU, make sure that the voltage is under 1VAC.
    Note This step is to guarantee the input energy from MDP is measured.
    Note Make sure the Main Breaker is in the Red ON (I) position.
