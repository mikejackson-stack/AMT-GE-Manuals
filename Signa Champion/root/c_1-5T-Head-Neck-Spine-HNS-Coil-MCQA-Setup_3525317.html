Required Conditions
The appropriate coil configuration file must be installed to run this tool. See “Section 4 - Coil Config” of the HNS Coil Installation Procedure document.

Overview

Follow this process to prepare for the MCQA test.

Note The coil consists of five units: Posterior Head, Face, Chest, Horseshoe, and TL. This is done to ensure modularity in design and diagnostics. The MCQA test for the coil is conducted to three configurations: TL unit only, Posterior Head Chest Horseshoe TL, and Posterior Head Face.
Note Coils do not ship with phantoms. Phantoms come in a unified phantom set with the MR system.

HNS Unified Phantom MCQA Setup

Mode 1: Posterior Head Face Section

  1. Place Posterior Head Face section on the table.
  2. Place HNS positioner and then place large cylindrical unified phantom on the positioner.
  3. Carefully align the face component part of the coil with the connector pins over this posterior head face section and latch both the coil sections into place. (The scanner will not operate if the coil sections are not latched correctly.)
  4. Connect the coil to the system LPCA.
  5. Landmark the coil on the markings present on the face component indicated through arrows and press Advance to scan.
  6. Perform the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance Tool test.
    Note In the MCQA tool, select 1.5T_HNS_FP_by_GE_PosteriorHead_Face.Unified.
    Notice: If the wrong coil database selection is made, MCQA will not be able to proceed.

Mode 2: Posterior Head Chest, Horseshoe and TL Section

  1. Place the Posterior Head Face section, horseshoe adapter, and TL section of the coil on table.
  2. Place the HNS phantom Positioner, large cylindrical unified phantom, and TL unified phantom. The TL unified phantom shall be positioned over the junction of the TL section and head components.
  3. Ensure that TL unified phantom is in contact with the cylindrical unified phantom.
  4. Connect the chest part to the posterior head section.
  5. Landmark on the markings over the chest section of the coil.
  6. Press Advance to scan.
  7. Perform the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance Tool test.
    Note In the MCQA tool, select 1.5T_HNS_FP_by_GE_PostHeadChestHorseshoeTL.Unified if it is a Silent HNS coil , otherwise select 1.5T_L_HNS_FP_by_GE_PostHeadChestHorseshoeTL.Unified.
    Notice: If the wrong coil database selection is made, MCQA will not be able to proceed.

Mode 3: TL Section

  1. Place the TL section of the HNS coil on the table.
  2. Place TL unified phantoms on the TL section of the coil.
  3. Align the TL unified phantoms properly in S/I direction and R/L direction of the TL section of the coil.
  4. Connect the coil to Port A of system LPCA.
  5. Landmark on #5 present on the side of the TL section of the coil and press Advance to scan.
  6. Perform the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance Tool test.
    Note In the MCQA tool, select 1.5T_HNS_by_GE_TL.Unitied.
    Notice: If the wrong coil database selection is made, MCQA will not be able to proceed.
